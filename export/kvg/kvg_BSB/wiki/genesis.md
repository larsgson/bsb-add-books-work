# Genesis 1–30: what the team's text changed

The Kuni team's Genesis 1–30 (871 verses, the same translators as Ruth and Jonah, published by
the team; `import/non-free/kvg/KVG-KuNT/text/gen001.txt`–`gen030.txt`) joined the corpus on
15.10.2026. Before that the store's OT terms rested on the NT, Ruth and Jonah, and on our own
Genesis 1 test draft (`_eval/genesis-test/gen001.txt`, chrF 65.8; `_eval/gen001_report.md`).
This page lists what Genesis corrected. Each correction is a store entry with `supersedes`
(the old entry drops out of the verse packs); the generated block at the end shows them.

**For Genesis 31–50 read this page first**, then [OT narrative](ot-narrative.md) (the formulas)
and [looking ahead](genesis-31-50.md) (the terms the next chapters need).

## The ten corrections that matter most

1. **YHWH is Evezøza.** Genesis has Evezøza in 153 verses and Yawe in none; Yawe is Ruth's
   alone. The open question of term:H3068.h is closed (term:H3068.g, name:yahweh.g). "LORD God"
   is "Evezøza Mbumbukiam" (17 verses, GEN 2:7). Ruth's formulas with Yawe are superseded.
2. **The day formula** (gen001_report, point 1): "Yaq qapøis, vøndōpave." (night fell, day
   dawned), then "Ndakin khøuwa gè-menás." (now it is the second day) with gè- + the numeral
   (GEN 1:8–31); the first day is "Khøuwa qa-kopo, nqánek" (1:5). Not the NT's
   *mø̀ndømø-N-éz*. Elsewhere "on the third day" is "khøuwa misika-te ndøgo" (22:4), and dates
   keep *gimø-N-ez* (7:11, 8:4). See ordinal.g on [counting](grammar/existence-naming-counting.md).
3. **Day as daylight is paveat** (gen001_report, point 2): "God called the light *paveat*"
   (1:5); "forty days and forty nights" = "paveat foti-a, lova foti" (7:12). khøuwa stays the
   day as a unit of time.
4. **The heavens are khaiya**, heaven as God's place is **yan**: "Ibugukhokhof, Mbumbukiam
   khaiya neká manqei-qape tekhakheinám." (1:1); "from heaven" = yan-te ndo꞉go (21:17, 22:11).
   The expanse is løvøzu (1:6–8). Our draft had these the other way round.
5. **Good is qanimáv** in the refrain "Mbumbukiam āv geneqeív nqǽgo, ndøgo qanimáv." (1:10–25),
   very good "qanimáv tantáv" (1:31); "and it was so" = "Yaq matev āv tønefakhán." (6×).
6. **Bless** is the verb *khakheinøqav-* for "bless you" (bèkhakheinøqavém "may he bless you",
   14:19, 28:3; ndæ̀khakheinøqavém "I will bless you", 12:2, 22:17) beside the hand-laying
   *zenda … ve* of a father's blessing (27:4, 27:27); the blessing itself is "zenda awam matev"
   (27:36). "Blessed be the LORD" = "Evezøza-qa iz, yàbeto-eqawát" (9:26, 24:27). The Ruth gap
   "no optative of khakheinøv" is closed.
7. **Offspring is -zi with a possessor**: "qogi qazi" (your offspring, 12:7, 13:15, 15:5,
   22:17), "zogi zøzi" (9:9), "tegi zi" (5:1). Ruth's *nakheis* is children.
8. **Brother is eveqase** (his brother, 4:8; qaveqase your brother, 4:9), brothers
   **zapikia-za** (9:25; Joseph's brothers in ACT 7:13), a woman's brother **anganømeo**
   (24:29), a man's sister **sævømiu** (20:2). The NT's *nøfu* is a fellow believer and a polite
   address ("Nǿfuap" to strangers, 29:4).
9. **Father is eve** with the possessor (tege eve, 25:19; tugé eve, 29:12); *tat* is "my father"
   and the address "Tát!" (27:34). Mother is evo. Love between people is **poev** (24:67, 29:20),
   not the NT's *kuku* (grace, kindness).
10. **The team explains in the text.** Name meanings follow in brackets, "(Taqa manqa mokho:
    …)" 31 times (feature name_meaning_brackets); compressed verses are expanded (1:2 runs to
    seven short sentences; gen001_report point 3: our 1:2 scored 38.6). Draft accordingly.

Other changes: silver is *silva*, money *moni* (term:H3701.g); wages and reward *yaq-fia*
(29:15); the birthright "yo-khøuwa touzu iz" (25:31–34); firstborn *yo-khøuwa*; curse "soqain-té
qatǿvé" (3:14) and the wish *bèsoqoév* (9:25); create = make = *khakhein-*; the earth
*manqei-qape*, a land *manqei*, dry land *manqei pap*; the book title is **Iqandengaz**
(heading of the team's Genesis), not *Ibugukhokhof* ("In the beginning").

## Creation vocabulary (Gen 1)

Our Genesis 1 draft's terms were each checked against the team's verse; most changed:
separate *qat-* (tøqatatat, 1:6), formless and void "bugug eqeieqei mbaín" (1:2), vegetation
*avøeavøe* and seed-bearing plants "gagayag nanga-us" (1:11), seed *nanga*, fruit trees "tae
nanga-us", rule over the day *nøzøwat* (1:16) and over animals *kawaezat* (1:26, 1:28), signs
*khakhatap* (1:14; also the sign of the covenant, 9:12), teem and fill *khaz-* (1:20, 1:28),
image *bugug* (1:26–27), "let there be" *betéfakhan* (1:6), "let us make" *zíkhakheinøvém*
(1:26), "be fruitful" *vø̀mønøzumít* and "multiply" "bawan bezø̄-khouwev" (1:28). What stayed:
*pipisi* birds, *khandi gigiap* living creatures, "søvakha gigiap nanak" livestock, "iz … mbo-akhá"
for naming, "anganeam neká sævam, tèkhakheinøvém" (male and female he created them, 1:27).

Quotations on this page are from the Kuni team's Genesis (Mbumbukiam-qa Manqat Mbomambaqape,
Kuni; credit in `../CREDITS.md`).
