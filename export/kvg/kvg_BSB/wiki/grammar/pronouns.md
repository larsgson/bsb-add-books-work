# Pronouns and demonstratives

- no (I), qo (you sg), ndego (he), ndugu (she), ni (we), zo (you pl), ndigu (they):
  Luk 1:18-19, 1:38, 2:48-49, 15:17-19.
- ngenek (this man), ngunuk (this woman), nginik (these people): Luk 1:36 "ngunuk sóg",
  Mat 21:10 "ngenek", Luk 1:6 "Nginik ezoqa menas".
- nqanek (this thing), ndøgo (that), "ta X ndøgo" (that X): Luk 1:20, 2:4 "Ta vemiav-qa iz".
- "ndigu ezoqa menas" (the two of them, Luk 1:7); "ekezan mba" (by himself, Luk 1:62 gloss).

## In Ruth and Jonah (human, 2015)

- Demonstratives confirmed: "Ngenek ezoqam" (this man, RUT 1:2), "ngunuk sævam" (this woman,
  4:11), "nginik sakhei menas" (these two women, 1:19), "nqanek khae" (this field, 2:3), "ta
  khøuwa-te ndøgo" (in those days, 1:1). As the subject of a new scene the human often picks up
  the referent with one of them: "Nginik nakhei angana menas" (these two sons, 1:4).
- The subject pronoun repeats a named subject for contrast or a new turn: "Yaq ndugu matavap
  āv gunivé nqǽgo" (RUT 1:6), "Ndego Boaz pusa naqanøká gétayák" (3:18).
- 'also, even' "até X-a" confirmed: "Até zogí søvakha gigiáp-a" (even your animals, JON 3:7),
  "Até qó-a" (you too, RUT 1:15), "até nó-a" (I too, 1:17); 'only' mba: "Ni løvøte mbá
  mé꞉qatanó" (only death will part us, 1:17).

## The team's answer: one "we" (T4, 26.10.2026)

Kuni has one word for "we" (ni); inclusive and exclusive differ only in the subject prefix of the verb: a prefix that
is or contains ni is **exclusive**, one with zi is **inclusive** (claim:we.team). Choose the verb prefix by whether the
hearer is included.

The store follows (2026-10-26): zi- is 1PL.INCL (m:zi-:verb.team, no longer 1.PL.HORT) and ni- 1PL.EXCL
(m:ni-:verb.team). "Our" is nigi, nige with -ge harmonised to -gi (T13), not ni- + plural -gi: kept whole (w:nigi).
