# Speech and formulas

- Framed quote: "X āv gene-eín nqǽgo, “...”" (he said thus, Luk 1:13 with gembøe-, 1:63);
  she: "āv guni-eín nqǽgo" (Luk 1:25, 1:60); they: "āv gini-einím nqǽgo" (Luk 2:15).
- Short: "gembo-ein, “...”" (said to him, Mat 26:49), "gumbo-ein" (to her, Luk 2:34),
  "gezø-ein" (to them), "gimbo-einim" (they said to him/her, Act 12:15), "gezø-qavøiu"
  (he replied to them, Luk 7:22), "gundu-qavøiu" (she replied, Jhn 4:17), "guzø-qavøiu"
  (she replied to them), "gembobevap" (asked him, Luk 1:18), "gezø-akhá" (called them).
- Indirect content with "av nqægo, ..." and macron tones on its verb: "giyogem av nqægo,
  Evezøza tanakh-qapē gumbogo" (Luk 1:58), "ge꞉-otev, av nqægo, ... gìmbøe-khavozømēm"
  (Mat 2:16), "Yaq ndego āv gene-ewág nqǽgo: ..." (Act 20:3).
- A decision: "matavap āv genegó nqǽgo: Zelusalem taon-té genǿwáv" (Luk 9:51).
- Quotes “ ” (inner ‘ ’); a vocative stands first with its own stop: "Méli." (Luk 1:30),
  "Tát!" (Luk 15:12), "Nogi nakheis!" (1Jn 3:18), "Nǿfuap!" (brothers, Act 2:29).
- "Nqáe!" = Look!/Behold (Luk 1:20, 1:36, 2:10). "Gê," opens a question (Luk 1:18);
  "Āv taoká." = No, not so (Luk 1:60, Mat 26:35); "Unimanqatín." = truly.

## In Ruth and Jonah (human, 2015)

The NT frames are confirmed; the OT shows how they are distributed.

- **Short frames dominate**: "Yaq Naomi gumbo-ein, “...”" (RUT 2:2), "Yaq Boaz gezø-ein" (4:2).
  A **reply** takes the hither form: "Lut gundu-ein" (1:16), "Boaz gendo-ein" (2:11), "ndigu
  sasae ezoqam gindu-einim" (2:4), "guzø-qavøiu / gezø-qavøiu" (answered them, 1:20, JON 1:9).
- The long frame **"āv ... nqǽgo"** goes with questions ("āv gembøebeváp nqǽgo", RUT 2:5; "āv
  gimbøe-bevøpém nqǽgo", JON 1:11), prayer ("āv gene-guligulím nqǽgo", JON 4:2), orders ("manqa
  āv gezømbe-løvuá nqǽgo", RUT 2:15) and solemn speech ("āv gezømbe-eín nqǽgo", RUT 4:9).
- A speech can follow a hearing clause: "Yaq Lut nqanek manqat gu-ewag, gundu-ein, “Qanimáv. ...”"
  (3:5), or be summed up afterwards: "Naomi nqanek manqat gu-ein, yaq ..." (1:14).
- **Vocatives** stand first, and a name moves its accent to the first syllable: "Evézøza."
  (JON 1:14, 4:2), "Zíona." (JON 4:4), as NT "Méli." Kin and titles: "Sǽ-pakha." (my
  daughter, RUT 2:2, 3:1, 3:10 ...; **not** Ngólam, which Ruth never uses), "Nogí sæ-pakha-za."
  (my daughters, 1:11, 1:13), "Khanánqa." (sir, 2:13, and for "my friend", 4:1).
- **Interjections** open a reply and are often added: "Yáiyé!" (2:20, 4:17), "Qanimáv." (3:5,
  4:4, 4:11), "Tiá." (no!, 1:10), "Mbaín." (no, 1:11), "Ság." (good / enough, 3:13, JON 4:8),
  "Áe," (there!, in narrative, 3:8), "Éisa," (perhaps, JON 1:6, 3:9).
- **Blessings and wishes**: "Yawe zenda ndò꞉vé qo-te." (the LORD bless you, RUT 2:4), "Yawe
  ndø̀goát zo namba." (the LORD be with you, 2:4, 3:10), "Khanakhanákh Yawe-te, ndego ..."
  (blessed be the LORD who ..., 4:14). The NT's khakheinøv (bless) is not used.
- **Oath**: "No Yawe-qá bugug-té qataqambø-manqaté, ..." (I promise you before the LORD, 3:13);
  self-curse: "..., yaq Yawe kopømbaqapé. Bèngiænó." (1:17).
- 'Tell us' = "Qonimbí-eīn." (JON 1:8); 'tell me' = "sègenø-eín" (RUT 4:4).
- Prayers in Jonah address God in the 2nd person and end with the vocative: "..., qo-té
  qando-okhó, Evezøza." (JON 2:10).

See also [OT narrative and dialogue](../ot-narrative.md), which holds the narrative formulas.

## In Genesis 1–30 (the team, 15.10.2026)

Genesis confirms the frames on OT narrative (speech_frame.g; counts in 871 verses): the long
frame "āv g…-eín nqǽgo, “…”" 64 times, for God's words (GEN 1:3), for a speech to a group and for
solemn or reported speech; the short frames gembo-ein (to him) 44, gezø-ein (to them) 18,
gumbo-ein (to her) 13; she says "āv guni-eín nqǽgo" (29:33); answers with -qavøi- 52
(gembo-qavøiu, gendo-qavøiu); asked = gembobevap (4:9). Speech inside speech in ‘ ’ (24:7).
"Here I am" = "No nqǽya." (22:1); the formulas of narrative are on [OT narrative](../ot-narrative.md).

## The team's answers (26.10.2026)

- **gembo-ein / gembøe-ein** (T9): gembo-ein is "he said to him"; gembøe-ein goes between av and nqǽgo, "he said to him
  like this" (formula:said_to_him.team, formula:said_to_him_thus.team). Copy the corpus's marks (gembøe-eín).
- **vø̄-ein, vø̄-einim, vø̄e-ein** (T12): "and he/she said", "and they said", "and I said"; "até vø̄e-ein(im)" is "he/she
  (they) thought" (formula:and_he_said_seq.team … formula:they_thought.team).
