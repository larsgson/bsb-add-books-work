# Review overview: Kuni Genesis 31–45

Drafted one chapter after another (2026-10-15/16; 41–45 in a second run), each by a fresh agent from a verse pack built after the previous
chapter; the store and wiki were first brought up to date with the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30. These are drafts: every
choice below is for the Kuni team. Decided items become binding for later chapters (and go into the store).

## Mechanical fixes made in the consistency passes

(41–45: none needed; names and terms consistent: Joseph *Zosef*, Egypt *Izip*, the Hebrews *Iblu*, silver *silva* and money *moni*.)


- gen031:3, 13: *nango mòqoveséz* → *nango qonǿveséz*, God's command "return", which 32:10 quotes in the store's form
  (the team's Genesis 3:19); *mòqoveséz* is not in the corpus.
- gen036:26, 39: *Dison* → *Disan*, *Hadad* → *Hada* (the Hebrew's Dishan and Hadar; the Hadad of 36:35–36 stays).

## Open questions from the chapters

Each chapter's notes (`gen/gen0NN_notes.md`) list its least sure verses and its open questions in full; the gaps are on
the wiki's gaps page, and the questions for the team on `wiki/questions.md`.

- **31:** a heap of stones as witness (*khakhatap*, "sign"; no word for heap); the third flock colour; built forms for
  "ten times", the saddlebag, "the way of women". Least sure: v15, 27, 34, 35, 39.
- **32:** hip, dislocation and tendon; built verb forms (*qombøé-eín*, *nótakák*, *mòqo-kopømba-én*).
- **33:** "seven times"; "driven hard", "in my care", "pace" (v13–14); the vocative *Áta!*; Shalem as "safely" or a town
  (v18).
- **34:** how plainly to tell the rape, and *mambe matev* for "defiled"; bride price; intermarry, property; built forms
  in the speeches (v9–23).
- **35:** the drink offering as wine (the Hebrew doesn't name the liquid); midwife; "below" a town. Least sure: v2, 12,
  17–18.
- **36:** *Olibama* or *Oolibama*; King Shaul as *Sol* (the same as Saul?); one title per list or per name; hot springs.
- **37:** "torn to pieces" (v33); the plural prohibitives (v21–22, 27). Least sure: v7, 8, 10, 22, 26, 33, 35.
- **38:** how plainly to tell Onan's act (v9); "your father-in-law"; the breach and the meaning of Perez (v29). Least
  sure: v8, 9, 17, 24, 26, 29.
- **39:** personal attendant (v4); "did not concern himself" as "knew nothing" (v6, 8, 23); confined (v20). Least sure:
  v9, 13, 14, 17.
- **41:** fat and thin cattle; "interpret a dream" still open. Least sure: v16, 21, 32–34, 40, 49.
- **42:** an oath by Pharaoh's life; "the nakedness of the land" (built); "not spies" (*X mbá*, after JHN 1:21); the
  interpreter (a built agent noun). Least sure: v9, 15, 16, 21, 22, 33, 37, 38.
- **43:** surety (v9); "double"; pistachios and almonds; *Ság.* for "peace to you"; "became drunk" (v34).
- **44:** a built "we answered you" form; gray hair to Sheol; "equal to Pharaoh"; "in place of the boy". Least sure: v15,
  18, 20, 29–31, 33–34.
- **45:** "a father to Pharaoh"; a word for cart; built "you sold / sent me" forms; "his spirit revived" (v27). Least
  sure: v7, 8, 11, 19, 27.
- **40:** object marking in v15; "squeezed" built from the winepress noun; the word play "lift up / lift off the head"
  (v13, 19, 20). Least sure: v8, 13, 15, 17, 19.
