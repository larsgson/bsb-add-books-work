# Genesis 48 (gen048), Kuni: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 48 (22 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni,
translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen048.txt`) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30,
Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the
text: `gen048.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

**Style model.** Our Genesis 47 (the same scene: Jacob near death, Joseph at his bed), 35 (Luz, the
promise, Rachel's death on the way to Ephrath) and 46. The team's GEN 27 (Isaac's blindness, the
blessing of the sons, 'my son' *Áti!*), 17:6–8 and 28:3 (the promise), 24:40 (walk before God), 25:32
(about to die), 33:3 (bowed to the ground). NT models: HEB 11:21 (Jacob blessing Joseph's two sons:
*zenda gezømbove*), 2TI 3:11 (rescued me from all evils), ACT 9:19 (strength returned), MAT 12:13
(stretched out his arm), HEB 2:13 (the children God has given me).

Certainty: **high** = the construction is attested as used; **medium** = attested parts put together;
**low** = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked *(built)*.

## Open questions

- v6: 'called by the names of their brothers in their inheritance' told by its meaning; check with the team.
- v14: no word for crossing the hands (שִׂכֵּל); the draft says 'he put his hands this way'.
- v15: 'walked before God' as 'kept his word' (GEN 24:40) and God 'like a shepherd' looking after Jacob.
- v16: 'redeemed' rendered 'rescued from all evil' (2TI 3:11); *nqovotáz* (term:H1350) is still open.
- v21: 'bring you back' as 'you will be again in the land'; a causative form is wanted.
- v22: the ridge (שְׁכֶם) and the pun on Shechem are not kept; 'above your brothers' is weak.
- No YHWH in this chapter.
