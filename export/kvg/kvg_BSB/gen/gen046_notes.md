translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen046.txt`) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30,

**Style model.** Our Genesis 45 and 35–36 (the lists of Jacob's sons and Esau's line), 41:45–52 (Asenath,
Manasseh, Ephraim), the team's Genesis 10, 22, 25–26, 29–30. NT models: ACT 7:14–15 (Jacob's family going
down to Egypt, seventy-five persons), MAT 14:21 (not counted), MAT 28:10 (go and tell), MRK 10:20 (from
my youth), ACT 9:10 and 18:9 (a vision at night), JON 1:8 (occupation). Headings after the BSB's seven,
without tone marks.

Certainty: **high** = the construction is attested as used; **medium** = attested parts put together;
**low** = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked *(built)*.

## Open questions

1. **Names that fall together**: Eli (Eri) with the NT's Eli, Sela (Serah, Asher's daughter) with Sela (Shelah),
   Gela (Gera) with Gela (Gerar); and Zob for יוֹב, the same as the NT's Job. Should they be told apart?
2. **"Joseph will close your eyes"** (v4): rendered by its meaning; is *toqonanim* (when you die) right?
3. **"You are still alive"** (v30): the fixed *zua ngekhandî꞉vát* with *qo*: is there a 2sg form?
4. **The counts** (v15, 18, 26): *teti-tri*, *sikstin*, *siksti-siks* are built.
5. **Jacob's family is 70** (v27) in the Hebrew, 75 in ACT 7:14 (the LXX): kept 70.
6. Least sure: v4, v28, v30, v33.
