# Genesis 49 (gen049), Kuni: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 49 (33 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni,
translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen049.txt`) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30,
Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the
text: `gen049.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

**Style model.** Our Genesis 48 (Jacob's blessing of Ephraim and Manasseh, poetry lines), 47 (the burial wish)
and the team's GEN 27:27–29, 39–40 (Isaac's blessings: *bēfoyamat* bow down, *qógoák* you will not be), 9:25–27
(*bèsoqoév* cursed be, *yàbeto-eqawát* praised be), 25:8–10 (breathed his last, gathered to his people, buried in
the cave of Machpelah) and 23:17–20 (the field bought from the Hittites as a burial place). The blessings are
poetry: images are kept as likenesses (*andé X me*) where the corpus has the animal or thing, and told by their
meaning where it does not (feature image_to_meaning).

Certainty: **high** = the construction is attested as used; **medium** = attested parts put together;
**low** = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked *(built)*.

## Open questions

- v10: Shiloh is read as a name, as the BSB; its note 'the one to whom tribute belongs' is the other reading. Team's choice.
- v4: 'defiled' rendered as 'you did this evil thing' (GEN 4:10); a word for 'make unclean' is wanted.
- v6, v12, v21: no words for hamstring, dark (red) eyes, doe; told by meaning (gaps).
- v8, v16, v19: the Hebrew puns on Judah, Dan and Gad are not explained in brackets (the team gave the meanings of these
  names in GEN 29–30); the team may want a bracket as in GEN 11:9.
- v24–26: the divine titles (Mighty One of Jacob, Shepherd, Rock of Israel, Shaddai) and 'blessings' as 'good things'.
- YHWH: v18 (*Evézøza*, vocative).


## Style check

- Taken: fewer possessives in v11–12, 24 (*teqa*), as the human verses MAT 21:7, MRK 10:16.
- Kept: *andé … me* more often than the human verses: the blessings are a chain of likenesses (lion, donkey, snake, doe,
  vine, wolf); *-us* adjectives (strong, rich, fruitful) where the corpus has no other adjective; fewer tone marks than
  the human chapters (the poetry has many nouns and few verbs).
