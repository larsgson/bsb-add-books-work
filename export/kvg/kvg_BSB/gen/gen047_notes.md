## Not found in the corpus (built or gap)

- Built forms: *qazigeavun* (v4), *tizi-khonab* (v18), *wieáv* (v22), *qoqoni-khandi꞉z*, *qoqonigo* (v25),
  *toqove* (v29), *qonǿ-tonqogím* (v30), *tozombo-etoam* (v24), *foti-sevén* (v28), *qozǿ-vé* (v6).
- Gaps: in exchange for (v16–19, *yaq-fia*), pasture (v4), allotment / rations (v22), faithfulness (v29),
  a prohibitive of 'bury' (v29, turned into 'I do not want you to bury me').

## Open questions

- v21: the Hebrew 'moved them to the cities' (kept) against the BSB's 'reduced to servitude'.
- v29: faithfulness as keeping a promise; 'do not bury me' without a prohibitive.
- v31: *Mbumbukiam-qa iz vø̄-eqatat* adds God to 'bowed (in worship)', after HEB 11:21: keep, or the bowing alone?
- The fifth (v24, 26) and 147 (*foti-sevén*) stay built; no YHWH verse in this chapter.
