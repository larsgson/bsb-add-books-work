# Genesis 50 (gen050), Kuni: notes on the draft

**What this is.** A machine draft of Genesis 50 (26 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni,
translated from the Hebrew (`orig/heb/gen050.txt`) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The
agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30,
Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the
text: `gen050.tagged` (the `.txt` and `.align` are generated from it).

**Style model.** Our Genesis 47:29–31 and 49:29–33 (the burial wish and Jacob's death), the team's GEN 23:2
(*mutøkhop neka eiv matev*, mourning), 25:9 (burial at Machpelah), 27:45 (anger kept), 18:3 (if you are willing
toward me) and 30:2 (*Gê, no Mbumbukiàm?*), and the NT where it tells the same events: HEB 11:22 (Joseph's bones),
ACT 7:16 (the patriarchs carried to Canaan), JHN 19:39–40 (spices and wrapping), MRK 5:38 (loud mourning).

Certainty: **high** = the construction is attested as used; **medium** = attested parts put together;
**low** = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked *(built)*.

## New name forms

- *Atad* (v10–11), *Eibol-Mizlaim* (Abel-mizraim, v11), *Makil* (Machir, v23), all by the letter rules.

## Open questions

- v10–11: 'across the Jordan' is given as 'near the Jordan' (*avønin-te*); the team may want 'on the other side'.
- v11: the name Abel-mizraim ('mourning of the Egyptians') is not explained; a bracket as the team's GEN 11:9 is possible.
- v2, v26: embalming is told as wrapping the body with spices (no word for embalm); v26 coffin as a wooden bier.
- v23: 'born on Joseph's knees' (a sign of adoption) is told as 'Joseph received them on his knees'.
- v5: *qanøveséz* for 'I will return' is attested once and its person is not certain.
- v15–17: 'transgression and sin' as *manqa-zapazapa neka soqa matev*; the plea particle *sà* carries 'please'.
- YHWH: none in this chapter.

## Style check

- Taken: fewer *gi-* plural past verbs (*gindu-einim* as RUT 1:10) and fewer *manqei-te* (*Izip-te*, v22, v26).
- Kept: *gezø-* (he … to them) for Joseph's commands and speeches; *tae* (spices, coffin) and *-qa* (possessives,
  purpose) are content of this chapter; tone marks a little under the human range (19.3 per 100 words vs 19.6).
