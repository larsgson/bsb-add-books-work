## Register
- Narrative past with *Yaq* opening most sentences; short sentences; time phrases as sentences of their own.
- Joseph's brothers *zapikia-za*, one brother *eveqase*; "my father" in speech *tat*, a third person's *eve*.
- "your servants" before Pharaoh *qogi sasae ezoqam* (46:34).

## Open threads of the story
- Jacob lives in Goshen, 147 years old, near death; Joseph has sworn to bury him in Canaan (47:29–31). Gen 48:
  Joseph brings Manasseh and Ephraim; Jacob blesses them (blessing words: term:H1288, hand-laying as 47:7).
