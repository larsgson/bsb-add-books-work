## Open threads of the story
- Jacob has blessed his twelve sons and died in Egypt (49:33), having charged them to bury him in the cave of
  Machpelah (49:29–32). Done in Gen 50: Joseph mourns, the embalming, the burial in Canaan (Pharaoh's leave, the
  funeral at the threshing floor of Atad), the brothers' fear and Joseph's forgiveness, Joseph's death and his
  bones (50:24–26; HEB 11:22 tells it in Kuni).
