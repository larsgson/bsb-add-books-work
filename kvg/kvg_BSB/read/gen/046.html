Iqandengaz (Genesis), chapter 46

Zekop Izip manqei-te genøwav

1 Yaq Izlael, gigiap ate qambøegoam vø̄go, genøwáv. Yaq Besiba taon-te gemøfakhan. Yaq tege eve Aezek-ge Mbumbukiam-te, søvakha gigiap ndølaém.

2 Yaq ta lova ndøgo, Izlael umingiap ndøqeív. Ta mokho-te ndøgo, Mbumbukiam gèmbo-akhá, “Zékop! Zékop!” Yaq ndego gembo-qavøiu, “No nqǽya.”

3 Yaq Mbumbukiam gembo-ein, “No Mbumbukiam ndégo, qoge qave-gé Mbumbukiám. Izip manqei-te qonǿqáv. Møe ndø̀go᷄. Ndǿgo no qazi kopoav-qape nøtaqá-etoám. Tiqa bawan, kandambaqape ndǿgoát.

4 No qo namba, Izip manqei-te qatǿnáv. Neka no nqawa ndø̀tøndo-khatóq, nqanek manqei-te. Neka qo toqonanim ndøgo, Zosef ekezan, qo namba ndǿgoát.”

5 Yaq Zekop Besiba taon gè-iváv. Yaq Izlael-gi nakhei angana, tige eve Zekop, neka tigi nakheis neka tigi sakheis, manqei khagua wil-us-te sasa ndø-ituim. Manqei khagua wil-us ndigu, Felo ndøkhofotáv, ndego itu-qa.

6 Até tigi søvakha gigiap nanak neka gigiap ate qazømbegoam, Keinan manqei-te ndøgo, vø̄go. Yaq Zekop, tegi zi ate ndi꞉gu namba, Izip manqei-té ginøzáv.

Izip manqei-te ginøzav ezoqam

7 Ndego ekeza nakhei angana neka tigi nakhei angana, ekeza nakhei mbasi neka nakhei angana-gi nakhei mbasi, tegi zi ate ndi꞉gu, Izip manqei-té genø-itúb, te namba.

Lia-gi nakheis

8 Izlael-gi nakhei angana, ndigu Izip manqei-té ginøzáv, tiqa iziz nqánek: Zekop neka tegi nakhei angana. Luben, ndego Zekop-ge yo-khøuwa.

9 Luben-gi nakhei angana ngínik: Hanok neka Palu neka Ezlon neka Kami.

10 Simeon-gi nakhei angana ngínik: Zemuel neka Zamin neka Oad neka Zakin neka Zoal neka Sol, ndego Keinan-ak sævam-ge yo.

11 Livai-gi nakhei angana ngínik: Geson neka Koat neka Melali.

12 Zuda-gi nakhei angana ngínik: E neka Onan neka Sela neka Pelez neka Zela. Geté E neka Onan, Keinan manqei-te gipakhaez. Neka Pelez-gi nakhei angana ngínik: Ezlon neka Hamul.

13 Isaka-gi nakhei angana ngínik: Tola neka Puva neka Zob neka Simlon.

14 Zebulon-gi nakhei angana ngínik: Seled neka Elon neka Zaleel.

15 Nginik Lia-gi nakhei angana, ndigu Padan-Alam manqei-te, Zekop gimbo-fakhaz. Neka até tegu yu Daina-a. Ndigu ezoqa ate gi꞉goam, nakhei angana neka nakhei mbasi, teti-tri.

Zilpa-gi nakheis

16 Gad-gi nakhei angana ngínik: Zifion neka Hagi neka Suni neka Ezbon neka Eli neka Alodi neka Aleli.

17 Asa-gi nakheis ngínik: Imna neka Isva neka Isvi neka Belia, neka tigu sævømiu Sela. Neka Belia-gi nakhei angana ngínik: Heba neka Malkiel.

18 Nginik Zilpa-gi nakheis. Zilpa ndugu, Leiben, yu Lia, mbo-etoám. Yaq nginik Zekop gimbo-fakhaz. Ndigu ezoqa ate gi꞉goam, sikstin.

Laisol-gi nakheis

19 Zekop-gu zas Laisol, tugi nakhei angana ngínik: Zosef neka Benzamin.

20 Izip manqei-te ndøgo, Zosef nakhei angana gimbo-fakhaz: Manase neka Eflaim. Asenat, Potifela-gú yú, tu-te gimbofakhaz. Potifela ndego, On taon-ge iziz ezoqam.

21 Benzamin-gi nakhei angana ngínik: Bela neka Beka neka Asbel neka Gela neka Naeman neka Ei neka Los neka Mupim neka Hupim neka Ad.

22 Nginik Laisol-gi nakhei angana, ndigu Zekop gimbo-fakhaz. Ndigu ezoqa ate gi꞉goam, fotin.

Bila-gi nakheis

23 Den-ge yo ngenek: Husim.

24 Naftali-gi nakhei angana ngínik: Zazeel neka Guni neka Zeza neka Silem.

25 Nginik Bila-gi nakhei angana. Bila ndugu, Leiben, yu Laisol, mbo-etoám. Yaq nginik Zekop gimbo-fakhaz. Ndigu ezoqa ate gi꞉goam, seven.

26 Zekop namba ndigu, Izip manqei-té ginøzáv, ndigu tegi zi, ezoqa ate gi꞉goam, siksti-siks. Geté Zekop-gi nakhei angana-gi sakheis, gevebøpøteáv.

27 Neka Zosef-gi nakhei angana menas, ndigu Izip manqei-te ndøgo, Zosef gimbo-fakhaz. Yaq Zekop-gi ezoqam ate ndi꞉gu, ndigu Izip manqei-té ginøzáv, ezoqa ate gi꞉goam, seventi.

Zekop Izip manqei-te gemøfakhan

28 Yaq Zekop Zuda, ekeza megemege-te, Zosef-te ndøkhofotáv. Zosef nakhoa teqamekh, Gosen manqei-te. Yaq ndigu Gosen manqei-te gimøfakhaz.

29 Yaq Zosef ekeza manqei khagua wil-us gekhakheinam. Yaq Gosen manqei-te genøwáv, tege eve Izlael-te. Yaq tege eve-te gemøfakhan ndøgo, sège-møvøiú, vø̄vin. Nupøkhán mbá.

30 Yaq Izlael Zosef gembo-ein, “No qoqa bugug, nøkeza bøí qateqeív. Qo zua ngekhandî꞉vát. Yaq ndakin no kopømbaqapé, tænanim.”

31 Yaq Zosef ekeza zapikia-za neka tege eve-gi ezoqam gezø-ein, “No Felo-te qatǿnáv, manqat vømbō-ein: ‘Nogi zapikia-za neka tat-gi ezoqam, ndigu Keinan manqei-te giyagam, no-te ndøfakház.

32 Nginik ezoqam, ndigu sip keoge ezoqám. Ndigu søvakha gigiap nanak keoge ezoqám. Yaq tigi sip neka kao neka gigiap ate qazømbegoam, nqanek gindu-ituím.’

33 Felo zo tezø-akha, neka āv tezømbe-bevap nqǽgo, ‘Zo gekhā sasae zógo?’

34 Yaq zo āv qazømbøé-einím nqǽgo, ‘Ni qogi sasae ezoqam, søvakha gigiap nanak keoge ezoqám. Ni khapelavøqase qazigoam, atema ndakin. Ni neka nigi atanakha-zapazapa-za.’ Yaq zo kopømbaqapé, Gosen manqei-te tozoyagat. Zapa ndǿgo, sip keoge ezoqam ate ndi꞉gu, Izip manqei-ak-te khafeáp.”

More from the notes

Genesis 46 (gen046), Kuni: notes on the draft

What this is. A machine draft of Genesis 46 (34 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni, translated from the Hebrew (orig/heb/gen046.txt) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30, Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the text: gen046.tagged (the .txt and .align are generated from it).

Style model. Our Genesis 45 and 35–36 (the lists of Jacob's sons and Esau's line), 41:45–52 (Asenath, Manasseh, Ephraim), the team's Genesis 10, 22, 25–26, 29–30. NT models: ACT 7:14–15 (Jacob's family going down to Egypt, seventy-five persons), MAT 14:21 (not counted), MAT 28:10 (go and tell), MRK 10:20 (from my youth), ACT 9:10 and 18:9 (a vision at night), JON 1:8 (occupation). Headings after the BSB's seven, without tone marks.

Certainty: high = the construction is attested as used; medium = attested parts put together; low = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked (built).

New name forms

All by the letter rules (name:gen46_list.g46): Palu, Kami, Zemuel, Zamin, Oad, Zakin, Geson, Koat, Melali, Hamul, Tola, Puva, Zob, Simlon, Seled, Zaleel, Zifion, Hagi, Suni, Ezbon, Eli, Alodi, Aleli, Imna, Isva, Isvi, Belia, Sela (Serah), Heba, Malkiel, Beka, Asbel, Gela (Gera), Ei, Los, Mupim, Hupim, Ad, Husim, Zazeel, Zeza, Silem. Taken from the corpus (name:gen46_attested.g46): Hanok (25:4), Zoal (23:8), Elon (26:34), Naeman (LUK 4:27), Ezlon (MAT 1:3). Reused from our chapters: E, Onan, Sela, Pelez, Zela, Sol, Asenat, Potifela, On taon, Gosen. The vocative Zékop! (gen031:11).

Fixed renderings for the following chapters

  • "in all, N persons" Ndigu ezoqa ate gi꞉goam, N.; "born to X" X gimbo-fakhaz; shepherds sip keoge ezoqam (Gen 47:3); "what is your occupation?" Zo gekhā sasae zógo? (47:3); "from our youth until now" khapelavøqase qazigoam, atema ndakin; "for a long time" Nupøkhán mbá.

Not found in the corpus (built or gap)

  • granddaughters (v7), "his direct descendants" (v26), "show the way" (v28), the closing of the eyes (v4), "summons and asks" (v33).
  • Built forms: toqonanim (v4), itu-qa (v5, from gen045), teti-tri, sikstin, siksti-siks, tezø-akha, tezømbe-bevap, zógo in the question (v33), teqamekh with nakhoa (v28).

Style check and review lines

style.py --brief flags nginik and neka (20 and 65 for about 1 and 19) and manqei-te (18 for 3): the genealogy is the team's own list frame (genealogy_list.g, Genesis is left out of the style corpus) and Genesis names places with manqei; kept. matev and nøme are missing: no place for them in the lists. Marks per 100 words are low because the lists are names.

chapter_check.py review lines kept: the names Hamul, Tola, Seled, Ezbon, Imna, Beka read as "English words left in the draft" — they are names (by the letter rules). "Izip manqei-ak-te" in v34 is right: the Hebrew מִצְרַיִם here means the Egyptians (gen043:32 the same).

Open questions

  1. Names that fall together: Eli (Eri) with the NT's Eli, Sela (Serah, Asher's daughter) with Sela (Shelah), Gela (Gera) with Gela (Gerar); and Zob for יוֹב, the same as the NT's Job. Should they be told apart?
  2. "Joseph will close your eyes" (v4): rendered by its meaning; is toqonanim (when you die) right?
  3. "You are still alive" (v30): the fixed zua ngekhandî꞉vát with qo: is there a 2sg form?
  4. The counts (v15, 18, 26): teti-tri, sikstin, siksti-siks are built.
  5. Jacob's family is 70 (v27) in the Hebrew, 75 in ACT 7:14 (the LXX): kept 70.
  6. Least sure: v4, v28, v30, v33.
