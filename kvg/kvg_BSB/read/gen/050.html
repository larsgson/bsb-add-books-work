Iqandengaz (Genesis), chapter 50

Zekop ginø-otøvem

1 Yaq Zosef tege eve sège-møvøiú, vø̄vin, vø̄ngenøtam.

2 Yaq Zosef ekeza sasae ezoqam, mulømula etoam ezoqam, manqa vøzø̄-løvua, tege eve-qa kha, tae etet sisip mbomømboma namba bèzø-gutøvém. Yaq mulømula etoam ezoqam, Izlael-qa kha, tae etet sisip mbomømboma namba vø̄e-gutøvem.

3 Khøuwa foti qamømbo-navøem. Zapa ndǿgo, kha gutøveav matev ndøgo, khøuwa foti nqanek qagó. Yaq Izip ezoqam khøuwa seventi teqa yaq-te gè-eivám.

4 Yaq eiv khøuwa qamømbo-navøem, Zosef Felo-qa khoev ezoqam āv gezø-ein nqǽgo, “Zo poev tøzøgoat no-te, Felo āv qazømbøé-einím nqǽgo:

5 ‘Tat ndego no manqa mbusa genømbe-ve, av nqægo: “Nqáe! No avønín ndænaním. Nandi nqa ndøgo, no Keinan manqei-te qæ-khakheinám, ndǿgo qonǿ-otøvém.” Yaq ndakin, Tat bàtøkhæ-otáv. Yaq nango qanøveséz.’ ”

6 Yaq Felo gezø-qavøiu, “Sègeqáv. Qoge qave vø̀-otáv, av manqa mbusa qoqove ndægo.”

7 Yaq Zosef tege eve otav-qa genøqavíg. Yaq Felo-qa sasae ezoqam ate ndi꞉gu, neka teqa khoev-qa kawakawa-za, neka Izip manqei-qa kawakawa-za ate ndi꞉gu, Zosef namba ginøqavíg.

8 Neka Zosef-qa khoev ezoqam ate ndi꞉gu, neka tege zapikia-za, neka tege eve-qa khoev ezoqam, ate ginøqavíg. Tigi nakheis-mba neka tigi sip neka tigi kao, Gosen manqei-te gi-ivøvem.

9 Até manqei khagua wil-us neka hos-us ezoqam-a, Zosef namba ginøqavíg. Yaq ezoqa kopoáv kandambá ndøgoám.

10 Yaq ndigu Atad-qa bali nanga tøkuza manqei-te gimøfakhaz. Ndøgo Zoden kea avønin-te. Ndǿgo ndigu eiv neka osombo kandambá geqeiv. Yaq Zosef tege eve-qa yaq-te mutøkhop neka eiv matev ndøgó, khøuwa sevén.

11 Yaq Keinan-ak ezoqam, ta manqei-te gi꞉yagam, Atad-qa bali nanga tøkuza manqei-te mutøkhop neka eiv matev gi-ometam. Yaq āv gindu-einim nqǽgo, “Izip ezoqam-qa mutøkhop neka eiv matev nqanek kandambá.” Zapa ndǿgo, ta manqei iz nøme nqambógo, Eibol-Mizlaim. Ndøgo Zoden kea avønin-te.

12 Yaq Zekop-qa nakhei angana, av eve manqa gezø-løvuá ndægo, ndǿgo ndimatanam.

13 Ndigu eve Keinan manqei-té ginø-tonqogimám. Yaq Makpela nandi nqa-té ginø-otøvém, Mamle avønin-te. Ta manqei ndøgo, Eibleem Hit-ak ezoqam Eflon-te gewi, manqei pakha upøgim nonqo.

14 Yaq tege eve ginø-otøvém ndǿgo, Zosef nango Izip-te genøveséz. Ekeza zapikia-za neka ate ndi꞉gu, eve otav-qa namba ginøqavíg, até ginøveséz.

Zosef ekeza zapikia-za tanakh mbogó

15 Yaq Zosef-gi zapikia-za, tigi eve ge꞉nanim gi-ometam. Yaq āv gindu-einim nqǽgo, “Gê, Zosef ni-te mbøni nqosøgeap ndǿgoát? Yaq matev soqøsoqa ate ndægo, ni teqa-te nqazi꞉-matanam ndøgo, ndego nqawa ni-te ndø̀matanám.”

16 Yaq ndigu Zosef-te manqat ndøkhofotáz, āv nqǽgo, “Qoge eve, avønín ndø̀naním ndøgo, manqa āv gezø-løvuá nqǽgo:

17 ‘Zosef āv qazømbøé-einím nqǽgo: Qoge zapikia-za, tiqa manqa-zapazapa neka soqa matev, qo sà betaqambo-navøém. Zapa ndǿgo, ndigu qo-te matev soqøsoqa ndimatanam.’ Yaq ndakin, ni qave-gi Mbumbukiam-qa sasae ezoqám. Niqa manqa-zapazapa, sà betaqambo-navøemát.” Yaq ndigu nqanek manqat gi-einim ndøgo, Zosef gèvín.

18 Yaq tege zapikia-za até, tinduzáv ndǿgo, teqa megemege-te, ekeza kha, manqei me꞉lavawɨ́n. Yaq āv gindu-einim nqǽgo, “Nqáe! Ni qogi sasae ezoqám.”

19 Geté Zosef āv gezø-qavøiu nqǽgo, “Zo møe ndøgòne᷄m. Gê, no Mbumbukiàm?

20 Zoqa poev ndøgo, no-te matev soqøsoqa ndøgoám. Geté Mbumbukiam-qa poev ndøgo, matev eqeieqei ndøgoám. Ndego ezoqa kopoáv khandi꞉zat-qa, av ndakin nqǽgo.

21 Yaq nakémbá, zo møe ndøgòne᷄m. No zo neka zogi nakheis, lou søtazǿ-etoám.” Yaq Zosef tanakh mbogó ti-te, neka manqat eqeieqei vøzø̄-ein.

Zosef ndønanim

22 Yaq Zosef neka tege eve-qa khoev ezoqam, Izip-te giyagam. Yaq Zosef viav wan andled ten vømbōndap.

23 Yaq Zosef Eflaim-gi zi-gi nakheis ge-omet. Até Manase-ge yo Makil-gi nakheis, ndigu ndøfakhán ndøgo, Zosef ndigu, ekeza ndømøkuí-te, ge꞉khatib.

24 Yaq Zosef ekeza zapikia-za āv gezø-ein nqǽgo, “No avønín ndænaním. Geté Mbumbukiam unimanqatín, zo-te okha tenøwáv. Yaq ndego zo, nqanek manqei-te ndøgo, ta manqei-te tozó-tonqogím, ma ndego manqa mbusa ge꞉ve, Eibleem neka Aezek neka Zekop-te.”

25 Yaq Zosef Izlael-gi nakheis manqa vøzø̄-løvua, av nqægo, “Mbumbukiam unimanqatín, zo-te okha tenøwáv. Yaq zo nqanek manqei tozo-ivøvem, noqa bazag vø̀-upatét.”

26 Yaq Zosef sasa ndønanim, viav wan andled ten qandap. Yaq ezoqam teqa kha, tae etet sisip mbomømboma namba, vø̄e-gutøvem. Yaq tae kumøkuma mokho-te sasa ndø-itu, Izip-te.

1 Yaq Zosef tege eve sège-møvøiú, vø̄vin, vø̄ngenøtam.

'fell on his face' as 'embraced him' (sège-møvøiú, our 46:29); GEN 29:11 vø̄vin, GEN 27:27 vø̄ngenøtam. Medium.

וַיְצַ֨ו יוֹסֵ֤ף אֶת־עֲבָדָיו֙ אֶת־הָרֹ֣פְאִ֔ים לַחֲנֹ֖ט אֶת־אָבִ֑יו וַיַּחַנְט֥וּ הָרֹפְאִ֖ים אֶת־יִשְׂרָאֵֽל׃

Genesis 50 (gen050), Kuni: notes on the draft

What this is. A machine draft of Genesis 50 (26 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni, translated from the Hebrew (orig/heb/gen050.txt) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30, Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the text: gen050.tagged (the .txt and .align are generated from it).

Style model. Our Genesis 47:29–31 and 49:29–33 (the burial wish and Jacob's death), the team's GEN 23:2 (mutøkhop neka eiv matev, mourning), 25:9 (burial at Machpelah), 27:45 (anger kept), 18:3 (if you are willing toward me) and 30:2 (Gê, no Mbumbukiàm?), and the NT where it tells the same events: HEB 11:22 (Joseph's bones), ACT 7:16 (the patriarchs carried to Canaan), JHN 19:39–40 (spices and wrapping), MRK 5:38 (loud mourning).

Certainty: high = the construction is attested as used; medium = attested parts put together; low = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked (built).

Built forms (store entries added)

  • bèzø-gutøvém (let them wrap; v2), kha gutøveav matev (v3), otav-qa (to bury; v7, v14), lou søtazǿ-etoám (I will feed you, pl; v21), tenøwáv (he will go to see; v24–25), tae kumøkuma (coffin; v26).
  • Not in the store as separate entries, checked against the corpus by pattern only: genømbe-ve (he made me give; v5), qæ-khakheinám (I made; v5), qonǿ-otøvém (bury me, 2sg; v5), vø̀-otáv (and bury; v6), tozó-tonqogím (v24).

New name forms

  • Atad (v10–11), Eibol-Mizlaim (Abel-mizraim, v11), Makil (Machir, v23), all by the letter rules.

Open questions

  • v10–11: 'across the Jordan' is given as 'near the Jordan' (avønin-te); the team may want 'on the other side'.
  • v11: the name Abel-mizraim ('mourning of the Egyptians') is not explained; a bracket as the team's GEN 11:9 is possible.
  • v2, v26: embalming is told as wrapping the body with spices (no word for embalm); v26 coffin as a wooden bier.
  • v23: 'born on Joseph's knees' (a sign of adoption) is told as 'Joseph received them on his knees'.
  • v5: qanøveséz for 'I will return' is attested once and its person is not certain.
  • v15–17: 'transgression and sin' as manqa-zapazapa neka soqa matev; the plea particle sà carries 'please'.
  • YHWH: none in this chapter.

Style check

  • Taken: fewer gi- plural past verbs (gindu-einim as RUT 1:10) and fewer manqei-te (Izip-te, v22, v26).
  • Kept: gezø- (he … to them) for Joseph's commands and speeches; tae (spices, coffin) and -qa (possessives, purpose) are content of this chapter; tone marks a little under the human range (19.3 per 100 words vs 19.6).

Zekop ginø-otøvem

1 Yaq Zosef tege eve sège-møvøiú,
vø̄vin,
vø̄ngenøtam.
2 Yaq Zosef ekeza sasae ezoqam, mulømula etoam ezoqam, manqa vøzø̄-løvua,
tege eve-qa kha, tae etet sisip mbomømboma namba bèzø-gutøvém.
Yaq mulømula etoam ezoqam, Izlael-qa kha, tae etet sisip mbomømboma namba vø̄e-gutøvem.
3 Khøuwa foti qamømbo-navøem.
Zapa ndǿgo, kha gutøveav matev ndøgo, khøuwa foti nqanek qagó.
Yaq Izip ezoqam khøuwa seventi teqa yaq-te gè-eivám.
4 Yaq eiv khøuwa qamømbo-navøem,
Zosef Felo-qa khoev
ezoqam āv gezø-ein nqǽgo,
“Zo poev tøzøgoat no-te,
Felo
āv qazømbøé-einím nqǽgo:
5 ‘Tat ndego no manqa mbusa genømbe-ve,
av nqægo:
“Nqáe!
No avønín ndænaním.
Nandi nqa ndøgo,
no Keinan manqei-te qæ-khakheinám,
ndǿgo qonǿ-otøvém.”
Yaq ndakin,
Tat bàtøkhæ-otáv.
Yaq nango qanøveséz.’ ”
6 Yaq Felo gezø-qavøiu,
“Sègeqáv.
Qoge qave vø̀-otáv,
av manqa mbusa qoqove ndægo.”
7 Yaq Zosef
tege eve otav-qa genøqavíg.
Yaq Felo-qa sasae ezoqam ate ndi꞉gu, neka teqa khoev-qa kawakawa-za, neka Izip manqei-qa kawakawa-za ate ndi꞉gu, Zosef namba ginøqavíg.
8 Neka Zosef-qa khoev ezoqam ate ndi꞉gu, neka tege zapikia-za, neka tege eve-qa khoev ezoqam,
ate ginøqavíg. Tigi nakheis-mba neka tigi sip neka tigi kao, Gosen manqei-te gi-ivøvem.
9 Até manqei khagua wil-us neka hos-us ezoqam-a, Zosef namba ginøqavíg.
Yaq ezoqa kopoáv kandambá ndøgoám.
10 Yaq ndigu Atad-qa bali nanga tøkuza manqei-te gimøfakhaz.
Ndøgo Zoden kea avønin-te.
Ndǿgo ndigu eiv neka osombo kandambá geqeiv.
Yaq Zosef tege eve-qa yaq-te mutøkhop neka eiv matev ndøgó, khøuwa sevén.
11 Yaq Keinan-ak ezoqam, ta manqei-te gi꞉yagam, Atad-qa bali nanga tøkuza manqei-te mutøkhop neka eiv matev gi-ometam.
Yaq āv gindu-einim nqǽgo,
“Izip ezoqam-qa mutøkhop neka eiv matev nqanek kandambá.”
Zapa ndǿgo, ta manqei iz nøme nqambógo, Eibol-Mizlaim.
Ndøgo Zoden kea avønin-te.
12 Yaq Zekop-qa nakhei angana,
av eve manqa gezø-løvuá ndægo, ndǿgo ndimatanam.
13 Ndigu eve Keinan manqei-té ginø-tonqogimám.
Yaq Makpela nandi nqa-té ginø-otøvém,
Mamle avønin-te. Ta manqei ndøgo, Eibleem Hit-ak ezoqam Eflon-te gewi, manqei pakha upøgim nonqo.
14 Yaq tege eve ginø-otøvém ndǿgo, Zosef nango Izip-te genøveséz. Ekeza zapikia-za neka ate ndi꞉gu,
eve otav-qa namba ginøqavíg,
até ginøveséz.

Zosef ekeza zapikia-za tanakh mbogó

15 Yaq Zosef-gi zapikia-za,
tigi eve ge꞉nanim gi-ometam.
Yaq āv gindu-einim nqǽgo,
“Gê, Zosef ni-te mbøni nqosøgeap ndǿgoát?
Yaq matev soqøsoqa ate ndægo,
ni teqa-te nqazi꞉-matanam ndøgo, ndego nqawa ni-te ndø̀matanám.”
16 Yaq ndigu Zosef-te manqat ndøkhofotáz,
āv nqǽgo,
“Qoge eve, avønín ndø̀naním ndøgo,
manqa āv gezø-løvuá nqǽgo:
17 ‘Zosef āv qazømbøé-einím nqǽgo:
Qoge zapikia-za, tiqa manqa-zapazapa neka soqa matev, qo sà betaqambo-navøém.
Zapa ndǿgo, ndigu qo-te matev soqøsoqa ndimatanam.’
Yaq ndakin, ni qave-gi Mbumbukiam-qa sasae ezoqám. Niqa manqa-zapazapa, sà betaqambo-navøemát.”
Yaq ndigu nqanek manqat gi-einim ndøgo, Zosef gèvín.
18 Yaq tege zapikia-za até, tinduzáv ndǿgo,
teqa megemege-te, ekeza kha, manqei me꞉lavawɨ́n.
Yaq āv gindu-einim nqǽgo,
“Nqáe!
Ni qogi sasae ezoqám.”
19 Geté Zosef āv gezø-qavøiu nqǽgo,
“Zo møe ndøgòne᷄m.
Gê, no Mbumbukiàm?
20 Zoqa poev ndøgo, no-te matev soqøsoqa ndøgoám.
Geté Mbumbukiam-qa poev ndøgo, matev eqeieqei ndøgoám.
Ndego ezoqa kopoáv khandi꞉zat-qa, av ndakin nqǽgo.
21 Yaq nakémbá, zo møe ndøgòne᷄m.
No zo neka zogi nakheis, lou søtazǿ-etoám.”
Yaq Zosef tanakh mbogó ti-te,
neka manqat eqeieqei vøzø̄-ein.

Zosef ndønanim

22 Yaq Zosef neka tege eve-qa khoev ezoqam, Izip-te giyagam.
Yaq Zosef viav wan andled ten vømbōndap.
23 Yaq Zosef Eflaim-gi zi-gi nakheis ge-omet.
Até Manase-ge yo Makil-gi nakheis, ndigu ndøfakhán ndøgo, Zosef ndigu, ekeza ndømøkuí-te, ge꞉khatib.
24 Yaq Zosef ekeza zapikia-za āv gezø-ein nqǽgo,
“No avønín ndænaním.
Geté Mbumbukiam unimanqatín, zo-te okha tenøwáv.
Yaq ndego zo, nqanek manqei-te ndøgo, ta manqei-te tozó-tonqogím,
ma ndego manqa mbusa ge꞉ve, Eibleem neka Aezek neka Zekop-te.”
25 Yaq Zosef Izlael-gi nakheis manqa vøzø̄-løvua,
av nqægo,
“Mbumbukiam unimanqatín, zo-te okha tenøwáv.
Yaq zo nqanek manqei tozo-ivøvem, noqa bazag vø̀-upatét.”
26 Yaq Zosef sasa ndønanim, viav wan andled ten qandap.
