1 Yaq Zekop ekeza nakhei angana gezø-akha, gezø-ein, “Vø̀møváb! Yaq ndǿgo, no zo nqazø-manqate, ande gekha matev me zo-te tøfakhan, khøuwa vaev-te.
וַיִּקְרָ֥א יַעֲקֹ֖ב אֶל־בָּנָ֑יו וַיֹּ֗אמֶר הֵאָֽסְפוּ֙ וְאַגִּ֣ידָה לָכֶ֔ם אֵ֛ת אֲשֶׁר־יִקְרָ֥א אֶתְכֶ֖ם בְּאַחֲרִ֥ית הַיָּמִֽים׃

gezø-akha (called them); vø̀møváb (come together); ACT 20:22 ande gekha matev me … (what will happen); 'days to come' as 'later days' (vaev-te, GEN 27:40). Medium.
2 Zekop-gi nakhei angana! Vø̀møváb, neka manqat vø̄yogem. Zoge zøve, Izlael-qa manqat vø̄yogem.
הִקָּבְצ֥וּ וְשִׁמְע֖וּ בְּנֵ֣י יַעֲקֹ֑ב וְשִׁמְע֖וּ אֶל־יִשְׂרָאֵ֥ל אֲבִיכֶֽם׃
BSB 49:2 Come together and listen, O sons of Jacob; listen to your father Israel.
vocative first; vø̄yogem (listen, chained); zoge zøve (your pl father, 9× in the corpus). Medium.
3 Lúben! Qo noge yo-khøuwá. Qo noqa bazaføgakh, neka noqa umingiap-qa ibugukhokhof. Qo vizap-ús kandambaqapé, neka bazaføgakh-ús kandambaqapé.
רְאוּבֵן֙ בְּכֹ֣רִי אַ֔תָּה כֹּחִ֖י וְרֵאשִׁ֣ית אוֹנִ֑י יֶ֥תֶר שְׂאֵ֖ת וְיֶ֥תֶר עָֽז׃
BSB 49:3 Reuben, you are my firstborn, my might, and the beginning of my strength, excelling in honor, excelling in power.
yo-khøuwa (firstborn, 48:18); vocative accent on the first syllable (feature vocative.2); 'excelling in' as 'very honoured, very strong' (vizap-ús, 2TH 3:1; feature having_suffix). Medium/low.
4 Geté qo andé ibøkha me, bazaføgakh mbaín. Qo sekemba ezoqa kandambaqape qógoák. Zapa ndǿgo, qo qoge qave-qa venanqei nonqo-te qoqo-oq. Yaq noqa venanqei nonqo ndøgo, matev soqaqape qoqo-matanam.
פַּ֤חַז כַּמַּ֨יִם֙ אַל־תּוֹתַ֔ר כִּ֥י עָלִ֖יתָ מִשְׁכְּבֵ֣י אָבִ֑יךָ אָ֥ז חִלַּ֖לְתָּ יְצוּעִ֥י עָלָֽה׃פ
BSB 49:4 Uncontrolled as the waters, you will no longer excel, because you went up to your father’s bed, onto my couch, and defiled it.
'unstable' = 'without strength' (JAS 1:8); sekemba … qógoák (no longer; GEN 27:39 qógoák, binding NEG -ák); ACT 11:3 qoqo-oq (you went in, binding 2sg qo-); GEN 4:10 matev soqaqape qoqo-matanam (you did this evil thing, binding qo-) for 'defiled'. Medium.
5 Simeon neka Livai, ndigu zapikia-za. Tiqa kaiya, khaneam møqæ matev-qa nakhag gigiáp.
שִׁמְע֥וֹן וְלֵוִ֖י אַחִ֑ים כְּלֵ֥י חָמָ֖ס מְכֵרֹתֵיהֶֽם׃
BSB 49:5 Simeon and Levi are brothers; their swords are weapons of violence.
kaiya (swords, our 34:25); GEN 6:11 khaneam møqæ matev (violence); 2CO 6:7 nakhag gigiáp (weapons). Medium.
6 No tiqa manqa ovøyam-te nógoák. No tiqa bawan namba nótøkuák. Zapa ndǿgo, ndigu qaqa ndøgó, ezoqa pakhapakha vøzø̄-khanæmam. Neka ekeza poev-te mba, kao-qa tokhotapak vø̄kepøtøzem.
בְּסֹדָם֙ אַל־תָּבֹ֣א נַפְשִׁ֔י בִּקְהָלָ֖ם אַל־תֵּחַ֣ד כְּבֹדִ֑י כִּ֤י בְאַפָּם֙ הָ֣רְגוּ אִ֔ישׁ וּבִרְצֹנָ֖ם עִקְּרוּ־שֽׁוֹר׃
BSB 49:6 May I never enter their council; may I never join their assembly. For they kill men in their anger, and hamstring oxen on a whim.
manqa ovøyam (council, MAT 10:17); nógoák (I will not be, 2×, binding NEG -ák); nótøkuák (built, nó- + tøkua join, ACT 5:13, + -ák); MRK 3:5 qaqa ndøgó; our 34:25 pakhapakha vøzø̄-khanæmam; hamstring = 'cut the legs of the cattle' (kao, vø̄kepøtøzem): gap. Medium/low.
7 Tiqa qaqa bèsoqoév. Zapa ndǿgo, kandambaqapé. Neka tiqa qaqa bèsoqoév. Zapa ndǿgo, khobos-qapé. No ndigu, Zekop-gi zi mokho-te, ndæ̀zø-panqasam. Neka Izlael-gi zi mokho-te, ndæ̀zø-ngeasám.
אָר֤וּר אַפָּם֙ כִּ֣י עָ֔ז וְעֶבְרָתָ֖ם כִּ֣י קָשָׁ֑תָה אֲחַלְּקֵ֣ם בְּיַעֲקֹ֔ב וַאֲפִיצֵ֖ם בְּיִשְׂרָאֵֽל׃ס
BSB 49:7 Cursed be their anger, for it is strong, and their wrath, for it is cruel! I will disperse them in Jacob and scatter them in Israel.
GEN 9:25 bèsoqoév (cursed be); 1PE 5:8 khobos-qape (fierce); GEN 11:8 panqasam and JHN 10:12 ngeasám (scatter) with 1sg future ndæ̀zø- (48:4) (built). 'Cursed be' repeated for the second line. Medium/low.
8 Zúda! Qogi zapikia-za, qoqa iz yàbeto-eqawát. Qoqa zenda, qogi qaqa ezoqam-qa monqo-te ndǿgoát. Qoge qave-gi nakhei angana, qoqa megemege-te bēfoyamat.
יְהוּדָ֗ה אַתָּה֙ יוֹד֣וּךָ אַחֶ֔יךָ יָדְךָ֖ בְּעֹ֣רֶף אֹיְבֶ֑יךָ יִשְׁתַּחֲוּ֥וּלְךָ֖ בְּנֵ֥י אָבִֽיךָ׃
BSB 49:8 Judah, your brothers shall praise you. Your hand shall be on the necks of your enemies; your father’s sons shall bow down to you.
GEN 9:26 iz, yàbeto-eqawát (let them praise the name) echoes the team's meaning of Judah at 29:35 (Iz eqawát), so the pun stays without a bracket; neck monqo (LUK 17:2); GEN 27:29 qoqa megemege-te bēfoyamat (bow down to you). Medium.
9 Zuda, ndego andé layon ndakinak me. Áti! Qo søvakha vømbō-khana, yaq nango qoqotøndoqav. Ndego gèkhafutáv, sasa gebuv, andé layon me, andé layon sævam me. Gê, gekha ezoqām, ndego gembo-tiû?
גּ֤וּר אַרְיֵה֙ יְהוּדָ֔ה מִטֶּ֖רֶף בְּנִ֣י עָלִ֑יתָ כָּרַ֨ע רָבַ֧ץ כְּאַרְיֵ֛ה וּכְלָבִ֖יא מִ֥י יְקִימֶֽנּוּ׃

layon (glossary), ndakinak (young, JHN 12:14 donki ndakinak); 'from the prey' as 'you killed game and came back' (vømbō-khana; ACT 10:33 qoqotøndoqav, binding qo-); crouch = gèkhafutáv (bowed down); lioness layon sævam (as REV 4:7 kao anganeám); rouse gembo-tiû (built, ACT 12:7 vø̄tiu woke him; circumflex of the question). Low.
10 Kawa ezoqam-qa khamokh, Zuda-te ndøgo, gemǿ-navøemák. Neka kawa tatuag, teqa tokhotapak livin-te ndøgo, gemǿ-navøemák. Atema Sailo tendowav. Yaq bawabawan ate ndægo, teqa manqat giyogem.

BSB 49:11 He ties his donkey to the vine, his colt to the choicest branch. He washes his garments in wine, his robes in the blood of grapes.
waen tae (vine, JHN 15:1); donki nakhasam (colt, LUK 19:30); tie gemopotavám (built: 3sg on gumopotavám, MAT 21:2 gémopotavát); wash gesunguz (REV 7:14 type); grapes as 'the vine's fruit' (waen nanga). Low/medium.

חַכְלִילִ֥י עֵינַ֖יִם מִיָּ֑יִן וּלְבֶן־שִׁנַּ֖יִם מֵחָלָֽב׃פ

'darker than wine' as 'dark because of wine' (bøivun-ús, (built, having darkness): gap (no 'dark red'); MAT 28:3 ùnime꞉-papaqá (very white); tete milk (1CO 3:2). Low.
13 Zebulon, ndaola ndøgeg-te temøqóm. Neka ndego, palai khagua-qa pu ndǿgoát. Neka teqa manqei vaev, atema Saedon taon-te.

BSB 49:13 Zebulun shall dwell by the seashore and become a harbor for ships; his border shall extend to Sidon.
ndaola ndøgeg (seashore, HEB 11:12); GEN 13:12 temøqóm; harbour khagua pu (ACT 27:8, 12) with palai khagua (ship, ACT 20:13); Saedon (NT). Medium.

יִשָּׂשכָ֖ר חֲמֹ֣ר גָּ֑רֶם רֹבֵ֖ץ בֵּ֥ין הַֽמִּשְׁפְּתָֽיִם׃
BSB 49:14 Issachar is a strong donkey, lying down between the sheepfolds.
Isaka (team 30:18); sheepfold sip-qa khoev (JHN 10:1); gebuv (lay down). Medium.

BSB 49:15 He saw that his resting place was good and that his land was pleasant, so he bent his shoulder to the burden and submitted to labor as a servant.
indirect speech av nqægo; pusup (rest, store); viniv (burden), nqanqa (shoulder); 'forced labour' as 'a servant to other people': low. Medium/low.

דָּ֖ן יָדִ֣ין עַמּ֑וֹ כְּאַחַ֖ד שִׁבְטֵ֥י יִשְׂרָאֵֽל׃

Genesis 49 (gen049), Kuni: notes on the draft

What this is. A machine draft of Genesis 49 (33 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni, translated from the Hebrew (orig/heb/gen049.txt) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30, Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the text: gen049.tagged (the .txt and .align are generated from it).

Style model. Our Genesis 48 (Jacob's blessing of Ephraim and Manasseh, poetry lines), 47 (the burial wish) and the team's GEN 27:27–29, 39–40 (Isaac's blessings: bēfoyamat bow down, qógoák you will not be), 9:25–27 (bèsoqoév cursed be, yàbeto-eqawát praised be), 25:8–10 (breathed his last, gathered to his people, buried in the cave of Machpelah) and 23:17–20 (the field bought from the Hittites as a burial place). The blessings are poetry: images are kept as likenesses (andé X me) where the corpus has the animal or thing, and told by their meaning where it does not (feature image_to_meaning).

Certainty: high = the construction is attested as used; medium = attested parts put together; low = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked (built).

  • name 3063 Zúda (v8): the vocative, with the accent on the first syllable (feature vocative.2, as Evézøza, Zíona); the narrative form Zuda stays (v9, v10). Same for Lúben (v3).

  • Sailo (Shiloh, v10), by the letter rules (sh → s, long i → ai). Vocatives Lúben, Zúda.

Open questions

  • v10: Shiloh is read as a name, as the BSB; its note 'the one to whom tribute belongs' is the other reading. Team's choice.
  • v4: 'defiled' rendered as 'you did this evil thing' (GEN 4:10); a word for 'make unclean' is wanted.
  • v6, v12, v21: no words for hamstring, dark (red) eyes, doe; told by meaning (gaps).
  • v8, v16, v19: the Hebrew puns on Judah, Dan and Gad are not explained in brackets (the team gave the meanings of these names in GEN 29–30); the team may want a bracket as in GEN 11:9.
  • v24–26: the divine titles (Mighty One of Jacob, Shepherd, Rock of Israel, Shaddai) and 'blessings' as 'good things'.
  • YHWH: v18 (Evézøza, vocative).

Style check

  • Taken: fewer possessives in v11–12, 24 (teqa), as the human verses MAT 21:7, MRK 10:16.
  • Kept: andé … me more often than the human verses: the blessings are a chain of likenesses (lion, donkey, snake, doe, vine, wolf); -us adjectives (strong, rich, fruitful) where the corpus has no other adjective; fewer tone marks than the human chapters (the poetry has many nouns and few verbs).
