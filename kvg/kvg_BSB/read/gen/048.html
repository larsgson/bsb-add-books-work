Iqandengaz (Genesis), chapter 48

Zekop Eflaim neka Manase zenda gezømbove

1 Taqa zita-te, ezoqa nøme, Zosef āv gimbøe-einím nqǽgo, “Qoge qave, enqoni mbogó.” Yaq Zosef tege eve-te ndøwav, ekeza nakhei angana menas namba, Manase neka Eflaim.

2 Yaq ezoqa nøme, Zekop āv gimbøe-einím nqǽgo, “Nqáe! Qoge yo Zosef, qo-te ndøfakhán.” Yaq Izlael, bazaføgakh nqawa qamøndæ-vesez. Ndego venanqei nonqo-te sègeqóm.

3 Yaq Zekop Zosef gembo-ein, “Mbumbukiam, ndego Bazaføgakh-Qape, Luz taon-te, Keinan manqei-te, vømønø̄-fakhan. Yaq ndǿgo, ndego no gekhakheinømbem.

4 Yaq ndego āv genømbe-eín nqǽgo, ‘Nqáe! No qazi kopoav-qape nøtaqá-etoám. Qogi qazi, ezoqa bawabawan kopoav ndǿgoát. Neka nqanek manqei, qogi qazi, ndigu qoqa zita-te tiqanet, ate nonqó ndæ̀zø-etoám.’

5 Yaq ndakin, qogi nakhei angana menas, ndigu Izip manqei-te qoqomanaz, no qo-te fakhaneav qægoam, ndigu nogí. Eflaim neka Manase, nogí ndǿgoát, av Luben neka Simeon ndi꞉gu.

6 Geté nakheis, ndigu tiqa zita-te qoqomanaz, ndigu qogí ndǿgoát. Tiqa manqei ndøgo, tiqa zapikia-za-qa iziz-te ndǿgoát.

7 Geté no, Padan manqei-te qatøndoqavan ndøgo, Laisol, Keinan manqei-te, nakhoa-te gunaním. Eflat vemiav zua qambaqapé. Noqa mbøni nqosøgeap kandambá ndøgoám. Yaq no tu ndǿgo, Eflat vemiav-qa nakhoa-te, qæ-otøvém.” (Iz nøme nqambógo, Betliém.)

8 Yaq Izlael Zosef-gi nakhei angana ge-omet. Yaq gembobevap, “Nginik gekha ezoqâm?”

9 Yaq Zosef tege eve gembo-qavøiu, “Ngínik nogi nakhei angana, ndigu Mbumbukiam genø-etoam, nqanek manqei-te.” Yaq tege eve gembo-ein, “Ndigu no-te voqotøndó-itūb. Yaq zenda tøzømbove.”

10 Izlael ùnime꞉-lawaqape-év, yaq teqa bøi waev vømbø̄i-navøem. Ndego kopømba mbaín, eqeieqei te-omet. Yaq Zosef ndigu tege eve avønin-te genø-itúb. Yaq tege eve ndigu kopokopo vø̄ngenøtam, neka sège-møvøiú.

11 Yaq Izlael Zosef gembo-ein, “No matavapøteáv, av nqægo, qoqa bugug nango tæ-omet. Geté nqáe! Mbumbukiam-qa yaq-te, até qogi nakheís-a, no mø̀e-omét.”

12 Yaq Zosef ndigu, tege eve-qa manazap-te, gè-upøzó. Yaq ndego gèkhafutáv, bugug até manqei-té.

13 Yaq Zosef ndigu menas gèndáf: Eflaim, teqa nakeamo zenda-te. Ndøgo Izlael-qa yogea zenda-té. Neka Manase, teqa yogea zenda-te. Ndøgo Izlael-qa nakeamo zenda-té. Yaq ndigu tege eve avønin-te genø-itúb.

14 Geté Izlael teqa nakeamo pingim sège-ovøyám, Eflaim-qa kawa-te vø̄ve. Eflaim ndego okhov-qase. Neka teqa yogea zenda, Manase-qa kawa-te vø̄ve. Izlael zenda nqanek matev-te ndøvé. Geté Manase ndego yo-khøuwá.

15 Yaq ndego Zosef zenda gembøeve, ge-ein, //1 “Mbumbukiam, ndego nogi atanakha-zapazapa-za, Eibleem neka Aezek, teqa manqat o-mba gimbøe-ndapem, //2 Mbumbukiam, ndego noqa yage ate nqægo, atema nqægo khaiya, andé sip keoge ezoqam me, no gènø-kewagám, //1 16 Enzol ezoqam, ndego matev soqøsoqa ate ndægo-te, no mø̀ndø-khandí꞉n, //2 ndego nginik nakhasam bèkhakheinøvém. //1 Ti mokho-te, noqa iz neka nogi atanakha-zapazapa-za, Eibleem neka Aezek, tiqa iziz, begó. //2 Neka manqei-qape-te, bawan bèzø-khouwevtét.”

17 Zosef ge-omet, av nqægo, tege eve teqa nakeamo zenda, Eflaim-qa kawa-te ndøvé. Yaq ndego khanakhanakh ùnime꞉-mbaín. Yaq ndego tege eve-qa zenda vø̄ndap, Eflaim-qa kawa-te ndøgo, Manase-qa kawa-te khofoe-qa.

18 Yaq Zosef tege eve gembo-ein, “Tát! Āv taoká. Ngenek ndego yo-khøuwá. Qoqa nakeamo zenda, teqa kawa-te qonǿvé.”

19 Geté tege eve poeveáv. Yaq ge-ein, “Áti! No mø̀tenøtén. No mø̀tenøtén. Até ndegó-a, ezoqa bawan kandambaqape ndǿgoát. Neka até ndegó-a, kandambaqape ndǿgoát. Geté tege eveqase okhov-qase, ndego Manase ùnime꞉-løvúmb. Neka tegi qazi, ezoqa bawabawan kopoav ndǿgoát.”

20 Yaq ta khøuwa-te ndøgo, Zekop ndigu zenda gezømbove, ge-ein, //1 “Izlael-ak ezoqa, ekeza nakheis zenda tizømbove ndøgo, zoqa iz-te, āv ti-einim nqǽgo, //2 ‘Mbumbukiam qo bèkhakheinøqavém, andé Eflaim neka Manase me.’” Yaq ndego Eflaim, Manase-qa megemege-te ndøvé.

21 Yaq Izlael Zosef gembo-ein, “Nqáe! No avønín ndænaním. Geté Mbumbukiam zo namba ndǿgoát. Teqa bazaføgakh-te, zo nqawa zogi atanakha-zapazapa-za-qa manqei-te zógoát.

22 Neka no qo, qoqa zapikia-za-qa ova-te, manqei popotap kopo nøtaqá-etoám. Ta manqei ndøgo, no Amo-ak-qa zenda-te qændap, noqa kaiya neka noqa føif namba.”

Genesis 48 (gen048), Kuni: notes on the draft

What this is. A machine draft of Genesis 48 (22 verses, Hebrew numbering = BSB numbering) in Kuni, translated from the Hebrew (orig/heb/gen048.txt) with the BSB as the only assisting translation. The agent does not know Kuni: every word and construction was looked up in the Kuni team's Genesis 1–30, Ruth, Jonah and the NT, the verse pack and the wiki. Status: experiment, not reviewed. Source of the text: gen048.tagged (the .txt and .align are generated from it).

Style model. Our Genesis 47 (the same scene: Jacob near death, Joseph at his bed), 35 (Luz, the promise, Rachel's death on the way to Ephrath) and 46. The team's GEN 27 (Isaac's blindness, the blessing of the sons, 'my son' Áti!), 17:6–8 and 28:3 (the promise), 24:40 (walk before God), 25:32 (about to die), 33:3 (bowed to the ground). NT models: HEB 11:21 (Jacob blessing Joseph's two sons: zenda gezømbove), 2TI 3:11 (rescued me from all evils), ACT 9:19 (strength returned), MAT 12:13 (stretched out his arm), HEB 2:13 (the children God has given me).

Certainty: high = the construction is attested as used; medium = attested parts put together; low = built by analogy, not found as such. Built forms are marked (built).

Verb marking

  • 48:6 qoqomanaz carries 2.SG.IND qo-; 48:10 kopømba mbaín (NEG on mbaín); 48:11 matavapøteáv (-eav).
  • unmarked 48:11 NEG.IND: the negation is the past negative ending of matavapøteáv (-teáv, 1CO 1:16 'I do not remember'); the store's morph view does not split this word, so the check does not see it.

Built forms (store entries added)

  • qoqomanaz (you fathered; v5, v6), qatøndoqavan (when I came back; v7), tøzømbove (that I may bless them; v9), tizømbove (when they bless; v20), gènø-kewagám (he looked after me; v15), gimbøe-ndapem (they kept his word; v15), qæ-otøvém (I buried; v7), khofoe-qa (to move it; v17).

New name forms

  • none new: Luz taon (GEN 28:19), Padan manqei (the Hebrew's Paddan; 35:9 has Padan-Alam), Eflat vemiav and Betliém (our 35:16–19), Amo-ak (GEN 10:16).

Open questions

  • v6: 'called by the names of their brothers in their inheritance' told by its meaning; check with the team.
  • v14: no word for crossing the hands (שִׂכֵּל); the draft says 'he put his hands this way'.
  • v15: 'walked before God' as 'kept his word' (GEN 24:40) and God 'like a shepherd' looking after Jacob.
  • v16: 'redeemed' rendered 'rescued from all evil' (2TI 3:11); nqovotáz (term:H1350) is still open.
  • v21: 'bring you back' as 'you will be again in the land'; a causative form is wanted.
  • v22: the ridge (שְׁכֶם) and the pun on Shechem are not kept; 'above your brothers' is weak.
  • No YHWH in this chapter.

Style check

  • Taken: pingim sège-ovøyám (MAT 12:13) for 'stretched out his hand'; fewer -te ndøgo (v3, v12).
  • Kept: ezoqam fewer and manqat fewer than the human verses (the chapter has no 'man' or 'word' to render); manqei-te and zenda more (place names with their class noun; the hands of v13–18 are the story).
