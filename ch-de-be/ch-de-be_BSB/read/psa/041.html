לַמְנַצֵּ֗חַ מִזְמ֥וֹר לְדָוִֽד׃
2 Glücklech isch dä, wo a der Schwach dänkt; am böse Tag rettet ne der HERR.
אַ֭שְׁרֵי מַשְׂכִּ֣יל אֶל־דָּ֑ל בְּי֥וֹם רָ֝עָ֗ה יְֽמַלְּטֵ֥הוּ יְהוָֽה׃
BSB 41:1 For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. Blessed is the one who cares for the poor; the LORD will deliver him in the day of trouble.
מַשְׂכִּיל אֶל־דָּל → wo a der Schwach dänkt (who cares for the poor)
3 Der HERR bhüetet ne und haltet ne am Läbe, im Land wird me säge, er syg glücklech. Du tuesch ne nid em Wille vo syne Finde uslifere!
יְהוָ֤ה׀יִשְׁמְרֵ֣הוּ וִֽ֭יחַיֵּהוּ וְאֻשַּׁ֣ר בָּאָ֑רֶץ וְאַֽל־תִּ֝תְּנֵ֗הוּ בְּנֶ֣פֶשׁ אֹיְבָֽיו׃
BSB 41:2 The LORD will protect and preserve him; He will bless him in the land and refuse to give him over to the will of his foes.
וְאֻשַּׁר בָּאָרֶץ → im Land wird me säge, er syg glücklech (he will be blessed in the land)
Qere.
4 Der HERR hilft ihm uf sym Chrankebett; sys ganze Lager machsch du nöi i syr Chrankheit.
יְֽהוָ֗ה יִ֭סְעָדֶנּוּ עַל־עֶ֣רֶשׂ דְּוָ֑י כָּל־מִ֝שְׁכָּב֗וֹ הָפַ֥כְתָּ בְחָלְיֽוֹ׃
BSB 41:3 The LORD will sustain him on his bed of illness and restore him from his bed of sickness.
כָּל־מִשְׁכָּבוֹ הָפַכְתָּ → sys ganze Lager machsch du nöi (You restore him from his bed)
5 I ha gseit: „HERR, häb Erbarme mit mer, mach my Seel gsund, denn i ha gäge di gsündiget!“
אֲֽנִי־אָ֭מַרְתִּי יְהוָ֣ה חָנֵּ֑נִי רְפָאָ֥ה נַ֝פְשִׁ֗י כִּי־חָטָ֥אתִי לָֽךְ׃
BSB 41:4 I said, “O LORD, be gracious to me; heal me, for I have sinned against You.”
6 Myni Finde säge Böses über mi: „Wenn stirbt er ächt und wenn isch sy Name vergässe?“
אוֹיְבַ֗י יֹאמְר֣וּ רַ֣ע לִ֑י מָתַ֥י יָ֝מ֗וּת וְאָבַ֥ד שְׁמֽוֹ׃
BSB 41:5 My enemies say with malice: “When will he die and be forgotten?”
7 Wen eine chunt, für mi z gseh, redt er falsch Züüg; sys Härz sammlet Böses; er geit use und verzellt's.
וְאִם־בָּ֤א לִרְא֨וֹת׀שָׁ֤וְא יְדַבֵּ֗ר לִבּ֗וֹ יִקְבָּץ־אָ֥וֶן ל֑וֹ יֵצֵ֖א לַח֣וּץ יְדַבֵּֽר׃
BSB 41:6 My visitor speaks falsehood; he gathers slander in his heart; he goes out and spreads it abroad.
8 Alli zäme, wo mi hasse, rede im Gheime gäge mi, gäge mi plane si Böses für mi:
יַ֗חַד עָלַ֣י יִ֭תְלַחֲשׁוּ כָּל־שֹׂנְאָ֑י עָלַ֓י׀יַחְשְׁב֖וּ רָעָ֣ה לִֽי׃
BSB 41:7 All who hate me whisper against me; they imagine the worst for me:
9 „E bösi Chrankheit isch über ne cho, und wo n er jitz lyt, steit er nid meh uf.“
דְּֽבַר־בְּ֭לִיַּעַל יָצ֣וּק בּ֑וֹ וַאֲשֶׁ֥ר שָׁ֝כַ֗ב לֹא־יוֹסִ֥יף לָקֽוּם׃
BSB 41:8 “A vile disease has been poured into him; he will never get up from where he lies!”
דְּבַר־בְּלִיַּעַל → E bösi Chrankheit (A vile disease)
10 Sogar my guete Fründ, won i ihm vertrouet ha, wo mys Brot isst, git mer e Stupf mit em Fuess.
גַּם־אִ֤ישׁ שְׁלוֹמִ֨י׀אֲשֶׁר־בָּטַ֣חְתִּי ב֭וֹאוֹכֵ֣ל לַחְמִ֑י הִגְדִּ֖יל עָלַ֣י עָקֵֽב׃
BSB 41:9 Even my close friend whom I trusted, the one who shared my bread, has lifted up his heel against me.
11 Aber du, HERR, häb Erbarme mit mer und hilf mer wider uf, dass i ne cha zrüggzale!
וְאַתָּ֤ה יְהוָ֗ה חָנֵּ֥נִי וַהֲקִימֵ֑נִי וַֽאֲשַׁלְּמָ֥ה לָהֶֽם׃
BSB 41:10 But You, O LORD, be gracious to me and raise me up, that I may repay them.
12 Dra merke n i, dass du Fröid a mir hesch: dass my Find nid über mi cha juble.
בְּזֹ֣את יָ֭דַעְתִּי כִּֽי־חָפַ֣צְתָּ בִּ֑י כִּ֤י לֹֽא־יָרִ֖יעַ אֹיְבִ֣י עָלָֽי׃
BSB 41:11 By this I know that You delight in me, for my enemy does not triumph over me.
13 Mi aber hesch du ghalte, wil i ufrichtig bi und mi vor dys Gsicht gstellt für geng.
וַאֲנִ֗י בְּ֭תֻמִּי תָּמַ֣כְתָּ בִּ֑י וַתַּצִּיבֵ֖נִי לְפָנֶ֣יךָ לְעוֹלָֽם׃
BSB 41:12 In my integrity You uphold me and set me in Your presence forever.
בְּתֻמִּי → wil i ufrichtig bi (in my integrity)
14 Globet syg der HERR, der Gott vo Israel, vo Ewigkeit zu Ewigkeit! Amen, ja, Amen!
בָּ֘ר֤וּךְ יְהוָ֨ה׀אֱלֹ֘הֵ֤י יִשְׂרָאֵ֗ל מֵֽ֭הָעוֹלָם וְעַ֥ד הָעוֹלָ֗ם אָ֘מֵ֥ן׀וְאָמֵֽן׃
BSB 41:13 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. Amen and Amen.
אָמֵן וְאָמֵן → Amen, ja, Amen! (Amen and Amen)
