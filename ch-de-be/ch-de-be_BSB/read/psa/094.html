1 Gott vo der Rach, HERR, Gott vo der Rach, zeig di im Glanz!
אֵל־נְקָמ֥וֹת יְהוָ֑ה אֵ֖ל נְקָמ֣וֹת הוֹפִֽיַע׃
BSB 94:1 O LORD, God of vengeance, O God of vengeance, shine forth.
אֵל נְקָמוֹת → Gott vo der Rach (God of vengeance)
הוֹפִיעַ → zeig di im Glanz (shine forth)
Wie Ps 80,2.
2 Stand uf, du Richter vo der Ärde, zal dene, wo stolz sy, zrügg, was si verdient hei!
הִ֭נָּשֵׂא שֹׁפֵ֣ט הָאָ֑רֶץ הָשֵׁ֥ב גְּ֝מ֗וּל עַל־גֵּאִֽים׃
BSB 94:2 Rise up, O Judge of the earth; render a reward to the proud.
3 Wi lang no sölle d Gottlose, HERR, wi lang no sölle d Gottlose juble?
עַד־מָתַ֖י רְשָׁעִ֥ים׀יְהוָ֑ה עַד־מָ֝תַ֗י רְשָׁעִ֥ים יַעֲלֹֽזוּ׃
BSB 94:3 How long will the wicked, O LORD, how long will the wicked exult?
4 Si schwätze und rede hochmüetig; alli, wo Unrächt tüe, plagiere.
יַבִּ֣יעוּ יְדַבְּר֣וּ עָתָ֑ק יִֽ֝תְאַמְּר֗וּ כָּל־פֹּ֥עֲלֵי אָֽוֶן׃
BSB 94:4 They pour out arrogant words; all workers of iniquity boast.
יַבִּיעוּ → Si schwätze (They pour out)
5 Dys Volk, HERR, verschla si, und dys Erb plage si.
עַמְּךָ֣ יְהוָ֣ה יְדַכְּא֑וּ וְֽנַחֲלָתְךָ֥ יְעַנּֽוּ׃
BSB 94:5 They crush Your people, O LORD; they oppress Your heritage.
6 D Witwe und der Frömd bringe si um, und d Waise morde si.
אַ֭לְמָנָה וְגֵ֣ר יַהֲרֹ֑גוּ וִֽיתוֹמִ֣ים יְרַצֵּֽחוּ׃
BSB 94:6 They kill the widow and the foreigner; they murder the fatherless.
7 Si säge: „Der HERR gseht's nid, der Gott vom Jakob merkt's nid.“
וַ֭יֹּ֣אמְרוּ לֹ֣א יִרְאֶה־יָּ֑הּ וְלֹא־יָ֝בִ֗ין אֱלֹהֵ֥י יַעֲקֹֽב׃
BSB 94:7 They say, “The LORD does not see; the God of Jacob pays no heed.”
8 Merket's, dihr Dumme im Volk! Und dihr Dumme, wenn wärdet dihr gschyd?
בִּ֭ינוּ בֹּעֲרִ֣ים בָּעָ֑ם וּ֝כְסִילִ֗ים מָתַ֥י תַּשְׂכִּֽילוּ׃
BSB 94:8 Take notice, O senseless among the people! O fools, when will you be wise?
9 Dä, wo ds Ohr gmacht het, söll dä nid ghöre? Dä, wo ds Oug gschaffe het, söll dä nid gseh?
הֲנֹ֣טַֽע אֹ֭זֶןהֲלֹ֣א יִשְׁמָ֑ע אִֽם־יֹ֥צֵֽר עַ֝֗יִן הֲלֹ֣א יַבִּֽיט׃
BSB 94:9 He who affixed the ear, can He not hear? He who formed the eye, can He not see?
10 Dä, wo d Völker straft, söll dä nid zrächtwyse? Dä, wo d Mönsche lehrt, was si wüsse!
הֲיֹסֵ֣ר גּ֭וֹיִם הֲלֹ֣א יוֹכִ֑יחַ הַֽמְלַמֵּ֖ד אָדָ֣ם דָּֽעַת׃
BSB 94:10 He who admonishes the nations, does He not discipline? He who teaches man, does He lack knowledge?
11 Der HERR kennt d Gedanke vom Mönsch: dass si nume ne Huuch sy.
יְֽהוָ֗ה יֹ֭דֵעַ מַחְשְׁב֣וֹת אָדָ֑ם כִּי־הֵ֥מָּה הָֽבֶל׃
BSB 94:11 The LORD knows the thoughts of man, that they are futile.
12 Glücklech isch der Ma, wo du tuesch strafe, HERR, und wo du us dym Gsetz lehrsch,
אַשְׁרֵ֤י׀הַגֶּ֣בֶר אֲשֶׁר־תְּיַסְּרֶ֣נּוּ יָּ֑הּ וּֽמִתּוֹרָתְךָ֥ תְלַמְּדֶֽנּוּ׃
BSB 94:12 Blessed is the man You discipline, O LORD, and teach from Your law,
13 für ihm Rue z gä vor de böse Täg, bis für e Gottlose es Loch grabe isch.
לְהַשְׁקִ֣יט ל֭וֹמִ֣ימֵי רָ֑ע עַ֤ד יִכָּרֶ֖ה לָרָשָׁ֣ע שָֽׁחַת׃
BSB 94:13 to grant him relief from days of trouble, until a pit is dug for the wicked.
שָׁחַת → es Loch (a pit)
14 Denn der HERR verlat sys Volk nid, und sys Erb lat er nid im Stich.
כִּ֤י׀לֹא־יִטֹּ֣שׁ יְהוָ֣ה עַמּ֑וֹ וְ֝נַחֲלָת֗וֹ לֹ֣א יַעֲזֹֽב׃
BSB 94:14 For the LORD will not forsake His people; He will never abandon His heritage.
15 Denn ds Gricht chunt wider zur Grächtigkeit, und alli, wo es ufrichtigs Härz hei, folge ihm.
כִּֽי־עַד־צֶ֭דֶק יָשׁ֣וּב מִשְׁפָּ֑ט וְ֝אַחֲרָ֗יו כָּל־יִשְׁרֵי־לֵֽב׃
BSB 94:15 Surely judgment will again be righteous, and all the upright in heart will follow it.
16 Wär steit für mi uf gäge di Böse? Wär stellt sech für mi gäge die, wo Unrächt tüe?
מִֽי־יָק֣וּם לִ֭י עִם־מְרֵעִ֑ים מִֽי־יִתְיַצֵּ֥ב לִ֝י עִם־פֹּ֥עֲלֵי אָֽוֶן׃
BSB 94:16 Who will rise up for me against the wicked? Who will stand for me against the workers of iniquity?
17 Wen der HERR mer nid ghulfe hätt, wär my Seel scho bald im Land vom Schwyge gsi.
לוּלֵ֣י יְ֭הוָה עֶזְרָ֣תָה לִּ֑י כִּמְעַ֓ט׀שָֽׁכְנָ֖ה דוּמָ֣ה נַפְשִֽׁי׃
BSB 94:17 Unless the LORD had been my helper, I would soon have dwelt in the abode of silence.
דוּמָה → im Land vom Schwyge (in the silence)
18 Won i gseit ha: „My Fuess verlüürt der Halt!“, het mi dy Tröiji, HERR, ghalte.
אִם־אָ֭מַרְתִּי מָ֣טָה רַגְלִ֑י חַסְדְּךָ֥ יְ֝הוָ֗ה יִסְעָדֵֽנִי׃
BSB 94:18 If I say, “My foot is slipping,” Your loving devotion, O LORD, supports me.
19 Wen vil Sorge i mir sy, de tröschtet my Seel dy Troscht.
בְּרֹ֣ב שַׂרְעַפַּ֣י בְּקִרְבִּ֑י תַּ֝נְחוּמֶ֗יךָ יְֽשַׁעַשְׁע֥וּ נַפְשִֽׁי׃
BSB 94:19 When anxiety overwhelms me, Your consolation delights my soul.
20 Cha ne Thron vom Verderbe mit dir verbunde sy, wo Leid macht mit em Gsetz?
הַֽ֭יְחָבְרְךָ כִּסֵּ֣א הַוּ֑וֹת יֹצֵ֖ר עָמָ֣ל עֲלֵי־חֹֽק׃
BSB 94:20 Can a corrupt throne be Your ally — one devising mischief by decree?
כִּסֵּא הַוּוֹת → ne Thron vom Verderbe (a corrupt throne)
21 Si chöme zäme gäge ds Läbe vom Grächte und verurteile unschuldigs Bluet.
יָ֭גוֹדּוּ עַל־נֶ֣פֶשׁ צַדִּ֑יק וְדָ֖ם נָקִ֣י יַרְשִֽׁיעוּ׃
BSB 94:21 They band together against the righteous and condemn the innocent to death.
22 Aber der HERR isch mer zur Burg worde, und my Gott zum Felse vo myr Zueflucht.
וַיְהִ֬י יְהוָ֣ה לִ֣י לְמִשְׂגָּ֑ב וֵ֝אלֹהַ֗י לְצ֣וּר מַחְסִֽי׃
BSB 94:22 But the LORD has been my stronghold, and my God is my rock of refuge.
23 Er lat ires Unrächt uf se sälber zrüggcho und vernichtet se wäge irer Schlächtigkeit; vernichte wird se der HERR, üse Gott.
וַיָּ֤שֶׁב עֲלֵיהֶ֨ם׀אֶת־אוֹנָ֗ם וּבְרָעָתָ֥ם יַצְמִיתֵ֑ם יַ֝צְמִיתֵ֗ם יְהוָ֥ה אֱלֹהֵֽינוּ׃
BSB 94:23 He will bring upon them their own iniquity and destroy them for their wickedness. The LORD our God will destroy them.
