BSB 93:2 Your throne was established long ago; You are from all eternity.

נָשְׂא֤וּ נְהָר֨וֹת׀יְֽהוָ֗ה נָשְׂא֣וּ נְהָר֣וֹת קוֹלָ֑ם יִשְׂא֖וּ נְהָר֣וֹת דָּכְיָֽם׃
BSB 93:3 The floodwaters have risen, O LORD; the rivers have raised their voice; the seas lift up their pounding waves.

מִקֹּל֨וֹת׀מַ֤יִם רַבִּ֗ים אַדִּירִ֣ים מִשְׁבְּרֵי־יָ֑ם אַדִּ֖יר בַּמָּר֣וֹם יְהוָֽה׃
BSB 93:4 Above the roar of many waters— the mighty breakers of the sea— the LORD on high is majestic.

BSB 93:5 Your testimonies are fully confirmed; holiness adorns Your house, O LORD, for all the days to come.
