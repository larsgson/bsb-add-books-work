Ds Buech vo de Psalme, Psalm 79

D Völker sy i dys Erb ychecho

1 E Psalm vom Asaf.

1 E Psalm vom Asaf. Gott, d Völker sy i dys Erb ynecho, si hei dy heilige Tämpel unrein gmacht, si hei Jerusalem zu Ruine gmacht.
מִזְמ֗וֹר לְאָ֫סָ֥ףאֱֽלֹהִ֡ים בָּ֤אוּ גוֹיִ֨ם׀בְּֽנַחֲלָתֶ֗ךָ טִ֭מְּאוּ אֶת־הֵיכַ֣ל קָדְשֶׁ֑ךָ שָׂ֖מוּ אֶת־יְרוּשָׁלִַ֣ם לְעִיִּֽים׃
BSB 79:1 A Psalm of Asaph. The nations, O God, have invaded Your inheritance; they have defiled Your holy temple and reduced Jerusalem to rubble.
טִמְּאוּ → unrein gmacht (defiled)
2 Si hei d Lyche vo dyne Chnächte de Vögel am Himel z ässe ggä, ds Fleisch vo dyne Tröie de Tier vom Fäld.
נָֽתְנ֡וּ אֶת־נִבְלַ֬ת עֲבָדֶ֗יךָ מַ֭אֲכָל לְע֣וֹף הַשָּׁמָ֑יִם בְּשַׂ֥ר חֲ֝סִידֶ֗יךָ לְחַיְתוֹ־אָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 79:2 They have given the corpses of Your servants as food to the birds of the air, the flesh of Your saints to the beasts of the earth.
חֲסִידֶיךָ → dyne Tröie (Your saints)
חָסִיד zu Tröiji.
3 Si hei ires Bluet vergosse wi Wasser rund um Jerusalem, und es het niemer gä, wo se begrabe het.
שָׁפְכ֬וּ דָמָ֨ם׀כַּמַּ֗יִם סְֽבִ֘יב֤וֹת יְֽרוּשָׁלִָ֗ם וְאֵ֣ין קוֹבֵֽר׃
BSB 79:3 They have poured out their blood like water all around Jerusalem, and there is no one to bury the dead.
4 Mir sy zum Gspött worde für üsi Nachbere, zum Spott und zum Gspött für die rund um üüs.
הָיִ֣ינוּ חֶ֭רְפָּה לִשְׁכֵנֵ֑ינוּ לַ֥עַג וָ֝קֶ֗לֶס לִסְבִיבוֹתֵֽינוּ׃
BSB 79:4 We have become a reproach to our neighbors, a scorn and derision to those around us.
לַעַג וָקֶלֶס → zum Spott und zum Gspött (scorned and mocked)
5 Wi lang no, HERR? Bisch du für geng toub? Söll dy Yfer wi Füür brönne?
עַד־מָ֣ה יְ֭הוָה תֶּאֱנַ֣ף לָנֶ֑צַח תִּבְעַ֥ר כְּמוֹ־אֵ֝֗שׁ קִנְאָתֶֽךָ׃
BSB 79:5 How long, O LORD? Will You be angry forever? Will Your jealousy burn like fire?
6 Tue dy Töubi über d Völker schütte, wo di nid kennet, und über d Chünigrych, wo dy Name nid aarüefe!
שְׁפֹ֤ךְ חֲמָתְךָ֨ אֶֽל־הַגּוֹיִם֮ אֲשֶׁ֪ר לֹא־יְדָ֫ע֥וּךָ וְעַ֥ל מַמְלָכ֑וֹת אֲשֶׁ֥ר בְּ֝שִׁמְךָ֗ לֹ֣א קָרָֽאוּ׃
BSB 79:6 Pour out Your wrath on the nations that do not acknowledge You, on the kingdoms that refuse to call on Your name,
7 Denn si hei der Jakob gfrässe und sy Wonig verwüeschtet.
כִּ֭י אָכַ֣ל אֶֽת־יַעֲקֹ֑ב וְֽאֶת־נָוֵ֥הוּ הֵשַֽׁמּוּ׃
BSB 79:7 for they have devoured Jacob and devastated his homeland.
8 Rächne üüs d Schuld vo de früechere Generatione nid aa! Gschwind söll dys Erbarme üüs etgäge cho, denn mir sy ganz schwach worde.
אַֽל־תִּזְכָּר־לָנוּ֮ עֲוֺנֹ֪ת רִאשֹׁ֫נִ֥ים מַ֭הֵר יְקַדְּמ֣וּנוּ רַחֲמֶ֑יךָ כִּ֖י דַלּ֣וֹנוּ מְאֹֽד׃
BSB 79:8 Do not hold past sins against us; let Your compassion come quickly, for we are brought low.
עֲוֺנֹת רִאשֹׁנִים → d Schuld vo de früechere Generatione (the iniquities of our fathers)
9 Hilf üüs, Gott vo üsere Rettig, wäge der Herrlechkeit vo dym Name! Rett is und vergib üsi Sünde um dys Name wille!
עָזְרֵ֤נוּ׀אֱלֹ֘הֵ֤י יִשְׁעֵ֗נוּ עַל־דְּבַ֥ר כְּבֽוֹד־שְׁמֶ֑ךָ וְהַצִּילֵ֥נוּ וְכַפֵּ֥ר עַל־חַ֝טֹּאתֵ֗ינוּ לְמַ֣עַן שְׁמֶֽךָ׃
BSB 79:9 Help us, O God of our salvation, for the glory of Your name; deliver us and atone for our sins, for the sake of Your name.
10 Warum sölle d Völker säge: „Wo isch iren Gott?“ D Völker sölle vor üsne Ouge erfahre, dass du d Rach nimmsch für ds Bluet vo dyne Chnächte, wo vergosse worden isch!
לָ֤מָּה׀יֹאמְר֣וּ הַגּוֹיִם֮ אַיֵּ֪ה אֱֽלֹהֵ֫יהֶ֥ם יִוָּדַ֣ע בַּגּוֹיִ֣ם לְעֵינֵ֑ינוּ נִ֝קְמַ֗ת דַּֽם־עֲבָדֶ֥יךָ הַשָּׁפֽוּךְ׃
BSB 79:10 Why should the nations ask, “Where is their God?” Before our eyes, make known among the nations Your vengeance for the bloodshed of Your servants.
11 Ds Gjammer vo de Gfangene söll vor di cho! Mit dym starche Arm bhalt die am Läbe, wo zum Tod verurteilt sy!
תָּ֤ב֣וֹא לְפָנֶיךָ֮ אֶנְקַ֪ת אָ֫סִ֥יר כְּגֹ֥דֶל זְרוֹעֲךָ֑ ה֝וֹתֵ֗ר בְּנֵ֣י תְמוּתָֽה׃
BSB 79:11 May the groans of the captives reach You; by the strength of Your arm preserve those condemned to death.
בְּנֵי תְמוּתָה → die, wo zum Tod verurteilt sy (those condemned to death)
12 Zal üsne Nachbere sibemal i iri Schoss zrügg der Spott, wo si di mit verspottet hei, Herr!
וְהָ֘שֵׁ֤ב לִשְׁכֵנֵ֣ינוּ שִׁ֭בְעָתַיִם אֶל־חֵיקָ֑ם חֶרְפָּ֘תָ֤ם אֲשֶׁ֖ר חֵרְפ֣וּךָ אֲדֹנָֽי׃
BSB 79:12 Pay back into the laps of our neighbors sevenfold the reproach they hurled at You, O Lord.
שִׁבְעָתַיִם אֶל־חֵיקָם → sibemal i iri Schoss (sevenfold into their laps)
13 Mir aber, dys Volk und d Schaf vo dyr Weid, danke dir für geng; vo Generation zu Generation verzelle mer vo dym Lob.
וַאֲנַ֤חְנוּ עַמְּךָ֨׀וְצֹ֥אן מַרְעִיתֶךָ֮ נ֤וֹדֶ֥ה לְּךָ֗ לְע֫וֹלָ֥ם לְדֹ֥ר וָדֹ֑ר נְ֝סַפֵּ֗ר תְּהִלָּתֶֽךָ׃
BSB 79:13 Then we Your people, the sheep of Your pasture, will thank You forever; from generation to generation we will declare Your praise.
