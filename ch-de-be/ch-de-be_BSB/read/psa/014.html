BSB 14:1 For the choirmaster. Of David. The fool says in his heart, “There is no God.” They are corrupt; their acts are vile. There is no one who does good.

BSB 14:2 The LORD looks down from heaven upon the sons of men to see if any understand, if any seek God.
3 Alli sy abgfalle, zäme sy si schlächt worde; kene tuet öppis Guets, nid en einzige.
הַכֹּ֥ל סָר֮ יַחְדָּ֪ו נֶ֫אֱלָ֥חוּ אֵ֤ין עֹֽשֵׂה־ט֑וֹב אֵ֝֗ין גַּם־אֶחָֽד׃
BSB 14:3 All have turned away, they have together become corrupt; there is no one who does good, not even one.
סָר → sy abgfalle (have turned away)
4 Merke si's de nie, all die, wo Unrächt tüe, wo mys Volk frässe, wi we si Brot ässe, und der HERR nid aarüefe?
הֲלֹ֥א יָדְעוּ֮ כָּל־פֹּ֪עֲלֵ֫י אָ֥וֶן אֹכְלֵ֣י עַ֭מִּי אָ֣כְלוּ לֶ֑חֶם יְ֝הוָ֗ה לֹ֣א קָרָֽאוּ׃
BSB 14:4 Will the workers of iniquity never learn? They devour my people like bread; they refuse to call upon the LORD.
אֹכְלֵי עַמִּי אָכְלוּ לֶחֶם → wo mys Volk frässe, wi we si Brot ässe (devour my people like bread)
5 Da überchöme si der Schrecke; denn Gott isch bi der Generation vo de Grächte.
שָׁ֤ם׀פָּ֣חֲדוּ פָ֑חַד כִּֽי־אֱ֝לֹהִ֗ים בְּד֣וֹר צַדִּֽיק׃
BSB 14:5 There they are, overwhelmed with dread, for God is in the company of the righteous.
בְּדוֹר צַדִּיק → bi der Generation vo de Grächte (in the company of the righteous)
6 Dihr wettet d Plän vom Arme z Schande mache, aber der HERR isch sy Zueflucht.
עֲצַת־עָנִ֥י תָבִ֑ישׁוּ כִּ֖י יְהוָ֣ה מַחְסֵֽהוּ׃
BSB 14:6 You sinners frustrate the plans of the oppressed, yet the LORD is their shelter.
7 Wen doch d Rettig für Israel vo Zion chäm! Wen der HERR sym Volk ds Glück zrügg git, de jublet Jakob, de fröit sech Israel.
מִ֥י יִתֵּ֣ן מִצִּיּוֹן֮ יְשׁוּעַ֪ת יִשְׂרָ֫אֵ֥ל בְּשׁ֣וּב יְ֭הוָה שְׁב֣וּת עַמּ֑וֹ יָגֵ֥ל יַ֝עֲקֹ֗ב יִשְׂמַ֥ח יִשְׂרָֽאֵל׃
BSB 14:7 Oh, that the salvation of Israel would come from Zion! When the LORD restores His captive people, let Jacob rejoice, let Israel be glad!
בְּשׁוּב … שְׁבוּת → ds Glück zrügg git (restores His captive people)
