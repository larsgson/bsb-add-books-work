1 Danket em HERR, rüefet sy Name aa! Machet syni Tate under de Völker bekannt!
הוֹד֣וּ לַ֭יהוָה קִרְא֣וּ בִּשְׁמ֑וֹ הוֹדִ֥יעוּ בָ֝עַמִּ֗ים עֲלִילוֹתָֽיו׃
BSB 105:1 Give thanks to the LORD, call upon His name; make known His deeds among the nations.
2 Singet ihm, singet ihm Psalme, redet vo allne syne Wunder!
שִֽׁירוּ־ל֭וֹזַמְּרוּ־ל֑וֹ שִׂ֝֗יחוּ בְּכָל־נִפְלְאוֹתָֽיו׃
BSB 105:2 Sing to Him, sing praises to Him; tell of all His wonders.
3 Rüemet öich mit sym heilige Name! Ds Härz vo dene, wo der HERR sueche, söll sech fröie!
הִֽ֭תְהַלְלוּ בְּשֵׁ֣ם קָדְשׁ֑וֹ יִ֝שְׂמַ֗ח לֵ֤ב׀מְבַקְשֵׁ֬י יְהוָֽה׃
BSB 105:3 Glory in His holy name; let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice.
4 Fraget nach em HERR und nach syr Chraft, suechet sys Gsicht geng!
דִּרְשׁ֣וּ יְהוָ֣ה וְעֻזּ֑וֹ בַּקְּשׁ֖וּ פָנָ֣יו תָּמִֽיד׃
BSB 105:4 Seek out the LORD and His strength; seek His face always.
5 Dänket a syni Wunder, wo n er ta het, a syni Zeiche und d Urteil, won er gsproche het,
זִכְר֗וּ נִפְלְאוֹתָ֥יו אֲשֶׁר־עָשָׂ֑ה מֹ֝פְתָ֗יו וּמִשְׁפְּטֵי־פִֽיו׃
BSB 105:5 Remember the wonders He has done, His marvels, and the judgments He has pronounced,
6 dihr Nachkomme vom Abraham, sym Chnächt, dihr Söhn vom Jakob, syni Userwählte!
זֶ֭רַע אַבְרָהָ֣ם עַבְדּ֑וֹ בְּנֵ֖י יַעֲקֹ֣ב בְּחִירָֽיו׃
BSB 105:6 O offspring of His servant Abraham, O sons of Jacob, His chosen ones.
7 Är isch der HERR, üse Gott; über di ganzi Ärde gö syni Urteil.
ה֭וּא יְהוָ֣ה אֱלֹהֵ֑ינוּ בְּכָל־הָ֝אָ֗רֶץ מִשְׁפָּטָֽיו׃
BSB 105:7 He is the LORD our God; His judgments carry throughout the earth.
8 Er dänkt für geng a sy Bund, a ds Wort, won er für tuusig Generatione befole het,
זָכַ֣ר לְעוֹלָ֣ם בְּרִית֑וֹ דָּבָ֥ר צִ֝וָּ֗ה לְאֶ֣לֶף דּֽוֹר׃
BSB 105:8 He remembers His covenant forever, the word He ordained for a thousand generations—
9 a dä Bund, won er mit em Abraham gschlosse het, und a sy Schwur gägenüber em Isaak.
אֲשֶׁ֣ר כָּ֭רַת אֶת־אַבְרָהָ֑ם וּשְׁב֖וּעָת֣וֹ לְיִשְׂחָֽק׃
BSB 105:9 the covenant He made with Abraham, and the oath He swore to Isaac.
10 Er het ne für e Jakob als Gsetz fescht gmacht, für Israel als ewige Bund,
וַיַּֽעֲמִידֶ֣הָ לְיַעֲקֹ֣ב לְחֹ֑ק לְ֝יִשְׂרָאֵ֗ל בְּרִ֣ית עוֹלָֽם׃
BSB 105:10 He confirmed it to Jacob as a decree, to Israel as an everlasting covenant:
11 und gseit: „Dir gibe n i ds Land Kanaan als Stück vo öiem Erb.“
לֵאמֹ֗ר לְךָ֗ אֶתֵּ֥ן אֶת־אֶֽרֶץ־כְּנָ֑עַן חֶ֝֗בֶל נַחֲלַתְכֶֽם׃
BSB 105:11 “I will give you the land of Canaan as the portion of your inheritance.”
12 Denn sy si nume weni gsi, und Frömdi drinn.
בִּֽ֭הְיוֹתָם מְתֵ֣י מִסְפָּ֑ר כִּ֝מְעַ֗ט וְגָרִ֥ים בָּֽהּ׃
BSB 105:12 When they were few in number, few indeed, and strangers in the land,
13 Si sy umezoge vo eim Volk zum andere, vo eim Chünigrych zum andere Volk.
וַֽ֭יִּתְהַלְּכוּ מִגּ֣וֹי אֶל־גּ֑וֹי מִ֝מַּמְלָכָ֗ה אֶל־עַ֥ם אַחֵֽר׃
BSB 105:13 they wandered from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another.
14 Er het niemerem erloubt, ne Unrächt z tue, und wäge ne het er Chünige gstraft:
לֹֽא־הִנִּ֣יחַ אָדָ֣ם לְעָשְׁקָ֑ם וַיּ֖וֹכַח עֲלֵיהֶ֣ם מְלָכִֽים׃
BSB 105:14 He let no man oppress them; He rebuked kings on their behalf:
15 „Rüeret die nid aa, won i gsalbet ha, und tüet myne Prophete nüüt Böses!“
אַֽל־תִּגְּע֥וּ בִמְשִׁיחָ֑י וְ֝לִנְבִיאַי אַל־תָּרֵֽעוּ׃
BSB 105:15 “Do not touch My anointed ones! Do no harm to My prophets!”
בִמְשִׁיחָי → die, won i gsalbet ha (My anointed ones)

וַיִּקְרָ֣א רָ֭עָב עַל־הָאָ֑רֶץ כָּֽל־מַטֵּה־לֶ֥חֶם שָׁבָֽר׃
BSB 105:16 He called down famine on the land and cut off all their supplies of food.

17 Er het vor ne här e Ma gschickt: als Sklav isch der Josef verchouft worde.
שָׁלַ֣ח לִפְנֵיהֶ֣ם אִ֑ישׁ לְ֝עֶ֗בֶד נִמְכַּ֥ר יוֹסֵֽף׃
BSB 105:17 He sent a man before them— Joseph, sold as a slave.
18 Si hei sy Füess mit Fessle plaget, a sy Hals isch e Chetti cho,
עִנּ֣וּ בַכֶּ֣בֶל רַגְל֑וֹ בַּ֝רְזֶ֗ל בָּ֣אָה נַפְשֽׁוֹ׃
BSB 105:18 They bruised his feet with shackles and placed his neck in irons,

BSB 105:19 until his prediction came true and the word of the LORD proved him right.

BSB 105:20 The king sent and released him; the ruler of peoples set him free.
21 Er het ne zum Herr über sys Huus gmacht und zum Herrscher über alls, was er het,

BSB 105:21 He made him master of his household, ruler over all his substance,
22 für syni Füerer nach sym Wille z leite und syne Eltischte Wysheit z lehre.

BSB 105:22 to instruct his princes as he pleased and teach his elders wisdom.

BSB 105:26 He sent Moses His servant, and Aaron, whom He had chosen.

שָֽׂמוּ־בָ֭ם דִּבְרֵ֣י אֹתוֹתָ֑יו וּ֝מֹפְתִ֗ים בְּאֶ֣רֶץ חָֽם׃

BSB 105:35 They devoured every plant in their land and consumed the produce of their soil.
