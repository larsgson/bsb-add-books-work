BSB 29:2 Ascribe to the LORD the glory due His name; worship the LORD in the splendor of His holiness.
הָבוּ לַיהוָה → Gät em HERR (Ascribe to the LORD)

BSB 29:8 The voice of the LORD shakes the wilderness; the LORD shakes the Wilderness of Kadesh.

יְ֭הוָה לַמַּבּ֣וּל יָשָׁ֑ב וַיֵּ֥שֶׁב יְ֝הוָ֗ה מֶ֣לֶךְ לְעוֹלָֽם׃
BSB 29:10 The LORD sits enthroned over the flood; the LORD is enthroned as King forever.
לַמַּבּוּל → über der Sintfluet (over the flood)
