BSB 109:1 For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. O God of my praise, be not silent.
2 Denn der Muul vom Gottlose und ds Muul vom Lugner hei sech gäge mi ufta; si rede gäge mi mit Lüge.
כִּ֤י פִ֪י רָשָׁ֡ע וּֽפִי־מִ֭רְמָה עָלַ֣י פָּתָ֑חוּ דִּבְּר֥וּ אִ֝תִּ֗י לְשׁ֣וֹן שָֽׁקֶר׃
BSB 109:2 For wicked and deceitful mouths open against me; they speak against me with lying tongues.
3 Mit Wort, wo hässig sy, sy si rings um mi und kämpfe gäge mi ohni Grund.
וְדִבְרֵ֣י שִׂנְאָ֣ה סְבָב֑וּנִי וַיִּֽלָּחֲמ֥וּנִי חִנָּֽם׃
BSB 109:3 They surround me with hateful words and attack me without cause.
4 Für myni Liebi verchlage si mi, aber i – i bätte.
תַּֽחַת־אַהֲבָתִ֥י יִשְׂטְנ֗וּנִי וַאֲנִ֥י תְפִלָּֽה׃
BSB 109:4 In return for my love they accuse me, but I am a man of prayer.
וַאֲנִי תְפִלָּה → aber i – i bätte (but I am a man of prayer)
5 Si tüe mir Böses für Guets, und si hasse mi für myni Liebi.
וַיָּ֘שִׂ֤ימוּ עָלַ֣י רָ֭עָה תַּ֣חַת טוֹבָ֑ה וְ֝שִׂנְאָ֗ה תַּ֣חַת אַהֲבָתִֽי׃
BSB 109:5 They repay me evil for good, and hatred for my love.
6 Stell e Gottlose über ne und eine, wo ne verchlagt, söll a syr rächte Syte sta!
הַפְקֵ֣ד עָלָ֣יו רָשָׁ֑ע וְ֝שָׂטָ֗ן יַעֲמֹ֥ד עַל־יְמִינֽוֹ׃
BSB 109:6 Set over him a wicked man; let an accuser stand at his right hand.
7 Wen er grichtet wird, söll er schuldig usecho, und sys Gebätt söll zur Sünd wärde!
בְּ֭הִשָּׁ֣פְטוֹ יֵצֵ֣א רָשָׁ֑ע וּ֝תְפִלָּת֗וֹ תִּהְיֶ֥ה לַֽחֲטָאָֽה׃
BSB 109:7 When he is tried, let him be found guilty, and may his prayer be regarded as sin.
8 Syni Täg sölle weni sy, sy Dienscht söll en andere übernä!
יִֽהְיֽוּ־יָמָ֥יו מְעַטִּ֑ים פְּ֝קֻדָּת֗וֹ יִקַּ֥ח אַחֵֽר׃
BSB 109:8 May his days be few; may another take his position.
9 Syni Chind sölle Waise wärde und sy Frou e Witwe!
יִֽהְיוּ־בָנָ֥יו יְתוֹמִ֑ים וְ֝אִשְׁתּוֹ אַלְמָנָֽה׃
BSB 109:9 May his children be fatherless and his wife a widow.
10 Syni Chind sölle umeloufe und bättle und us irne Ruine use ga sueche!
וְנ֤וֹעַ יָנ֣וּעוּ בָנָ֣יו וְשִׁאֵ֑לוּ וְ֝דָרְשׁ֗וּ מֵחָרְבוֹתֵיהֶֽם׃
BSB 109:10 May his children wander as beggars, seeking sustenance far from their ruined homes.
מֵחָרְבוֹתֵיהֶם → us irne Ruine use (from their ruined homes)
11 Der Schuldner söll alls näh, was er het, und Frömdi sölle usplündere, won er derfür gschaffet het!
יְנַקֵּ֣שׁ נ֭וֹשֶׁה לְכָל־אֲשֶׁר־ל֑וֹ וְיָבֹ֖זּוּ זָרִ֣ים יְגִיעֽוֹ׃
BSB 109:11 May the creditor seize all he owns, and strangers plunder the fruits of his labor.
נוֹשֶׁה → Der Schuldner (the creditor)
Korpuswort; gemeint der Gläubiger.
12 Niemer söll ihm Tröiji zeige, und niemer söll Erbarme ha mit syne Waise!
אַל־יְהִי־ל֭וֹמֹשֵׁ֣ךְ חָ֑סֶד וְֽאַל־יְהִ֥י ח֝וֹנֵ֗ן לִיתוֹמָֽיו׃
BSB 109:12 May there be no one to extend kindness to him, and no one to favor his fatherless children.
13 Syni Nachkomme sölle usgrottet wärde, i der nächschte Generation söll ire Name nümme da sy!
יְהִֽי־אַחֲרִית֥וֹ לְהַכְרִ֑ית בְּד֥וֹר אַ֝חֵ֗ר יִמַּ֥ח שְׁמָֽם׃
BSB 109:13 May his descendants be cut off; may their name be blotted out from the next generation.
14 D Schuld vo syne Vätter söll vor em HERR im Gedächtnis blybe, und d Sünd vo syr Mueter söll nid vergässe wärde!
יִזָּכֵ֤ר׀עֲוֺ֣ן אֲ֭בֹתָיו אֶל־יְהוָ֑ה וְחַטַּ֥את אִ֝מּ֗וֹ אַל־תִּמָּֽח׃
BSB 109:14 May the iniquity of his fathers be remembered before the LORD, and the sin of his mother never be blotted out.
15 Si sölle geng vor em HERR sy, dass er se vo der Ärde us em Gedächtnis tüej usrotte!
יִהְי֣וּ נֶֽגֶד־יְהוָ֣ה תָּמִ֑יד וְיַכְרֵ֖ת מֵאֶ֣רֶץ זִכְרָֽם׃
BSB 109:15 May their sins always remain before the LORD, that He may cut off their memory from the earth.
16 Denn er het nid dra dänkt, Tröiji z zeige, sondern er het der Arm und Bedürftig gjagt und dä, wo im Härz bedrückt isch, für ne z töde.
יַ֗עַן אֲשֶׁ֤ר׀לֹ֥א זָכַר֮ עֲשׂ֪וֹת חָ֥סֶד וַיִּרְדֹּ֡ף אִישׁ־עָנִ֣י וְ֭אֶבְיוֹן וְנִכְאֵ֨ה לֵבָ֬ב לְמוֹתֵֽת׃
BSB 109:16 For he never thought to show kindness, but pursued the poor and needy and brokenhearted, even to their death.
17 Er het der Fluech gärn gha – so söll er über ihn cho! Am Säge het er ke Fröid gha – so söll er wyt vo ihm blybe!
וַיֶּאֱהַ֣ב קְ֭לָלָה וַתְּבוֹאֵ֑הוּ וְֽלֹא־חָפֵ֥ץ בִּ֝בְרָכָ֗ה וַתִּרְחַ֥ק מִמֶּֽנּוּ׃
BSB 109:17 The cursing that he loved, may it fall on him; the blessing in which he refused to delight, may it be far from him.
18 Er het der Fluech aagleit wi sys Chleid – so söll er wi Wasser i sys Innere cho und wi Öl i syni Chnoche!
וַיִּלְבַּ֥שׁ קְלָלָ֗ה כְּמַ֫דּ֥וֹ וַתָּבֹ֣א כַמַּ֣יִם בְּקִרְבּ֑וֹ וְ֝כַשֶּׁ֗מֶן בְּעַצְמוֹתָֽיו׃
BSB 109:18 The cursing that he wore like a coat, may it soak into his body like water, and into his bones like oil.
בְקִרְבּוֹ → i sys Innere (into his body)
19 Er söll ihm sy wi nes Chleid, won er aaleit, und wi ne Gurt, won er geng treit!
תְּהִי־ל֭וֹכְּבֶ֣גֶד יַעְטֶ֑ה וּ֝לְמֵ֗זַח תָּמִ֥יד יַחְגְּרֶֽהָ׃
BSB 109:19 May it be like a robe wrapped about him, like a belt tied forever around him.
20 Das söll der Lohn sy vo dene, wo mi aachlage, vo em HERR, vo dene, wo Böses rede gäge mi!
זֹ֤את פְּעֻלַּ֣ת שֹׂ֭טְנַי מֵאֵ֣ת יְהוָ֑ה וְהַדֹּבְרִ֥ים רָ֝֗ע עַל־נַפְשִֽׁי׃
BSB 109:20 May this be the LORD’s reward to my accusers, to those who speak evil against me.
21 Aber du, HERR, my Herr, handle a mir wäge dym Name! Wil dy Tröiji guet isch, rett mi!
וְאַתָּ֤ה׀יְה֘וִ֤ה אֲדֹנָ֗י עֲֽשֵׂה־אִ֭תִּי לְמַ֣עַן שְׁמֶ֑ךָ כִּי־ט֥וֹב חַ֝סְדְּךָ֗ הַצִּילֵֽנִי׃
BSB 109:21 But You, O GOD, the Lord, deal kindly with me for the sake of Your name; deliver me by the goodness of Your loving devotion.
22 Denn i bi arm und bedürftig, und mys Härz isch troffe i mir.
כִּֽי־עָנִ֣י וְאֶבְי֣וֹן אָנֹ֑כִי וְ֝לִבִּ֗י חָלַ֥ל בְּקִרְבִּֽי׃
BSB 109:22 For I am poor and needy; my heart is wounded within me.
23 Wi ne Schatte, wen er lang wird, verschwinde n i, i wirde abgschüttlet wi ne Höigümper.
כְּצֵל־כִּנְטוֹת֥וֹ נֶהֱלָ֑כְתִּי נִ֝נְעַ֗רְתִּי כָּֽאַרְבֶּֽה׃
BSB 109:23 I am fading away like a lengthening shadow; I am shaken off like a locust.
נִנְעַרְתִּי כָּאַרְבֶּה → i wirde abgschüttlet wi ne Höigümper (I am shaken off like a locust)
24 Myni Chnöi sy schwach vom Faschte, und mys Fleisch het abgno, es isch nüüt meh dranne.
בִּ֭רְכַּי כָּשְׁל֣וּ מִצּ֑וֹם וּ֝בְשָׂרִ֗י כָּחַ֥שׁ מִשָּֽׁמֶן׃
BSB 109:24 My knees are weak from fasting, and my body grows lean and gaunt.
כָּחַשׁ מִשָּׁמֶן → het abgno, es isch nüüt meh dranne (has become gaunt)
25 Und i bi ne zum Gspött worde; wen me mi gseht, schüttlet me der Chopf.
וַאֲנִ֤י׀הָיִ֣יתִי חֶרְפָּ֣ה לָהֶ֑ם יִ֝רְא֗וּנִי יְנִיע֥וּן רֹאשָֽׁם׃
BSB 109:25 I am an object of scorn to my accusers; when they see me, they shake their heads.
26 Hilf mer, HERR, my Gott! Rett mi nach dyr Tröiji,
עָ֭זְרֵנִי יְהוָ֣ה אֱלֹהָ֑י ה֭וֹשִׁיעֵ֣נִי כְחַסְדֶּֽךָ׃
BSB 109:26 Help me, O LORD my God; save me according to Your loving devotion.
27 dass si merke, dass das dy Hand isch, dass du, HERR, es gmacht hesch!
וְֽ֭יֵדְעוּ כִּי־יָ֣דְךָ זֹּ֑את אַתָּ֖ה יְהוָ֣ה עֲשִׂיתָֽהּ׃
BSB 109:27 Let them know that this is Your hand, that You, O LORD, have done it.
28 Si sölle flueche, aber du sägnisch. Si sy ufgstande und sölle z Schande wärde, aber dy Chnächt söll sech fröie!
יְקַֽלְלוּ־הֵמָּה֮ וְאַתָּ֪ה תְבָ֫רֵ֥ךְ קָ֤מוּ׀וַיֵּבֹ֗שׁוּ וְֽעַבְדְּךָ֥ יִשְׂמָֽח׃
BSB 109:28 Though they curse, You will bless. When they rise up, they will be put to shame, but Your servant will rejoice.
29 Die, wo mi aachlage, sölle mit Schand aagleit wärde und i iri eigeti Schand ygwicklet sy wi i ne Mantel!
יִלְבְּשׁ֣וּ שׂוֹטְנַ֣י כְּלִמָּ֑ה וְיַעֲט֖וּ כַמְעִ֣יל בָּשְׁתָּֽם׃
BSB 109:29 May my accusers be clothed with disgrace; may they wear their shame like a robe.
30 I wott em HERR mit mym Muul sehr danke, und mitts under vilne wott i ne lobe.
א֘וֹדֶ֤ה יְהוָ֣ה מְאֹ֣ד בְּפִ֑י וּבְת֖וֹךְ רַבִּ֣ים אֲהַֽלְלֶֽנּוּ׃
BSB 109:30 With my mouth I will thank the LORD profusely; I will praise Him in the presence of many.
31 Denn er steit a der rächte Syte vom Arme, für ne z rette vor dene, wo sys Läbe verurteile.
כִּֽי־יַ֭עֲמֹד לִימִ֣ין אֶבְי֑וֹן לְ֝הוֹשִׁ֗יעַ מִשֹּׁפְטֵ֥י נַפְשֽׁוֹ׃
BSB 109:31 For He stands at the right hand of the needy one, to save him from the condemners of his soul.
