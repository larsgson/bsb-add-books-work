1 E Psalm. Es Lied für d Yweih vom Huus. Vom David.
מִזְמ֡וֹר שִׁיר־חֲנֻכַּ֖ת הַבַּ֣יִת לְדָוִֽד׃
שִׁיר־חֲנֻכַּת הַבַּיִת → Es Lied für d Yweih vom Huus (A song for the dedication of the house)
»Tämpelweih« Joh 10,22 (Store).
2 I wott di rüeme, HERR, denn du hesch mi use zoge und hesch myni Finde nid la juble über mi.
אֲרוֹמִמְךָ֣ יְ֭הוָה כִּ֣י דִלִּיתָ֑נִי וְלֹא־שִׂמַּ֖חְתָּ אֹיְבַ֣י לִֽי׃
BSB 30:1 A Psalm. A song for the dedication of the temple. Of David. I will exalt You, O LORD, for You have lifted me up and have not allowed my foes to rejoice over me.
דִלִּיתָנִי → hesch mi use zoge (You have lifted me up)
Bild: aus dem Brunnen ziehen.
3 HERR, my Gott, i ha zu dir um Hilf grüeft, und du hesch mi gsund gmacht.
יְהוָ֥ה אֱלֹהָ֑י שִׁוַּ֥עְתִּי אֵ֝לֶ֗יךָ וַתִּרְפָּאֵֽנִי׃
BSB 30:2 O LORD my God, I cried to You for help, and You healed me.
4 HERR, du hesch my Seel us em Toterych ufe bbracht, du hesch mi am Läbe bhalte, dass i nid i d Grueb ha müesse abe.
יְֽהוָ֗ה הֶֽעֱלִ֣יתָ מִן־שְׁא֣וֹל נַפְשִׁ֑י חִ֝יִּיתַ֗נִי מִיָּֽרְדִי־בֽוֹר׃
BSB 30:3 O LORD, You pulled me up from Sheol; You spared me from descending into the Pit.
מִיָּרְדִי־בוֹר → dass i nid i d Grueb ha müesse abe (from descending to the Pit)
Qere.
5 Singet em HERR Psalme, dihr, wo ihm tröi syt, und danket sym heilige Name!
זַמְּר֣וּ לַיהוָ֣ה חֲסִידָ֑יו וְ֝הוֹד֗וּ לְזֵ֣כֶר קָדְשֽׁוֹ׃
BSB 30:4 Sing to the LORD, O you His saints, and praise His holy name.
זֵכֶר קָדְשׁוֹ → sym heilige Name (His holy name)
»Gedächtnis«.
6 Denn sy Töubi duuret nume ne Ougeblick, sy Gnad es Läbe lang. Am Aabe chunt ds Briegge, aber am Morge d Fröid.
כִּ֤י רֶ֨גַע׀בְּאַפּוֹ֮ חַיִּ֪ים בִּרְצ֫וֹנ֥וֹ בָּ֭עֶרֶב יָלִ֥ין בֶּ֗כִי וְלַבֹּ֥קֶר רִנָּֽה׃
BSB 30:5 For His anger is fleeting, but His favor lasts a lifetime. Weeping may stay the night, but joy comes in the morning.
רֶגַע … חַיִּים → nume ne Ougeblick … es Läbe lang (but a moment … a lifetime)
7 I ha gseit, won es mer guet ggange isch: „I chume für geng nid zum Wanke.“
וַ֭אֲנִי אָמַ֣רְתִּי בְשַׁלְוִ֑י בַּל־אֶמּ֥וֹט לְעוֹלָֽם׃
BSB 30:6 In prosperity I said, “I will never be shaken.”
בַּל־אֶמּוֹט → I chume … nid zum Wanke (I will never be shaken)
Fest.
8 HERR, i dyr Gnad hesch du mi uf e feschte Bärg gstellt. Du hesch dys Gsicht versteckt – da bin i erchlüpft.
יְֽהוָ֗ה בִּרְצוֹנְךָ֮ הֶעֱמַ֪דְתָּה לְֽהַרְרִ֫י עֹ֥ז הִסְתַּ֥רְתָּ פָנֶ֗יךָ הָיִ֥יתִי נִבְהָֽל׃
BSB 30:7 O LORD, You favored me; You made my mountain stand strong. When You hid Your face, I was dismayed.
לְהַרְרִי עֹז → uf e feschte Bärg (made my mountain stand strong)
Text schwierig.
9 Zu dir, HERR, han i grüeft, und zum Herr han i um Gnad bbätte:
אֵלֶ֣יךָ יְהוָ֣ה אֶקְרָ֑א וְאֶל־אֲ֝דֹנָ֗י אֶתְחַנָּֽן׃
BSB 30:8 To You, O LORD, I called, and I begged my Lord for mercy:
10 „Was hesch du für ne Gwinn vo mym Tod, wen i i d Grueb abe mues? Cha der Stoub di lobe? Cha er dy Tröiji verchünte?
מַה־בֶּ֥צַע בְּדָמִי֮ בְּרִדְתִּ֪י אֶ֫ל־שָׁ֥חַת הֲיוֹדְךָ֥ עָפָ֑ר הֲיַגִּ֥יד אֲמִתֶּֽךָ׃
BSB 30:9 “What gain is there in my bloodshed, in my descent to the Pit? Will the dust praise You? Will it proclaim Your faithfulness?
11 Los, HERR, und häb Erbarme mit mer! HERR, bis du my Hälfer!“
שְׁמַע־יְהוָ֥ה וְחָנֵּ֑נִי יְ֝הוָההֱֽיֵה־עֹזֵ֥ר לִֽי׃
BSB 30:10 Hear me, O LORD, and have mercy; O LORD, be my helper.”
12 Du hesch mys Chlage i ne Tanz verwandlet für mi, du hesch mer der Sack abzoge und mi mit Fröid aagleit,
הָפַ֣כְתָּ מִסְפְּדִי֮ לְמָח֪וֹל לִ֥י פִּתַּ֥חְתָּ שַׂקִּ֑י וַֽתְּאַזְּרֵ֥נִי שִׂמְחָֽה׃
BSB 30:11 You turned my mourning into dancing; You peeled off my sackcloth and clothed me with joy,
שַׂקִּי → der Sack (my sackcloth)
Trauerkleid.
13 dass my Seel dir cha Psalme singe und nid still isch. HERR, my Gott, für geng wott i dir danke!
לְמַ֤עַן׀יְזַמֶּרְךָ֣ כָ֭בוֹד וְלֹ֣א יִדֹּ֑ם יְהוָ֥ה אֱ֝לֹהַ֗י לְעוֹלָ֥ם אוֹדֶֽךָּ׃
BSB 30:12 that my heart may sing Your praises and not be silent. O LORD my God, I will give thanks forever.
כָבוֹד → my Seel (my heart)
Wörtlich »Ehre/Herrlichkeit« (wie Ps 16,9).
