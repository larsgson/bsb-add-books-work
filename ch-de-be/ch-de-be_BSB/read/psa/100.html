BSB 100:1 A Psalm of thanksgiving. Make a joyful noise to the LORD, all the earth.
2 Dienet em HERR mit Fröid, chömet vor sys Gsicht und jublet!
עִבְד֣וּ אֶת־יְהוָ֣ה בְּשִׂמְחָ֑ה בֹּ֥אוּ לְ֝פָנָ֗יו בִּרְנָנָֽה׃
BSB 100:2 Serve the LORD with gladness; come into His presence with joyful songs.
בִּרְנָנָה → und jublet (with joyful songs)
3 Wüsset, dass der HERR Gott isch! Är het üüs gmacht, und mir ghöre ihm: mir sy sys Volk und d Schaf vo syr Weid.
דְּע֗וּ כִּֽי־יְהוָה֮ ה֤וּא אֱלֹ֫הִ֥ים הֽוּא־עָ֭שָׂנוּ וְל֣וֹ אֲנַ֑חְנוּ עַ֝מּ֗וֹ וְצֹ֣אן מַרְעִיתֽוֹ׃
BSB 100:3 Know that the LORD is God. It is He who made us, and we are His; we are His people, and the sheep of His pasture.
דְּעוּ → Wüsset (Know)
4 Chömet mit Dank dür syni Tor, mit Lob i sy Vorhof! Danket ihm, lobet sy Name!
בֹּ֤אוּ שְׁעָרָ֨יו׀בְּתוֹדָ֗ה חֲצֵרֹתָ֥יו בִּתְהִלָּ֑ה הֽוֹדוּ־ל֝֗וֹ בָּרֲכ֥וּ שְׁמֽוֹ׃
BSB 100:4 Enter His gates with thanksgiving and His courts with praise; give thanks to Him and bless His name.
חֲצֵרֹתָיו → i sy Vorhof (His courts)
Singular wie Ps 96,8.
5 Denn der HERR isch guet, sy Tröiji blybt für geng, und sy Zueverlässigkeit vo Generation zu Generation.

BSB 100:5 For the LORD is good, and His loving devotion endures forever; His faithfulness continues to all generations.
