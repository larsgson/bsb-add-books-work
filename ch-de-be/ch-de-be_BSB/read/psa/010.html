Hebräisch: Westminster Leningrad Codex (gemeinfrei), über MACULA Hebrew (CC BY 4.0, Clear Bible); Griechisch: Berean Greek Bible; Englisch: Berean Standard Bible (beide gemeinfrei). Die Kommentare sind die Notizen des Übersetzungsagenten.
1 HERR, wiso steisch du so wyt ewägg? Wiso tuesch du di verstecke i Zyte vo Not?
לָמָ֣ה יְ֭הוָה תַּעֲמֹ֣ד בְּרָח֑וֹק תַּ֝עְלִ֗ים לְעִתּ֥וֹת בַּצָּרָֽה׃
BSB 10:1 Why, O LORD, do You stand far off? Why do You hide in times of trouble?
2 Voll Stolz jagt der Gottlos hinder em Arme här; si sölle i de Plän gfange wärde, wo si sälber gmacht hei!
בְּגַאֲוַ֣ת רָ֭שָׁע יִדְלַ֣ק עָנִ֑י יִתָּפְשׂ֓וּ׀בִּמְזִמּ֖וֹת ז֣וּ חָשָֽׁבוּ׃
BSB 10:2 In pride the wicked pursue the needy; let them be caught in the schemes they devise.
3 Denn der Gottlos rüemt sech mit de Glüscht vo sym Härz, und dä, wo nie gnue überchunt, tuet flueche und verachtet der HERR.
כִּֽי־הִלֵּ֣ל רָ֭שָׁע עַל־תַּאֲוַ֣ת נַפְשׁ֑וֹ וּבֹצֵ֥עַ בֵּ֝רֵ֗ךְ נִ֘אֵ֥ץ׀יְהוָֽה׃
BSB 10:3 For the wicked man boasts in the cravings of his heart; he blesses the greedy and reviles the LORD.
וּבֹצֵעַ בֵּרֵךְ → dä, wo nie gnue überchunt, tuet flueche (he blesses the greedy)
bērēk euphemistisch »fluchen« (wie Hiob 1,5); Subjekt der Habgierige.
4 Der Gottlos isch so stolz, dass er nid nach ihm fragt: „Es git ke Gott!“, das sy all syni Gedanke.
רָשָׁ֗ע כְּגֹ֣בַהּ אַ֭פּוֹ בַּל־יִדְרֹ֑שׁ אֵ֥ין אֱ֝לֹהִ֗ים כָּל־מְזִמּוֹתָֽיו׃
BSB 10:4 In his pride the wicked man does not seek Him; in all his schemes there is no God.
כְּגֹבַהּ אַפּוֹ → so stolz (in his pride)
Wörtlich »nach der Höhe seiner Nase«.
5 Syni Wääge gratet ihm geng; dyni Gricht sy wyt obe, wyt ewägg vo ihm; über all syni Gägner lachet er nume.
יָ֘חִ֤ילוּ דְרָכָ֨יו׀בְּכָל־עֵ֗ת מָר֣וֹם מִ֭שְׁפָּטֶיךָ מִנֶּגְדּ֑וֹ כָּל־צ֝וֹרְרָ֗יו יָפִ֥יחַ בָּהֶֽם׃
BSB 10:5 He is secure in his ways at all times; Your lofty judgments are far from him; he sneers at all his foes.
יָחִילוּ דְרָכָו → Syni Wääge gratet ihm (He is secure in his ways)
6 Er seit i sym Härz: „Mi bringt nüüt zum Wanke! Vo Generation zu Generation chunt mer nüüt Böses.“
אָמַ֣ר בְּ֭לִבּוֹ בַּל־אֶמּ֑וֹט לְדֹ֥ר וָ֝דֹ֗ר אֲשֶׁ֣ר לֹֽא־בְרָֽע׃
BSB 10:6 He says to himself, “I will not be moved; from age to age I am free of distress.”
7 Sys Muul isch voll Flueche, voll Betrug und Gwalt; under syr Zunge isch Eländ und Unrächt.
אָלָ֤ה פִּ֣יהוּ מָ֭לֵא וּמִרְמ֣וֹת וָתֹ֑ךְ תַּ֥חַת לְ֝שׁוֹנ֗וֹ עָמָ֥ל וָאָֽוֶן׃
BSB 10:7 His mouth is full of cursing, deceit, and violence; trouble and malice are under his tongue.
8 Er wartet versteckt bi de Dörfer, versteckt bringt er der Unschuldig um; syni Ouge luege versteckt nach em Schwache.
יֵשֵׁ֤ב׀בְּמַאְרַ֬ב חֲצֵרִ֗ים בַּֽ֭מִּסְתָּרִים יַהֲרֹ֣ג נָקִ֑י עֵ֝ינָ֗יו לְֽחֵלְכָ֥ה יִצְפֹּֽנוּ׃
BSB 10:8 He lies in wait near the villages; in ambush he slays the innocent; his eyes watch in stealth for the helpless.
בְּמַאְרַב חֲצֵרִים → wartet versteckt bi de Dörfer (lies in wait near the villages)
»Hinterhalt« nicht belegt.
9 Er wartet im Versteck wi ne Löi i sym Versteck, er wartet, für der Arm z packe; er packt der Arm und zieht ne i sys Netz.
יֶאֱרֹ֬ב בַּמִּסְתָּ֨ר׀כְּאַרְיֵ֬ה בְסֻכֹּ֗ה יֶ֭אֱרֹב לַחֲט֣וֹף עָנִ֑י יַחְטֹ֥ף עָ֝נִ֗י בְּמָשְׁכ֥וֹ בְרִשְׁתּֽוֹ׃
BSB 10:9 He lies in wait like a lion in a thicket; he lurks to seize the oppressed; he catches the lowly in his net.
10 Dä wird verdrückt, er bückt sech, und di Schwache gheie under syr Macht.
יִדְכֶּ֥ה יָשֹׁ֑חַ וְנָפַ֥ל בַּ֝עֲצוּמָ֗יו חֵ֣יל כָּאִֽים׃
BSB 10:10 They are crushed and beaten down; the helpless fall prey to his strength.
חֵיל כָּאִים / חֵלְכָאִים → di Schwache (the helpless)
Ketiv/Qere unklar.
11 Er seit i sym Härz: „Gott het's vergässe, er het sys Gsicht versteckt, er gseht nie meh öppis.“
אָמַ֣ר בְּ֭לִבּוֹ שָׁ֣כַֽח אֵ֑ל הִסְתִּ֥יר פָּ֝נָ֗יו בַּל־רָאָ֥ה לָנֶֽצַח׃
BSB 10:11 He says to himself, “God has forgotten; He hides His face and never sees.”
12 Stand uf, HERR! Gott, lüpf dy Hand uf! Vergiss di Arme nid!
קוּמָ֤ה יְהוָ֗ה אֵ֭ל נְשָׂ֣א יָדֶ֑ךָ אַל־תִּשְׁכַּ֥ח עֲנָוִֽים׃
BSB 10:12 Arise, O LORD! Lift up Your hand, O God! Do not forget the helpless.
13 Wiso darf der Gottlos Gott verachte und i sym Härz säge: „Du fragsch nid nache!“?
עַל־מֶ֤ה׀נִאֵ֖ץ רָשָׁ֥ע׀אֱלֹהִ֑ים אָמַ֥ר בְּ֝לִבּ֗וֹ לֹ֣א תִּדְרֹֽשׁ׃
BSB 10:13 Why has the wicked man renounced God? He says to himself, “You will never call me to account.”
14 Du hesch's doch gseh; denn du luegsch uf Eländ und Chummer, für es i dy Hand z näh. Der Schwach verlat sech uf di; du bisch de Waisechinder ire Hälfer.
רָאִ֡תָה כִּֽי־אַתָּ֤ה׀עָ֘מָ֤ל וָכַ֨עַס׀תַּבִּיט֮ לָתֵ֪ת בְּיָ֫דֶ֥ךָ עָ֭לֶיךָ יַעֲזֹ֣ב חֵלֶ֑כָה יָ֝ת֗וֹם אַתָּ֤ה׀הָיִ֬יתָ עוֹזֵֽר׃
BSB 10:14 But You have regarded trouble and grief; You see to repay it by Your hand. The victim entrusts himself to You; You are the helper of the fatherless.
יָתוֹם → de Waisechinder (the fatherless)
Plural belegt (Jak 1,27).
15 Verschla der Arm vom Gottlose und vom Böse! Gang em Böse vo ihm nache, bis du nüüt meh findsch!
שְׁ֭בֹר זְר֣וֹעַ רָשָׁ֑ע וָ֝רָ֗ע תִּֽדְרוֹשׁ־רִשְׁע֥וֹ בַל־תִּמְצָֽא׃
BSB 10:15 Break the arm of the wicked and evildoer; call him to account for his wickedness until none is left to be found.
שְׁבֹר זְרוֹעַ → Verschla der Arm (Break the arm)
16 Der HERR isch Chünig für geng und ewig; d Völker sy us sym Land verschwunde.
יְהוָ֣ה מֶ֭לֶךְ עוֹלָ֣ם וָעֶ֑ד אָבְד֥וּ ג֝וֹיִ֗ם מֵֽאַרְצֽוֹ׃
BSB 10:16 The LORD is King forever and ever; the nations perish from His land.
17 Was d Arme sech wünsche, hesch du ghört, HERR; du machsch ires Härz fescht, du hesch es offes Ohr,
תַּאֲוַ֬ת עֲנָוִ֣ים שָׁמַ֣עְתָּ יְהוָ֑ה תָּכִ֥ין לִ֝בָּ֗ם תַּקְשִׁ֥יב אָזְנֶֽךָ׃
BSB 10:17 You have heard, O LORD, the desire of the humble; You will strengthen their hearts. You will incline Your ear,
תַּקְשִׁיב אָזְנֶךָ → du hesch es offes Ohr (You will incline Your ear)
18 für de Waisechinder und dene, wo bedrückt sy, zum Rächt z verhälfe, so dass der Mönsch vo der Ärde nid meh Angscht cha mache.
לִשְׁפֹּ֥ט יָת֗וֹם וָ֫דָ֥ךְ בַּל־יוֹסִ֥יף ע֑וֹד לַעֲרֹ֥ץ אֱ֝נ֗וֹשׁ מִן־הָאָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 10:18 to vindicate the fatherless and oppressed, that the men of the earth may strike terror no more.
