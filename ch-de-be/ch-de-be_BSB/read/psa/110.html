BSB 110:1 A Psalm of David. The LORD said to my Lord: “Sit at My right hand until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet.”
2 Der HERR streckt dys mächtige Zepter us Zion uus: „Tue regiere mitts under dyne Finde!“
מַטֵּֽה־עֻזְּךָ֗ יִשְׁלַ֣ח יְ֭הוָה מִצִּיּ֑וֹן רְ֝דֵ֗ה בְּקֶ֣רֶב אֹיְבֶֽיךָ׃
BSB 110:2 The LORD extends Your mighty scepter from Zion: “Rule in the midst of Your enemies.”
3 Dys Volk chunt freiwillig am Tag, wo dys Heer i Chrieg zieht, i der heilige Pracht. Us em Schoss vom Morgerot chunt dir der Tou vo dyne junge Jahr.
עַמְּךָ֣ נְדָבֹת֮ בְּי֪וֹם חֵ֫ילֶ֥ךָ בְּֽהַדְרֵי־קֹ֭דֶשׁמֵרֶ֣חֶם מִשְׁחָ֑ר לְ֝ךָ֗ טַ֣ל יַלְדֻתֶֽיךָ׃
BSB 110:3 Your people shall be willing on Your day of battle. Arrayed in holy splendor, from the womb of the dawn, to You belongs the dew of Your youth.
עַמְּךָ נְדָבֹת → Dys Volk chunt freiwillig (Your people will volunteer)

נִשְׁבַּ֤ע יְהוָ֨ה׀וְלֹ֥א יִנָּחֵ֗ם אַתָּֽה־כֹהֵ֥ן לְעוֹלָ֑ם עַל־דִּ֝בְרָתִ֗י מַלְכִּי־צֶֽדֶק׃
BSB 110:4 The LORD has sworn and will not change His mind: “You are a priest forever in the order of Melchizedek.”
5 Der Herr a dyr rächte Hand verschlat Chünige am Tag vo syr Töubi.
אֲדֹנָ֥י עַל־יְמִֽינְךָ֑ מָחַ֖ץ בְּיוֹם־אַפּ֣וֹ מְלָכִֽים׃
BSB 110:5 The Lord is at Your right hand; He will crush kings in the day of His wrath.
אֲדֹנָי → Der Herr (The Lord)
Adonai, nicht der Gottesname.

יָדִ֣ין בַּ֭גּוֹיִם מָלֵ֣א גְוִיּ֑וֹת מָ֥חַץ רֹ֝֗אשׁ עַל־אֶ֥רֶץ רַבָּֽה׃
BSB 110:6 He will judge the nations, heaping up the dead; He will crush the leaders far and wide.
רֹאשׁ → der Chopf (the chiefs)
Singular wie im MT.
7 Us em Bach am Wäg trinkt er; drum lüpft er der Chopf höch.
מִ֭נַּחַל בַּדֶּ֣רֶךְ יִשְׁתֶּ֑ה עַל־כֵּ֝֗ן יָרִ֥ים רֹֽאשׁ׃
BSB 110:7 He will drink from the brook by the road; therefore He will lift up His head.
