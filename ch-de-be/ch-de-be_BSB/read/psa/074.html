BSB 74:1 A Maskil of Asaph. Why have You rejected us forever, O God? Why does Your anger smolder against the sheep of Your pasture?

זְכֹ֤ר עֲדָתְךָ֨׀קָ֘נִ֤יתָ קֶּ֗דֶם גָּ֭אַלְתָּ שֵׁ֣בֶט נַחֲלָתֶ֑ךָ הַר־צִ֝יּ֗וֹן זֶ֤ה׀שָׁכַ֬נְתָּ בּֽוֹ׃
BSB 74:2 Remember Your congregation, which You purchased long ago and redeemed as the tribe of Your inheritance — Mount Zion, where You dwell.
3 Gang mit dyne Schritte zu de Ruine, wo geng blybe! Alls het der Find im Heiligtum verwüeschtet.
הָרִ֣ימָה פְ֭עָמֶיךָ לְמַשֻּׁא֣וֹת נֶ֑צַח כָּל־הֵרַ֖ע אוֹיֵ֣ב בַּקֹּֽדֶשׁ׃
BSB 74:3 Turn Your steps to the everlasting ruins, to everything in the sanctuary the enemy has destroyed.
4 Dyni Gägner hei brüelet mitts i dyr Versammlig, si hei iri eigete Zeiche als Zeiche ufgstellt.
שָׁאֲג֣וּ צֹ֭רְרֶיךָ בְּקֶ֣רֶב מוֹעֲדֶ֑ךָ שָׂ֖מוּ אוֹתֹתָ֣ם אֹתֽוֹת׃
BSB 74:4 Your foes have roared within Your meeting place; they have unfurled their banners as signs,
בְּקֶרֶב מוֹעֲדֶךָ → mitts i dyr Versammlig (in Your meeting place)
5 Me het gmeint, es syg wi bi eim, wo ds Bieli lüpft ufe zwüsche de Böim vom Wald.
יִ֭וָּדַע כְּמֵבִ֣יא לְמָ֑עְלָה בִּֽסֲבָךְ־עֵ֝֗ץ קַרְדֻּמּֽוֹת׃
BSB 74:5 like men wielding axes in a thicket of trees

BSB 74:6 and smashing all the carvings with hatchets and picks.

שִׁלְח֣וּ בָ֭אֵשׁ מִקְדָּשֶׁ֑ךָ לָ֝אָ֗רֶץ חִלְּל֥וּ מִֽשְׁכַּן־שְׁמֶֽךָ׃
BSB 74:7 They have burned Your sanctuary to the ground; they have defiled the dwelling place of Your Name.
8 Si hei i irem Härz gseit: „Mir wei se alli zäme plage!“ Si hei alli Hüser vo Gott im Land verbrönnt.
אָמְר֣וּ בְ֭לִבָּם נִינָ֣ם יָ֑חַד שָׂרְפ֖וּ כָל־מוֹעֲדֵי־אֵ֣ל בָּאָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 74:8 They said in their hearts, “We will crush them completely.” They burned down every place where God met us in the land.

אֽוֹתֹתֵ֗ינוּ לֹ֥א רָ֫אִ֥ינוּ אֵֽין־ע֥וֹד נָבִ֑יא וְלֹֽא־אִ֝תָּ֗נוּ יֹדֵ֥עַ עַד־מָֽה׃
BSB 74:9 There are no signs for us to see. There is no longer any prophet. And none of us knows how long this will last.
10 Wi lang no, Gott, söll der Gägner spotte, söll der Find dy Name verachte für geng?
עַד־מָתַ֣י אֱ֭לֹהִים יְחָ֣רֶף צָ֑ר יְנָ֘אֵ֤ץ אוֹיֵ֖ב שִׁמְךָ֣ לָנֶֽצַח׃
BSB 74:10 How long, O God, will the enemy taunt You? Will the foe revile Your name forever?
11 Warum hesch du dy Hand zrüggzoge, dy rächti Hand? Zie se use us dym Schoss und mach es Änd!
לָ֤מָּה תָשִׁ֣יב יָ֭דְךָ וִֽימִינֶ֑ךָ מִקֶּ֖רֶב חֵֽיקְךָ֣ כַלֵּֽה׃
BSB 74:11 Why do You withdraw Your strong right hand? Stretch it out to destroy them!
12 Gott isch doch my Chünig vo alters här, dä, wo Rettig bringt mitts uf der Ärde.
וֵ֭אלֹהִים מַלְכִּ֣י מִקֶּ֑דֶם פֹּעֵ֥ל יְ֝שׁוּע֗וֹת בְּקֶ֣רֶב הָאָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 74:12 Yet God is my King from ancient times, working salvation on the earth.
13 Du hesch mit dyr Chraft ds Meer teilt, du hesch d Chöpf vo de Ungheuer im Wasser broche.
אַתָּ֤ה פוֹרַ֣רְתָּ בְעָזְּךָ֣ יָ֑ם שִׁבַּ֖רְתָּ רָאשֵׁ֥י תַ֝נִּינִ֗ים עַל־הַמָּֽיִם׃
BSB 74:13 You divided the sea by Your strength; You smashed the heads of the dragons of the sea;
תַנִּינִים → Ungheuer (sea monsters)

אַתָּ֣ה רִ֭צַּצְתָּ רָאשֵׁ֣י לִוְיָתָ֑ן תִּתְּנֶ֥נּוּ מַ֝אֲכָ֗ל לְעָ֣ם לְצִיִּֽים׃
BSB 74:14 You crushed the heads of Leviathan; You fed him to the creatures of the desert.

BSB 74:15 You broke open the fountain and the flood; You dried up the ever-flowing rivers.
16 Dir ghört der Tag, dir o d Nacht; du hesch Mond und Sunne ygrichtet.
לְךָ֣ י֭וֹם אַף־לְךָ֥ לָ֑יְלָה אַתָּ֥ה הֲ֝כִינ֗וֹתָ מָא֥וֹר וָשָֽׁמֶשׁ׃
BSB 74:16 The day is Yours, and also the night; You established the moon and the sun.
17 Du hesch alli Gränze vo der Ärde feschtgleit; Summer und Winter – du hesch se gmacht.
אַתָּ֣ה הִ֭צַּבְתָּ כָּל־גְּבוּל֣וֹת אָ֑רֶץ קַ֥יִץ וָ֝חֹ֗רֶף אַתָּ֥ה יְצַרְתָּם׃
BSB 74:17 You set all the boundaries of the earth; You made the summer and winter.
18 Dänk dra: Der Find het gspottet, HERR, und es dumms Volk het dy Name verachtet.
זְכָר־זֹ֗את א֭וֹיֵב חֵרֵ֣ף׀יְהוָ֑ה וְעַ֥ם נָ֝בָ֗ל נִֽאֲצ֥וּ שְׁמֶֽךָ׃
BSB 74:18 Remember how the enemy has mocked You, O LORD, how a foolish people has spurned Your name.
19 Tue ds Läbe vo dym Tübli nid de wilde Tier uslifere, ds Läbe vo dyne Arme vergiss nid für geng!
אַל־תִּתֵּ֣ן לְ֭חַיַּת נֶ֣פֶשׁ תּוֹרֶ֑ךָ חַיַּ֥ת עֲ֝נִיֶּ֗יךָ אַל־תִּשְׁכַּ֥ח לָנֶֽצַח׃
BSB 74:19 Do not deliver the soul of Your dove to beasts; do not forget the lives of Your afflicted forever.
תּוֹרֶךָ → dym Tübli (Your dove)

20 Lueg uf der Bund! Denn di fyschtere Egge vom Land sy voll Hüser vo der Gwalt.

BSB 74:20 Consider Your covenant, for haunts of violence fill the dark places of the land.
21 Dä, wo plaget isch, söll nid mit Schand zrüggcho; der Arm und der Bedürftig sölle dy Name lobe!
אַל־יָשֹׁ֣ב דַּ֣ךְ נִכְלָ֑ם עָנִ֥י וְ֝אֶבְי֗וֹן יְֽהַלְל֥וּ שְׁמֶֽךָ׃
BSB 74:21 Do not let the oppressed retreat in shame; may the poor and needy praise Your name.
22 Stand uf, Gott, füer dy Sach! Dänk dra, wi der Dumm di der ganz Tag verspottet!
קוּמָ֣ה אֱ֭לֹהִים רִיבָ֣ה רִיבֶ֑ךָ זְכֹ֥ר חֶרְפָּתְךָ֥ מִנִּי־נָ֝בָ֗ל כָּל־הַיּֽוֹם׃
BSB 74:22 Rise up, O God; defend Your cause! Remember how the fool mocks You all day long.
23 Vergiss nid ds Gschrei vo dyne Gägner, der Lärme vo dene, wo sech gäge di wehre, wo geng lüter wird!
אַל־תִּ֭שְׁכַּח ק֣וֹל צֹרְרֶ֑יךָ שְׁא֥וֹן קָ֝מֶ֗יךָ עֹלֶ֥ה תָמִֽיד׃
BSB 74:23 Do not disregard the clamor of Your adversaries, the uproar of Your enemies that ascends continually.

und es dumms Volk het dy Name verachtet. //1
19 Tue ds Läbe vo dym Tübli nid de wilde Tier uslifere, //2
ds Läbe vo dyne Arme vergiss nid für geng! //1
20 Lueg uf der Bund! //2
Denn di fyschtere Egge vom Land sy voll Hüser vo der Gwalt. //1
21 Dä, wo plaget isch, söll nid mit Schand zrüggcho; //2
der Arm und der Bedürftig sölle dy Name lobe! //1
22 Stand uf, //2
Gott, //2
füer dy Sach! //2
Dänk dra, wi der Dumm di der ganz Tag verspottet! //1
23 Vergiss nid ds Gschrei vo dyne Gägner, //2 der Lärme vo dene,
wo sech gäge di wehre, //2
wo geng lüter wird!
