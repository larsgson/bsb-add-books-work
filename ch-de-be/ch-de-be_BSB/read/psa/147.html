Hebräisch: Westminster Leningrad Codex (gemeinfrei), über MACULA Hebrew (CC BY 4.0, Clear Bible); Griechisch: Berean Greek Bible; Englisch: Berean Standard Bible (beide gemeinfrei). Die Kommentare sind die Notizen des Übersetzungsagenten.
1 Halleluja! Ja, es isch guet, üsem Gott Psalme z singe; denn es isch schön, und es ghört sech, ihn z lobe.
הַ֥לְלוּ יָ֨הּ׀כִּי־ט֭וֹב זַמְּרָ֣ה אֱלֹהֵ֑ינוּ כִּֽי־נָ֝עִים נָאוָ֥ה תְהִלָּֽה׃
BSB 147:1 Hallelujah! How good it is to sing praises to our God, how pleasant and lovely to praise Him!
נָאוָה תְהִלָּה → es ghört sech, ihn z lobe (praise is fitting)
2 Der HERR bouet Jerusalem uuf, er sammlet die vo Israel, wo verstosse sy.
בּוֹנֵ֣ה יְרוּשָׁלִַ֣ם יְהוָ֑ה נִדְחֵ֖י יִשְׂרָאֵ֣ל יְכַנֵּֽס׃
BSB 147:2 The LORD builds up Jerusalem; He gathers the exiles of Israel.
3 Er heilt die, wo ds Härz bbroche isch, und tuet iri Wunde verbinde.
הָ֭רֹפֵא לִשְׁב֣וּרֵי לֵ֑ב וּ֝מְחַבֵּ֗שׁ לְעַצְּבוֹתָֽם׃
BSB 147:3 He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds.
לִשְׁבוּרֵי לֵב → die, wo ds Härz bbroche isch (the brokenhearted)
4 Er zellt d Zahl vo de Stärne, alli rüeft er mit Name.
מוֹנֶ֣ה מִ֭סְפָּר לַכּוֹכָבִ֑ים לְ֝כֻלָּ֗ם שֵׁמ֥וֹת יִקְרָֽא׃
BSB 147:4 He determines the number of the stars; He calls them each by name.
5 Üse Herr isch gross und starch; sy Verstand het ke Änd.
גָּד֣וֹל אֲדוֹנֵ֣ינוּ וְרַב־כֹּ֑חַ לִ֝תְבוּנָת֗וֹ אֵ֣ין מִסְפָּֽר׃
BSB 147:5 Great is our Lord, and mighty in power; His understanding has no limit.
6 Der HERR hilft de Demüetige uuf, d Gottlose bringt er bis uf e Bode abe.
מְעוֹדֵ֣ד עֲנָוִ֣ים יְהוָ֑ה מַשְׁפִּ֖יל רְשָׁעִ֣ים עֲדֵי־אָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 147:6 The LORD sustains the humble, but casts the wicked to the ground.
7 Singet em HERR mit Dank, singet üsem Gott Psalme mit der Zither!
עֱנ֣וּ לַיהוָ֣ה בְּתוֹדָ֑ה זַמְּר֖וּ לֵאלֹהֵ֣ינוּ בְכִנּֽוֹר׃
BSB 147:7 Sing to the LORD with thanksgiving; make music on the harp to our God,
8 Er deckt der Himel mit Wulche zue, er macht Räge parat für d Ärde, er lat uf de Bärge Gras wachse.
הַֽמְכַסֶּ֬ה שָׁמַ֨יִם׀בְּעָבִ֗ים הַמֵּכִ֣ין לָאָ֣רֶץ מָטָ֑ר הַמַּצְמִ֖יחַ הָרִ֣ים חָצִֽיר׃
BSB 147:8 who covers the sky with clouds, who prepares rain for the earth, who makes grass to grow on the hills.
9 Er git em Veh sys Ässe, de junge Rabe, wen si rüefe.
נוֹתֵ֣ן לִבְהֵמָ֣ה לַחְמָ֑הּ לִבְנֵ֥י עֹ֝רֵ֗ב אֲשֶׁ֣ר יִקְרָֽאוּ׃
BSB 147:9 He provides food for the animals, and for the young ravens when they call.
10 Er het nid Fröid a der Chraft vom Ross, er het nid Gfalle a de Bei vom Ma.
לֹ֤א בִגְבוּרַ֣ת הַסּ֣וּס יֶחְפָּ֑ץ לֹֽא־בְשׁוֹקֵ֖י הָאִ֣ישׁ יִרְצֶֽה׃
BSB 147:10 He does not delight in the strength of the horse; He takes no pleasure in the legs of a man.
בְּשׁוֹקֵי הָאִישׁ → a de Bei vom Ma (in the legs of a man)
11 Der HERR het Gfalle a dene, wo Ehrfurcht vor ihm hei, a dene, wo uf sy Tröiji hoffe.
רוֹצֶ֣ה יְ֭הוָה אֶת־יְרֵאָ֑יו אֶת־הַֽמְיַחֲלִ֥ים לְחַסְדּֽוֹ׃
BSB 147:11 The LORD is pleased with those who fear Him, who hope in His loving devotion.
12 Jerusalem, lob der HERR! Zion, lob dy Gott!
שַׁבְּחִ֣י יְ֭רוּשָׁלִַם אֶת־יְהוָ֑ה הַֽלְלִ֖י אֱלֹהַ֣יִךְ צִיּֽוֹן׃
BSB 147:12 Exalt the LORD, O Jerusalem; praise your God, O Zion!
13 Denn er macht d Rigel vo dyne Tor starch, er sägnet dyni Chinder i dir.
כִּֽי־חִ֭זַּק בְּרִיחֵ֣י שְׁעָרָ֑יִךְ בֵּרַ֖ךְ בָּנַ֣יִךְ בְּקִרְבֵּֽךְ׃
BSB 147:13 For He strengthens the bars of your gates and blesses the children within you.
14 Er git dym Gebiet Fride, mit em beschte Weize macht er di satt.
הַשָּׂם־גְּבוּלֵ֥ךְ שָׁל֑וֹם חֵ֥לֶב חִ֝טִּ֗ים יַשְׂבִּיעֵֽךְ׃
BSB 147:14 He makes peace at your borders; He fills you with the finest wheat.
חֵלֶב חִטִּים → em beschte Weize (the finest wheat)
15 Er schickt sys Wort uf d Ärde, ganz gschwind louft sys Wort.
הַשֹּׁלֵ֣חַ אִמְרָת֣וֹ אָ֑רֶץ עַד־מְ֝הֵרָ֗ה יָר֥וּץ דְּבָרֽוֹ׃
BSB 147:15 He sends forth His command to the earth; His word runs swiftly.
16 Er git Schnee wi Wulle, er verströit der Ryffe wi Äsche.
הַנֹּתֵ֣ן שֶׁ֣לֶג כַּצָּ֑מֶר כְּ֝פ֗וֹר כָּאֵ֥פֶר יְפַזֵּֽר׃
BSB 147:16 He spreads the snow like wool; He scatters the frost like ashes;
17 Er lat syni Hagelchörner abe gheie wi Bröche; wär cha besta, wen er's so chalt macht?
מַשְׁלִ֣יךְ קַֽרְח֣וֹ כְפִתִּ֑ים לִפְנֵ֥י קָ֝רָת֗וֹ מִ֣י יַעֲמֹֽד׃
BSB 147:17 He casts forth His hail like pebbles. Who can withstand His icy blast?
לִפְנֵי קָרָתוֹ → wen er's so chalt macht (before His freezing cold)
18 Er schickt sys Wort, und es lat se schmelze; er lat sy Luft wääje, und d Wasser rünne.
יִשְׁלַ֣ח דְּבָר֣וֹ וְיַמְסֵ֑ם יַשֵּׁ֥ב ר֝וּח֗וֹ יִזְּלוּ־מָֽיִם׃
BSB 147:18 He sends forth His word and melts them; He unleashes His winds, and the waters flow.
19 Er seit em Jakob sys Wort, syni Ornige und syni Urteil em Israel.
מַגִּ֣יד דְּבָרָ֣יו לְיַעֲקֹ֑ב חֻקָּ֥יו וּ֝מִשְׁפָּטָ֗יו לְיִשְׂרָאֵֽל׃
BSB 147:19 He declares His word to Jacob, His statutes and judgments to Israel.
20 So het er's mit keim andere Volk gmacht, und vo syne Urteil wüsse si nüüt. Halleluja!
לֹ֘א עָ֤שָׂה כֵ֨ן׀לְכָל־גּ֗וֹי וּמִשְׁפָּטִ֥ים בַּל־יְדָע֗וּם הַֽלְלוּ־יָֽהּ׃
BSB 147:20 He has done this for no other nation; they do not know His judgments. Hallelujah!
