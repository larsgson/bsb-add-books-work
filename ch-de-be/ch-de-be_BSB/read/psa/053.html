1 Für e Chorleiter. Nach Machalat. E Maskil vom David.

//1 2 Der Dumm seit i sym Härz: //2 „Es git ke Gott.“ //1 Si sy schlächt, si tüe gruusigs Unrächt; //2 kene tuet öppis Guets. //1 3 Gott luegt vom Himel abe uf d Mönsche, //2 für z luege, //2 öb eine Verstand het, //2 öb eine Gott suecht. //1 4 Alli sy abgfalle, //2 zäme sy si schlächt worde; //1 kene tuet öppis Guets, //2 nid en einzige. //1 5 Merke si's de nie, die, wo Unrächt tüe, //2 wo mys Volk frässe, wi we si Brot ässe, //2 und Gott nid aarüefe? //1 6 Da überchöme si der Schrecke, //2 wo doch ke Grund zum Schrecke gsi isch; //2 denn Gott het d Chnoche vo dene verströit, wo gäge di im Lager gstande sy. //2 Du hesch se z Schande gmacht, //2 denn Gott het se verstosse. //1 7 Wen doch d Rettig für Israel vo Zion chäm! //2 Wen Gott sym Volk ds Glück zrügg git, //2 de jublet Jakob, //2 de fröit sech Israel.

1 Für e Chorleiter. Nach Machalat. E Maskil vom David.
לַמְנַצֵּ֥חַ עַֽל־מָחֲלַ֗ת מַשְׂכִּ֥יל לְדָוִֽד׃

Store (unübersetzt).
2 Der Dumm seit i sym Härz: „Es git ke Gott.“ Si sy schlächt, si tüe gruusigs Unrächt; kene tuet öppis Guets.
אָ֘מַ֤ר נָבָ֣ל בְּ֭לִבּוֹ אֵ֣ין אֱלֹהִ֑ים הִֽ֝שְׁחִ֗יתוּ וְהִֽתְעִ֥יבוּ עָ֝֗וֶל אֵ֣ין עֹֽשֵׂה־טֽוֹב׃
BSB 53:1 For the choirmaster. According to Mahalath. A Maskil of David. The fool says in his heart, “There is no God.” They are corrupt; their ways are vile. There is no one who does good.
וְהִתְעִיבוּ עָוֶל → si tüe gruusigs Unrächt (their ways are vile)
Ps 14,1 עֲלִילָה »Züüg«.
3 Gott luegt vom Himel abe uf d Mönsche, für z luege, öb eine Verstand het, öb eine Gott suecht.
אֱֽלֹהִ֗ים מִשָּׁמַיִם֮ הִשְׁקִ֪יף עַֽל־בְּנֵ֫י אָדָ֥ם לִ֭רְאוֹת הֲיֵ֣שׁ מַשְׂכִּ֑יל דֹּ֝רֵ֗שׁ אֶת־אֱלֹהִֽים׃
BSB 53:2 God looks down from heaven upon the sons of men to see if any understand, if any seek God.
4 Alli sy abgfalle, zäme sy si schlächt worde; kene tuet öppis Guets, nid en einzige.
כֻּלּ֥וֹ סָג֮ יַחְדָּ֪ו נֶ֫אֱלָ֥חוּ אֵ֤ין עֹֽשֵׂה־ט֑וֹב אֵ֝֗ין גַּם־אֶחָֽד׃
BSB 53:3 All have turned away, they have together become corrupt; there is no one who does good, not even one.
5 Merke si's de nie, die, wo Unrächt tüe, wo mys Volk frässe, wi we si Brot ässe, und Gott nid aarüefe?
הֲלֹ֥א יָדְעוּ֮ פֹּ֤עֲלֵ֫י אָ֥וֶן אֹכְלֵ֣י עַ֭מִּי אָ֣כְלוּ לֶ֑חֶם אֱ֝לֹהִ֗ים לֹ֣א קָרָֽאוּ׃
BSB 53:4 Will the workers of iniquity never learn? They devour my people like bread; they refuse to call upon God.
6 Da überchöme si der Schrecke, wo doch ke Grund zum Schrecke gsi isch; denn Gott het d Chnoche vo dene verströit, wo gäge di im Lager gstande sy. Du hesch se z Schande gmacht, denn Gott het se verstosse.
שָׁ֤ם׀פָּֽחֲדוּ־פַחַד֮ לֹא־הָ֪יָה֫ פָ֥חַד כִּֽי־אֱלֹהִ֗ים פִּ֭זַּר עַצְמ֣וֹת חֹנָ֑ךְ הֱ֝בִשֹׁ֗תָה כִּֽי־אֱלֹהִ֥ים מְאָסָֽם׃
BSB 53:5 There they are, overwhelmed with dread, where there was nothing to fear. For God has scattered the bones of those who besieged you. You put them to shame, for God has despised them.
לֹא־הָיָה פָחַד → wo doch ke Grund zum Schrecke gsi isch (where there was nothing to fear)
חֹנָךְ → wo gäge di im Lager gstande sy (of those who besieged you)
מְאָסָם → het se verstosse (has despised them)
7 Wen doch d Rettig für Israel vo Zion chäm! Wen Gott sym Volk ds Glück zrügg git, de jublet Jakob, de fröit sech Israel.
מִ֥י יִתֵּ֣ן מִצִּיּוֹן֮ יְשֻׁע֪וֹת יִשְׂרָ֫אֵ֥ל בְּשׁ֣וּב אֱ֭לֹהִים שְׁב֣וּת עַמּ֑וֹ יָגֵ֥ל יַ֝עֲקֹ֗ב יִשְׂמַ֥ח יִשְׂרָאֵֽל׃
BSB 53:6 Oh, that the salvation of Israel would come from Zion! When God restores His captive people, let Jacob rejoice, let Israel be glad!
