Uf Gott vertroue n i, i ha ke Angscht

1 Für e Chorleiter. Nach der Wys „Stummi Toube wyt ewäg“. Vom David. E Michtam, wo ne d Philister z Gat packt hei.
לַמְנַצֵּ֤חַ׀עַל־י֬וֹנַת אֵ֣לֶם רְ֭חֹקִים לְדָוִ֣ד מִכְתָּ֑ם בֶּֽאֱחֹ֨ז אֹת֖וֹ פְלִשְׁתִּ֣ים בְּגַֽת׃
עַל־יוֹנַת אֵלֶם רְחֹקִים → Nach der Wys „Stummi Toube wyt ewäg“ (To the tune of “A Dove on Distant Oaks”)
אֵלֶם als »stumm« (MT), BSB liest אֵלִים »Eichen«.
פְלִשְׁתִּים בְּגַת → d Philister z Gat (the Philistines in Gath)
Namen (Store).
2 Häb Erbarme mit mer, Gott, denn Mönsche sy hinder mer här, der ganz Tag plage mi die, wo gäge mi chrieg!
חָנֵּ֣נִי אֱ֭לֹהִים כִּֽי־שְׁאָפַ֣נִי אֱנ֑וֹשׁ כָּל־הַ֝יּ֗וֹם לֹחֵ֥ם יִלְחָצֵֽנִי׃
BSB 56:1 For the choirmaster. To the tune of “A Dove on Distant Oaks.” A Miktam of David, when the Philistines seized him in Gath. Be merciful to me, O God, for men are hounding me; all day they press their attack.
3 Myni Finde sy hinder mer här der ganz Tag; denn vil chrieg gäge mi voller Stolz.
שָׁאֲפ֣וּ שׁ֭וֹרְרַי כָּל־הַיּ֑וֹם כִּֽי־רַבִּ֨ים לֹחֲמִ֖ים לִ֣י מָרֽוֹם׃
BSB 56:2 My enemies pursue me all day long, for many proudly assail me.
4 Am Tag, wo n i Angscht ha, vertroue n i uf di.
י֥וֹם אִירָ֑א אֲ֝נִ֗י אֵלֶ֥יךָ אֶבְטָֽח׃
BSB 56:3 When I am afraid, I put my trust in You.
יוֹם אִירָא → Am Tag, wo n i Angscht ha (When I am afraid)
5 Uf Gott – sys Wort lob i –, uf Gott vertroue n i, i ha ke Angscht. Was cha mir e Mönsch scho mache?
בֵּאלֹהִים֮ אֲהַלֵּ֪ל דְּבָ֫ר֥וֹ בֵּאלֹהִ֣ים בָּ֭טַחְתִּי לֹ֣א אִירָ֑א מַה־יַּעֲשֶׂ֖ה בָשָׂ֣ר לִֽי׃
BSB 56:4 In God, whose word I praise — in God I trust. I will not be afraid. What can man do to me?
6 Der ganz Tag bringe si myni Wort dürenand; gäge mi sy all iri Gedanke uf ds Böse us.
כָּל־הַ֭יּוֹם דְּבָרַ֣י יְעַצֵּ֑בוּ עָלַ֖י כָּל־מַחְשְׁבֹתָ֣ם לָרָֽע׃
BSB 56:5 All day long they twist my words; all their thoughts are on my demise.
יְעַצֵּבוּ → bringe … dürenand (twist)
7 Si chöme zäme, si verstecke sech, si tüe uf myni Schritte ufpasse, wil si mer nach em Läbe sy.
יָג֤וּרוּ׀יִצְפּ֗וֹנוּ הֵ֭מָּה עֲקֵבַ֣י יִשְׁמֹ֑רוּ כַּ֝אֲשֶׁ֗ר קִוּ֥וּ נַפְשִֽׁי׃
BSB 56:6 They conspire, they lurk, they watch my steps while they wait to take my life.
8 Wäge irem Unrächt söll's für se ke Rettig gä! I dyr Töubi bring d Völker z Fall, Gott!
עַל־אָ֥וֶן פַּלֶּט־לָ֑מוֹ בְּ֝אַ֗ף עַמִּ֤ים׀הוֹרֵ֬ד אֱלֹהִֽים׃
BSB 56:7 In spite of such sin, will they escape? In Your anger, O God, cast down the nations.
9 Du hesch zellt, wi mängisch i ha müesse umeloufe; tue myni Träne i dy Schluuch! Stö si nid i dym Buech?
נֹדִי֮ סָפַ֪רְתָּ֫ה אָ֥תָּה שִׂ֣ימָה דִמְעָתִ֣י בְנֹאדֶ֑ךָ הֲ֝לֹ֗א בְּסִפְרָתֶֽךָ׃
BSB 56:8 You have taken account of my wanderings. Put my tears in Your bottle — are they not in Your book?
בְנֹאדֶךָ → i dy Schluuch (in Your bottle)
Store.
10 De müesse myni Finde zrügg, am Tag, wo n i rüefe. Das weis i: Gott isch für mi.
אָ֥֨ז יָ֘שׁ֤וּבוּ אוֹיְבַ֣י אָ֭חוֹר בְּי֣וֹם אֶקְרָ֑א זֶה־יָ֝דַ֗עְתִּי כִּֽי־אֱלֹהִ֥ים לִֽי׃
BSB 56:9 Then my enemies will retreat on the day I cry for help. By this I will know that God is on my side.
11 Uf Gott – i lobe sys Wort –, uf e HERR – i lobe sys Wort –,
בֵּֽ֭אלֹהִים אֲהַלֵּ֣ל דָּבָ֑ר בַּ֝יהוָ֗ה אֲהַלֵּ֥ל דָּבָֽר׃
BSB 56:10 In God, whose word I praise, in the LORD, whose word I praise,
12 uf Gott vertroue n i, i ha ke Angscht. Was cha mir e Mönsch scho mache?
בֵּֽאלֹהִ֣ים בָּ֭טַחְתִּי לֹ֣א אִירָ֑א מַה־יַּעֲשֶׂ֖ה אָדָ֣ם לִֽי׃
BSB 56:11 in God I trust; I will not be afraid. What can man do to me?
13 I ha dir, Gott, öppis versproche; i wott dir Opfer zum Dank bringe.
עָלַ֣י אֱלֹהִ֣ים נְדָרֶ֑יךָ אֲשַׁלֵּ֖ם תּוֹדֹ֣ת לָֽךְ׃
BSB 56:12 Your vows are upon me, O God; I will render thank offerings to You.
עָלַי … נְדָרֶיךָ → I ha dir … öppis versproche (Your vows are upon me)
14 Denn du hesch my Seel vom Tod grettet, ja, myni Füess nid la stolpere, dass i vor Gott cha läbe im Liecht vom Läbe.
כִּ֤י הִצַּ֪לְתָּ נַפְשִׁ֡י מִמָּוֶת֮ הֲלֹ֥א רַגְלַ֗י מִ֫דֶּ֥חִי לְ֭הִֽתְהַלֵּךְ לִפְנֵ֣י אֱלֹהִ֑ים בְּ֝א֗וֹר הַֽחַיִּֽים׃
BSB 56:13 For You have delivered my soul from death, and my feet from stumbling, that I may walk before God in the light of life.
