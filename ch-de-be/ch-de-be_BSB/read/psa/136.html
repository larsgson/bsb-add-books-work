BSB 136:14 and led Israel through the midst, His loving devotion endures forever.

BSB 136:18 and slaughtered mighty kings— His loving devotion endures forever.

BSB 136:19 Sihon king of the Amorites His loving devotion endures forever.

BSB 136:20 and Og king of Bashan— His loving devotion endures forever.

BSB 136:21 and He gave their land as an inheritance, His loving devotion endures forever.
22 als Erb für Israel, sy Chnächt, denn sy Tröiji blybt für geng!
נַ֭חֲלָה לְיִשְׂרָאֵ֣ל עַבְדּ֑וֹ כִּ֖י לְעוֹלָ֣ם חַסְדּֽוֹ׃
BSB 136:22 a heritage to His servant Israel. His loving devotion endures forever.
23 wo a üüs dänkt het, wo mir ganz unde gsi sy, denn sy Tröiji blybt für geng!
שֶׁ֭בְּשִׁפְלֵנוּ זָ֣כַר לָ֑נוּ כִּ֖י לְעוֹלָ֣ם חַסְדּֽוֹ׃
BSB 136:23 He remembered us in our low estate His loving devotion endures forever.

BSB 136:24 and freed us from our enemies. His loving devotion endures forever.
25 wo allne Läbige z ässe git, denn sy Tröiji blybt für geng!

BSB 136:25 He gives food to every creature. His loving devotion endures forever.
לְכָל־בָּשָׂר → allne Läbige (every creature)

BSB 136:26 Give thanks to the God of heaven! His loving devotion endures forever.
