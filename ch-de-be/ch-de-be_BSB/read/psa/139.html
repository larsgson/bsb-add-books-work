1 Für e Chorleiter. Vom David. E Psalm. HERR, du hesch mi prüeft und weisch alls vo mir.
לַ֭מְנַצֵּחַ לְדָוִ֣ד מִזְמ֑וֹריְהוָ֥ה חֲ֝קַרְתַּ֗נִי וַתֵּדָֽע׃
BSB 139:1 For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. O LORD, You have searched me and known me.
2 Du weisch, wen i mi sitze und wen i wider uf bi; du versteisch myni Gedanke vo wytem.
אַתָּ֣ה יָ֭דַעְתָּ שִׁבְתִּ֣י וְקוּמִ֑י בַּ֥נְתָּה לְ֝רֵעִ֗י מֵרָחֽוֹק׃
BSB 139:2 You know when I sit and when I rise; You understand my thoughts from afar.
3 Ob i gange oder lige – du luegsch es dir aa, und alli myni Wääge sy dir bekannt.
אָרְחִ֣י וְרִבְעִ֣י זֵרִ֑יתָ וְֽכָל־דְּרָכַ֥י הִסְכַּֽנְתָּה׃
BSB 139:3 You search out my path and my lying down; You are aware of all my ways.
זֵרִיתָ → du luegsch es dir aa (You search out)
4 Denn es isch no kes Wort uf myr Zunge, lue, HERR, du weisch scho alls.
כִּ֤י אֵ֣ין מִ֭לָּה בִּלְשׁוֹנִ֑י הֵ֥ן יְ֝הוָ֗ה יָדַ֥עְתָּ כֻלָּֽהּ׃
BSB 139:4 Even before a word is on my tongue, You know all about it, O LORD.
5 Vo hinde und vo vorne umgisch du mi und dy Hand isch uf mi gleit.
אָח֣וֹר וָקֶ֣דֶם צַרְתָּ֑נִי וַתָּ֖שֶׁת עָלַ֣י כַּפֶּֽכָה׃
BSB 139:5 You hem me in behind and before; You have laid Your hand upon me.
6 Das z wüsse isch mer z wunderbar, es isch z höch, i cha's nid begryffe.
פְּלִ֣יאָֽה דַ֣עַת מִמֶּ֑נִּי נִ֝שְׂגְּבָ֗ה לֹא־א֥וּכַֽל לָֽהּ׃
BSB 139:6 Such knowledge is too wonderful for me, too lofty for me to attain.
7 Wo söll i häre ga vor dym Geischt, und wo söll i häre flie vor dym Gsicht?
אָ֭נָ֥ה אֵלֵ֣ךְ מֵרוּחֶ֑ךָ וְ֝אָ֗נָה מִפָּנֶ֥יךָ אֶבְרָֽח׃
BSB 139:7 Where can I go to escape Your Spirit? Where can I flee from Your presence?
8 Wen i i Himel ufestyge – dert bisch du! Wen i mys Bett im Toterych mache – lue, da bisch du!
אִם־אֶסַּ֣ק שָׁ֭מַיִם שָׁ֣ם אָ֑תָּה וְאַצִּ֖יעָה שְּׁא֣וֹל הִנֶּֽךָּ׃
BSB 139:8 If I ascend to the heavens, You are there; if I make my bed in Sheol, You are there.
9 Nimen i d Flügel vom Morgerot, wohne n i am letschte Änd vom Meer,
אֶשָּׂ֥א כַנְפֵי־שָׁ֑חַר אֶ֝שְׁכְּנָ֗ה בְּאַחֲרִ֥ית יָֽם׃
BSB 139:9 If I rise on the wings of the dawn, if I settle by the farthest sea,
כַנְפֵי־שָׁחַר → d Flügel vom Morgerot (the wings of the dawn)
10 o dert füert mi dy Hand, und dy rächti Hand haltet mi.
גַּם־שָׁ֭ם יָדְךָ֣ תַנְחֵ֑נִי וְֽתֹאחֲזֵ֥נִי יְמִינֶֽךָ׃
BSB 139:10 even there Your hand will guide me; Your right hand will hold me fast.
11 Wen i säge: „Ja, d Fyschteri söll mi zuedecke, und d Nacht um mi söll Liecht sy“,
וָ֭אֹמַר אַךְ־חֹ֣שֶׁךְ יְשׁוּפֵ֑נִי וְ֝לַ֗יְלָה א֣וֹר בַּעֲדֵֽנִי׃
BSB 139:11 If I say, “Surely the darkness will hide me, and the light become night around me”—
12 so isch o d Fyschteri nid fyschter für di, und d Nacht isch häll wi der Tag; d Fyschteri isch wi ds Liecht.
גַּם־חֹשֶׁךְ֮ לֹֽא־יַחְשִׁ֪יךְ מִ֫מֶּ֥ךָ וְ֭לַיְלָה כַּיּ֣וֹם יָאִ֑יר כַּ֝חֲשֵׁיכָ֗ה כָּאוֹרָֽה׃
BSB 139:12 even the darkness is not dark to You, but the night shines like the day, for darkness is as light to You.
13 Denn du hesch mys Innerschte gschaffe, du hesch mi im Lyb vo myr Mueter zämegfüegt.
כִּֽי־אַ֭תָּה קָנִ֣יתָ כִלְיֹתָ֑י תְּ֝סֻכֵּ֗נִי בְּבֶ֣טֶן אִמִּֽי׃
BSB 139:13 For You formed my inmost being; You knit me together in my mother’s womb.
14 I danke dir, dass i so wunderbar gmacht bi; wunderbar sy dyni Wärch, und my Seel weiss das ganz guet.
אֽוֹדְךָ֗ עַ֤ל כִּ֥י נוֹרָא֗וֹת נִ֫פְלֵ֥יתִי נִפְלָאִ֥ים מַעֲשֶׂ֑יךָ וְ֝נַפְשִׁ֗י יֹדַ֥עַת מְאֹֽד׃
BSB 139:14 I praise You, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made. Marvelous are Your works, and I know this very well.
15 Myni Chnoche sy dir nid versteckt gsi, wo n i im Gheime gmacht worde bi, schön gwobe i de Tiefine vo der Ärde.
לֹא־נִכְחַ֥ד עָצְמִ֗י מִ֫מֶּ֥ךָּ אֲשֶׁר־עֻשֵּׂ֥יתִי בַסֵּ֑תֶר רֻ֝קַּ֗מְתִּי בְּֽתַחְתִּיּ֥וֹת אָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 139:15 My frame was not hidden from You when I was made in secret, when I was woven together in the depths of the earth.
רֻקַּמְתִּי → schön gwobe (woven together)
16 Dyni Ouge hei mi gseh, wo n i no nid fertig gsi bi, und i dym Buech sy alli ufgschribe gsi, d Täg, wo bestimmt gsi sy, wo no kene vo ne gsi isch.
גָּלְמִ֤י׀רָ֘א֤וּ עֵינֶ֗יךָ וְעַֽל־סִפְרְךָ֮ כֻּלָּ֪ם יִכָּ֫תֵ֥בוּ יָמִ֥ים יֻצָּ֑רוּ וְל֖וֹ אֶחָ֣ד בָּהֶֽם׃
BSB 139:16 Your eyes saw my unformed body; all my days were written in Your book and ordained for me before one of them came to be.
גָּלְמִי → wo n i no nid fertig gsi bi (my unformed body)
17 Und für mi, wi wärtvoll sy dyni Gedanke, Gott! Wi gross isch ire Zahl!
וְלִ֗י מַה־יָּקְר֣וּ רֵעֶ֣יךָ אֵ֑ל מֶ֥ה עָ֝צְמוּ רָאשֵׁיהֶֽם׃
BSB 139:17 How precious to me are Your thoughts, O God, how vast is their sum!
18 Wett i se zelle, si wäre meh als der Sand. I erwache, und i bi geng no bi dir.
אֶ֭סְפְּרֵם מֵח֣וֹל יִרְבּ֑וּן הֱ֝קִיצֹ֗תִי וְעוֹדִ֥י עִמָּֽךְ׃
BSB 139:18 If I were to count them, they would outnumber the grains of sand; and when I awake, I am still with You.
הֱקִיצֹתִי → I erwache (When I awake)
19 Wen du doch di Gottlose wettisch umbringe, Gott! Dihr Lüt, wo Bluet vergosse hei, göt wäg vo mir!
אִם־תִּקְטֹ֖ל אֱל֥וֹהַּ׀רָשָׁ֑ע וְאַנְשֵׁ֥י דָ֝מִ֗ים ס֣וּרוּ מֶֽנִּי׃
BSB 139:19 O God, that You would slay the wicked— away from me, you bloodthirsty men—
20 Die rede vo dir mit böser Absicht, dyni Finde bruuche dy Name vergäbe.
אֲשֶׁ֣ר יֹ֭אמְרֻךָ לִמְזִמָּ֑ה נָשֻׂ֖א לַשָּׁ֣וְא עָרֶֽיךָ׃
BSB 139:20 who speak of You deceitfully; Your enemies take Your name in vain.
נָשֻׂא לַשָּׁוְא → bruuche dy Name vergäbe (take Your name in vain)
Text unsicher.
21 Söll i die nid hasse, wo di hasse, HERR? I hasse se und verachte die, wo sech gäge di stelle!
הֲלֽוֹא־מְשַׂנְאֶ֖יךָ יְהוָ֥ה׀אֶשְׂנָ֑א וּ֝בִתְקוֹמְמֶ֗יךָ אֶתְקוֹטָֽט׃
BSB 139:21 Do I not hate those who hate You, O LORD, and detest those who rise against You?
22 I hasse se ganz und gar; si sy mer zu Finde worde.
תַּכְלִ֣ית שִׂנְאָ֣ה שְׂנֵאתִ֑ים לְ֝אוֹיְבִ֗ים הָ֣יוּ לִֽי׃
BSB 139:22 I hate them with perfect hatred; I count them as my enemies.
23 Lue mi gnau aa, Gott, und lue, was i mym Härz isch! Lue mi dür und dür aa und lue, was i dänke!
חָקְרֵ֣נִי אֵ֭ל וְדַ֣ע לְבָבִ֑י בְּ֝חָנֵ֗נִי וְדַ֣ע שַׂרְעַפָּֽי׃
BSB 139:23 Search me, O God, and know my heart; test me and know my concerns.
24 Und lue, ob i uf eme böse Wäg bi, und füer mi uf em Wäg für alli Zyt!
וּרְאֵ֗ה אִם־דֶּֽרֶךְ־עֹ֥צֶב בִּ֑י וּ֝נְחֵ֗נִי בְּדֶ֣רֶךְ עוֹלָֽם׃
BSB 139:24 See if there is any offensive way in me; lead me in the way everlasting.
דֶּרֶךְ עוֹלָם → uf em Wäg für alli Zyt (the everlasting way)
