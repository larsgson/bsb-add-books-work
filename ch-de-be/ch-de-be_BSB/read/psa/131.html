1 Es Wallfahrtslied. Vom David. HERR, mys Härz isch nid hochmüetig, und myni Ouge luege nid vo obenabe; i gibe mi nid ab mit Sache, wo z gross und z wunderbar für mi sy.
שִׁ֥יר הַֽמַּֽעֲל֗וֹת לְדָ֫וִ֥דיְהוָ֤ה׀לֹא־גָבַ֣הּ לִ֭בִּי וְלֹא־רָמ֣וּ עֵינַ֑י וְלֹֽא־הִלַּ֓כְתִּי׀בִּגְדֹל֖וֹת וּבְנִפְלָא֣וֹת מִמֶּֽנִּי׃
BSB 131:1 A song of ascents. Of David. My heart is not proud, O LORD, my eyes are not haughty. I do not aspire to great things or matters too lofty for me.
לֹא־רָמוּ עֵינַי → myni Ouge luege nid vo obenabe (my eyes are not haughty)
2 Nei, i ha my Seel zur Rue bbracht und still gmacht; wi nes Chind, wo nümm a der Bruscht trinkt, bi syr Mueter, wi nes Chind isch my Seel i mir.
אִם־לֹ֤א שִׁוִּ֨יתִי׀וְדוֹמַ֗מְתִּי נַ֫פְשִׁ֥י כְּ֭גָמֻל עֲלֵ֣י אִמּ֑וֹ כַּגָּמֻ֖ל עָלַ֣י נַפְשִֽׁי׃
BSB 131:2 Surely I have stilled and quieted my soul; like a weaned child with his mother, like a weaned child is my soul within me.
כְּגָמֻל → wi nes Chind, wo nümm a der Bruscht trinkt (like a weaned child)
3 Israel söll hoffe uf e HERR, vo jetz aa bis i Ewigkeit!
יַחֵ֣ל יִ֭שְׂרָאֵל אֶל־יְהוָ֑ה מֵֽ֝עַתָּ֗ה וְעַד־עוֹלָֽם׃
BSB 131:3 O Israel, put your hope in the LORD, both now and forevermore.
