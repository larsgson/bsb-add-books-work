BSB 54:1 For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. A Maskil of David. When the Ziphites went to Saul and said, “Is David not hiding among us?” Save me, O God, by Your name, and vindicate me by Your might!

BSB 54:2 Hear my prayer, O God; listen to the words of my mouth.

BSB 54:3 For strangers rise up against me, and ruthless men seek my life — men with no regard for God. Selah

BSB 54:4 Surely God is my helper; the Lord is the sustainer of my soul.
7 Er söll myne Finde das Böse zrüggzale; i dyr Tröiji mach se z nüüt!

BSB 54:5 He will reward my enemies with evil. In Your faithfulness, destroy them.

בִּנְדָבָ֥ה אֶזְבְּחָה־לָּ֑ךְ א֤וֹדֶה שִּׁמְךָ֖ יְהוָ֣ה כִּי־טֽוֹב׃
BSB 54:6 Freely I will sacrifice to You; I will praise Your name, O LORD, for it is good.
בִּנְדָבָה → Freiwillig (freely)
9 Denn er het mi us jeder Not grettet, und mys Oug het uf myni Finde abe gluegt.
כִּ֣י מִכָּל־צָ֭רָה הִצִּילָ֑נִי וּ֝בְאֹיְבַ֗י רָאֲתָ֥ה עֵינִֽי׃
BSB 54:7 For He has delivered me from every trouble, and my eyes have looked in triumph on my foes.
