Ds Buech vo de Psalme, Psalm 115

Nid üüs, HERR, nid üüs

1 Nid üüs, HERR, nid üüs, nei, dym Name gib d Ehr, wäge dyr Tröiji und wäge dyr Wahrheit!
לֹ֤א לָ֥נוּ יְהוָ֗ה לֹ֫א לָ֥נוּ כִּֽי־לְ֭שִׁמְךָ תֵּ֣ן כָּב֑וֹד עַל־חַ֝סְדְּךָ֗ עַל־אֲמִתֶּֽךָ׃
BSB 115:1 Not to us, O LORD, not to us, but to Your name be the glory, because of Your loving devotion, because of Your faithfulness.
2 Warum sölle d Völker säge: „Wo isch ächt ire Gott?“
לָ֭מָּה יֹאמְר֣וּ הַגּוֹיִ֑ם אַיֵּה־נָ֝֗א אֱלֹהֵיהֶֽם׃
BSB 115:2 Why should the nations say, “Where is their God?”
3 Üse Gott isch im Himel; alls, wo n ihm gfallt, macht er.
וֵֽאלֹהֵ֥ינוּ בַשָּׁמָ֑יִם כֹּ֭ל אֲשֶׁר־חָפֵ֣ץ עָשָֽׂה׃
BSB 115:3 Our God is in heaven; He does as He pleases.
כֹּל אֲשֶׁר־חָפֵץ עָשָׂה → alls, wo n ihm gfallt, macht er (He does as He pleases)
4 Iri Götze sy Silber und Guld, es Wärch vo de Händ vo Mönsche.
עֲֽ֭צַבֵּיהֶם כֶּ֣סֶף וְזָהָ֑ב מַ֝עֲשֵׂ֗ה יְדֵ֣י אָדָֽם׃
BSB 115:4 Their idols are silver and gold, made by the hands of men.
5 Si hei es Muul, aber rede chöi si nid; si hei Ouge, aber gseh chöi si nid.
פֶּֽה־לָ֭הֶם וְלֹ֣א יְדַבֵּ֑רוּ עֵינַ֥יִם לָ֝הֶ֗ם וְלֹ֣א יִרְאֽוּ׃
BSB 115:5 They have mouths, but cannot speak; they have eyes, but cannot see;
6 Si hei Ohre, aber ghöre chöi si nid; si hei e Nase, aber schmöcke chöi si nid.
אָזְנַ֣יִם לָ֭הֶם וְלֹ֣א יִשְׁמָ֑עוּ אַ֥ף לָ֝הֶ֗ם וְלֹ֣א יְרִיחֽוּן׃
BSB 115:6 they have ears, but cannot hear; they have noses, but cannot smell;
7 Si hei Händ, aber aarüere chöi si nid; si hei Füess, aber loufe chöi si nid; si bringe ke Ton us irem Hals.
יְדֵיהֶ֤ם׀וְלֹ֬א יְמִישׁ֗וּן רַ֭גְלֵיהֶם וְלֹ֣א יְהַלֵּ֑כוּ לֹֽא־יֶ֝הְגּ֗וּ בִּגְרוֹנָֽם׃
BSB 115:7 they have hands, but cannot feel; they have feet, but cannot walk; they cannot even clear their throats.
לֹא־יֶהְגּוּ בִּגְרוֹנָם → si bringe ke Ton us irem Hals (no sound from their throats)
8 Wi si sölle die wärde, wo se mache, alli, wo uf se vertroue!
כְּ֭מוֹהֶם יִהְי֣וּ עֹשֵׂיהֶ֑ם כֹּ֭ל אֲשֶׁר־בֹּטֵ֣חַ בָּהֶֽם׃
BSB 115:8 Those who make them become like them, as do all who trust in them.
9 Israel, vertrouet uf e HERR! Är isch iri Hilf und ire Schild.
יִ֭שְׂרָאֵל בְּטַ֣ח בַּיהוָ֑ה עֶזְרָ֖ם וּמָגִנָּ֣ם הֽוּא׃
BSB 115:9 O Israel, trust in the LORD! He is their help and shield.
10 Huus vom Aaron, vertrouet uf e HERR! Är isch iri Hilf und ire Schild.
בֵּ֣ית אַ֭הֲרֹן בִּטְח֣וּ בַיהוָ֑ה עֶזְרָ֖ם וּמָגִנָּ֣ם הֽוּא׃
BSB 115:10 O house of Aaron, trust in the LORD! He is their help and shield.
11 Dihr, wo Ehrfurcht vor em HERR heit, vertrouet uf e HERR! Är isch iri Hilf und ire Schild.
יִרְאֵ֣י יְ֭הוָה בִּטְח֣וּ בַיהוָ֑ה עֶזְרָ֖ם וּמָגִנָּ֣ם הֽוּא׃
BSB 115:11 You who fear the LORD, trust in the LORD! He is their help and shield.
12 Der HERR dänkt a üüs, er sägnet, er sägnet ds Huus Israel, er sägnet ds Huus vom Aaron.
יְהוָה֮ זְכָרָ֪נוּ יְבָ֫רֵ֥ךְ יְ֭בָרֵךְ אֶת־בֵּ֣ית יִשְׂרָאֵ֑ל יְ֝בָרֵ֗ךְ אֶת־בֵּ֥ית אַהֲרֹֽן׃
BSB 115:12 The LORD is mindful of us; He will bless us. He will bless the house of Israel; He will bless the house of Aaron;
13 Er sägnet die, wo Ehrfurcht vor em HERR hei, di Chlyne und di Grosse.
יְ֭בָרֵךְ יִרְאֵ֣י יְהוָ֑ה הַ֝קְּטַנִּ֗ים עִם־הַגְּדֹלִֽים׃
BSB 115:13 He will bless those who fear the LORD— small and great alike.
14 Der HERR söll öich meh gä, öich und öine Chind!
יֹסֵ֣ף יְהוָ֣ה עֲלֵיכֶ֑ם עֲ֝לֵיכֶ֗ם וְעַל־בְּנֵיכֶֽם׃
BSB 115:14 May the LORD give you increase, both you and your children.
יֹסֵף יְהוָה עֲלֵיכֶם → Der HERR söll öich meh gä (May the LORD cause you to flourish)
15 Dihr syt gsägnet vom HERR, wo Himel und Ärde gmacht het!
בְּרוּכִ֣ים אַ֭תֶּם לַיהוָ֑ה עֹ֝שֵׂ֗ה שָׁמַ֥יִם וָאָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 115:15 May you be blessed by the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth.
16 Der Himel isch der Himel vom HERR, aber d Ärde het er de Mönsche ggä.
הַשָּׁמַ֣יִם שָׁ֭מַיִם לַיהוָ֑ה וְ֝הָאָ֗רֶץ נָתַ֥ן לִבְנֵי־אָדָֽם׃
BSB 115:16 The highest heavens belong to the LORD, but the earth He has given to mankind.
הַשָּׁמַיִם שָׁמַיִם לַיהוָה → Der Himel isch der Himel vom HERR (The highest heavens belong to the LORD)
17 D Tote lobe der HERR nid, o nid alli, wo i ds Land vom Schwyge abe gö.
לֹ֣א הַ֭מֵּתִים יְהַֽלְלוּ־יָ֑הּ וְ֝לֹ֗א כָּל־יֹרְדֵ֥י דוּמָֽה׃
BSB 115:17 It is not the dead who praise the LORD, nor any who descend into silence.
18 Aber mir lobe der HERR vo jetz a bis i alli Ewigkeit. Halleluja!
וַאֲנַ֤חְנוּ׀נְבָ֘רֵ֤ךְ יָ֗הּ מֵֽעַתָּ֥ה וְעַד־עוֹלָ֗ם הַֽלְלוּ־יָֽהּ׃
BSB 115:18 But it is we who will bless the LORD, both now and forevermore. Hallelujah!
