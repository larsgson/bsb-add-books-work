1 Für e Chorleiter. Nach der Wys „Verdirb nid“. Vom David. E Michtam, wo der Saul Lüt gschickt het, und si ds Huus bewacht hei, für ne z töde.
לַמְנַצֵּ֣חַ אַל־תַּשְׁחֵת֮ לְדָוִ֪ד מִ֫כְתָּ֥ם בִּשְׁלֹ֥חַ שָׁא֑וּל וַֽיִּשְׁמְר֥וּ אֶת־הַ֝בַּ֗יִת לַהֲמִיתֽוֹ׃
2 Rett mi vor myne Finde, my Gott! Bhüet mi vor dene, wo gäge mi sy!
הַצִּילֵ֖נִי מֵאֹיְבַ֥י׀אֱלֹהָ֑י מִּמִתְקוֹמְמַ֥י תְּשַׂגְּבֵֽנִי׃
BSB 59:1 For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A Miktam of David, when Saul sent men to watch David’s house in order to kill him. Deliver me from my enemies, O my God; protect me from those who rise against me.
3 Rett mi vor dene, wo Unrächt tüe, und hilf mer vor de Mörder!
הַ֭צִּילֵנִי מִפֹּ֣עֲלֵי אָ֑וֶן וּֽמֵאַנְשֵׁ֥י דָ֝מִ֗ים הוֹשִׁיעֵֽנִי׃
BSB 59:2 Deliver me from workers of iniquity, and save me from men of bloodshed.
אַנְשֵׁי דָמִים → de Mörder (men of bloodshed)
Wie Ps 55,24.
4 Denn lue, si passe uf mys Läbe uf, Starchi chöme gäge mi zäme – nid wäge myr Schuld und nid wäge myr Sünd, HERR!
כִּ֤י הִנֵּ֪ה אָֽרְב֡וּ לְנַפְשִׁ֗י יָג֣וּרוּ עָלַ֣י עַזִ֑ים לֹא־פִשְׁעִ֖י וְלֹא־חַטָּאתִ֣י יְהוָֽה׃
BSB 59:3 See how they lie in wait for me. Fierce men conspire against me for no transgression or sin of my own, O LORD.
5 Ohni dass i Unrächt ta ha, renne si und mache sech parat. Wach uf, chumm mer z Hilf und lueg!
בְּֽלִי־עָ֭וֺן יְרוּצ֣וּן וְיִכּוֹנָ֑נוּ ע֖וּרָה לִקְרָאתִ֣י וּרְאֵה׃
BSB 59:4 For no fault of my own, they move swiftly to attack me. Arise to help me, and take notice.
6 Du, HERR, Gott Zebaoth, Gott vo Israel, wach uf, für all di Heide z strafe! Häb mit kene Erbarme, wo untröi sy und Unrächt tüe! Sela
וְאַתָּ֤ה יְהוָֽה־אֱלֹהִ֥ים׀צְבָא֡וֹת אֱלֹ֘הֵ֤י יִשְׂרָאֵ֗ל הָקִ֗יצָה לִפְקֹ֥ד כָּֽל־הַגּוֹיִ֑ם אַל־תָּחֹ֨ן כָּל־בֹּ֖גְדֵי אָ֣וֶן סֶֽלָה׃
BSB 59:5 O LORD God of Hosts, the God of Israel, rouse Yourself to punish all the nations; show no mercy to the wicked traitors. Selah
בֹּגְדֵי אָוֶן → wo untröi sy und Unrächt tüe (wicked traitors)
7 Am Aabe chöme si zrügg, si chnurre wi d Hünd und stryche um d Stadt ume.
יָשׁ֣וּבוּ לָ֭עֶרֶב יֶהֱמ֥וּ כַכָּ֗לֶב וִיס֥וֹבְבוּ עִֽיר׃
BSB 59:6 They return in the evening, snarling like dogs and prowling around the city.
8 Lue, si spöie Wüescht's us irem Muul, Schwärter sy uf irne Lippe: „Denn wär ghört's scho?“
הִנֵּ֤ה׀יַבִּ֘יע֤וּן בְּפִיהֶ֗ם חֲ֭רָבוֹת בְּשִׂפְתוֹתֵיהֶ֑ם כִּי־מִ֥י שֹׁמֵֽעַ׃
BSB 59:7 See what they spew from their mouths— sharp words from their lips: “For who can hear us?”
יַבִּיעוּן → si spöie … us (they spew)
9 Aber du, HERR, du tuesch über se lache, du tuesch über all d Heide spotte.
וְאַתָּ֣ה יְ֭הוָה תִּשְׂחַק־לָ֑מוֹ תִּ֝לְעַ֗ג לְכָל־גּוֹיִֽם׃
BSB 59:8 But You, O LORD, laugh at them; You scoff at all the nations.
10 My Chraft, uf di wott i warte, denn Gott isch my Burg.
עֻ֭זּוֹ אֵלֶ֣יךָ אֶשְׁמֹ֑רָה כִּֽי־אֱ֝לֹהִ֗ים מִשְׂגַּבִּֽי׃
BSB 59:9 I will keep watch for You, O my strength, because You, O God, are my fortress.
עֻזּוֹ → My Chraft (O my Strength)
Mit Handschriften/V. 18 עֻזִּי.
11 My Gott, wo mer tröi isch, chunt zu mer; Gott lat mi uf myni Finde abe luege.
אֱלֹהֵ֣י חַסְדִּ֣י יְקַדְּמֵ֑נִי אֱ֝לֹהִ֗ים יַרְאֵ֥נִי בְשֹׁרְרָֽי׃
BSB 59:10 My God of loving devotion will come to meet me; God will let me stare down my foes.
12 Tue se nid töde, süsch vergisst mys Volk es! La se dür dy Macht umeloufe und bring se z Fall, du üse Schild, Herr!
אַל־תַּהַרְגֵ֤ם׀פֶּֽן־יִשְׁכְּח֬וּ עַמִּ֗י הֲנִיעֵ֣מוֹ בְ֭חֵילְךָ וְהוֹרִידֵ֑מוֹ מָֽגִנֵּ֣נוּ אֲדֹנָֽי׃
BSB 59:11 Do not kill them, or my people will forget. Scatter them by Your power, and bring them down, O Lord, our shield.
הֲנִיעֵמוֹ → La se … umeloufe (scatter them)
13 Ds Muul sündiget mit jedem Wort vo irne Lippe, drum sölle si i irem Stolz gfange wärde, wäge de Fluech und de Lugine, wo si säge.
חַטַּאת־פִּ֗ימוֹ דְּֽבַר־שְׂפָ֫תֵ֥ימוֹ וְיִלָּכְד֥וּ בִגְאוֹנָ֑ם וּמֵאָלָ֖ה וּמִכַּ֣חַשׁ יְסַפֵּֽרוּ׃
BSB 59:12 By the sins of their mouths and the words of their lips, let them be trapped in their pride, in the curses and lies they utter.
14 Mach mit ne fertig i der Wuet, mach fertig, dass si nümm da sy, dass me merkt, dass Gott über Jakob regiert bis a d Änd vo der Ärde! Sela
כַּלֵּ֥ה בְחֵמָה֮ כַּלֵּ֪ה וְֽאֵ֫ינֵ֥מוֹ וְֽיֵדְע֗וּ כִּֽי־אֱ֭לֹהִים מֹשֵׁ֣ל בְּיַעֲקֹ֑ב לְאַפְסֵ֖י הָאָ֣רֶץ סֶֽלָה׃
BSB 59:13 Consume them in wrath; consume them till they are no more, so it may be known to the ends of the earth that God rules over Jacob. Selah
15 Am Aabe chöme si zrügg, si chnurre wi d Hünd und stryche um d Stadt ume.
וְיָשׁ֣וּבוּ לָ֭עֶרֶב יֶהֱמ֥וּ כַכָּ֗לֶב וִיס֥וֹבְבוּ עִֽיר׃
BSB 59:14 They return in the evening, snarling like dogs and prowling around the city.
16 Si loufe ume für z ässe; wen si nid satt wärde, de chnurre si.
הֵ֭מָּה יְנִיע֣וּן לֶאֱכֹ֑ל אִם־לֹ֥א יִ֝שְׂבְּע֗וּ וַיָּלִֽינוּ׃
BSB 59:15 They scavenge for food, and growl if they are not satisfied.
וַיָּלִינוּ → de chnurre si (they growl)
17 Aber ig wott vo dyr Chraft singe und am Morge vo dyr Tröiji juble; denn du bisch my Burg gsi und my Zueflucht am Tag, wo n i i Not gsi bi.
וַאֲנִ֤י׀אָשִׁ֣יר עֻזֶּךָ֮ וַאֲרַנֵּ֥ן לַבֹּ֗קֶר חַ֫סְדֶּ֥ךָ כִּֽי־הָיִ֣יתָ מִשְׂגָּ֣ב לִ֑י וּ֝מָנ֗וֹס בְּי֣וֹם צַר־לִֽי׃
BSB 59:16 But I will sing of Your strength and proclaim Your loving devotion in the morning. For You are my fortress, my refuge in times of trouble.
18 My Chraft, dir wott i Psalme singe, denn Gott isch my Burg, my Gott, wo mer tröi isch.
עֻ֭זִּי אֵלֶ֣יךָ אֲזַמֵּ֑רָה כִּֽי־אֱלֹהִ֥ים מִ֝שְׂגַּבִּ֗י אֱלֹהֵ֥י חַסְדִּֽי׃
BSB 59:17 To You, O my strength, I sing praises, for You, O God, are my fortress, my God of loving devotion.
