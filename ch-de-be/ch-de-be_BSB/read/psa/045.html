Es Lied zur Hochzyt vom Chünig

2 Mys Härz isch voll vo me schöne Wort; i säge mys Lied für e Chünig. My Zunge isch wi ds Fädere vo me Schriftgelehrte, wo gschickt isch.
רָ֘חַ֤שׁ לִבִּ֨י׀דָּ֘בָ֤ר ט֗וֹב אֹמֵ֣ר אָ֭נִי מַעֲשַׂ֣י לְמֶ֑לֶךְ לְ֝שׁוֹנִ֗י עֵ֤ט׀סוֹפֵ֬ר מָהִֽיר׃
BSB 45:1 For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Lilies.” A Maskil of the sons of Korah. A love song. My heart is stirred by a noble theme as I recite my verses to the king; my tongue is the pen of a skillful writer.
עֵט סוֹפֵר מָהִיר → ds Fädere vo me Schriftgelehrte, wo gschickt isch (the pen of a skillful writer)
סוֹפֵר im Korpus »Schriftgelehrte«.
3 Du bisch der schönschte vo allne Mönsche, Gnad isch uf dyni Lippe gschüttet; drum het di Gott gsägnet für geng.
יָפְיָפִ֡יתָ מִבְּנֵ֬י אָדָ֗ם ה֣וּצַק חֵ֭ן בְּשְׂפְתוֹתֶ֑יךָ עַל־כֵּ֤ן בֵּֽרַכְךָ֖ אֱלֹהִ֣ים לְעוֹלָֽם׃
BSB 45:2 You are the most handsome of men; grace has anointed your lips; therefore God has blessed you forever.
4 Gürt dys Schwärt a d Hüft, du Starche, dy Pracht und dy Herrlechkeit!
חֲגֽוֹר־חַרְבְּךָ֣ עַל־יָרֵ֣ךְ גִּבּ֑וֹר ה֝וֹדְךָ֗ וַהֲדָרֶֽךָ׃
BSB 45:3 Strap your sword at your side, O mighty warrior; appear in your majesty and splendor.
5 I dyr Herrlechkeit hesch Glück – ryte uus für d Sach vo der Wahrheit und vo der Demuet und der Grächtigkeit, und dy rächti Hand söll di Wunder lehre!
וַהֲדָ֬רְךָ֨׀צְלַ֬ח רְכַ֗ב עַֽל־דְּבַר־אֱ֭מֶת וְעַנְוָה־צֶ֑דֶק וְתוֹרְךָ֖ נוֹרָא֣וֹת יְמִינֶֽךָ׃
BSB 45:4 In your splendor ride forth in victory on behalf of truth and humility and justice; may your right hand show your awesome deeds.
צְלַח רְכַב → hesch Glück – ryte uus (ride forth victoriously)
6 Dyni Pfyle sy scharf, Völker gheie under dir; si träffe ds Härz vo de Finde vom Chünig.
חִצֶּ֗יךָ שְׁנ֫וּנִ֥ים עַ֭מִּים תַּחְתֶּ֣יךָ יִפְּל֑וּ בְּ֝לֵ֗ב אוֹיְבֵ֥י הַמֶּֽלֶךְ׃
BSB 45:5 Your arrows pierce the hearts of the king’s foes; the nations fall beneath your feet.
7 Dy Thron, Gott, steit für geng und ewig; es Zepter, wo grad isch, isch ds Zepter vo dyr Chünigsherrschaft.
כִּסְאֲךָ֣ אֱ֭לֹהִים עוֹלָ֣ם וָעֶ֑ד שֵׁ֥בֶט מִ֝ישֹׁ֗ר שֵׁ֣בֶט מַלְכוּתֶֽךָ׃
BSB 45:6 Your throne, O God, endures forever and ever, and justice is the scepter of Your kingdom.
8 Du hesch d Grächtigkeit lieb gha und d Gottlosigkeit ghasset; drum het di Gott, dy Gott, gsalbet mit Fröidenöl vor dyne Gspane.
אָהַ֣בְתָּ צֶּדֶק֮ וַתִּשְׂנָ֫א רֶ֥שַׁע עַל־כֵּ֤ן׀מְשָׁחֲךָ֡ אֱלֹהִ֣ים אֱ֭לֹהֶיךָ שֶׁ֥מֶן שָׂשׂ֗וֹן מֵֽחֲבֵרֶֽיךָ׃
BSB 45:7 You have loved righteousness and hated wickedness; therefore God, your God, has anointed you above your companions with the oil of joy.
9 Myrrhe und Aloe und Zimet sy all dyni Chleider; us em Palascht vo Elfebei bringe di d Harfe Fröid.
מֹר־וַאֲהָל֣וֹת קְ֭צִיעוֹת כָּל־בִּגְדֹתֶ֑יךָ מִֽן־הֵ֥יכְלֵי שֵׁ֝֗ן מִנִּ֥י שִׂמְּחֽוּךָ׃
BSB 45:8 All your garments are fragrant with myrrh and aloes and cassia; from palaces of ivory the harps make you glad.
מִנִּי → d Harfe (stringed instruments)
10 D Töchtere vo Chünige sy under dyne edle Froue; d Chünigin steit a dyr rächte Syte i Guld vo Ofir.
בְּנ֣וֹת מְ֭לָכִים בְּיִקְּרוֹתֶ֑יךָ נִצְּבָ֥ה שֵׁגַ֥ל לִֽ֝ימִינְךָ֗ בְּכֶ֣תֶם אוֹפִֽיר׃
BSB 45:9 The daughters of kings are among your honored women; the queen stands at your right hand, adorned with the gold of Ophir.
בְּכֶתֶם אוֹפִיר → i Guld vo Ofir (in gold of Ophir)
Name (Store).
11 Los, Tochter, lueg und los guet zue! Vergiss dys Volk und ds Huus vo dym Vatter!
שִׁמְעִי־בַ֣ת וּ֭רְאִי וְהַטִּ֣י אָזְנֵ֑ךְ וְשִׁכְחִ֥י עַ֝מֵּ֗ךְ וּבֵ֥ית אָבִֽיךְ׃
BSB 45:10 Listen, O daughter! Consider and incline your ear: Forget your people and your father’s house,
12 De het der Chünig Verlange nach dyr Schöni. Wil er dy Herr isch, so tue di vor ihm bücke!
וְיִתְאָ֣ו הַמֶּ֣לֶךְ יָפְיֵ֑ךְ כִּי־ה֥וּא אֲ֝דֹנַ֗יִךְ וְהִשְׁתַּֽחֲוִי־לֽוֹ׃
BSB 45:11 and the king will desire your beauty; bow to him, for he is your lord.
13 Und d Tochter Tyrus chunt mit Gschänk; di Ryche im Volk bitte um dy Gunscht.
וּבַֽת־צֹ֨ר׀בְּ֭מִנְחָה פָּנַ֥יִךְ יְחַלּ֗וּ עֲשִׁ֣ירֵי עָֽם׃
BSB 45:12 The Daughter of Tyre will come with a gift; men of wealth will seek your favor.
בַת־צֹר → d Tochter Tyrus (the daughter of Tyre)
14 Ganz herrlech isch d Tochter vom Chünig drinne; us Guld gwobe isch ires Chleid.
כָּל־כְּבוּדָּ֣ה בַת־מֶ֣לֶךְ פְּנִ֑ימָה מִֽמִּשְׁבְּצ֖וֹת זָהָ֣ב לְבוּשָֽׁהּ׃
BSB 45:13 All glorious is the princess in her chamber; her gown is embroidered with gold.
15 I prächtige Chleider wird si zum Chünig gfüert; jungi Froue hinder ere, iri Fründinne, wärde zu dir bbracht.
לִרְקָמוֹת֮ תּוּבַ֪ל לַ֫מֶּ֥לֶךְ בְּתוּל֣וֹת אַ֭חֲרֶיהָ רֵעוֹתֶ֑יהָ מ֖וּבָא֣וֹת לָֽךְ׃
BSB 45:14 In colorful garments she is led to the king; her virgin companions are brought before you.
16 Si wärde gfüert mit Fröid und Juble und chöme i Palascht vom Chünig.
תּ֭וּבַלְנָהבִּשְׂמָחֹ֣ת וָגִ֑יל תְּ֝בֹאֶ֗ינָה בְּהֵ֣יכַל מֶֽלֶךְ׃
BSB 45:15 They are led in with joy and gladness; they enter the palace of the king.
17 A Stell vo dyne Vätter wärde dyni Söhn sy; du wirsch se als Fürschte ysetze im ganze Land.
תַּ֣חַת אֲ֭בֹתֶיךָ יִהְי֣וּ בָנֶ֑יךָ תְּשִׁיתֵ֥מוֹ לְ֝שָׂרִ֗ים בְּכָל־הָאָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 45:16 Your sons will succeed your fathers; you will make them princes throughout the land.
18 I wott derfür sorge, dass me a dy Name dänkt i allne Generatione; drum wärde di d Völker lobe für geng und ewig.
אַזְכִּ֣ירָה שִׁ֭מְךָ בְּכָל־דֹּ֣ר וָדֹ֑ר עַל־כֵּ֥ן עַמִּ֥ים יְ֝הוֹדֻ֗ךָ לְעֹלָ֥ם וָעֶֽד׃
BSB 45:17 I will commemorate your name through all generations; therefore the nations will praise you forever and ever.
בְּכָל־דֹּר וָדֹר → i allne Generatione (through all generations)
