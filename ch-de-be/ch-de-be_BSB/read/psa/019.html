BSB 19:3 Without speech or language, without a sound to be heard,

BSB 19:5 Like a bridegroom emerging from his chamber, like a champion rejoicing to run his course,

BSB 19:6 it rises at one end of the heavens and runs its circuit to the other; nothing is deprived of its warmth.

BSB 19:7 The Law of the LORD is perfect, reviving the soul; the testimony of the LORD is trustworthy, making wise the simple.

יִרְאַ֤ת יְהוָ֨ה׀טְהוֹרָה֮ עוֹמֶ֪דֶת לָ֫עַ֥ד מִֽשְׁפְּטֵי־יְהוָ֥ה אֱמֶ֑ת צָֽדְק֥וּ יַחְדָּֽו׃

11 Si sy meh wärt als Guld, als vil subers Guld; und si sy süess, meh als Hung, meh als der bescht Hung.

BSB 19:13 Keep Your servant also from willful sins; may they not rule over me. Then I will be blameless and cleansed of great transgression.
