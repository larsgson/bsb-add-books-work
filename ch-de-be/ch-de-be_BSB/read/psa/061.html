BSB 61:1 For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. Of David. Hear my cry, O God; attend to my prayer.
3 Vom Änd vo der Ärde rüefe n i zu dir, wen mys Härz schwach wird. Füer mi uf e Felse, wo z höch isch für mi!
מִקְצֵ֤ה הָאָ֨רֶץ׀אֵלֶ֣יךָ אֶ֭קְרָא בַּעֲטֹ֣ף לִבִּ֑י בְּצוּר־יָר֖וּם מִמֶּ֣נִּי תַנְחֵֽנִי׃
BSB 61:2 From the ends of the earth I call out to You whenever my heart is faint. Lead me to the rock that is higher than I.
בְּצוּר־יָרוּם מִמֶּנִּי → uf e Felse, wo z höch isch für mi (to the rock that is higher than I)
4 Denn du bisch my Zueflucht gsi, e feschte Turm gäge der Find.
כִּֽי־הָיִ֣יתָ מַחְסֶ֣ה לִ֑י מִגְדַּל־עֹ֝֗ז מִפְּנֵ֥י אוֹיֵֽב׃
BSB 61:3 For You have been my refuge, a tower of strength against the enemy.
מִגְדַּל־עֹז → e feschte Turm (a strong tower)

5 I wott für geng i dym Zält z Gascht sy, i wott Zueflucht sueche im Schutz vo dyne Flügel. Sela
אָג֣וּרָה בְ֭אָהָלְךָ עוֹלָמִ֑ים אֶֽחֱסֶ֨ה בְסֵ֖תֶר כְּנָפֶ֣יךָ סֶּֽלָה׃
BSB 61:4 Let me dwell in Your tent forever and take refuge in the shelter of Your wings. Selah
אָגוּרָה בְאָהָלְךָ → i dym Zält z Gascht sy (dwell in Your tent)
גּוּר »z Gascht sy« (Ps 15).
6 Denn du, Gott, hesch ghört, was i dir versproche ha, du hesch mer ds Erb gä vo dene, wo dy Name förchte.
כִּֽי־אַתָּ֣ה אֱ֭לֹהִים שָׁמַ֣עְתָּ לִנְדָרָ֑י נָתַ֥תָּ יְ֝רֻשַּׁ֗ת יִרְאֵ֥י שְׁמֶֽךָ׃
BSB 61:5 For You have heard my vows, O God; You have given me the inheritance reserved for those who fear Your name.
נְדָרָי → was i dir versproche ha (my vows)
Wie Ps 50,14.
7 Gib em Chünig zu syne Tage no Tage derzue, syni Jahr sölle sy wi vo Generation zu Generation!
יָמִ֣ים עַל־יְמֵי־מֶ֣לֶךְ תּוֹסִ֑יף שְׁ֝נוֹתָ֗יו כְּמוֹ־דֹ֥ר וָדֹֽר׃
BSB 61:6 Increase the days of the king’s life; may his years span many generations.
8 Er söll für geng vor Gott uf em Thron sitze; schick Tröiji und Wahrheit, dass si ne bhüete!
יֵשֵׁ֣ב ע֭וֹלָם לִפְנֵ֣י אֱלֹהִ֑ים חֶ֥סֶד וֶ֝אֱמֶ֗ת מַ֣ן יִנְצְרֻֽהוּ׃
BSB 61:7 May he sit enthroned in God’s presence forever; appoint Your loving devotion and Your faithfulness to guard him.
חֶסֶד וֶאֱמֶת מַן → schick Tröiji und Wahrheit (appoint loving devotion and faithfulness)
9 So wott i dym Name für geng Psalme singe, für Tag für Tag z erfülle, was i versproche ha.
כֵּ֤ן אֲזַמְּרָ֣ה שִׁמְךָ֣ לָעַ֑ד לְֽשַׁלְּמִ֥י נְדָרַ֗י י֣וֹם׀יֽוֹם׃
BSB 61:8 Then I will ever sing praise to Your name and fulfill my vows day by day.
