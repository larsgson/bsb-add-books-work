1 E Psalm vom Asaf. Ja, Gott isch guet zu Israel, zu dene, wo es subers Härz hei.
מִזְמ֗וֹר לְאָ֫סָ֥ףאַ֤ךְ ט֭וֹב לְיִשְׂרָאֵ֥ל אֱלֹהִ֗ים לְבָרֵ֥י לֵבָֽב׃
BSB 73:1 A Psalm of Asaph. Surely God is good to Israel, to those who are pure in heart.
אַךְ → Ja (Surely)
אַךְ hier beteuernd (vgl. Ps 62 »nume«).
2 Aber ig – fasch hätte myni Füess afa stolpere, fasch hätte myni Schritte der Halt verlore.
וַאֲנִ֗י כִּ֭מְעַט נָטָ֣יוּ רַגְלָ֑י כְּ֝אַ֗יִן שֻׁפְּכ֥וּ אֲשֻׁרָֽי׃
BSB 73:2 But as for me, my feet had almost stumbled; my steps had nearly slipped.
3 Denn i ha Nyd gha uf die, wo stolz sy, won i gseh ha, wi guet's de Gottlose geit.
כִּֽי־קִ֭נֵּאתִי בַּֽהוֹלְלִ֑ים שְׁל֖וֹם רְשָׁעִ֣ים אֶרְאֶֽה׃
BSB 73:3 For I envied the arrogant when I saw the prosperity of the wicked.
4 Denn si hei kener Schmärze bis zu irem Tod, und ire Lyb isch gsund.
כִּ֤י אֵ֖ין חַרְצֻבּ֥וֹת לְמוֹתָ֗ם וּבָרִ֥יא אוּלָֽם׃
BSB 73:4 They have no struggle in their death; their bodies are well-fed.
5 I der Müej vo de Mönsche sy si nid, und si wärde nid plaget wi anderi Lüt.
בַּעֲמַ֣ל אֱנ֣וֹשׁ אֵינֵ֑מוֹ וְעִם־אָ֝דָ֗ם לֹ֣א יְנֻגָּֽעוּ׃
BSB 73:5 They are free of the burdens others carry; they are not afflicted like other men.
6 Drum isch der Stolz iri Chetti am Hals, Gwalt isch ds Chleid, wo se zuedeckt.
לָ֭כֵן עֲנָקַ֣תְמוֹ גַאֲוָ֑ה יַעֲטָף־שִׁ֝֗ית חָמָ֥ס לָֽמוֹ׃
BSB 73:6 Therefore pride is their necklace; a garment of violence covers them.
7 Iri Ouge luege us em Überfluss use, und ires Härz isch voll vo böse Gedanke, wo überloufe.
יָ֭צָא מֵחֵ֣לֶב עֵינֵ֑מוֹ עָ֝בְר֗וּ מַשְׂכִּיּ֥וֹת לֵבָֽב׃
BSB 73:7 From their prosperity proceeds iniquity; the imaginations of their hearts run wild.
מֵחֵלֶב → us em Überfluss (From their prosperity)
חֵלֶב »Fett« übertragen.
8 Si spotte und rede mit böse Wort vo Gwalt, vo obe abe rede si.
יָמִ֤יקוּ׀וִידַבְּר֣וּ בְרָ֣ע עֹ֑שֶׁק מִמָּר֥וֹם יְדַבֵּֽרוּ׃
BSB 73:8 They mock and speak with malice; with arrogance they threaten oppression.
9 Mit irem Muul gö si gäge der Himel aa, und iri Zunge geit dür d Ärde.
שַׁתּ֣וּ בַשָּׁמַ֣יִם פִּיהֶ֑ם וּ֝לְשׁוֹנָ֗ם תִּֽהֲלַ֥ךְ בָּאָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 73:9 They set their mouths against the heavens, and their tongues strut across the earth.
10 Drum chehrt sys Volk zu ne um und trinkt vil Wasser.
לָכֵ֤ן׀יָשׁ֣וּב עַמּ֣וֹ הֲלֹ֑ם וּמֵ֥י מָ֝לֵ֗א יִמָּ֥צוּ לָֽמוֹ׃
BSB 73:10 So their people return to this place and drink up waters in abundance.
וּמֵי מָלֵא יִמָּצוּ → trinkt vil Wasser (drink up waters in abundance)
Schwieriger Vers; nach dem MT.
11 Si säge: „Wi sött Gott das wüsse? Weiss der Höchscht öppis dervo?“
וְֽאָמְר֗וּ אֵיכָ֥ה יָדַֽע־אֵ֑ל וְיֵ֖שׁ דֵּעָ֣ה בְעֶלְיֽוֹן׃
BSB 73:11 The wicked say, “How can God know? Does the Most High have knowledge?”
12 Lue, so sy di Gottlose: geng ohni Sorge wärde si geng rych und rych.
הִנֵּה־אֵ֥לֶּה רְשָׁעִ֑ים וְשַׁלְוֵ֥י ע֝וֹלָ֗ם הִשְׂגּוּ־חָֽיִל׃
BSB 73:12 Behold, these are the wicked — always carefree as they increase their wealth.
13 Ja, für nüüt han i mys Härz suber bhalte und myni Händ gwäsche als Unschuldige.
אַךְ־רִ֭יק זִכִּ֣יתִי לְבָבִ֑י וָאֶרְחַ֖ץ בְּנִקָּי֣וֹן כַּפָּֽי׃
BSB 73:13 Surely in vain I have kept my heart pure; in innocence I have washed my hands.
14 I bi der ganz Tag plaget worde, und jede Morge bin i gstraft worde.
וָאֱהִ֣י נָ֭גוּעַ כָּל־הַיּ֑וֹם וְ֝תוֹכַחְתִּ֗י לַבְּקָרִֽים׃
BSB 73:14 For I am afflicted all day long and punished every morning.
15 Wen i gseit hätt: „I wott o so rede“, lue, de wär i der Generation vo dyne Chind untröi worde.
אִם־אָ֭מַרְתִּי אֲסַפְּרָ֥ה כְמ֑וֹ הִנֵּ֤ה ד֭וֹר בָּנֶ֣יךָ בָגָֽדְתִּי׃
BSB 73:15 If I had said, “I will speak this way,” then I would have betrayed Your children.
16 Won i drüber überleit ha, für das z begryffe, isch's mer schwär vorcho,
וָֽ֭אֲחַשְּׁבָה לָדַ֣עַת זֹ֑את עָמָ֖ל ה֣וּא בְעֵינָֽי׃
BSB 73:16 When I tried to understand all this, it was troublesome in my sight
17 bis i i ds Heiligtum vo Gott cho bi; da han i iri Änd verstande.
עַד־אָ֭בוֹא אֶל־מִקְדְּשֵׁי־אֵ֑ל אָ֝בִ֗ינָה לְאַחֲרִיתָֽם׃
BSB 73:17 until I entered God’s sanctuary; then I discerned their end.
18 Ja, du tuesch se uf e Bode stelle, wo glatt isch, du lasch se gheie is Verderbe.
אַ֣ךְ בַּ֭חֲלָקוֹת תָּשִׁ֣ית לָ֑מוֹ הִ֝פַּלְתָּ֗ם לְמַשּׁוּאֽוֹת׃
BSB 73:18 Surely You set them on slick ground; You cast them down into ruin.
בַּחֲלָקוֹת → uf e Bode, wo glatt isch (on slick ground)
19 Wi sy si im Nu verwüeschtet! Si sy fertig, ganz ewäg vor luter Schrecke.
אֵ֤יךְ הָי֣וּ לְשַׁמָּ֣ה כְרָ֑גַע סָ֥פוּ תַ֝֗מּוּ מִן־בַּלָּהֽוֹת׃
BSB 73:19 How suddenly they are laid waste, completely swept away by terrors!
20 Wi ne Troum, wen me erwachet, Herr, so verachtisch du iri Bilder, wen du wach wirsch.
כַּחֲל֥וֹם מֵהָקִ֑יץ אֲ֝דֹנָי בָּעִ֤יר׀צַלְמָ֬ם תִּבְזֶֽה׃
BSB 73:20 Like one waking from a dream, so You, O Lord, awaken and despise their form.
בָּעִיר → wen du wach wirsch (awaken)
21 Won i im Härz bitter gsi bi und es mer im Innerschte weh ta het,
כִּ֭י יִתְחַמֵּ֣ץ לְבָבִ֑י וְ֝כִלְיוֹתַ֗י אֶשְׁתּוֹנָֽן׃
BSB 73:21 When my heart was grieved and I was pierced within,
22 da bin i dumm gsi und ha nüüt verstande; wi ds Veh bin i vor dir gsi.
וַאֲנִי־בַ֭עַר וְלֹ֣א אֵדָ֑ע בְּ֝הֵמ֗וֹת הָיִ֥יתִי עִמָּֽךְ׃
BSB 73:22 I was senseless and ignorant; I was a brute beast before You.
בְּהֵמוֹת → wi ds Veh (a brute beast)
Wie Ps 49.
23 Aber ig bi geng bi dir; du hesch my rächti Hand gno.
וַאֲנִ֣י תָמִ֣יד עִמָּ֑ךְ אָ֝חַ֗זְתָּ בְּיַד־יְמִינִֽי׃
BSB 73:23 Yet I am always with You; You hold my right hand.
24 Mit dym Rat füersch du mi, und nachär nimmsch du mi uuf i d Herrlechkeit.
בַּעֲצָתְךָ֥ תַנְחֵ֑נִי וְ֝אַחַ֗ר כָּב֥וֹד תִּקָּחֵֽנִי׃
BSB 73:24 You guide me with Your counsel, and later receive me in glory.
כָּבוֹד תִּקָּחֵנִי → nimmsch du mi uuf i d Herrlechkeit (receive me in glory)
25 Wän han i im Himel? Und näbe dir wott i nüüt uf der Ärde.
מִי־לִ֥י בַשָּׁמָ֑יִם וְ֝עִמְּךָ֗ לֹא־חָפַ֥צְתִּי בָאָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 73:25 Whom have I in heaven but You? And on earth I desire no one besides You.
26 Wen o mys Fleisch und mys Härz am Änd sy, Gott isch der Felse vo mym Härz und my Teil für geng.
כָּלָ֥ה שְׁאֵרִ֗י וּלְבָ֫בִ֥י צוּר־לְבָבִ֥י וְחֶלְקִ֗י אֱלֹהִ֥ים לְעוֹלָֽם׃
BSB 73:26 My flesh and my heart may fail, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever.
27 Denn lue, die, wo wyt ewäg vo dir sy, gö zgrund; du tuesch alli vernichte, wo dir untröi sy.
כִּֽי־הִנֵּ֣ה רְחֵקֶ֣יךָ יֹאבֵ֑דוּ הִ֝צְמַ֗תָּה כָּל־זוֹנֶ֥ה מִמֶּֽךָּ׃
BSB 73:27 Those far from You will surely perish; You destroy all who are unfaithful to You.
28 Aber für mi isch's guet, nach bi Gott z sy; bim Herr, em HERR, han i my Zueflucht gfunde, für vo allne dyne Wärch z verzelle.
וַאֲנִ֤י׀קִֽרֲבַ֥ת אֱלֹהִ֗ים לִ֫י־ט֥וֹב שַׁתִּ֤י׀בַּאדֹנָ֣י יְהֹוִ֣ה מַחְסִ֑י לְ֝סַפֵּ֗ר כָּל־מַלְאֲכוֹתֶֽיךָ׃
BSB 73:28 But as for me, it is good to draw near to God. I have made the Lord GOD my refuge, that I may proclaim all Your works.
