1 Halleluja! Danket em HERR, denn er isch guet, sy Tröiji blybt für geng!
הַֽלְלוּ יָ֨הּהוֹד֣וּ לַיהוָ֣ה כִּי־ט֑וֹב כִּ֖י לְעוֹלָ֣ם חַסְדּֽוֹ׃
BSB 106:1 Hallelujah! Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever.
2 Wär cha di grosse Tate vom HERR verzelle, wär cha sys ganze Lob la ghöre?
מִ֗י יְ֭מַלֵּל גְּבוּר֣וֹת יְהוָ֑ה יַ֝שְׁמִ֗יעַ כָּל־תְּהִלָּתֽוֹ׃
BSB 106:2 Who can describe the mighty acts of the LORD or fully proclaim His praise?
3 Glücklech sy die, wo ds Rächt halte, wo geng tüe, was grächt isch!
אַ֭שְׁרֵי שֹׁמְרֵ֣י מִשְׁפָּ֑ט עֹשֵׂ֖ה צְדָקָ֣ה בְכָל־עֵֽת׃
BSB 106:3 Blessed are those who uphold justice, who practice righteousness at all times.
4 Dänk a mi, HERR, we du dym Volk gnädig bisch; lue nach mir und hilf mer,
זָכְרֵ֣נִי יְ֭הוָה בִּרְצ֣וֹן עַמֶּ֑ךָ פָּ֝קְדֵ֗נִי בִּישׁוּעָתֶֽךָ׃
BSB 106:4 Remember me, O LORD, in Your favor to Your people; visit me with Your salvation,
5 dass i ds Glück vo dyne Userwählte gseh, dass i mi fröie mit der Fröid vo dym Volk, dass i mi rüeme mit dym Erb!
לִרְא֤וֹת׀בְּט֘וֹבַ֤ת בְּחִירֶ֗יךָ לִ֭שְׂמֹחַ בְּשִׂמְחַ֣ת גּוֹיֶ֑ךָ לְ֝הִתְהַלֵּ֗ל עִם־נַחֲלָתֶֽךָ׃
BSB 106:5 that I may see the prosperity of Your chosen ones, and rejoice in the gladness of Your nation, and give glory with Your inheritance.
6 Mir hei gsündiget wi üsi Vätter, mir hei Unrächt ta, mir sy gottlos gsi.
חָטָ֥אנוּ עִם־אֲבוֹתֵ֗ינוּ הֶעֱוִ֥ינוּ הִרְשָֽׁעְנוּ׃
BSB 106:6 We have sinned like our fathers; we have done wrong and acted wickedly.
7 Üsi Vätter i Ägypte hei dyni Wunder nid begriffe, si hei nid dänkt a d Grössi vo dyr Tröiji und hei sech am Meer gäge ne gstellt, am rote Meer.
אֲב֘וֹתֵ֤ינוּ בְמִצְרַ֨יִם׀לֹא־הִשְׂכִּ֬ילוּ נִפְלְאוֹתֶ֗יךָ לֹ֣א זָ֭כְרוּ אֶת־רֹ֣ב חֲסָדֶ֑יךָ וַיַּמְר֖וּ עַל־יָ֣ם בְּיַם־סֽוּף׃
BSB 106:7 Our fathers in Egypt did not grasp Your wonders or remember Your abundant kindness; but they rebelled by the sea, there at the Red Sea.
וַיַּמְרוּ → hei sech … gäge ne gstellt (they rebelled)
Auch V. 33.43.
8 Und doch het er se grettet wäge sym Name, für sy Macht bekannt z mache.
וַֽ֭יּוֹשִׁיעֵם לְמַ֣עַן שְׁמ֑וֹ לְ֝הוֹדִ֗יעַ אֶת־גְּבוּרָתֽוֹ׃
BSB 106:8 Yet He saved them for the sake of His name, to make His power known.
9 Er het ds rote Meer aagfahre, und es isch tröchnet, und er het se dür d Tiefine gfüert wi dür ne Wüeschti.
וַיִּגְעַ֣ר בְּיַם־ס֭וּף וַֽיֶּחֱרָ֑ב וַיּוֹלִיכֵ֥ם בַּ֝תְּהֹמ֗וֹת כַּמִּדְבָּֽר׃
BSB 106:9 He rebuked the Red Sea, and it dried up; He led them through the depths as through a desert.
10 Er het se grettet us der Hand vo däm, wo se ghasset het, und se erlöst us der Hand vom Find.
וַֽ֭יּוֹשִׁיעֵם מִיַּ֣ד שׂוֹנֵ֑א וַ֝יִּגְאָלֵ֗ם מִיַּ֥ד אוֹיֵֽב׃
BSB 106:10 He saved them from the hand that hated them; He redeemed them from the hand of the enemy.
11 Ds Wasser het iri Finde zuedeckt, nid en einzige vo ne isch übrig bblibe.
וַיְכַסּוּ־מַ֥יִם צָרֵיהֶ֑ם אֶחָ֥ד מֵ֝הֶ֗ם לֹ֣א נוֹתָֽר׃
BSB 106:11 The waters covered their foes; not one of them remained.
12 De hei si a syni Wort gloubt, si hei sys Lob gsunge.
וַיַּאֲמִ֥ינוּ בִדְבָרָ֑יו יָ֝שִׁ֗ירוּ תְּהִלָּתֽוֹ׃
BSB 106:12 Then they believed His promises and sang His praise.

מִֽ֭הֲרוּ שָׁכְח֣וּ מַעֲשָׂ֑יו לֹֽא־חִ֝כּ֗וּ לַעֲצָתֽוֹ׃
BSB 106:13 Yet they soon forgot His works and failed to wait for His counsel.

BSB 106:14 They craved intensely in the wilderness and tested God in the desert.

וַיִּתֵּ֣ן לָ֭הֶם שֶׁאֱלָתָ֑ם וַיְשַׁלַּ֖ח רָז֣וֹן בְּנַפְשָֽׁם׃

BSB 106:16 In the camp they envied Moses, as well as Aaron, the holy one of the LORD.
17 D Ärde het sech ufta und der Datan verschlückt und d Gruppe vom Abiram zuedeckt.

BSB 106:17 The earth opened up and swallowed Dathan; it covered the assembly of Abiram.
18 Es Füür het i irer Gruppe brönnt, e Flamme het d Gottlose verbrönnt.
וַתִּבְעַר־אֵ֥שׁ בַּעֲדָתָ֑ם לֶ֝הָבָ֗ה תְּלַהֵ֥ט רְשָׁעִֽים׃
BSB 106:18 Then fire blazed through their company; flames consumed the wicked.

BSB 106:22 wondrous works in the land of Ham, and awesome deeds by the Red Sea.

וַיֹּ֗אמֶר לְֽהַשְׁמִ֫ידָ֥ם לוּלֵ֡י מֹ֘שֶׁ֤ה בְחִיר֗וֹ עָמַ֣ד בַּפֶּ֣רֶץ לְפָנָ֑יו לְהָשִׁ֥יב חֲ֝מָת֗וֹ מֵֽהַשְׁחִֽית׃

BSB 106:38 They shed innocent blood— the blood of their sons and daughters, whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan, and the land was polluted with blood.

BSB 106:40 So the anger of the LORD burned against His people, and He abhorred His own inheritance.

BSB 106:41 He delivered them into the hand of the nations, and those who hated them ruled over them.

BSB 106:42 Their enemies oppressed them and subdued them under their hand.

BSB 106:43 Many times He rescued them, but they were bent on rebellion and sank down in their iniquity.
44 Aber er het uf iri Not gluegt, wo n er ires Rüefe ghört het.

BSB 106:46 He made them objects of compassion to all who held them captive.

BSB 106:47 Save us, O LORD our God, and gather us from the nations, that we may give thanks to Your holy name, that we may glory in Your praise.

BSB 106:48 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. Let all the people say, “Amen!” Hallelujah!
