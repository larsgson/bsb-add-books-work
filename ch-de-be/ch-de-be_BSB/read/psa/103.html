1 Vom David. Lob der HERR, my Seel, und alls, wo i mir isch, sy heilige Name!
לְדָוִ֨ד׀בָּרֲכִ֣י נַ֭פְשִׁי אֶת־יְהוָ֑ה וְכָל־קְ֝רָבַ֗י אֶת־שֵׁ֥ם קָדְשֽׁוֹ׃
BSB 103:1 Of David. Bless the LORD, O my soul; all that is within me, bless His holy name.
2 Lob der HERR, my Seel, und vergiss nid alls, won er dir Guets ta het!
בָּרֲכִ֣י נַ֭פְשִׁי אֶת־יְהוָ֑ה וְאַל־תִּ֝שְׁכְּחִ֗י כָּל־גְּמוּלָֽיו׃
BSB 103:2 Bless the LORD, O my soul, and do not forget all His kind deeds —
גְּמוּלָיו → alls, won er dir Guets ta het (all His kind deeds)
3 Är vergit dir alli dyni Schuld, är heilt alli dyni Chrankheite;
הַסֹּלֵ֥חַ לְכָל־עֲוֺנֵ֑כִי הָ֝רֹפֵ֗א לְכָל־תַּחֲלֻאָֽיְכִי׃
BSB 103:3 He who forgives all your iniquities and heals all your diseases,
4 är rettet dys Läbe us der Grueb, är setzt dir Tröiji und Erbarme wi ne Chrone uf;
הַגּוֹאֵ֣ל מִשַּׁ֣חַת חַיָּ֑יְכִי הַֽ֝מְעַטְּרֵ֗כִי חֶ֣סֶד וְרַחֲמִֽים׃
BSB 103:4 who redeems your life from the Pit and crowns you with loving devotion and compassion,
הַמְעַטְּרֵכִי → setzt dir … wi ne Chrone uf (crowns you)
5 är macht dys Läbe satt mit Guetem, dass du wider jung wirsch wi nen Adler.
הַמַּשְׂבִּ֣יַע בַּטּ֣וֹב עֶדְיֵ֑ךְ תִּתְחַדֵּ֖שׁ כַּנֶּ֣שֶׁר נְעוּרָֽיְכִי׃
BSB 103:5 who satisfies you with good things, so that your youth is renewed like the eagle’s.
עֶדְיֵךְ → dys Läbe (your desires)
Unsicheres Wort; nach dem Zusammenhang.
6 Der HERR schaffet Grächtigkeit und Rächt für alli, wo plaget wärde.
עֹשֵׂ֣ה צְדָק֣וֹת יְהוָ֑ה וּ֝מִשְׁפָּטִ֗ים לְכָל־עֲשׁוּקִֽים׃
BSB 103:6 The LORD executes righteousness and justice for all the oppressed.
7 Er het em Mose syni Wääge zeigt, de Söhn vo Israel syni Tate.
יוֹדִ֣יעַ דְּרָכָ֣יו לְמֹשֶׁ֑ה לִבְנֵ֥י יִ֝שְׂרָאֵ֗ל עֲלִילֽוֹתָיו׃
BSB 103:7 He made known His ways to Moses, His deeds to the people of Israel.
8 Barmhärzig und gnädig isch der HERR, voll Geduld und rych a Tröiji.
רַח֣וּם וְחַנּ֣וּן יְהוָ֑ה אֶ֖רֶךְ אַפַּ֣יִם וְרַב־חָֽסֶד׃
BSB 103:8 The LORD is compassionate and gracious, slow to anger, abounding in loving devotion.
9 Er chlagt nid geng aa und blybt nid für geng toub.
לֹֽא־לָנֶ֥צַח יָרִ֑יב וְלֹ֖א לְעוֹלָ֣ם יִטּֽוֹר׃
BSB 103:9 He will not always accuse us, nor harbor His anger forever.
10 Er het nid mit üüs gmacht nach üsne Sünde und üüs nid zrüggzalt nach üsne Fähler.
לֹ֣א כַ֭חֲטָאֵינוּ עָ֣שָׂה לָ֑נוּ וְלֹ֥א כַ֝עֲוֺנֹתֵ֗ינוּ גָּמַ֥ל עָלֵֽינוּ׃
BSB 103:10 He has not dealt with us according to our sins or repaid us according to our iniquities.
11 Denn so höch wi der Himel über der Ärde isch, so gross isch sy Tröiji über dene, wo Ehrfurcht vor ihm hei.
כִּ֤י כִגְבֹ֣הַּ שָׁ֭מַיִם עַל־הָאָ֑רֶץ גָּבַ֥ר חַ֝סְדּ֗וֹ עַל־יְרֵאָֽיו׃
BSB 103:11 For as high as the heavens are above the earth, so great is His loving devotion for those who fear Him.
12 So wyt wi der Oschte vom Weschte isch, so wyt tuet er üsi Fähler vo üüs wägg.
כִּרְחֹ֣ק מִ֭זְרָח מִֽמַּֽעֲרָ֑ב הִֽרְחִ֥יק מִ֝מֶּ֗נּוּ אֶת־פְּשָׁעֵֽינוּ׃
BSB 103:12 As far as the east is from the west, so far has He removed our transgressions from us.
13 Wi ne Vatter Erbarme het mit de Chind, so het der HERR Erbarme mit dene, wo Ehrfurcht vor ihm hei.
כְּרַחֵ֣ם אָ֭ב עַל־בָּנִ֑ים רִחַ֥ם יְ֝הוָ֗ה עַל־יְרֵאָֽיו׃
BSB 103:13 As a father has compassion on his children, so the LORD has compassion on those who fear Him.
14 Denn är weiss, wi mer gmacht sy, er dänkt dra, dass mer Stoub sy.
כִּי־ה֖וּא יָדַ֣ע יִצְרֵ֑נוּ זָ֝כ֗וּר כִּי־עָפָ֥ר אֲנָֽחְנוּ׃
BSB 103:14 For He knows our frame; He is mindful that we are dust.
יִצְרֵנוּ → wi mer gmacht sy (our frame)
15 E Mönsch – syni Täg sy wi ds Gras; wi ne Blueme uf em Fäld, so wachst er.
אֱ֭נוֹשׁ כֶּחָצִ֣יר יָמָ֑יו כְּצִ֥יץ הַ֝שָּׂדֶ֗ה כֵּ֣ן יָצִֽיץ׃
BSB 103:15 As for man, his days are like grass— he blooms like a flower of the field;
16 Wen der Luft drüber geit, de isch si nümme da, und ire Platz kennt se nid meh.
כִּ֤י ר֣וּחַ עָֽבְרָה־בּ֣וֹ וְאֵינֶ֑נּוּ וְלֹא־יַכִּירֶ֖נּוּ ע֣וֹד מְקוֹמֽוֹ׃
BSB 103:16 when the wind passes over, it vanishes, and its place remembers it no more.
17 Aber d Tröiji vom HERR blybt vo Ewigkeit zu Ewigkeit über dene, wo Ehrfurcht vor ihm hei, und sy Grächtigkeit bi de Chind vo de Chind,
וְחֶ֤סֶד יְהוָ֨ה׀מֵעוֹלָ֣ם וְעַד־ע֭וֹלָם עַל־יְרֵאָ֑יו וְ֝צִדְקָת֗וֹ לִבְנֵ֥י בָנִֽים׃
BSB 103:17 But from everlasting to everlasting the loving devotion of the LORD extends to those who fear Him, and His righteousness to their children’s children —
בְנֵי בָנִים → de Chind vo de Chind (children's children)
18 bi dene, wo sy Bund halte und a syni Gebot dänke, für se z tue.
לְשֹׁמְרֵ֥י בְרִית֑וֹ וּלְזֹכְרֵ֥י פִ֝קֻּדָ֗יו לַעֲשׂוֹתָֽם׃
BSB 103:18 to those who keep His covenant and remember to obey His precepts.
19 Der HERR het sy Thron im Himel ufgstellt, und sy Chünigrych regiert über alls.
יְֽהוָ֗ה בַּ֭שָּׁמַיִם הֵכִ֣ין כִּסְא֑וֹ וּ֝מַלְכוּת֗וֹ בַּכֹּ֥ל מָשָֽׁלָה׃
BSB 103:19 The LORD has established His throne in heaven, and His kingdom rules over all.
20 Lobet der HERR, dihr syni Ängel, dihr Starche, wo sys Wort usfüere, wo uf d Stimm vo sym Wort lose!
בָּרֲכ֥וּ יְהוָ֗ה מַלְאָ֫כָ֥יו גִּבֹּ֣רֵי כֹ֭חַ עֹשֵׂ֣י דְבָר֑וֹ לִ֝שְׁמֹ֗עַ בְּק֣וֹל דְּבָרֽוֹ׃
BSB 103:20 Bless the LORD, all His angels mighty in strength who carry out His word, who hearken to the voice of His command.
גִּבֹּרֵי כֹחַ → dihr Starche (mighty in strength)
21 Lobet der HERR, alli syni Heer, dihr syni Diener, wo sy Wille tüe!
בָּרֲכ֣וּ יְ֭הוָה כָּל־צְבָאָ֑יו מְ֝שָׁרְתָ֗יו עֹשֵׂ֥י רְצוֹנֽוֹ׃
BSB 103:21 Bless the LORD, all His hosts, you servants who do His will.
22 Lobet der HERR, alli syni Wärch a allne Orte, won er regiert! Lob der HERR, my Seel!
בָּרֲכ֤וּ יְהוָ֨ה׀כָּֽל־מַעֲשָׂ֗יו בְּכָל־מְקֹמ֥וֹת מֶמְשַׁלְתּ֑וֹ בָּרֲכִ֥י נַ֝פְשִׁ֗י אֶת־יְהוָֽה׃
BSB 103:22 Bless the LORD, all His works in all places of His dominion. Bless the LORD, O my soul!
