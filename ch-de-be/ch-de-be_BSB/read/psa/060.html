Mit Gott wei mer Grosses tue

1 Für e Chorleiter. Nach der Wys „Lilie vom Zügnis“. E Michtam vom David. Zum Lehre.

2 Won er gäge Aram-Naharajim und gäge Aram-Zoba Chrieg gfüert het und der Joab zrüggcho isch und Edom im Salztal gschlage het, zwölf Tuusig Ma.

בְּהַצּוֹת֨וֹ׀אֶ֥ת אֲרַ֣ם נַהֲרַיִם֮ וְאֶת־אֲרַ֪ם צ֫וֹבָ֥ה וַיָּ֤שָׁב יוֹאָ֗ב וַיַּ֣ךְ אֶת־אֱד֣וֹם בְּגֵיא־מֶ֑לַח שְׁנֵ֖ים עָשָׂ֣ר אָֽלֶף׃
3 Gott, du hesch is verstosse, du hesch is verbroche, du bisch toub gsi – bring is wider zrügg!
אֱ֭לֹהִים זְנַחְתָּ֣נוּ פְרַצְתָּ֑נוּ אָ֝נַ֗פְתָּ תְּשׁ֣וֹבֵ֥ב לָֽנוּ׃
BSB 60:1 For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Lily of the Covenant.” A Miktam of David for instruction. When he fought Aram-naharaim and Aram-zobah, and Joab returned and struck down 12,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt. You have rejected us, O God; You have broken us; You have been angry; restore us!
4 Du hesch ds Land la zittere, du hesch es la spalte. Heil syni Spält, denn es chunt is Wanke!
הִרְעַ֣שְׁתָּה אֶ֣רֶץ פְּצַמְתָּ֑הּ רְפָ֖ה שְׁבָרֶ֣יהָ כִי־מָֽטָה׃
BSB 60:2 You have shaken the land and torn it open. Heal its fractures, for it is quaking.
5 Du hesch dys Volk Schwärs la gseh, du hesch is Wy z trinke gä, wo eim schwindlig macht.
הִרְאִ֣יתָה עַמְּךָ֣ קָשָׁ֑ה הִ֝שְׁקִיתָ֗נוּ יַ֣יִן תַּרְעֵלָֽה׃
BSB 60:3 You have shown Your people hardship; we are staggered from the wine You made us drink.
יַיִן תַּרְעֵלָה → Wy …, wo eim schwindlig macht (wine to make us stagger)
6 Du hesch dene, wo di förchte, e Fahne gä, dass si chöi flie vor em Pfyleboge. Sela
נָ֘תַ֤תָּה לִּירֵאֶ֣יךָ נֵּ֭ס לְהִתְנוֹסֵ֑ס מִ֝פְּנֵ֗י קֹ֣שֶׁט סֶֽלָה׃
BSB 60:4 You have raised a banner for those who fear You, that they may flee the bow. Selah
מִפְּנֵי קֹשֶׁט → vor em Pfyleboge (from the bow)
7 Dass die, wo du lieb hesch, grettet wärde, hilf mit dyr rächte Hand und gib üüs Antwort!
לְ֭מַעַן יֵחָלְצ֣וּן יְדִידֶ֑יךָ הוֹשִׁ֖יעָה יְמִֽינְךָ֣ וַעֲנֵֽנִי׃
BSB 60:5 Respond and save us with Your right hand, that Your beloved may be delivered.
8 Gott het gredt i sym Heiligtum: „I wott juble, i wott Sichem verteile und ds Tal vo Sukkot mässe und verteile.
אֱלֹהִ֤ים׀דִּבֶּ֥ר בְּקָדְשׁ֗וֹ אֶ֫עְלֹ֥זָה אֲחַלְּקָ֥ה שְׁכֶ֑ם וְעֵ֖מֶק סֻכּ֣וֹת אֲמַדֵּֽד׃
BSB 60:6 God has spoken from His sanctuary: “I will triumph! I will parcel out Shechem and apportion the Valley of Succoth.
9 Mir ghört Gilead, und mir ghört Manasse, und Efraim isch der Helm vo mym Chopf, Juda isch my Zepter.
לִ֤י גִלְעָ֨ד׀וְלִ֬י מְנַשֶּׁ֗ה וְ֭אֶפְרַיִם מָע֣וֹז רֹאשִׁ֑י יְ֝הוּדָ֗ה מְחֹֽקְקִי׃
BSB 60:7 Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine; Ephraim is My helmet, Judah is My scepter.
מָעוֹז רֹאשִׁי → der Helm vo mym Chopf (My helmet)
10 Moab isch mys Becki zum Wäsche, uf Edom rüere n i my Sandale. Über mi juble, Philischtäa!“
מוֹאָ֤ב׀סִ֬יר רַחְצִ֗י עַל־אֱ֭דוֹם אַשְׁלִ֣יךְ נַעֲלִ֑י עָ֝לַ֗י פְּלֶ֣שֶׁת הִתְרֹעָֽעִֽי׃
BSB 60:8 Moab is My washbasin; upon Edom I toss My sandal; over Philistia I shout in triumph.”
סִיר רַחְצִי → mys Becki zum Wäsche (My washbasin)
עָלַי פְּלֶשֶׁת הִתְרֹעָעִי → Über mi juble, Philischtäa! (over Philistia I shout in triumph)
MT; Ps 108,10 anders.
11 Wär bringt mi i di feschti Stadt? Wär füert mi bis uf Edom?
מִ֣י יֹ֭בִלֵנִי עִ֣יר מָצ֑וֹר מִ֖י נָחַ֣נִי עַד־אֱדֽוֹם׃
BSB 60:9 Who will bring me to the fortified city? Who will lead me to Edom?
12 Hesch nid du, Gott, is verstosse? Du geisch nid use, Gott, mit üsne Heer!
הֲלֹֽא־אַתָּ֣ה אֱלֹהִ֣ים זְנַחְתָּ֑נוּ וְֽלֹא־תֵצֵ֥א אֱ֝לֹהִ֗ים בְּצִבְאוֹתֵֽינוּ׃
BSB 60:10 Have You not rejected us, O God? Will You no longer march out, O God, with our armies?
13 Gib üüs Hilf gäge der Find! Denn d Hilf vo Mönsche isch für nüüt.
הָֽבָה־לָּ֣נוּ עֶזְרָ֣ת מִצָּ֑ר וְ֝שָׁ֗וְא תְּשׁוּעַ֥ת אָדָם׃
BSB 60:11 Give us aid against the enemy, for the help of man is worthless.
14 Mit Gott wei mer Grosses tue, und är trappet üsi Finde zäme.
בֵּֽאלֹהִ֥ים נַעֲשֶׂה־חָ֑יִל וְ֝ה֗וּא יָב֥וּס צָרֵֽינוּ׃
BSB 60:12 With God we will perform with valor, and He will trample our enemies.
