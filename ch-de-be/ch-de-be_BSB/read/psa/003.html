3 Vil säge vo mir: „Es git für ihn ke Rettig bi Gott.“ Sela
רַבִּים֮ אֹמְרִ֪ים לְנַ֫פְשִׁ֥י אֵ֤ין יְֽשׁוּעָ֓תָה לּ֬וֹ בֵֽאלֹהִ֬ים סֶֽלָה׃

BSB 3:5 I lie down and sleep; I wake again, for the LORD sustains me.

BSB 3:6 I will not fear the myriads set against me on every side.

BSB 3:7 Arise, O LORD! Save me, O my God! Strike all my enemies on the jaw; break the teeth of the wicked.

Mundart und Stil
