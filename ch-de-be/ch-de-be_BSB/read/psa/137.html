BSB 137:1 By the rivers of Babylon we sat and wept when we remembered Zion.
2 A d Weide dert hei mer üsi Zither ghänkt.
עַֽל־עֲרָבִ֥ים בְּתוֹכָ֑הּ תָּ֝לִ֗ינוּ כִּנֹּרוֹתֵֽינוּ׃
BSB 137:2 There on the willows we hung our harps,
עֲרָבִים → Weide (willows (poplars))
3 Denn dert hei die, wo üüs gfange gno hei, vo üüs es Lied verlangt, und die, wo üüs plaget hei, Fröid: „Singet üüs es Lied vo Zion!“
כִּ֤י שָׁ֨ם שְֽׁאֵל֪וּנוּ שׁוֹבֵ֡ינוּ דִּבְרֵי־שִׁ֭יר וְתוֹלָלֵ֣ינוּ שִׂמְחָ֑ה שִׁ֥ירוּ לָ֝֗נוּ מִשִּׁ֥יר צִיּֽוֹן׃
BSB 137:3 for there our captors requested a song; our tormentors demanded songs of joy: “Sing us a song of Zion.”
וְתוֹלָלֵינוּ שִׂמְחָה → und die, wo üüs plaget hei, Fröid (our tormentors … mirth)
4 Wi sölle mer ds Lied vom HERR singe i eme frömde Land?
אֵ֗יךְ נָשִׁ֥יר אֶת־שִׁיר־יְהוָ֑ה עַ֝֗ל אַדְמַ֥ת נֵכָֽר׃
BSB 137:4 How can we sing a song of the LORD in a foreign land?
5 Wen i di vergisse, Jerusalem, de söll my rächti Hand vergässe, was si cha!
אִֽם־אֶשְׁכָּחֵ֥ךְ יְֽרוּשָׁלִָ֗ם תִּשְׁכַּ֥ח יְמִינִֽי׃
BSB 137:5 If I forget you, O Jerusalem, may my right hand cease to function.
תִּשְׁכַּח יְמִינִי → söll my rächti Hand vergässe, was si cha (may my right hand forget its skill)

6 My Zunge söll mer obe im Muul blybe hange, wen i nid a di dänke, wen i nid Jerusalem über alli myni Fröid stelle!
תִּדְבַּ֥ק־לְשׁוֹנִ֨י׀לְחִכִּי֮ אִם־לֹ֪א אֶ֫זְכְּרֵ֥כִי אִם־לֹ֣א אַ֭עֲלֶה אֶת־יְרוּשָׁלִַ֑ם עַ֝֗ל רֹ֣אשׁ שִׂמְחָתִֽי׃
BSB 137:6 May my tongue cling to the roof of my mouth if I do not remember you, if I do not exalt Jerusalem as my greatest joy!
7 Dänk, HERR, a d Söhn vo Edom, a e Tag vo Jerusalem, wo si gseit hei: „Rysset se ab, rysset se ab bis uf e Grund!“
זְכֹ֤ר יְהוָ֨ה׀לִבְנֵ֬י אֱד֗וֹם אֵת֮ י֤וֹם יְֽרוּשָׁ֫לִָ֥ם הָ֭אֹ֣מְרִים עָ֤רוּ׀עָ֑רוּ עַ֝֗ד הַיְס֥וֹד בָּֽהּ׃
BSB 137:7 Remember, O LORD, the sons of Edom on the day Jerusalem fell: “Destroy it,” they said, “tear it down to its foundations!”
עָרוּ עָרוּ → Rysset se ab, rysset se ab (Tear it down)
8 Tochter Babylon, du, wo verwüeschtet wirsch! Glücklech isch dä, wo dir vergilt, was du üüs aata hesch!
בַּת־בָּבֶ֗ל הַשְּׁד֫וּדָ֥ה אַשְׁרֵ֥י שֶׁיְשַׁלֶּם־לָ֑ךְ אֶת־גְּ֝מוּלֵ֗ךְ שֶׁגָּמַ֥לְתְּ לָֽנוּ׃
BSB 137:8 O Daughter of Babylon, doomed to destruction, blessed is he who repays you as you have done to us.
הַשְּׁדוּדָה → du, wo verwüeschtet wirsch (O daughter … doomed to destruction)
9 Glücklech isch dä, wo dyni chlyne Chinder packt und se a de Felse verschlat!
אַשְׁרֵ֤י׀שֶׁיֹּאחֵ֓ז וְנִפֵּ֬ץ אֶֽת־עֹ֝לָלַ֗יִךְ אֶל־הַסָּֽלַע׃
BSB 137:9 Blessed is he who seizes your infants and dashes them against the rocks.
