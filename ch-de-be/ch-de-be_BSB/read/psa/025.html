1 Vom David. Zu dir, HERR, lüpf i my Seel.
לְדָוִ֡דאֵלֶ֥יךָ יְ֝הוָ֗ה נַפְשִׁ֥י אֶשָּֽׂא׃
BSB 25:1 Of David. To You, O LORD, I lift up my soul;
נַפְשִׁי אֶשָּׂא → lüpf i my Seel (I lift up my soul)
2 My Gott, uf di vertrouen i; la mi nid z Schande wärde, la myni Finde nid über mi juble!
אֱֽלֹהַ֗י בְּךָ֣ בָ֭טַחְתִּי אַל־אֵב֑וֹשָׁה אַל־יַֽעַלְצ֖וּ אֹיְבַ֣י לִֽי׃
BSB 25:2 in You, my God, I trust. Do not let me be put to shame; do not let my enemies exult over me.
אַל־אֵבוֹשָׁה → La mi nid z Schande wärde (do not let me be put to shame)
Fest (`formula:al_evosha`).
3 Nei, ke einzige, wo uf di wartet, wird z Schande, z Schande wärde die, wo ohni Grund untröi sy.
גַּ֣ם כָּל־קֹ֭וֶיךָ לֹ֣א יֵבֹ֑שׁוּ יֵ֝בֹ֗שׁוּ הַבּוֹגְדִ֥ים רֵיקָֽם׃
BSB 25:3 Surely none who wait for You will be put to shame; but those who engage in treachery without cause will be disgraced.
הַבּוֹגְדִים רֵיקָם → die, wo ohni Grund untröi sy (the treacherous without cause)
4 Zeig mer dyni Wääge, HERR, lehr mi, wi me uf dyne Wääge geit!
דְּרָכֶ֣יךָ יְ֭הוָה הוֹדִיעֵ֑נִי אֹ֖רְחוֹתֶ֣יךָלַמְּדֵֽנִי׃
BSB 25:4 Show me Your ways, O LORD; teach me Your paths.
5 Füer mi i dyr Tröiji und lehr mi, denn du bisch der Gott vo myr Rettig; i warte uf di der ganz Tag.
הַדְרִ֘יכֵ֤נִי בַאֲמִתֶּ֨ךָ׀וְֽלַמְּדֵ֗נִי כִּֽי־אַ֭תָּה אֱלֹהֵ֣י יִשְׁעִ֑י אוֹתְךָ֥ קִ֝וִּ֗יתִי כָּל־הַיּֽוֹם׃
BSB 25:5 Guide me in Your truth and teach me, for You are the God of my salvation; all day long I wait for You.
6 Dänk a dys Erbarme, HERR, und a dyni Tröiji, denn die sy scho vo Ewigkeit här.
זְכֹר־רַחֲמֶ֣יךָ יְ֭הוָה וַחֲסָדֶ֑יךָ כִּ֖י מֵעוֹלָ֣ם הֵֽמָּה׃
BSB 25:6 Remember, O LORD, Your compassion and loving devotion, for they are from age to age.
7 A d Sünde, won i jung bi gsi, und a mys Unrächt dänk nid; dänk a mi nach dyr Tröiji, du, wil du guet bisch, HERR!
חַטֹּ֤אות נְעוּרַ֨י׀וּפְשָׁעַ֗י אַל־תִּ֫זְכֹּ֥ר כְּחַסְדְּךָ֥ זְכָר־לִי־אַ֑תָּה לְמַ֖עַן טוּבְךָ֣ יְהוָֽה׃
BSB 25:7 Remember not the sins of my youth, nor my rebellious acts; remember me according to Your loving devotion, because of Your goodness, O LORD.
חַטֹּאות נְעוּרַי → d Sünde, won i jung bi gsi (the sins of my youth)
8 Der HERR isch guet und grad; drum zeigt er de Sünder der Wäg.
טוֹב־וְיָשָׁ֥ר יְהוָ֑ה עַל־כֵּ֤ן יוֹרֶ֖ה חַטָּאִ֣ים בַּדָּֽרֶךְ׃
BSB 25:8 Good and upright is the LORD; therefore He shows sinners the way.
9 Er füert di Demüetige nach em Rächt und lehrt di Demüetige sy Wäg.
יַדְרֵ֣ךְ עֲ֭נָוִים בַּמִּשְׁפָּ֑ט וִֽילַמֵּ֖ד עֲנָוִ֣ים דַּרְכּֽוֹ׃
BSB 25:9 He guides the humble in what is right and teaches them His way.
10 Alli Wääge vom HERR sy Tröiji und Wahrheit für die, wo sy Bund und syni Zügnis halte.
כָּל־אָרְח֣וֹת יְ֭הוָה חֶ֣סֶד וֶאֱמֶ֑ת לְנֹצְרֵ֥י בְ֝רִית֗וֹ וְעֵדֹתָֽיו׃
BSB 25:10 All the LORD’s ways are loving and faithful to those who keep His covenant and His decrees.
חֶסֶד וֶאֱמֶת → Tröiji und Wahrheit (loving devotion and faithfulness)
Paar fest für den Psalter (`formula:hesed_emet`).
11 Um dys Name wille, HERR, vergib mer my Schuld, denn si isch gross!
לְמַֽעַן־שִׁמְךָ֥ יְהוָ֑ה וְֽסָלַחְתָּ֥ לַ֝עֲוֺנִ֗י כִּ֣י רַב־הֽוּא׃
BSB 25:11 For the sake of Your name, O LORD, forgive my iniquity, for it is great.
12 Wär isch es, dä Mönsch, wo Ehrfurcht vor em HERR het? Däm zeigt er der Wäg, won er söll wähle.
מִי־זֶ֣ה הָ֭אִישׁ יְרֵ֣א יְהוָ֑ה י֝וֹרֶ֗נּוּ בְּדֶ֣רֶךְ יִבְחָֽר׃
BSB 25:12 Who is the man who fears the LORD? He will instruct him in the path chosen for him.
13 Er sälber blybt im Glück, und syni Nachkomme erbe ds Land.
נַ֭פְשׁוֹ בְּט֣וֹב תָּלִ֑ין וְ֝זַרְע֗וֹ יִ֣ירַשׁ אָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 25:13 His soul will dwell in prosperity, and his descendants will inherit the land.
14 Der HERR isch e Fründ vo dene, wo ne förchte, und sy Bund tuet er ne z wüsse.
ס֣וֹד יְ֭הוָה לִירֵאָ֑יו וּ֝בְרִית֗וֹ לְהוֹדִיעָֽם׃
BSB 25:14 The LORD confides in those who fear Him, and reveals His covenant to them.
סוֹד יְהוָה → Der HERR isch e Fründ (The LORD confides)
סוֹד »vertraute Gemeinschaft«.
15 Myni Ouge luege geng zum HERR, denn är zieht myni Füess us em Netz.
עֵינַ֣י תָּ֭מִיד אֶל־יְהוָ֑ה כִּ֤י הֽוּא־יוֹצִ֖יא מֵרֶ֣שֶׁת רַגְלָֽי׃
BSB 25:15 My eyes are always on the LORD, for He will free my feet from the mesh.
16 Chehr di zu mir und häb Erbarme mit mer, denn i bi alleini und arm.
פְּנֵה־אֵלַ֥י וְחָנֵּ֑נִי כִּֽי־יָחִ֖יד וְעָנִ֣י אָֽנִי׃
BSB 25:16 Turn to me and be gracious, for I am lonely and afflicted.
17 D Not i mym Härz isch gross worde; füer mi use us myne Sorge!
צָר֣וֹת לְבָבִ֣י הִרְחִ֑יבוּ מִ֝מְּצֽוּקוֹתַ֗י הוֹצִיאֵֽנִי׃
BSB 25:17 The troubles of my heart increase; free me from my distress.
18 Lueg my Not und my Müei aa, und vergib mer all myni Sünde!
רְאֵ֣ה עָ֭נְיִי וַעֲמָלִ֑י וְ֝שָׂ֗א לְכָל־חַטֹּאותָֽי׃
BSB 25:18 Consider my affliction and trouble, and take away all my sins.
19 Lueg myni Finde aa, wi vil dass es sy, und wi si mi mit böser Gwalt hasse!
רְאֵֽה־אוֹיְבַ֥י כִּי־רָ֑בּוּ וְשִׂנְאַ֖ת חָמָ֣ס שְׂנֵאֽוּנִי׃
BSB 25:19 Consider my enemies, for they are many, and they hate me with vicious hatred.
20 Bhüet mys Läbe und rett mi! La mi nid z Schande wärde, denn bi dir suechen i Zueflucht.
שָׁמְרָ֣ה נַ֭פְשִׁי וְהַצִּילֵ֑נִי אַל־אֵ֝ב֗וֹשׁ כִּֽי־חָסִ֥יתִי בָֽךְ׃
BSB 25:20 Guard my soul and deliver me; let me not be put to shame, for I take refuge in You.
אַל־אֵבוֹשָׁה → La mi nid z Schande wärde (do not let me be put to shame)
Fest (`formula:al_evosha`).
21 Was ufrichtig und grad isch, söll mi bhüete, denn i warte uf di.
תֹּם־וָיֹ֥שֶׁר יִצְּר֑וּנִי כִּ֝֗י קִוִּיתִֽיךָ׃
BSB 25:21 May integrity and uprightness preserve me, because I wait for You.
תֹּם־וָיֹשֶׁר → Was ufrichtig und grad isch (integrity and uprightness)
22 Befrei, Gott, Israel us all syne Nöt!
פְּדֵ֣ה אֱ֭לֹהִים אֶת־יִשְׂרָאֵ֑ל מִ֝כֹּ֗ל צָֽרוֹתָיו׃
BSB 25:22 Redeem Israel, O God, from all its distress.
