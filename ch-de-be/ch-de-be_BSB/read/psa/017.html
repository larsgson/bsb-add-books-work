1 Es Gebätt vom David. Los, HERR, uf my grächti Sach, gib acht uf mys Rüefe! Los uf mys Gebätt; es chunt nid us falsche Lippe.
תְּפִלָּ֗ה לְדָ֫וִ֥דשִׁמְעָ֤ה יְהוָ֨ה׀צֶ֗דֶק הַקְשִׁ֥יבָה רִנָּתִ֗י הַאֲזִ֥ינָה תְפִלָּתִ֑י בְּ֝לֹ֗א שִׂפְתֵ֥י מִרְמָֽה׃
BSB 17:1 A prayer of David. Hear, O LORD, my righteous plea; listen to my cry. Give ear to my prayer— it comes from lips free of deceit.
תְּפִלָּה לְדָוִד → Es Gebätt vom David (A prayer of David)
Überschrift (Ps 86; 90; 102; 142 entsprechend).
צֶדֶק → my grächti Sach (my righteous plea)
2 Vo dir söll mys Rächt cho, dyni Ouge sölle gseh, was rächt isch!
מִ֭לְּפָנֶיךָ מִשְׁפָּטִ֣י יֵצֵ֑א עֵ֝ינֶ֗יךָ תֶּחֱזֶ֥ינָה מֵישָׁרִֽים׃
BSB 17:2 May my vindication come from Your presence; may Your eyes see what is right.
3 Du hesch mys Härz prüeft, du hesch mi znacht bsuecht, du hesch mi uf d Prob gstellt und nüüt gfunde; i ha mer vorgno, dass mys Muul nid sündiget.
בָּ֘חַ֤נְתָּ לִבִּ֨י׀פָּ֘קַ֤דְתָּ לַּ֗יְלָה צְרַפְתַּ֥נִי בַל־תִּמְצָ֑א זַ֝מֹּתִ֗י בַּל־יַעֲבָר־פִּֽי׃
BSB 17:3 You have tried my heart; You have visited me in the night. You have tested me and found no evil; I have resolved not to sin with my mouth.
צְרַפְתַּנִי → uf d Prob gstellt (tested me)
Eigentlich »geläutert«.
4 Was d Mönsche mache – i ha mi a ds Wort vo dyne Lippe ghalte und bi de Wääge vo dene, wo Gwalt bruuche, nid nache.
לִפְעֻלּ֣וֹת אָ֭דָם בִּדְבַ֣ר שְׂפָתֶ֑יךָ אֲנִ֥י שָׁ֝מַ֗רְתִּי אָרְח֥וֹת פָּרִֽיץ׃
BSB 17:4 As for the deeds of men — by the word of Your lips I have avoided the ways of the violent.
פָּרִיץ → dene, wo Gwalt bruuche (the violent)
»Gwalttätig-« nicht belegt.
5 Myni Schritte sy fescht uf dyne Wääge bblibe, myni Füess sy nid is Wanke cho.
תָּמֹ֣ךְ אֲ֭שֻׁרַי בְּמַעְגְּלוֹתֶ֑יךָ בַּל־נָמ֥וֹטּוּ פְעָמָֽי׃
BSB 17:5 My steps have held to Your paths; my feet have not slipped.
6 Ig rüefe di aa, denn du gisch mer Antwort, Gott! Lose mer zue mit dym Ohr, ghör myni Wort!
אֲנִֽי־קְרָאתִ֣יךָ כִֽי־תַעֲנֵ֣נִי אֵ֑ל הַֽט־אָזְנְךָ֥ לִ֝֗י שְׁמַ֣ע אִמְרָתִֽי׃
BSB 17:6 I call on You, O God, for You will answer me. Incline Your ear to me; hear my words.
7 Zeig, wi wunderbar dy Tröiji isch, du, wo dene hilfsch, wo Zueflucht sueche vor dene, wo sech gäge dyni rächti Hand sech wehre!
הַפְלֵ֣ה חֲ֭סָדֶיךָ מוֹשִׁ֣יעַ חוֹסִ֑ים מִ֝מִּתְקוֹמְמִ֗ים בִּֽימִינֶֽךָ׃
BSB 17:7 Show the wonders of Your loving devotion, You who save by Your right hand those who seek refuge from their foes.
הַפְלֵה חֲסָדֶיךָ → Zeig, wi wunderbar dy Tröiji isch (Show the wonders of Your loving devotion)
8 Bhüet mi wi dys eigete Oug; versteck mi im Schatte vo dyne Flügel
שָׁ֭מְרֵנִי כְּאִישׁ֣וֹן בַּת־עָ֑יִן בְּצֵ֥ל כְּ֝נָפֶ֗יךָ תַּסְתִּירֵֽנִי׃
BSB 17:8 Keep me as the apple of Your eye; hide me in the shadow of Your wings
כְּאִישׁוֹן בַּת־עָיִן → wi dys eigete Oug (apple of Your eye)
Store.
9 vor de Gottlose, wo mir Gwalt aatüe, vor myne Finde, wo mer a ds Läbe wei und mi umgä.
מִפְּנֵ֣י רְ֭שָׁעִים ז֣וּ שַׁדּ֑וּנִי אֹיְבַ֥י בְּ֝נֶ֗פֶשׁ יַקִּ֥יפוּ עָלָֽי׃
BSB 17:9 from the wicked who assail me, from my mortal enemies who surround me.
10 Iri Härz hei si zuegha, ir Muul redt hochmüetig.
חֶלְבָּ֥מוֹ סָּגְר֑וּ פִּ֝֗ימוֹ דִּבְּר֥וּ בְגֵאֽוּת׃
BSB 17:10 They have closed their callous hearts; their mouths speak with arrogance.
חֶלְבָּמוֹ סָּגְרוּ → Iri Härz hei si zuegha (closed their callous hearts)
Wörtlich »ihr Fett haben sie verschlossen«.
11 Si hei myni Schritte verfolget; jitz umgä si mi, iri Ouge luege nume druf, für mi z Bode z wärfe.
אַ֭שֻּׁרֵינוּ עַתָּ֣ה סְבָב֑וּנוּ עֵינֵיהֶ֥ם יָ֝שִׁ֗יתוּ לִנְט֥וֹת בָּאָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 17:11 They have tracked us down, and now surround us; their eyes are set to cast us to the ground,
12 Si sy wi ne Löi, wo druf brönnt, öppis z verrisse, und wi ne junge Löi, wo im Versteck wartet.
דִּמְיֹנ֗וֹ כְּ֭אַרְיֵה יִכְס֣וֹף לִטְר֑וֹף וְ֝כִכְפִ֗יר יֹשֵׁ֥ב בְּמִסְתָּרִֽים׃
BSB 17:12 like a lion greedy for prey, like a young lion lurking in ambush.
13 Stand uf, HERR! Stell di ne i Wäg, wirf ne z Bode! Rett mys Läbe vor em Gottlose mit dym Schwärt,
קוּמָ֤ה יְהוָ֗ה קַדְּמָ֣ה פָ֭נָיו הַכְרִיעֵ֑הוּ פַּלְּטָ֥ה נַ֝פְשִׁ֗י מֵרָשָׁ֥ע חַרְבֶּֽךָ׃
BSB 17:13 Arise, O LORD, confront them! Bring them to their knees; deliver me from the wicked by Your sword,
14 vor de Mönsche, HERR, mit dyr Hand, vor de Mönsche vo dere Wält, wo ire Teil im Läbe hei! Mit däm, wo du parat hesch, machsch du ire Buuch voll, iri Chinder wärde satt und lö, was übrig blybt, irne Chinder zrügg.
מִֽמְתִ֥ים יָדְךָ֨׀יְהוָ֡ה מִֽמְתִ֬ים מֵחֶ֗לֶד חֶלְקָ֥ם בַּֽחַיִּים֮ וּֽצְפוּנְךָ֮ תְּמַלֵּ֪א בִ֫טְנָ֥ם יִשְׂבְּע֥וּ בָנִ֑ים וְהִנִּ֥יחוּ יִ֝תְרָ֗ם לְעוֹלְלֵיהֶֽם׃
BSB 17:14 from such men, O LORD, by Your hand— from men of the world whose portion is in this life. May You fill the bellies of Your treasured ones and satisfy their sons, so they leave their abundance to their children.
צְפוּנְךָ → däm, wo du parat hesch (Your treasured ones)
Text und Deutung schwierig: Strafe für die Gottlosen (so hier) oder Segen für die Frommen (BSB).
15 Aber ig wott i Grächtigkeit dys Gsicht gseh; i wott satt wärde, wen i erwache, a dyr Gstalt.
אֲנִ֗י בְּ֭צֶדֶק אֶחֱזֶ֣ה פָנֶ֑יךָ אֶשְׂבְּעָ֥ה בְ֝הָקִ֗יץ תְּמוּנָתֶֽךָ׃
BSB 17:15 As for me, I will behold Your face in righteousness; when I awake, I will be satisfied in Your presence.
בְּהָקִיץ → wen i erwache (when I awake)
