2 I ha uf e HERR gwartet und gwartet, und er het sech zu mir abe bückt und het mys Rüefe ghört.
קַוֺּ֣ה קִוִּ֣יתִי יְהוָ֑ה וַיֵּ֥ט אֵ֝לַ֗י וַיִּשְׁמַ֥ע שַׁוְעָתִֽי׃
BSB 40:1 For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. I waited patiently for the LORD; He inclined to me and heard my cry.
קַוֹּה קִוִּיתִי → I ha … gwartet und gwartet (I waited patiently)
Figura etymologica.
3 Er het mi us der Grueb vom Verderbe ufe zoge, us em Dräck und Lätt, er het myni Füess uf e Felse gstellt und myni Schritte fescht gmacht.
וַיַּעֲלֵ֤נִי׀מִבּ֥וֹר שָׁאוֹן֮ מִטִּ֪יט הַיָּ֫וֵ֥ן וַיָּ֖קֶם עַל־סֶ֥לַע רַגְלַ֗י כּוֹנֵ֥ן אֲשֻׁרָֽי׃
BSB 40:2 He lifted me up from the pit of despair, out of the miry clay; He set my feet upon a rock, and made my footsteps firm.
מִטִּיט הַיָּוֵן → us em Dräck und Lätt (out of the miry clay)
4 Er het mer es nöis Lied i ds Muul gleit, es Loblied für üse Gott. Vili gseh's und förchte sech und vertroue uf e HERR.
וַיִּתֵּ֬ן בְּפִ֨י׀שִׁ֥יר חָדָשׁ֮ תְּהִלָּ֪ה לֵֽאלֹ֫הֵ֥ינוּ יִרְא֣וּ רַבִּ֣ים וְיִירָ֑אוּ וְ֝יִבְטְח֗וּ בַּיהוָֽה׃
BSB 40:3 He put a new song in my mouth, a hymn of praise to our God. Many will see and fear and put their trust in the LORD.
5 Glücklech isch dä Ma, wo sys Vertroue uf e HERR setzt und sech nid a die, wo stolz sy, haltet und a die, wo dene Lugine nacheloufe!
אַ֥שְֽׁרֵי הַגֶּ֗בֶר אֲשֶׁר־שָׂ֣ם יְ֭הֹוָה מִבְטַח֑וֹ וְֽלֹא־פָנָ֥ה אֶל־רְ֝הָבִ֗ים וְשָׂטֵ֥י כָזָֽב׃
BSB 40:4 Blessed is the man who has made the LORD his trust, who has not turned to the proud, nor to those who lapse into falsehood.
רְהָבִים … שָׂטֵי כָזָב → die, wo stolz sy … die, wo dene Lugine nacheloufe (the proud … those who run after lies)
6 Vil hesch du gmacht, HERR, my Gott, dyni Wunder und dyni Gedanke für üüs – niemer isch wi du! Wett i se verchünte und dervo rede, es wäre z vil, für se z zelle.
רַבּ֤וֹת עָשִׂ֨יתָ׀אַתָּ֤ה׀יְהוָ֣ה אֱלֹהַי֮ נִֽפְלְאֹתֶ֥יךָ וּמַחְשְׁבֹתֶ֗יךָ אֵ֫לֵ֥ינוּ אֵ֤ין׀עֲרֹ֬ךְ אֵלֶ֗יךָ אַגִּ֥ידָה וַאֲדַבֵּ֑רָה עָ֝צְמ֗וּ מִסַּפֵּֽר׃
BSB 40:5 Many, O LORD my God, are the wonders You have done, and the plans You have for us — none can compare to You — if I proclaim and declare them, they are more than I can count.
7 Opfer und Gabe hesch du nid welle – Ohre hesch du mer ufta –, Brandopfer und Sündopfer hesch du nid verlangt.
זֶ֤בַח וּמִנְחָ֨ה׀לֹֽא־חָפַ֗צְתָּ אָ֭זְנַיִם כָּרִ֣יתָ לִּ֑י עוֹלָ֥ה וַ֝חֲטָאָ֗ה לֹ֣א שָׁאָֽלְתָּ׃
BSB 40:6 Sacrifice and offering You did not desire, but my ears You have opened. Burnt offerings and sin offerings You did not require.
אָזְנַיִם כָּרִיתָ לִּי → Ohre hesch du mer ufta (You have opened my ears)
Fussnote zu Hebr 10,5.
8 Da han i gseit: „Lue, i chume! I der Buechrolle isch vo mir gschribe.
אָ֣ז אָ֭מַרְתִּי הִנֵּה־בָ֑אתִי בִּמְגִלַּת־סֵ֝֗פֶר כָּת֥וּב עָלָֽי׃
BSB 40:7 Then I said, “Here I am, I have come — it is written about me in the scroll:
9 Dy Wille z erfülle, my Gott, isch my Fröid, und dys Gsetz isch i mym Innerschte.“
לַֽעֲשֽׂוֹת־רְצוֹנְךָ֣ אֱלֹהַ֣י חָפָ֑צְתִּי וְ֝ת֥וֹרָתְךָ֗בְּת֣וֹךְ מֵעָֽי׃
BSB 40:8 I delight to do Your will, O my God; Your law is within my heart.”
10 I ha Grächtigkeit verchündet i der grosse Gmeind; lue, myni Lippe han i nid gschlosse, HERR, du weisch es.
בִּשַּׂ֤רְתִּי צֶ֨דֶק׀בְּקָ֘הָ֤ל רָ֗ב הִנֵּ֣ה שְׂ֭פָתַי לֹ֣א אֶכְלָ֑א יְ֝הוָ֗ה אַתָּ֥ה יָדָֽעְתָּ׃
BSB 40:9 I proclaim righteousness in the great assembly; behold, I do not seal my lips, as You, O LORD, do know.
11 Dy Grächtigkeit han i nid versteckt i mym Härz; vo dyr Zueverlässigkeit und vo dyr Rettig han i gredt; i ha dy Tröiji und dy Wahrheit nid verschwige vor der grosse Gmeind.
צִדְקָתְךָ֬ לֹא־כִסִּ֨יתִי׀בְּת֬וֹךְ לִבִּ֗י אֱמוּנָתְךָ֣ וּתְשׁוּעָתְךָ֣ אָמָ֑רְתִּי לֹא־כִחַ֥דְתִּי חַסְדְּךָ֥ וַ֝אֲמִתְּךָ֗ לְקָהָ֥ל רָֽב׃
BSB 40:10 I have not covered up Your righteousness in my heart; I have declared Your faithfulness and salvation; I have not concealed Your loving devotion and faithfulness from the great assembly.
חַסְדְּךָ וַאֲמִתְּךָ → dy Tröiji und dy Wahrheit (Your loving devotion and faithfulness)
Fest (`formula:hesed_emet`).
12 Du, HERR, du hesch mer dys Erbarme nid abgschlage; dy Tröiji und dy Wahrheit bhüete mi geng.
אַתָּ֤ה יְהוָ֗ה לֹא־תִכְלָ֣א רַחֲמֶ֣יךָ מִמֶּ֑נִּי חַסְדְּךָ֥ וַ֝אֲמִתְּךָ֗ תָּמִ֥יד יִצְּרֽוּנִי׃
BSB 40:11 O LORD, do not withhold Your mercy from me; Your loving devotion and faithfulness will always guard me.
13 Denn Böses ohni Zal het mi umgä, myni Sünde hei mi packt, und i cha nid meh gseh. Es sy meh als Haar uf mym Chopf, und mys Härz het mi verla.
כִּ֤י אָפְפ֥וּ־עָלַ֨י׀רָע֡וֹת עַד־אֵ֬ין מִסְפָּ֗ר הִשִּׂיג֣וּנִי עֲ֭וֺנֹתַי וְלֹא־יָכֹ֣לְתִּי לִרְא֑וֹת עָצְמ֥וּ מִשַּֽׂעֲר֥וֹת רֹ֝אשִׁ֗י וְלִבִּ֥י עֲזָבָֽנִי׃
BSB 40:12 For evils without number surround me; my sins have overtaken me, so that I cannot see. They are more than the hairs of my head, and my heart has failed within me.
14 Lass es dir gfalle, HERR, mi z rette! HERR, chumm mer gschwind z Hilf!
רְצֵ֣ה יְ֭הוָה לְהַצִּילֵ֑נִי יְ֝הוָ֗ה לְעֶזְרָ֥תִי חֽוּשָׁה׃
BSB 40:13 Be pleased, O LORD, to deliver me; hurry, O LORD, to help me.
15 Si sölle sech schäme und alli zäme z Schande wärde, die, wo mer nach em Läbe sy, für mer's z näh! Si sölle zrügg müesse und z Schande wärde, die, wo mer Böses wei!
יֵ֘בֹ֤שׁוּ וְיַחְפְּר֨וּ׀יַחַד֮ מְבַקְשֵׁ֥י נַפְשִׁ֗י לִסְפּ֫וֹתָ֥הּ יִסֹּ֣גוּ אָ֭חוֹר וְיִכָּלְמ֑וּ חֲ֝פֵצֵ֗י רָעָתִֽי׃
BSB 40:14 May those who seek my life be ashamed and confounded; may those who wish me harm be repelled and humiliated.
16 Si sölle erchlüpfe wäge irer Schand, die, wo zu mir säge: „Aha, aha!“
יָ֭שֹׁמּוּ עַל־עֵ֣קֶב בָּשְׁתָּ֑ם הָאֹמְרִ֥ים לִ֝֗י הֶ֘אָ֥ח׀הֶאָֽח׃
BSB 40:15 May those who say to me, “Aha, aha!” be appalled at their own shame.
יָשֹׁמּוּ → Si sölle erchlüpfe (Let them be appalled)
17 Juble und sech fröie a dir sölle alli, wo di sueche; die, wo dy Rettig gärn hei, sölle geng säge: „Der HERR isch gross!“
יָ֘שִׂ֤ישׂוּ וְיִשְׂמְח֨וּ׀בְּךָ֗ כָּֽל־מְבַ֫קְשֶׁ֥יךָ יֹאמְר֣וּ תָ֭מִיד יִגְדַּ֣ל יְהוָ֑ה אֹֽ֝הֲבֵ֗י תְּשׁוּעָתֶֽךָ׃
BSB 40:16 May all who seek You rejoice and be glad in You; may those who love Your salvation always say, “The LORD be magnified!”
18 Aber ig bi arm und bedürftig; der Herr dänkt a mi. Du bisch my Hilf und my Retter; my Gott, warte nid z lang!
וַאֲנִ֤י׀עָנִ֣י וְאֶבְיוֹן֮ אֲדֹנָ֪י יַחֲשָׁ֫ב לִ֥י עֶזְרָתִ֣י וּמְפַלְטִ֣י אַ֑תָּה אֱ֝לֹהַ֗י אַל־תְּאַחַֽר׃
BSB 40:17 But I am poor and needy; may the Lord think of me. You are my helper and deliverer; O my God, do not delay.
עָנִי וְאֶבְיוֹן → arm und bedürftig (poor and needy)
