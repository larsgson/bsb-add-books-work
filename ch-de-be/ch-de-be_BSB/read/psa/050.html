BSB 50:1 A Psalm of Asaph. The Mighty One, God the LORD, speaks and summons the earth from where the sun rises to where it sets.
אֵל אֱלֹהִים יְהוָה → Der Mächtig, Gott, der HERR (The Mighty One, God, the LORD)
2 Vom Zion us, wo vollkomme schön isch, lüüchtet Gott uuf.
מִצִּיּ֥וֹן מִכְלַל־יֹ֗פִי אֱלֹהִ֥ים הוֹפִֽיעַ׃
BSB 50:2 From Zion, perfect in beauty, God shines forth.
מִכְלַל־יֹפִי → wo vollkomme schön isch (the perfection of beauty)
3 Üse Gott chunt und isch nid still; vor ihm här frisst es Füür, und rund um ihn isch e gwaltige Sturm.
יָ֤בֹ֥א אֱלֹהֵ֗ינוּ וְֽאַל־יֶ֫חֱרַ֥שׁ אֵשׁ־לְפָנָ֥יו תֹּאכֵ֑ל וּ֝סְבִיבָ֗יו נִשְׂעֲרָ֥ה מְאֹֽד׃
BSB 50:3 Our God approaches and will not be silent! Consuming fire precedes Him, and a tempest rages around Him.
4 Er rüeft em Himel obe und der Ärde zue, für über sys Volk Gricht z halte:
יִקְרָ֣א אֶל־הַשָּׁמַ֣יִם מֵעָ֑ל וְאֶל־הָ֝אָ֗רֶץ לָדִ֥ין עַמּֽוֹ׃
BSB 50:4 He summons the heavens above, and the earth, that He may judge His people:
5 „Bringet mer myni Tröie zäme, die, wo mit mir e Bund gschlosse hei bim Opfer!“
אִסְפוּ־לִ֥י חֲסִידָ֑י כֹּרְתֵ֖י בְרִיתִ֣י עֲלֵי־זָֽבַח׃
BSB 50:5 “Gather to Me My saints, who made a covenant with Me by sacrifice.”
חֲסִידָי → myni Tröie (My saints)
Wie Ps 4; 16 usw.
6 D Himel mache sy Grächtigkeit bekannt, denn Gott sälber isch Richter. Sela
וַיַּגִּ֣ידוּ שָׁמַ֣יִם צִדְק֑וֹ כִּֽי־אֱלֹהִ֓ים׀שֹׁפֵ֖ט ה֣וּא סֶֽלָה׃
BSB 50:6 And the heavens proclaim His righteousness, for God Himself is Judge. Selah
7 „Los, mys Volk, i wott rede, Israel, i wott gäge di bezüge: Gott, dy Gott, bin ig.
שִׁמְעָ֤ה עַמִּ֨י׀וַאֲדַבֵּ֗רָה יִ֭שְׂרָאֵל וְאָעִ֣ידָה בָּ֑ךְ אֱלֹהִ֖ים אֱלֹהֶ֣יךָ אָנֹֽכִי׃
BSB 50:7 “Hear, O My people, and I will speak, O Israel, and I will testify against you: I am God, your God.
8 Nid wäge dyne Opfer straf i di, dyni Brandopfer sy ja geng vor mir.
לֹ֣א עַל־זְ֭בָחֶיךָ אוֹכִיחֶ֑ךָ וְעוֹלֹתֶ֖יךָ לְנֶגְדִּ֣י תָמִֽיד׃
BSB 50:8 I do not rebuke you for your sacrifices, and your burnt offerings are ever before Me.
9 I nime ke Stier us dym Huus, ke Geisse us dym Hof.
לֹא־אֶקַּ֣ח מִבֵּיתְךָ֣ פָ֑ר מִ֝מִּכְלְאֹתֶ֗יךָ עַתּוּדִֽים׃
BSB 50:9 I have no need for a bull from your stall or goats from your pens,
10 Denn mir ghöre all Tier vom Wald, ds Veh uf de tuusig Bärge.
כִּי־לִ֥י כָל־חַיְתוֹ־יָ֑עַר בְּ֝הֵמ֗וֹת בְּהַרְרֵי־אָֽלֶף׃
BSB 50:10 for every beast of the forest is Mine — the cattle on a thousand hills.
בְּהַרְרֵי־אָלֶף → uf de tuusig Bärge (on a thousand hills)
11 All Vögel vo de Bärge sy mer bekannt, und was sech uf em Fäld rüert, ghört mir.
יָ֭דַעְתִּי כָּל־ע֣וֹף הָרִ֑ים וְזִ֥יז שָׂ֝דַ֗י עִמָּדִֽי׃
BSB 50:11 I know every bird in the mountains, and the creatures of the field are Mine.
12 Wen i Hunger hätt, würd i's dir nid säge; denn mir ghört d Wält und alls, was drinn isch.
אִם־אֶ֭רְעַב לֹא־אֹ֣מַר לָ֑ךְ כִּי־לִ֥י תֵ֝בֵ֗ל וּמְלֹאָֽהּ׃
BSB 50:12 If I were hungry, I would not tell you, for the world is Mine, and the fullness thereof.
13 Ässe i öppe ds Fleisch vo Stiere, trinke i ds Bluet vo Geisse?
הַֽ֭אוֹכַל בְּשַׂ֣ר אַבִּירִ֑ים וְדַ֖ם עַתּוּדִ֣ים אֶשְׁתֶּֽה׃
BSB 50:13 Do I eat the flesh of bulls, or drink the blood of goats?
14 Bring Gott Dank als Opfer und tue em Höchschte erfülle, was du versproche hesch!
זְבַ֣ח לֵאלֹהִ֣ים תּוֹדָ֑ה וְשַׁלֵּ֖ם לְעֶלְי֣וֹן נְדָרֶֽיךָ׃
BSB 50:14 Sacrifice a thank offering to God, and fulfill your vows to the Most High.
זְבַח … תּוֹדָה → Bring Gott Dank als Opfer (Sacrifice a thank offering)
Auch V. 23.
15 Rüef mi aa am Tag vo der Not, de rett i di, und du wirsch mi ehre!“
וּ֭קְרָאֵנִי בְּי֣וֹם צָרָ֑ה אֲ֝חַלֶּצְךָ֗ וּֽתְכַבְּדֵֽנִי׃
BSB 50:15 Call upon Me in the day of trouble; I will deliver you, and you will honor Me.”
16 Aber zum Gottlose seit Gott: „Was hesch du myni Vorschrifte z verzelle und my Bund i dys Muul z näh?
וְלָ֤רָשָׁ֨ע׀אָ֘מַ֤ר אֱלֹהִ֗ים מַה־לְּ֭ךָ לְסַפֵּ֣ר חֻקָּ֑י וַתִּשָּׂ֖א בְרִיתִ֣י עֲלֵי־פִֽיךָ׃
BSB 50:16 To the wicked, however, God says, “What right have you to recite My statutes and to bear My covenant on your lips?
17 Du hassisch ja, wen me di zrächtwyse wott, und lasch myni Wort hinder dir lige.
וְ֭אַתָּה שָׂנֵ֣אתָ מוּסָ֑ר וַתַּשְׁלֵ֖ךְ דְּבָרַ֣י אַחֲרֶֽיךָ׃
BSB 50:17 For you hate My instruction and cast My words behind you.
מוּסָר → wen me di zrächtwyse wott (instruction)
18 Wen du ne Schelm gsehsch, de hesch du Fröid a ihm, und mit Ehebrächer hesch du di zämeta.
אִם־רָאִ֣יתָ גַ֭נָּב וַתִּ֣רֶץ עִמּ֑וֹ וְעִ֖ם מְנָאֲפִ֣ים חֶלְקֶֽךָ׃
BSB 50:18 When you see a thief, you befriend him, and throw in your lot with adulterers.
גַנָּב → e Schelm (a thief)
Korpuswort.
19 Dys Muul lasch du la rede für ds Böse, und dy Zunge dienet em Betrug.
פִּ֭יךָ שָׁלַ֣חְתָּ בְרָעָ֑ה וּ֝לְשׁוֹנְךָ֗ תַּצְמִ֥יד מִרְמָֽה׃
BSB 50:19 You unleash your mouth for evil and harness your tongue to deceit.
20 Du sitzisch da und redsch gäge dy Brueder, gäge der Suhn vo dyr eigete Mueter bringsch du schlächti Gschichte uf.
תֵּ֭שֵׁב בְּאָחִ֣יךָ תְדַבֵּ֑ר בְּבֶֽן־אִ֝מְּךָ֗ תִּתֶּן־דֹּֽפִי׃
BSB 50:20 You sit and malign your brother; you slander your own mother’s son.
21 Das hesch du gmacht, und i ha gschwige; du hesch gmeint, i syg wi du. I straf di und lege dir's vor d Ouge!
אֵ֤לֶּה עָשִׂ֨יתָ׀וְֽהֶחֱרַ֗שְׁתִּי דִּמִּ֗יתָ הֱֽיוֹת־אֶֽהְיֶ֥ה כָמ֑וֹךָ אוֹכִיחֲךָ֖ וְאֶֽעֶרְכָ֣ה לְעֵינֶֽיךָ׃
BSB 50:21 You have done these things, and I kept silent; you thought I was just like you. But now I rebuke you and accuse you to your face.
22 Merket doch das, dihr, wo Gott vergässe! Süsch rysse n i öich i Bitze, und niemer cha öich rette.
בִּֽינוּ־נָ֣א זֹ֭את שֹׁכְחֵ֣י אֱל֑וֹהַּ פֶּן־אֶ֝טְרֹ֗ף וְאֵ֣ין מַצִּֽיל׃
BSB 50:22 Now consider this, you who forget God, lest I tear you to pieces, with no one to rescue you:
אֶטְרֹף → rysse n i öich i Bitze (I tear you to pieces)
23 Wär Dank als Opfer bringt, dä tuet mi ehre; und wär uf sy Wäg achtet, däm zeige n i d Rettig vo Gott.“
זֹבֵ֥חַ תּוֹדָ֗ה יְֽכַ֫בְּדָ֥נְנִי וְשָׂ֥ם דֶּ֑רֶךְ אַ֝רְאֶ֗נּוּ בְּיֵ֣שַׁע אֱלֹהִֽים׃
BSB 50:23 He who sacrifices a thank offering honors Me, and to him who rightly orders his way, I will show the salvation of God.”
