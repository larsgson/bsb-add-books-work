1 Halleluja! Lob der HERR, my Seel!
הַֽלְלוּ־יָ֡הּ הַלְלִ֥י נַ֝פְשִׁ֗י אֶת־יְהוָֽה׃
BSB 146:1 Hallelujah! Praise the LORD, O my soul.
2 I wott der HERR lobe, solang i läbe, i wott mym Gott Psalme singe, solang i da bi.
אֲהַלְלָ֣ה יְהוָ֣ה בְּחַיָּ֑י אֲזַמְּרָ֖ה לֵֽאלֹהַ֣י בְּעוֹדִֽי׃
BSB 146:2 I will praise the LORD all my life; I will sing praises to my God while I have my being.
3 Vertrouet nid uf Fürschte, uf e Mönsch, wo nid cha rette!
אַל־תִּבְטְח֥וּ בִנְדִיבִ֑ים בְּבֶן־אָדָ֓ם׀שֶׁ֤אֵֽין ל֥וֹ תְשׁוּעָֽה׃
BSB 146:3 Put not your trust in princes, in mortal man, who cannot save.
נְדִיבִים → Fürschte (princes)
4 Wen sy Geischt usegeit, chehrt er zrügg zu syr Ärde; a däm Tag sy syni Plän verlore.
תֵּצֵ֣א ר֭וּחוֹ יָשֻׁ֣ב לְאַדְמָת֑וֹ בַּיּ֥וֹם הַ֝ה֗וּא אָבְד֥וּ עֶשְׁתֹּנֹתָֽיו׃
BSB 146:4 When his spirit departs, he returns to the ground; on that very day his plans perish.
עֶשְׁתֹּנֹתָיו → syni Plän (his plans)
5 Glücklech isch dä, däm der Gott vo Jakob hilft, wo sy Hoffnig uf e HERR, sy Gott, setzt!
אַשְׁרֵ֗י שֶׁ֤אֵ֣ל יַעֲקֹ֣ב בְּעֶזְר֑וֹ שִׂ֝בְר֗וֹ עַל־יְהוָ֥ה אֱלֹהָֽיו׃
BSB 146:5 Blessed is he whose help is the God of Jacob, whose hope is in the LORD his God,
6 Är het Himel und Ärde gmacht, ds Meer und alls, was i ne isch; er isch tröi für geng.
עֹשֶׂ֤ה׀שָׁ֘מַ֤יִם וָאָ֗רֶץ אֶת־הַיָּ֥ם וְאֶת־כָּל־אֲשֶׁר־בָּ֑ם הַשֹּׁמֵ֖ר אֱמֶ֣ת לְעוֹלָֽם׃
BSB 146:6 the Maker of heaven and earth, the sea, and everything in them. He remains faithful forever.
הַשֹּׁמֵר אֱמֶת לְעוֹלָם → er isch tröi für geng (who keeps faith forever)
7 Er verschaffet dene Rächt, wo plaget wärde, er git dene, wo Hunger hei, Brot. Der HERR macht d Gfangene frei,
עֹשֶׂ֤ה מִשְׁפָּ֨ט׀לָעֲשׁוּקִ֗ים נֹתֵ֣ן לֶ֭חֶם לָרְעֵבִ֑ים יְ֝הוָ֗ה מַתִּ֥יר אֲסוּרִֽים׃
BSB 146:7 He executes justice for the oppressed and gives food to the hungry. The LORD sets the prisoners free,
8 der HERR tuet de Blinde d Ouge uuf, der HERR richtet die uuf, wo bückt sy, der HERR het d Grächte gärn.
יְהוָ֤ה׀פֹּ֘קֵ֤חַ עִוְרִ֗ים יְ֭הוָה זֹקֵ֣ף כְּפוּפִ֑ים יְ֝הוָ֗ה אֹהֵ֥ב צַדִּיקִֽים׃
BSB 146:8 the LORD opens the eyes of the blind, the LORD lifts those who are weighed down, the LORD loves the righteous.
9 Der HERR bhüetet d Frömde, Waise und Witwe hilft er uuf, aber der Wäg vo de Gottlose macht er chrumm.
יְהוָ֤ה׀שֹׁ֘מֵ֤ר אֶת־גֵּרִ֗ים יָת֣וֹם וְאַלְמָנָ֣ה יְעוֹדֵ֑ד וְדֶ֖רֶךְ רְשָׁעִ֣ים יְעַוֵּֽת׃
BSB 146:9 The LORD protects foreigners; He sustains the fatherless and the widow, but the ways of the wicked He frustrates.
יְעַוֵּת → macht er chrumm (He frustrates)
10 Der HERR isch Chünig für geng, dy Gott, Zion, vo Generation zu Generation. Halleluja!
יִמְלֹ֤ךְ יְהוָ֨ה׀לְעוֹלָ֗ם אֱלֹהַ֣יִךְ צִ֭יּוֹן לְדֹ֥ר וָדֹ֗ר הַֽלְלוּ־יָֽהּ׃
BSB 146:10 The LORD reigns forever, your God, O Zion, for all generations. Hallelujah!
