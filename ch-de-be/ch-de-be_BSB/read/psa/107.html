1 Danket em HERR, denn er isch guet, sy Tröiji blybt für geng!
הֹד֣וּ לַיהוָ֣ה כִּי־ט֑וֹב כִּ֖י לְעוֹלָ֣ם חַסְדּֽוֹ׃
BSB 107:1 Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever.
2 So sölle säge die, wo der HERR erlöst het, die, won er erlöst het us der Hand vom Find
יֹ֭אמְרוּ גְּאוּלֵ֣י יְהוָ֑ה אֲשֶׁ֥ר גְּ֝אָלָ֗ם מִיַּד־צָֽר׃
BSB 107:2 Let the redeemed of the LORD say so, whom He has redeemed from the hand of the enemy
3 und us de Länder zämegrüeft, vo Oschte und vo Weschte, vo Norde und vom Meer.
וּֽמֵאֲרָצ֗וֹת קִ֫בְּצָ֥ם מִמִּזְרָ֥ח וּמִֽמַּעֲרָ֑ב מִצָּפ֥וֹן וּמִיָּֽם׃
BSB 107:3 and gathered from the lands, from east and west, from north and south.
וּמִיָּם → vom Meer (south)
Hebräisch »Meer« (Westen); BSB deutet als Süden.
4 Si hei sech i der Wüeschti verloffe, i der Einsami ohni Wäg; e Stadt, wo me cha wohne, hei si nid gfunde.
תָּע֣וּ בַ֭מִּדְבָּר בִּישִׁימ֣וֹן דָּ֑רֶךְ עִ֥יר מ֝וֹשָׁ֗ב לֹ֣א מָצָֽאוּ׃
BSB 107:4 Some wandered in desert wastelands, finding no path to a city in which to dwell.
5 Si hei Hunger und Durscht gha, iri Seel isch i ne schwach worde.
רְעֵבִ֥ים גַּם־צְמֵאִ֑ים נַ֝פְשָׁ֗ם בָּהֶ֥ם תִּתְעַטָּֽף׃
BSB 107:5 They were hungry and thirsty; their soul fainted within them.
6 De hei si zum HERR grüeft i irer Not, und er het se us irer Angscht grettet.
וַיִּצְעֲק֣וּ אֶל־יְ֭הוָה בַּצַּ֣ר לָהֶ֑ם מִ֝מְּצֽוּקוֹתֵיהֶ֗ם יַצִּילֵֽם׃
BSB 107:6 Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and He delivered them from their distress.
7 Er het se uf e grade Wäg gfüert, dass si zu ne Stadt chöme, wo me cha wohne.
וַ֭יַּֽדְרִיכֵם בְּדֶ֣רֶךְ יְשָׁרָ֑ה לָ֝לֶ֗כֶת אֶל־עִ֥יר מוֹשָֽׁב׃
BSB 107:7 He led them on a straight path to reach a city where they could live.
8 Si sölle em HERR danke für sy Tröiji und für syni Wunder a de Mönsche!
יוֹד֣וּ לַיהוָ֣ה חַסְדּ֑וֹ וְ֝נִפְלְאוֹתָ֗יו לִבְנֵ֥י אָדָֽם׃
BSB 107:8 Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loving devotion and His wonders to the sons of men.
9 Denn er het d Seel, wo Durscht het, satt gmacht und di hungrigi Seel mit Guetem gfüllt.
כִּי־הִ֭שְׂבִּיעַ נֶ֣פֶשׁ שֹׁקֵקָ֑ה וְנֶ֥פֶשׁ רְ֝עֵבָה מִלֵּא־טֽוֹב׃
BSB 107:9 For He satisfies the thirsty and fills the hungry with good things.
10 Si sy im Fyschtere und im tiefe Schatte gsässe, gfange im Elände und i ysige Chetti,
יֹ֭שְׁבֵי חֹ֣שֶׁךְ וְצַלְמָ֑וֶת אֲסִירֵ֖י עֳנִ֣י וּבַרְזֶֽל׃
BSB 107:10 Some sat in darkness and in the shadow of death, prisoners in affliction and chains,
11 wil si sech gäge d Wort vo Gott gstellt hei und der Rat vom Höchschte verachtet hei.
כִּֽי־הִמְר֥וּ אִמְרֵי־אֵ֑ל וַעֲצַ֖ת עֶלְי֣וֹן נָאָֽצוּ׃
BSB 107:11 because they rebelled against the words of God and despised the counsel of the Most High.
12 Er het ires Härz mit Müej bbroche, si sy gfalle, und es isch niemer da gsi, wo ghulfe het.
וַיַּכְנַ֣ע בֶּעָמָ֣ל לִבָּ֑ם כָּ֝שְׁל֗וּ וְאֵ֣ין עֹזֵֽר׃
BSB 107:12 He humbled their hearts with hard labor; they stumbled, and there was no one to help.
13 De hei si zum HERR grüeft i irer Not, und er het ne us irer Angscht ghulfe.
וַיִּזְעֲק֣וּ אֶל־יְ֭הוָה בַּצַּ֣ר לָהֶ֑ם מִ֝מְּצֻֽקוֹתֵיהֶ֗ם יוֹשִׁיעֵֽם׃
BSB 107:13 Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and He saved them from their distress.
14 Er het se us em Fyschtere und us em tiefe Schatte usegfüert und iri Fessle verrisse.
יֽ֭וֹצִיאֵם מֵחֹ֣שֶׁךְ וְצַלְמָ֑וֶת וּמוֹסְר֖וֹתֵיהֶ֣ם יְנַתֵּֽק׃
BSB 107:14 He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death and broke away their chains.
15 Si sölle em HERR danke für sy Tröiji und für syni Wunder a de Mönsche!
יוֹד֣וּ לַיהוָ֣ה חַסְדּ֑וֹ וְ֝נִפְלְאוֹתָ֗יו לִבְנֵ֥י אָדָֽם׃
BSB 107:15 Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loving devotion and His wonders to the sons of men.
16 Denn er het Tor us Bronze verschlage und ysigi Stange verhoue.
כִּֽי־שִׁ֭בַּר דַּלְת֣וֹת נְחֹ֑שֶׁת וּבְרִיחֵ֖י בַרְזֶ֣ל גִּדֵּֽעַ׃
BSB 107:16 For He has broken down the gates of bronze and cut through the bars of iron.
בְרִיחֵי בַרְזֶל → ysigi Stange (bars of iron)

BSB 107:17 Fools, in their rebellious ways, and through their iniquities, suffered affliction.
18 Si hei vor allem Ässe es Gruuse gha, und si sy nach bis a d Tor vom Tod cho.
כָּל־אֹ֭כֶל תְּתַעֵ֣ב נַפְשָׁ֑ם וַ֝יַּגִּ֗יעוּ עַד־שַׁ֥עֲרֵי מָֽוֶת׃
BSB 107:18 They loathed all food and drew near to the gates of death.
תְּתַעֵב נַפְשָׁם → es Gruuse gha (loathed)
19 De hei si zum HERR grüeft i irer Not, und er het ne us irer Angscht ghulfe.
וַיִּזְעֲק֣וּ אֶל־יְ֭הוָה בַּצַּ֣ר לָהֶ֑ם מִ֝מְּצֻֽקוֹתֵיהֶ֗ם יוֹשִׁיעֵֽם׃
BSB 107:19 Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and He saved them from their distress.
20 Er het sys Wort gschickt und se gheilet und se us em Verderbe grettet.
יִשְׁלַ֣ח דְּ֭בָרוֹ וְיִרְפָּאֵ֑ם וִֽ֝ימַלֵּ֗ט מִשְּׁחִיתוֹתָֽם׃
BSB 107:20 He sent forth His word and healed them; He rescued them from the Pit.
מִשְּׁחִיתוֹתָם → us em Verderbe (from the Pit)
21 Si sölle em HERR danke für sy Tröiji und für syni Wunder a de Mönsche!
יוֹד֣וּ לַיהוָ֣ה חַסְדּ֑וֹ וְ֝נִפְלְאוֹתָ֗יו לִבְנֵ֥י אָדָֽם׃
BSB 107:21 Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loving devotion and His wonders to the sons of men.
22 Si sölle Opfer zum Danke bringe und juble und mit Fröid verzelle, was er gmacht het!
וְ֭יִזְבְּחוּ זִבְחֵ֣י תוֹדָ֑ה וִֽיסַפְּר֖וּ מַעֲשָׂ֣יו בְּרִנָּֽה׃
BSB 107:22 Let them offer sacrifices of thanksgiving and declare His works with rejoicing.

יוֹרְדֵ֣י הַ֭יָּם בָּאֳנִיּ֑וֹת עֹשֵׂ֥י מְ֝לָאכָ֗ה בְּמַ֣יִם רַבִּֽים׃
BSB 107:23 Others went out to sea in ships, conducting trade on the mighty waters.
24 die hei d Wärch vom HERR gseh und syni Wunder i der Tiefi.
הֵ֣מָּה רָ֭אוּ מַעֲשֵׂ֣י יְהוָ֑ה וְ֝נִפְלְאוֹתָ֗יו בִּמְצוּלָֽה׃
BSB 107:24 They saw the works of the LORD, and His wonders in the deep.
25 Er het gredt, und e Sturm isch ufcho und het d Wälle vom Meer höch ufglüpft.
וַיֹּ֗אמֶר וַֽ֭יַּעֲמֵד ר֣וּחַ סְעָרָ֑ה וַתְּרוֹמֵ֥ם גַּלָּֽיו׃
BSB 107:25 For He spoke and raised a tempest that lifted the waves of the sea.
26 Si sy ufe bis zum Himel und abe i d Tiefine; iri Seel isch i der Not vergange.
יַעֲל֣וּ שָׁ֭מַיִם יֵרְד֣וּ תְהוֹמ֑וֹת נַ֝פְשָׁ֗ם בְּרָעָ֥ה תִתְמוֹגָֽג׃
BSB 107:26 They mounted up to the heavens, then sunk to the depths; their courage melted in their anguish.

BSB 107:27 They reeled and staggered like drunkards, and all their skill was useless.

וַיִּצְעֲק֣וּ אֶל־יְ֭הוָה בַּצַּ֣ר לָהֶ֑ם וּֽ֝מִמְּצֽוּקֹתֵיהֶ֗ם יוֹצִיאֵֽם׃

וַיִּשְׂמְח֥וּ כִֽי־יִשְׁתֹּ֑קוּ וַ֝יַּנְחֵ֗ם אֶל־מְח֥וֹז חֶפְצָֽם׃
BSB 107:30 They rejoiced in the silence, and He guided them to the harbor they desired.

31 Si sölle em HERR danke für sy Tröiji und für syni Wunder a de Mönsche!
יוֹד֣וּ לַיהוָ֣ה חַסְדּ֑וֹ וְ֝נִפְלְאוֹתָ֗יו לִבְנֵ֥י אָדָֽם׃

BSB 107:32 Let them exalt Him in the assembly of the people and praise Him in the council of the elders.
