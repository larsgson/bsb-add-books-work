2 Der HERR söll dir Antwort gä am Tag vo der Not, der Name vom Gott vo Jakob söll di bhüete!
יַֽעַנְךָ֣ יְ֭הוָה בְּי֣וֹם צָרָ֑ה יְ֝שַׂגֶּבְךָ֗ שֵׁ֤ם׀אֱלֹהֵ֬י יַעֲקֹֽב׃
BSB 20:1 For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. May the LORD answer you in the day of trouble; may the name of the God of Jacob protect you.
יְשַׂגֶּבְךָ → söll di bhüete (protect you)
3 Er söll dir Hilf schicke us em Heiligtum, und us Zion söll er di understütze!
יִשְׁלַֽח־עֶזְרְךָ֥ מִקֹּ֑דֶשׁ וּ֝מִצִּיּ֗וֹן יִסְעָדֶֽךָּ׃
BSB 20:2 May He send you help from the sanctuary and sustain you from Zion.
4 Er söll a all dyni Gabe dänke und dyni Brandopfer aanä! Sela
יִזְכֹּ֥ר כָּל־מִנְחֹתֶ֑ךָ וְעוֹלָתְךָ֖ יְדַשְּׁנֶ֣ה סֶֽלָה׃
BSB 20:3 May He remember all your gifts and look favorably on your burnt offerings. Selah
יְדַשְּׁנֶה → aanä (look favorably on)
Wörtlich »fett machen« (als fett annehmen).
5 Er söll dir gä, was dys Härz wünscht, und all dyni Plän la grate!
יִֽתֶּן־לְךָ֥ כִלְבָבֶ֑ךָ וְֽכָל־עֲצָתְךָ֥ יְמַלֵּֽא׃
BSB 20:4 May He give you the desires of your heart and make all your plans succeed.
6 Mir wei juble über dy Rettig und im Name vo üsem Gott d Fahne ufstelle! Der HERR söll all dyni Bitte erfülle!
נְרַנְּנָ֤ה׀בִּ֘ישׁ֤וּעָתֶ֗ךָ וּבְשֵֽׁם־אֱלֹהֵ֥ינוּ נִדְגֹּ֑ל יְמַלֵּ֥א יְ֝הוָ֗ה כָּל־מִשְׁאֲלוֹתֶֽיךָ׃
BSB 20:5 May we shout for joy at your victory and raise a banner in the name of our God. May the LORD grant all your petitions.
נִדְגֹּל → d Fahne ufstelle (raise a banner)

7 Jitz weis i, dass der HERR däm hilft, won er gsalbet het. Er git ihm Antwort us sym heilige Himel mit der Chraft vo syr rächte Hand, wo rettet.
עַתָּ֤ה יָדַ֗עְתִּי כִּ֤י הוֹשִׁ֥יעַ׀יְהוָ֗ה מְשִׁ֫יח֥וֹ יַ֭עֲנֵהוּ מִשְּׁמֵ֣י קָדְשׁ֑וֹ בִּ֝גְבֻר֗וֹת יֵ֣שַׁע יְמִינֽוֹ׃
BSB 20:6 Now I know that the LORD saves His anointed; He answers him from His holy heaven with the saving power of His right hand.
מְשִׁיחוֹ → däm, won er gsalbet het (His anointed)
Wie Ps 18,51.
8 Di einte vertroue uf Wäge, di andere uf Ross, aber mir rüefe der Name vom HERR, üsem Gott, aa.
אֵ֣לֶּה בָ֭רֶכֶב וְאֵ֣לֶּה בַסּוּסִ֑ים וַאֲנַ֓חְנוּ׀בְּשֵׁם־יְהוָ֖ה אֱלֹהֵ֣ינוּ נַזְכִּֽיר׃
BSB 20:7 Some trust in chariots and others in horses, but we trust in the name of the LORD our God.
נַזְכִּיר → rüefe … aa (trust)
Wörtlich »wir rufen an / gedenken«.
רֶכֶב / סוּסִים → Wäge / Ross (chariots / horses)
9 Si sy zämegheit und gfalle, aber mir sy ufgstande und stande fescht.
הֵ֭מָּה כָּרְע֣וּ וְנָפָ֑לוּ וַאֲנַ֥חְנוּ קַּ֝֗מְנוּ וַנִּתְעוֹדָֽד׃
BSB 20:8 They collapse and fall, but we rise up and stand firm.
10 HERR, hilf! Der Chünig söll üüs Antwort gä am Tag, wo mer rüefe!
יְהוָ֥ה הוֹשִׁ֑יעָה הַ֝מֶּ֗לֶךְ יַעֲנֵ֥נוּ בְיוֹם־קָרְאֵֽנוּ׃
BSB 20:9 O LORD, save the king. Answer us on the day we call.
יְהוָה הוֹשִׁיעָה הַמֶּלֶךְ יַעֲנֵנוּ → HERR, hilf! Der Chünig söll üüs Antwort gä (O LORD, save the king. Answer us)
