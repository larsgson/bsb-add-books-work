1 Für e Chorleiter. Es Lied. E Psalm. Jublet Gott zue, di ganzi Ärde!
לַ֭מְנַצֵּחַ שִׁ֣יר מִזְמ֑וֹרהָרִ֥יעוּ לֵ֝אלֹהִים כָּל־הָאָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 66:1 For the choirmaster. A song. A Psalm. Make a joyful noise to God, all the earth!
2 Singet vo der Herrlechkeit vo sym Name, gät sym Lob Ehr!
זַמְּר֥וּ כְבֽוֹד־שְׁמ֑וֹ שִׂ֥ימוּ כָ֝ב֗וֹד תְּהִלָּתֽוֹ׃
BSB 66:2 Sing the glory of His name; make His praise glorious.
3 Säget zu Gott: „Wi fürchterlech sy dyni Wärch! Wäge dyr grosse Macht müesse dyni Finde vor dir chnöile.
אִמְר֣וּ לֵ֭אלֹהִים מַה־נּוֹרָ֣א מַעֲשֶׂ֑יךָ בְּרֹ֥ב עֻ֝זְּךָ֗ יְֽכַחֲשׁ֖וּ לְךָ֣ אֹיְבֶֽיךָ׃
BSB 66:3 Say to God, “How awesome are Your deeds! So great is Your power that Your enemies cower before You.
יְכַחֲשׁוּ לְךָ → müesse … vor dir chnöile (cower before You)
4 Di ganzi Ärde söll di aabätte und dir Psalme singe, si söll Psalme singe für dy Name!“ Sela
כָּל־הָאָ֤רֶץ׀יִשְׁתַּחֲו֣וּ לְ֭ךָ וִֽיזַמְּרוּ־לָ֑ךְ יְזַמְּר֖וּ שִׁמְךָ֣ סֶֽלָה׃
BSB 66:4 All the earth bows down to You; they sing praise to You; they sing praise to Your name.” Selah
5 Chömet und lueget, was Gott gmacht het, wi fürchterlech sys Tue isch gäge d Mönsche!
לְכ֣וּ וּ֭רְאוּ מִפְעֲל֣וֹת אֱלֹהִ֑ים נוֹרָ֥א עֲ֝לִילָ֗ה עַל־בְּנֵ֥י אָדָֽם׃
BSB 66:5 Come and see the works of God; how awesome are His deeds toward mankind.
6 Er het ds Meer i trochene Bode verwandlet; dür e Fluss sy si z Fuess gange. Dert hei mer is a ihm gfröit.
הָ֤פַךְ יָ֨ם׀לְֽיַבָּשָׁ֗ה בַּ֭נָּהָר יַֽעַבְר֣וּ בְרָ֑גֶל שָׁ֝֗ם נִשְׂמְחָה־בּֽוֹ׃
BSB 66:6 He turned the sea into dry land; they passed through the waters on foot; there we rejoiced in Him.
הָפַךְ יָם לְיַבָּשָׁה → Er het ds Meer i trochene Bode verwandlet (He turned the sea into dry land)
Schilfmeer/Jordan.
7 Er regiert mit syr Macht für geng; syni Ouge luege uf d Völker. Die, wo sech wehre, sölle nid stolz sy gäge ihn! Sela
מֹ֘שֵׁ֤ל בִּגְבוּרָת֨וֹ׀עוֹלָ֗ם עֵ֭ינָיו בַּגּוֹיִ֣ם תִּצְפֶּ֑ינָה הַסּוֹרְרִ֓ים׀אַל־יָר֖וּמוּ לָ֣מוֹ סֶֽלָה׃
BSB 66:7 He rules forever by His power; His eyes watch the nations. Do not let the rebellious exalt themselves. Selah
הַסּוֹרְרִים → Die, wo sech wehre (the rebellious)
8 Lobet, dihr Völker, üse Gott, und lat ds Lied vo sym Lob ghöre!
בָּרְכ֖וּ עַמִּ֥ים׀אֱלֹהֵ֑ינוּ וְ֝הַשְׁמִ֗יעוּ ק֣וֹל תְּהִלָּתֽוֹ׃
BSB 66:8 Bless our God, O peoples; let the sound of His praise be heard.
9 Är het üüs am Läbe bhalte und üsi Füess nid la wanke.
הַשָּׂ֣ם נַ֭פְשֵׁנוּ בַּֽחַיִּ֑ים וְלֹֽא־נָתַ֖ן לַמּ֣וֹט רַגְלֵֽנוּ׃
BSB 66:9 He preserves our lives and keeps our feet from slipping.
10 Denn du hesch is prüeft, Gott, du hesch is suber gmacht wi me Silber im Füür suber macht.
כִּֽי־בְחַנְתָּ֥נוּ אֱלֹהִ֑ים צְ֝רַפְתָּ֗נוּ כִּצְרָף־כָּֽסֶף׃
BSB 66:10 For You, O God, have tested us; You have refined us like silver.
צְרַפְתָּנוּ → du hesch is suber gmacht (You refined us)
11 Du hesch is i ds Netz gfüert, du hesch üüs e schwäri Lascht uf e Rügge gleit.
הֲבֵאתָ֥נוּ בַמְּצוּדָ֑ה שַׂ֖מְתָּ מוּעָקָ֣ה בְמָתְנֵֽינוּ׃
BSB 66:11 You led us into the net; You laid burdens on our backs.
12 Du hesch Mönsche über üsi Chöpf la ryte; mir sy dür Füür und dür Wasser gange, aber du hesch is usegfüert a ne Ort mit Überfluss.
הִרְכַּ֥בְתָּ אֱנ֗וֹשׁ לְרֹ֫אשֵׁ֥נוּ בָּֽאנוּ־בָאֵ֥שׁ וּבַמַּ֑יִם וַ֝תּוֹצִיאֵ֗נוּ לָֽרְוָיָֽה׃
BSB 66:12 You let men ride over our heads; we went through fire and water, but You brought us into abundance.
לָרְוָיָה → a ne Ort mit Überfluss (to abundance)
13 I wott mit Brandopfer i dys Huus cho, i wott dir erfülle, was i versproche ha,
אָב֣וֹא בֵיתְךָ֣ בְעוֹל֑וֹת אֲשַׁלֵּ֖ם לְךָ֣ נְדָרָֽי׃
BSB 66:13 I will enter Your house with burnt offerings; I will fulfill my vows to You —
14 was myni Lippe gseit hei und mys Muul versproche het, won i i Not gsi bi.
אֲשֶׁר־פָּצ֥וּ שְׂפָתָ֑י וְדִבֶּר־פִּ֝֗י בַּצַּר־לִֽי׃
BSB 66:14 the vows that my lips promised and my mouth spoke in my distress.
15 Brandopfer vo gmeschtete Tier wott i dir bringe mit em Rouch vo Schafböck; i wott Stiere mit Geisse opfere. Sela
עֹ֘ל֤וֹת מֵחִ֣ים אַעֲלֶה־לָּ֭ךְ עִם־קְטֹ֣רֶת אֵילִ֑ים אֶ֥עֱשֶֽׂה בָקָ֖ר עִם־עַתּוּדִ֣ים סֶֽלָה׃
BSB 66:15 I will offer You fatlings as burnt offerings, with the fragrant smoke of rams; I will offer bulls and goats. Selah
אֵילִים → Schafböck (rams)
Korpuswort.
16 Chömet, loset, i wott verzelle, alli, wo Gott förchte, was er für my Seel gmacht het!
לְכֽוּ־שִׁמְע֣וּ וַ֭אֲסַפְּרָה כָּל־יִרְאֵ֣י אֱלֹהִ֑ים אֲשֶׁ֖ר עָשָׂ֣ה לְנַפְשִֽׁי׃
BSB 66:16 Come and listen, all you who fear God, and I will declare what He has done for me.
17 Zu ihm han i mit mym Muul grüeft, und Lob isch uf myr Zunge gsi.
אֵלָ֥יו פִּֽי־קָרָ֑אתִי וְ֝רוֹמַ֗ם תַּ֣חַת לְשׁוֹנִֽי׃
BSB 66:17 I cried out to Him with my mouth and praised Him with my tongue.
18 Wen i Unrächt i mym Härz im Sinn gha hätt, hätt der Herr nid glost.
אָ֭וֶן אִם־רָאִ֣יתִי בְלִבִּ֑י לֹ֖א יִשְׁמַ֣ע׀אֲדֹנָֽי׃
BSB 66:18 If I had cherished iniquity in my heart, the Lord would not have listened.
19 Aber Gott het glost, er het uf d Stimm vo mym Gebätt gachtet.
אָ֭כֵן שָׁמַ֣ע אֱלֹהִ֑ים הִ֝קְשִׁ֗יב בְּק֣וֹל תְּפִלָּתִֽי׃
BSB 66:19 But God has surely heard; He has attended to the sound of my prayer.
20 Globet syg Gott, wo mys Gebätt nid abgwise het und sy Tröiji nid vo mir gno het!
בָּר֥וּךְ אֱלֹהִ֑ים אֲשֶׁ֥ר לֹֽא־הֵסִ֘יר תְּפִלָּתִ֥י וְ֝חַסְדּ֗וֹ מֵאִתִּֽי׃
BSB 66:20 Blessed be God, who has not rejected my prayer or withheld from me His loving devotion!
