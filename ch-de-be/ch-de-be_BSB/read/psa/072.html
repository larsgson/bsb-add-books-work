Hebräisch: Westminster Leningrad Codex (gemeinfrei), über MACULA Hebrew (CC BY 4.0, Clear Bible); Griechisch: Berean Greek Bible; Englisch: Berean Standard Bible (beide gemeinfrei). Die Kommentare sind die Notizen des Übersetzungsagenten.
1 Vom Salomo. Gott, gib em Chünig dys Rächt und em Sohn vom Chünig dy Grächtigkeit,
לִשְׁלֹמֹ֨ה׀אֱֽלֹהִ֗ים מִ֭שְׁפָּטֶיךָ לְמֶ֣לֶךְ תֵּ֑ן וְצִדְקָתְךָ֥ לְבֶן־מֶֽלֶךְ׃
BSB 72:1 Of Solomon. Endow the king with Your justice, O God, and the son of the king with Your righteousness.
2 dass er dys Volk mit Grächtigkeit regiert und dyni Arme nach em Rächt.
יָדִ֣ין עַמְּךָ֣ בְצֶ֑דֶק וַעֲנִיֶּ֥יךָ בְמִשְׁפָּֽט׃
BSB 72:2 May he judge Your people with righteousness and Your afflicted with justice.
3 D Bärge sölle em Volk Fride bringe und d Hoger Grächtigkeit.
יִשְׂא֤וּ הָרִ֓ים שָׁ֘ל֥וֹם לָעָ֑ם וּ֝גְבָע֗וֹת בִּצְדָקָֽה׃
BSB 72:3 May the mountains bring peace to the people, and the hills bring righteousness.
שָׁלוֹם → Fride (prosperity)
שָׁלוֹם »Fride« fest.
4 Er söll de Arme vom Volk zu irem Rächt verhälfe, de Chind vo de Bedürftige hälfe und dä, wo se plaget, verschla!
יִשְׁפֹּ֤ט׀עֲֽנִיֵּי־עָ֗ם י֭וֹשִׁיעַ לִבְנֵ֣י אֶבְי֑וֹן וִֽידַכֵּ֣א עוֹשֵֽׁק׃
BSB 72:4 May he vindicate the afflicted among the people; may he save the children of the needy and crush the oppressor.
5 Me söll di förchte, solang d Sunne schynt, und solang der Mond da isch, vo Generation zu Generation!
יִֽירָא֥וּךָ עִם־שָׁ֑מֶשׁ וְלִפְנֵ֥י יָ֝רֵ֗חַ דּ֣וֹר דּוֹרִֽים׃
BSB 72:5 May they fear him as long as the sun shines, as long as the moon remains, through all generations.
6 Er söll sy wi Räge, wo uf d Matte fallt, wi vil Räge, wo uf d Ärde chunt!
יֵ֭רֵד כְּמָטָ֣ר עַל־גֵּ֑ז כִּ֝רְבִיבִ֗ים זַרְזִ֥יף אָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 72:6 May he be like rain that falls on freshly cut grass, like spring showers that water the earth.
עַל־גֵּז → uf d Matte (on the mown grass)
»gemäht« weggelassen (kein Korpuswort).
7 I syne Täg söll der Grächt wachse, und es söll vil Fride sy, bis es ke Mond meh git!
יִֽפְרַח־בְּיָמָ֥יו צַדִּ֑יק וְרֹ֥ב שָׁ֝ל֗וֹם עַד־בְּלִ֥י יָרֵֽחַ׃
BSB 72:7 May the righteous flourish in his days and prosperity abound until the moon is no more.
8 Er söll regiere vo Meer zu Meer und vom Strom bis a d Änd vo der Ärde!
וְ֭יֵרְדְּ מִיָּ֣ם עַד־יָ֑ם וּ֝מִנָּהָ֗ר עַד־אַפְסֵי־אָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 72:8 May he rule from sea to sea, and from the Euphrates to the ends of the earth.
מִנָּהָר → vom Strom (from the Euphrates)
Der Euphrat gemeint; wörtlich gelassen.
9 Vor ihm sölle d Völker vo der Wüeschti chnöile, und syni Finde sölle Stoub frässe!
לְ֭פָנָיו יִכְרְע֣וּ צִיִּ֑ים וְ֝אֹיְבָ֗יו עָפָ֥ר יְלַחֵֽכוּ׃
BSB 72:9 May the nomads bow before him, and his enemies lick the dust.
צִיִּים → d Völker vo der Wüeschti (desert nomads)
עָפָר יְלַחֵכוּ → sölle Stoub frässe (lick the dust)
10 D Chünige vo Tarsis und vo de Insle sölle Gschänk bringe, d Chünige vo Scheba und Seba sölle Stüüre zahle!
מַלְכֵ֬י תַרְשִׁ֣ישׁ וְ֭אִיִּים מִנְחָ֣ה יָשִׁ֑יבוּ מַלְכֵ֥י שְׁבָ֥א וּ֝סְבָ֗א אֶשְׁכָּ֥ר יַקְרִֽיבוּ׃
BSB 72:10 May the kings of Tarshish and distant shores bring tribute; may the kings of Sheba and Seba offer gifts.
אֶשְׁכָּר → Stüüre (gifts)
»Tribut«.
11 Alli Chünige sölle sech vor ihm bücke, alli Völker sölle ihm diene!
וְיִשְׁתַּחֲווּ־ל֥וֹ כָל־מְלָכִ֑ים כָּל־גּוֹיִ֥ם יַֽעַבְדֽוּהוּ׃
BSB 72:11 May all kings bow down to him and all nations serve him.
12 Denn er rettet der Arm, wo um Hilf rüeft, und der Eländ, wo ke Hälfer het.
כִּֽי־יַ֭צִּיל אֶבְי֣וֹן מְשַׁוֵּ֑עַ וְ֝עָנִ֗י וְֽאֵין־עֹזֵ֥ר לֽוֹ׃
BSB 72:12 For he will deliver the needy who cry out and the afflicted who have no helper.
13 Er het Erbarme mit em Schwache und em Arme, und ds Läbe vo de Arme rettet er.
יָ֭חֹס עַל־דַּ֣ל וְאֶבְי֑וֹן וְנַפְשׁ֖וֹת אֶבְיוֹנִ֣ים יוֹשִֽׁיעַ׃
BSB 72:13 He will take pity on the poor and needy and save the lives of the oppressed.
14 Vo dene, wo se plage, und vo der Gwalt erlöst er se, und ires Bluet isch i syne Ouge tüür.
מִתּ֣וֹךְ וּ֭מֵחָמָס יִגְאַ֣ל נַפְשָׁ֑ם וְיֵיקַ֖ר דָּמָ֣ם בְּעֵינָֽיו׃
BSB 72:14 He will redeem them from oppression and violence, for their blood is precious in his sight.
15 Er söll läbe! Me söll ihm vo Guld us Scheba gä, me söll geng für ihn bätte und ne der ganz Tag sägne!
וִיחִ֗י וְיִתֶּן־לוֹ֮ מִזְּהַ֪ב שְׁ֫בָ֥א וְיִתְפַּלֵּ֣ל בַּעֲד֣וֹ תָמִ֑יד כָּל־הַ֝יּ֗וֹם יְבָרֲכֶֽנְהֽוּ׃
BSB 72:15 Long may he live! May gold from Sheba be given him. May people ever pray for him; may they bless him all day long.
16 Es söll vil Chorn gä im Land, bis ufe uf d Bärge, sy Frucht söll ruusche wi der Libanon; und us de Stedt söll's wachse wi ds Gras uf em Fäld.
יְהִ֤י פִסַּת־בַּ֨ר׀בָּאָרֶץ֮ בְּרֹ֪אשׁ הָ֫רִ֥ים יִרְעַ֣שׁ כַּלְּבָנ֣וֹן פִּרְי֑וֹ וְיָצִ֥יצוּ מֵ֝עִ֗יר כְּעֵ֣שֶׂב הָאָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 72:16 May there be an abundance of grain in the land; may it sway atop the hills. May its fruit trees flourish like the forests of Lebanon, the people of its cities like the grass of the field.
17 Sy Name söll blybe für geng; solang d Sunne schynt, söll sy Name wachse! I ihm sölle sech alli Völker sägne, si sölle säge: „Glücklech isch er!“
יְהִ֤י שְׁמ֨וֹ לְֽעוֹלָ֗ם לִפְנֵי־שֶׁמֶשׁ֮ יִנּ֪וֹן שְׁ֫מ֥וֹ וְיִתְבָּ֥רְכוּ ב֑וֹ כָּל־גּוֹיִ֥ם יְאַשְּׁרֽוּהוּ׃
BSB 72:17 May his name endure forever; may his name continue as long as the sun shines. In him may all nations be blessed; may they call him blessed.
יְאַשְּׁרוּהוּ → si sölle säge: „Glücklech isch er!“ (call him blessed)
אַשְׁרֵי »glücklech« wie Ps 1.
18 Globet syg der HERR, Gott, der Gott vo Israel, wo ganz alleini Wunder tuet!
בָּר֤וּךְ׀יְהוָ֣ה אֱ֭לֹהִים אֱלֹהֵ֣י יִשְׂרָאֵ֑ל עֹשֵׂ֖ה נִפְלָא֣וֹת לְבַדּֽוֹ׃
BSB 72:18 Blessed be the LORD God, the God of Israel, who alone does marvelous deeds.
19 Globet syg sy herrleche Name für geng, und di ganzi Ärde söll voll wärde vo syr Herrlechkeit! Amen, ja, Amen!
וּבָר֤וּךְ׀שֵׁ֥ם כְּבוֹד֗וֹ לְע֫וֹלָ֥ם וְיִמָּלֵ֣א כְ֭בוֹדוֹ אֶת־כֹּ֥ל הָאָ֗רֶץ אָ֘מֵ֥ן׀וְאָמֵֽן׃
BSB 72:19 And blessed be His glorious name forever; may all the earth be filled with His glory. Amen and amen.
20 Hie hören d Gebätt vom David, em Sohn vom Isai, uf.
כָּלּ֥וּ תְפִלּ֑וֹת דָּ֝וִ֗ד בֶּן־יִשָֽׁי׃
BSB 72:20 Thus conclude the prayers of David son of Jesse.
כָּלּוּ תְפִלּוֹת → Hie hören d Gebätt … uf (This concludes the prayers)
