BSB 149:1 Hallelujah! Sing to the LORD a new song — His praise in the assembly of the godly.
2 Israel söll sech fröie a däm, wo's gmacht het, d Chinder vo Zion sölle juble über ire Chünig!
יִשְׂמַ֣ח יִשְׂרָאֵ֣ל בְּעֹשָׂ֑יו בְּנֵֽי־צִ֝יּ֗וֹן יָגִ֥ילוּ בְמַלְכָּֽם׃
BSB 149:2 Let Israel rejoice in their Maker; let the children of Zion rejoice in their King.
3 Si sölle sy Name lobe mit Tanz, mit Tamburin und Zither sölle si ihm Psalme singe!
יְהַֽלְל֣וּ שְׁמ֣וֹ בְמָח֑וֹל בְּתֹ֥ף וְ֝כִנּ֗וֹר יְזַמְּרוּ־לֽוֹ׃
BSB 149:3 Let them praise His name with dancing, and make music to Him with tambourine and harp.
4 Denn der HERR het Fröid a sym Volk, er ehret di Demüetige mit Rettig.
כִּֽי־רוֹצֶ֣ה יְהוָ֣ה בְּעַמּ֑וֹ יְפָאֵ֥ר עֲ֝נָוִ֗ים בִּישׁוּעָֽה׃
BSB 149:4 For the LORD takes pleasure in His people; He adorns the afflicted with salvation.
יְפָאֵר → er ehret (He crowns)
5 D Fromme sölle juble i Herrlechkeit, si sölle juble uf irem Bett!
יַעְלְז֣וּ חֲסִידִ֣ים בְּכָב֑וֹד יְ֝רַנְּנ֗וּ עַל־מִשְׁכְּבוֹתָֽם׃
BSB 149:5 Let the saints exult in glory; let them shout for joy upon their beds.
עַל־מִשְׁכְּבוֹתָם → uf irem Bett (on their beds)
6 Ds Lob vo Gott söll i irem Muul sy, und es scharfs Schwärt i irne Händ,
רוֹמְמ֣וֹת אֵ֭ל בִּגְרוֹנָ֑ם וְחֶ֖רֶב פִּֽיפִיּ֣וֹת בְּיָדָֽם׃
BSB 149:6 May the high praises of God be in their mouths, and a double-edged sword in their hands,
רוֹמְמוֹת אֵל → Ds Lob vo Gott (the high praises of God)

BSB 149:7 to inflict vengeance on the nations and punishment on the peoples,
8 für iri Chünige mit Chettine z binde und iri grosse Herre mit ysige Fessle,
לֶאְסֹ֣ר מַלְכֵיהֶ֣ם בְּזִקִּ֑ים וְ֝נִכְבְּדֵיהֶ֗ם בְּכַבְלֵ֥י בַרְזֶֽל׃
BSB 149:8 to bind their kings with chains and their nobles with shackles of iron,
9 für ds Gricht a ne z halte, wo ufgschribe isch. Das isch e Ehr für alli syni Fromme. Halleluja!
לַעֲשׂ֤וֹת בָּהֶ֨ם׀מִשְׁפָּ֬ט כָּת֗וּב הָדָ֣ר ה֭וּא לְכָל־חֲסִידָ֗יו הַֽלְלוּ־יָֽהּ׃
BSB 149:9 to execute the judgment written against them. This honor is for all His saints. Hallelujah!
מִשְׁפָּט כָּתוּב → ds Gricht, wo ufgschribe isch (the sentence written)
