1 Vom David. Bis nid ufgregt wäge de Böse, häb ke Nyd uf die, wo Unrächt tüe!

BSB 37:1 Of David. Do not fret over those who do evil; do not envy those who do wrong.

BSB 37:4 Delight yourself in the LORD, and He will give you the desires of your heart.

BSB 37:5 Commit your way to the LORD; trust in Him, and He will do it.

BSB 37:7 Be still before the LORD and wait patiently for Him; do not fret when men prosper in their ways, when they carry out wicked schemes.
