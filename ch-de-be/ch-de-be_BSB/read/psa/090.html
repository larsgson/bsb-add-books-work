1 Es Gebätt vom Mose, em Ma vo Gott. Herr, du bisch üsi Wonig gsi vo Generation zu Generation.
תְּפִלָּה֮ לְמֹשֶׁ֪ה אִֽישׁ־הָאֱלֹ֫הִ֥יםאֲֽדֹנָ֗י מָע֣וֹן אַ֭תָּה הָיִ֥יתָ לָּ֗נוּ בְּדֹ֣ר וָדֹֽר׃
BSB 90:1 A prayer of Moses the man of God. Lord, You have been our dwelling place through all generations.
מָעוֹן → üsi Wonig (our dwelling place)
2 Bevor d Bärge uf d Wält cho sy und du d Ärde und d Wält erschaffe hesch, bisch du Gott vo Ewigkeit zu Ewigkeit.
בְּטֶ֤רֶם׀הָ֘רִ֤ים יֻלָּ֗דוּ וַתְּח֣וֹלֵֽל אֶ֣רֶץ וְתֵבֵ֑ל וּֽמֵעוֹלָ֥ם עַד־ע֝וֹלָ֗ם אַתָּ֥ה אֵֽל׃
BSB 90:2 Before the mountains were born or You brought forth the earth and the world, from everlasting to everlasting You are God.
3 Du lasch der Mönsch zum Stoub zrüggcho und seisch: „Chömet zrügg, dihr Mönschechind!“
תָּשֵׁ֣ב אֱ֭נוֹשׁ עַד־דַּכָּ֑א וַ֝תֹּ֗אמֶר שׁ֣וּבוּ בְנֵי־אָדָֽם׃
BSB 90:3 You return man to dust, saying, “Return, O sons of mortals.”
דַּכָּא → zum Stoub (to dust)
4 Denn tuusig Jahr sy i dyne Ouge wi der Tag vo geschter, wen er verby isch, und wi ne Wach i der Nacht.
כִּ֤י אֶ֪לֶף שָׁנִ֡ים בְּֽעֵינֶ֗יךָ כְּי֣וֹם אֶ֭תְמוֹל כִּ֣י יַעֲבֹ֑ר וְאַשְׁמוּרָ֥ה בַלָּֽיְלָה׃
BSB 90:4 For in Your sight a thousand years are but a day that passes, or a watch of the night.
5 Du nimmsch se ewäg, si sy wi ne Schlaf; am Morge sy si wi ds Gras, wo wider wachst:
זְ֭רַמְתָּם שֵׁנָ֣ה יִהְי֑וּ בַּ֝בֹּ֗קֶר כֶּחָצִ֥יר יַחֲלֹֽף׃
BSB 90:5 You sweep them away in their sleep; they are like the new grass of the morning—
שֵׁנָה יִהְיוּ → si sy wi ne Schlaf (in their sleep)
6 Am Morge isch es schön und wachst, am Aabe wird's müed und verdoret.
בַּ֭בֹּקֶר יָצִ֣יץ וְחָלָ֑ף לָ֝עֶ֗רֶב יְמוֹלֵ֥ל וְיָבֵֽשׁ׃
BSB 90:6 in the morning it springs up new, but by evening it fades and withers.
7 Denn mir gö zgrund dür dy Töubi, und dür dy Wuet erchlüpfe mer.
כִּֽי־כָלִ֥ינוּ בְאַפֶּ֑ךָ וּֽבַחֲמָתְךָ֥ נִבְהָֽלְנוּ׃
BSB 90:7 For we are consumed by Your anger and terrified by Your wrath.
8 Du hesch üsi Schuld vor di gstellt, üsi gheime Sünde i ds Liecht vo dym Gsicht.
שַׁתָּ֣ה עֲוֺנֹתֵ֣ינוּ לְנֶגְדֶּ֑ךָ עֲ֝לֻמֵ֗נוּ לִמְא֥וֹר פָּנֶֽיךָ׃
BSB 90:8 You have set our iniquities before You, our secret sins in the light of Your presence.
9 Denn all üsi Täg gö verby under dyr Wuet; mir bringe üsi Jahr z Änd wi ne Huuch.
כִּ֣י כָל־יָ֭מֵינוּ פָּנ֣וּ בְעֶבְרָתֶ֑ךָ כִּלִּ֖ינוּ שָׁנֵ֣ינוּ כְמוֹ־הֶֽגֶה׃
BSB 90:9 For all our days decline in Your fury; we finish our years with a sigh.
כְמוֹ־הֶגֶה → wi ne Huuch (with a sigh)
10 Üses Läbe duuret sibezg Jahr, und wen's höch chunt, achtzg Jahr; und ds Beschte dranne isch Müej und Leid; denn es geit gschwind verby, und mir flüge dervo.
יְמֵֽי־שְׁנוֹתֵ֨ינוּ בָהֶ֥ם שִׁבְעִ֪ים שָׁנָ֡ה וְאִ֤ם בִּגְבוּרֹ֨ת׀שְׁמ֘וֹנִ֤ים שָׁנָ֗ה וְ֭רָהְבָּם עָמָ֣ל וָאָ֑וֶן כִּי־גָ֥ז חִ֝֗ישׁ וַנָּעֻֽפָה׃
BSB 90:10 The length of our days is seventy years — or eighty if we are strong — yet their pride is but labor and sorrow, for they quickly pass, and we fly away.
רָהְבָּם → ds Beschte dranne (their pride)
11 Wär weiss, wi starch dy Töubi isch, und wi gross dy Wuet isch – so gross wi d Ehrfurcht, wo me dir schuldig isch?
מִֽי־י֭וֹדֵעַ עֹ֣ז אַפֶּ֑ךָ וּ֝כְיִרְאָתְךָ֗ עֶבְרָתֶֽךָ׃
BSB 90:11 Who knows the power of Your anger? Your wrath matches the fear You are due.
וּכְיִרְאָתְךָ עֶבְרָתֶךָ → so gross wi d Ehrfurcht, wo me dir schuldig isch (Your wrath matches the fear You are due)
12 Lehr is, üsi Täg z zelle, dass mer es Härz voll Wysheit übercho.
לִמְנ֣וֹת יָ֭מֵינוּ כֵּ֣ן הוֹדַ֑ע וְ֝נָבִ֗א לְבַ֣ב חָכְמָֽה׃
BSB 90:12 So teach us to number our days, that we may present a heart of wisdom.
לְבַב חָכְמָה → es Härz voll Wysheit (a heart of wisdom)
13 Chehr um, HERR! Wi lang no? Hab Erbarme mit dyne Chnächte!
שׁוּבָ֣ה יְ֭הוָה עַד־מָתָ֑י וְ֝הִנָּחֵ֗ם עַל־עֲבָדֶֽיךָ׃
BSB 90:13 Return, O LORD! How long will it be? Have compassion on Your servants.
14 Mach is am Morge satt mit dyr Tröiji, dass mer juble und üüs fröie all üsi Täg!
שַׂבְּעֵ֣נוּ בַבֹּ֣קֶר חַסְדֶּ֑ךָ וּֽנְרַנְּנָ֥ה וְ֝נִשְׂמְחָ֗ה בְּכָל־יָמֵֽינוּ׃
BSB 90:14 Satisfy us in the morning with Your loving devotion, that we may sing for joy and be glad all our days.
15 Mach is froh so mängi Täg, wi du is plaget hesch, so mängi Jahr, wi mer Böses gseh hei!
שַׂ֭מְּחֵנוּ כִּימ֣וֹת עִנִּיתָ֑נוּ שְׁ֝נ֗וֹת רָאִ֥ינוּ רָעָֽה׃
BSB 90:15 Make us glad for as many days as You have afflicted us, for as many years as we have seen evil.
16 Dyni Tate sölle dyne Chnächte sichtbar wärde, und dy Herrlechkeit irne Chind!
יֵרָאֶ֣ה אֶל־עֲבָדֶ֣יךָ פָעֳלֶ֑ךָ וַ֝הֲדָרְךָ֗ עַל־בְּנֵיהֶֽם׃
BSB 90:16 May Your work be shown to Your servants, and Your splendor to their children.
17 Der Herr, üse Gott, söll üüs gnädig sy! Mach ds Wärch vo üsne Händ für üüs fescht, ja, ds Wärch vo üsne Händ – mach's fescht!
וִיהִ֤י׀נֹ֤עַם אֲדֹנָ֥י אֱלֹהֵ֗ינוּ עָ֫לֵ֥ינוּ וּמַעֲשֵׂ֣ה יָ֭דֵינוּ כּוֹנְנָ֥ה עָלֵ֑ינוּ וּֽמַעֲשֵׂ֥ה יָ֝דֵ֗ינוּ כּוֹנְנֵֽהוּ׃
BSB 90:17 May the favor of the Lord our God rest upon us; establish for us the work of our hands— yes, establish the work of our hands!
נֹעַם → gnädig sy (the favor)
