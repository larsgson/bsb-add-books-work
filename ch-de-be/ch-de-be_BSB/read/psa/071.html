1 Bi dir, HERR, suechen i Zueflucht. La mi nid z Schande wärde für geng!
בְּךָֽ־יְהוָ֥ה חָסִ֑יתִי אַל־אֵב֥וֹשָׁה לְעוֹלָֽם׃
BSB 71:1 In You, O LORD, I have taken refuge; let me never be put to shame.
2 I dyr Grächtigkeit tue mi rette und befreie mi! Los mer zue mit dym Ohr und hilf mer!
בְּצִדְקָתְךָ֗ תַּצִּילֵ֥נִי וּֽתְפַלְּטֵ֑נִי הַטֵּֽה־אֵלַ֥י אָ֝זְנְךָ֗ וְהוֹשִׁיעֵֽנִי׃
BSB 71:2 In Your justice, rescue and deliver me; incline Your ear and save me.
3 Bis du mer e Felse, wo n i chan drinn wohne, wo n i geng cha häre cho! Du hesch befole, mi z rette, denn du bisch my Felse und my Burg.
הֱיֵ֤ה לִ֨י׀לְצ֥וּר מָע֡וֹן לָב֗וֹא תָּמִ֗יד צִוִּ֥יתָ לְהוֹשִׁיעֵ֑נִי כִּֽי־סַלְעִ֖י וּמְצוּדָתִ֣י אָֽתָּה׃
BSB 71:3 Be my rock of refuge, where I can always go. Give the command to save me, for You are my rock and my fortress.
צוּר מָעוֹן → e Felse, wo n i chan drinn wohne (a rock of refuge)
מָעוֹן »Wohnung«.
4 My Gott, rett mi us der Hand vom Gottlose, us der Hand vo däm, wo ungrächt und hert isch!
אֱֽלֹהַ֗י פַּ֭לְּטֵנִי מִיַּ֣ד רָשָׁ֑ע מִכַּ֖ף מְעַוֵּ֣ל וְחוֹמֵץ׃
BSB 71:4 Deliver me, O my God, from the hand of the wicked, from the grasp of the unjust and ruthless.
מְעַוֵּל וְחוֹמֵץ → vo däm, wo ungrächt und hert isch (the unjust and ruthless)
5 Denn du bisch my Hoffnig, Herr, HERR, uf di vertroue n i sit i jung bi.
כִּֽי־אַתָּ֥ה תִקְוָתִ֑י אֲדֹנָ֥י יְ֝הוִ֗ה מִבְטַחִ֥י מִנְּעוּרָֽי׃
BSB 71:5 For You are my hope, O Lord GOD, my confidence from my youth.
6 Uf di han i mi verla vo Geburt aa, us em Lyb vo myr Mueter hesch du mi zoge. Dir gilt geng mys Lob.
עָלֶ֤יךָ׀נִסְמַ֬כְתִּי מִבֶּ֗טֶן מִמְּעֵ֣י אִ֭מִּי אַתָּ֣ה גוֹזִ֑י בְּךָ֖ תְהִלָּתִ֣י תָמִֽיד׃
BSB 71:6 I have leaned on You since birth; You pulled me from my mother’s womb. My praise is always for You.
גוֹזִי → hesch du mi zoge (brought me forth)
Unsicheres Wort; wie Ps 22,10.
7 Für vil bin i wi nes Wunder worde, aber du bisch my starchi Zueflucht.
כְּ֭מוֹפֵת הָיִ֣יתִי לְרַבִּ֑ים וְ֝אַתָּ֗ה מַֽחֲסִי־עֹֽז׃
BSB 71:7 I have become a portent to many, but You are my strong refuge.
כְּמוֹפֵת → wi nes Wunder (like a portent)
8 Mys Muul isch voll vo dym Lob, der ganz Tag vo dyr Herrlechkeit.
יִמָּ֣לֵא פִ֭י תְּהִלָּתֶ֑ךָ כָּל־הַ֝יּ֗וֹם תִּפְאַרְתֶּֽךָ׃
BSB 71:8 My mouth is filled with Your praise and with Your splendor all day long.
9 Tue mi nid verstosse im Alter! Wen my Chraft vergeit, verla mi nid!
אַֽל־תַּ֭שְׁלִיכֵנִי לְעֵ֣ת זִקְנָ֑ה כִּכְל֥וֹת כֹּ֝חִ֗י אַֽל־תַּעַזְבֵֽנִי׃
BSB 71:9 Do not discard me in my old age; do not forsake me when my strength fails.
10 Denn myni Finde rede über mi, und die, wo uf mi luege, wo mys Läbe wei, hei zäme abgmacht:
כִּֽי־אָמְר֣וּ אוֹיְבַ֣י לִ֑י וְשֹׁמְרֵ֥י נַ֝פְשִׁ֗י נוֹעֲצ֥וּ יַחְדָּֽו׃
BSB 71:10 For my enemies speak against me, and those who lie in wait for my life conspire,
11 Si säge: „Gott het ne verla, verfolget ne und packet ne, denn es git niemer, wo ne rettet!“
לֵ֭אמֹר אֱלֹהִ֣ים עֲזָב֑וֹ רִֽדְפ֥וּ וְ֝תִפְשׂ֗וּהוּ כִּי־אֵ֥ין מַצִּֽיל׃
BSB 71:11 saying, “God has forsaken him; pursue him and seize him, for there is no one to rescue him.”
12 Gott, bis nid wyt ewäg vo mir! My Gott, chumm mer gschwind z Hilf!
אֱ֭לֹהִים אַל־תִּרְחַ֣ק מִמֶּ֑נִּי אֱ֝לֹהַ֗י לְעֶזְרָ֥תִי חֽוּשָׁה׃
BSB 71:12 Be not far from me, O God. Hurry, O my God, to help me.
13 Si sölle z Schande wärde und verschwinde, die, wo myr Seel Find sy, si sölle mit Spott und Unehr zuedeckt wärde, die, wo mer Böses wei!
יֵבֹ֣שׁוּ יִכְלוּ֮ שֹׂטְנֵ֪י נַ֫פְשִׁ֥י יַֽעֲט֣וּ חֶ֭רְפָּה וּכְלִמָּ֑ה מְ֝בַקְשֵׁ֗י רָעָתִֽי׃
BSB 71:13 May the accusers of my soul be ashamed and consumed; may those who seek my harm be covered with scorn and disgrace.
14 Aber ig wott geng hoffe und di no meh lobe als vorhär.
וַ֭אֲנִי תָּמִ֣יד אֲיַחֵ֑ל וְ֝הוֹסַפְתִּ֗י עַל־כָּל־תְּהִלָּתֶֽךָ׃
BSB 71:14 But I will always hope and will praise You more and more.
15 Mys Muul verzellt vo dyr Grächtigkeit, der ganz Tag vo dyr Rettig, o wen i nid weiss, wi vil es isch.
פִּ֤י׀יְסַפֵּ֬ר צִדְקָתֶ֗ךָ כָּל־הַיּ֥וֹם תְּשׁוּעָתֶ֑ךָ כִּ֤י לֹ֖א יָדַ֣עְתִּי סְפֹרֽוֹת׃
BSB 71:15 My mouth will declare Your righteousness and Your salvation all day long, though I cannot know their full measure.
לֹא יָדַעְתִּי סְפֹרוֹת → wen i nid weiss, wi vil es isch (though I cannot know their full measure)
16 I chume mit de grosse Tate vom Herr, em HERR; i wott dyr Grächtigkeit rüeme, nume dyr!
אָב֗וֹא בִּ֭גְבֻרוֹת אֲדֹנָ֣י יְהוִ֑ה אַזְכִּ֖יר צִדְקָתְךָ֣ לְבַדֶּֽךָ׃
BSB 71:16 I will come in the strength of the Lord GOD; I will proclaim Your righteousness — Yours alone.
17 Gott, du hesch mi glehrt sit i jung bi, und bis hütt verzelle n i vo dyne Wunder.
אֱֽלֹהִ֗ים לִמַּדְתַּ֥נִי מִנְּעוּרָ֑י וְעַד־הֵ֝֗נָּה אַגִּ֥יד נִפְלְאוֹתֶֽיךָ׃
BSB 71:17 O God, You have taught me from my youth, and to this day I proclaim Your marvelous deeds.
18 Und o wen i alt bi und wyssi Haar ha, Gott, verla mi nid, bis i dy Arm verchündet ha der Generation, allne, wo no chöme, dyni Macht!
וְגַ֤ם עַד־זִקְנָ֨ה׀וְשֵׂיבָה֮ אֱלֹהִ֪ים אַֽל־תַּעַ֫זְבֵ֥נִי עַד־אַגִּ֣יד זְרוֹעֲךָ֣ לְד֑וֹר לְכָל־יָ֝ב֗וֹא גְּבוּרָתֶֽךָ׃
BSB 71:18 Even when I am old and gray, do not forsake me, O God, until I proclaim Your power to the next generation, Your might to all who are to come.
זִקְנָה וְשֵׂיבָה → alt … wyssi Haar (old and gray)
19 Dy Grächtigkeit, Gott, geit bis i d Höchi; du hesch Grosses gmacht. Gott, wär isch wi du?
וְצִדְקָתְךָ֥ אֱלֹהִ֗ים עַד־מָ֫ר֥וֹם אֲשֶׁר־עָשִׂ֥יתָ גְדֹל֑וֹת אֱ֝לֹהִ֗ים מִ֣י כָמֽוֹךָ׃
BSB 71:19 Your righteousness reaches to the heavens, O God, You who have done great things. Who, O God, is like You?
20 Du hesch mi vil Not und Eländ la gseh; du wirsch mi wider läbig mache und mi us de Tiefine vo der Ärde wider usefüere.
אֲשֶׁ֤ר הִרְאִיתַ֨נִי׀צָר֥וֹת רַבּ֗וֹת וְרָ֫ע֥וֹת תָּשׁ֥וּב תְּחַיֵּ֑ינִי וּֽמִתְּהֹמ֥וֹת הָ֝אָ֗רֶץ תָּשׁ֥וּב תַּעֲלֵֽנִי׃
BSB 71:20 Though You have shown me many troubles and misfortunes, You will revive me once again. Even from the depths of the earth You will bring me back up.
מִתְּהֹמוֹת הָאָרֶץ → us de Tiefine vo der Ärde (from the depths of the earth)
21 Mach my Grössi meh und chehr di zu mir und tue mi tröschte!
תֶּ֤רֶב׀גְּֽדֻלָּתִ֗י וְתִסֹּ֥ב תְּֽנַחֲמֵֽנִי׃
BSB 71:21 You will increase my honor and comfort me once again.
22 O ig wott dir danke mit der Harfe für dy Tröiji, my Gott; i wott dir Psalme singe mit der Zither, du Heilige vo Israel!
גַּם־אֲנִ֤י׀אוֹדְךָ֣ בִכְלִי־נֶבֶל֮ אֲמִתְּךָ֪ אֱלֹ֫הָ֥י אֲזַמְּרָ֣ה לְךָ֣ בְכִנּ֑וֹר קְ֝ד֗וֹשׁ יִשְׂרָאֵֽל׃
BSB 71:22 So I will praise You with the harp for Your faithfulness, O my God; I will sing praise to You with the lyre, O Holy One of Israel.
23 Myni Lippe sölle juble, wen i dir Psalme singe, und o my Seel, wo du erlöst hesch.
תְּרַנֵּ֣נָּ֣ה שְׂ֭פָתַי כִּ֣י אֲזַמְּרָה־לָּ֑ךְ וְ֝נַפְשִׁ֗י אֲשֶׁ֣ר פָּדִֽיתָ׃
BSB 71:23 When I sing praise to You my lips will shout for joy, along with my soul, which You have redeemed.
24 O my Zunge redt der ganz Tag vo dyr Grächtigkeit, denn die sy z Schande worde und hei sech müesse schäme, wo mer Böses wei.
גַּם־לְשׁוֹנִ֗י כָּל־הַ֭יּוֹם תֶּהְגֶּ֣ה צִדְקָתֶ֑ךָ כִּי־בֹ֥שׁוּ כִֽי־חָ֝פְר֗וּ מְבַקְשֵׁ֥י רָעָתִֽי׃
BSB 71:24 My tongue will indeed proclaim Your righteousness all day long, for those who seek my harm are disgraced and confounded.
