1 Danket em HERR, denn er isch guet, sy Tröiji blybt für geng!
הוֹד֣וּ לַיהוָ֣ה כִּי־ט֑וֹב כִּ֖י לְעוֹלָ֣ם חַסְדּֽוֹ׃
BSB 118:1 Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever.
2 Israel söll doch säge: „Sy Tröiji blybt für geng!“
יֹֽאמַר־נָ֥א יִשְׂרָאֵ֑ל כִּ֖י לְעוֹלָ֣ם חַסְדּֽוֹ׃
BSB 118:2 Let Israel say, “His loving devotion endures forever.”
3 Ds Huus vom Aaron söll doch säge: „Sy Tröiji blybt für geng!“
יֹֽאמְרוּ־נָ֥א בֵֽית־אַהֲרֹ֑ן כִּ֖י לְעוֹלָ֣ם חַסְדּֽוֹ׃
BSB 118:3 Let the house of Aaron say, “His loving devotion endures forever.”
4 Die, wo Ehrfurcht vor em HERR hei, sölle doch säge: „Sy Tröiji blybt für geng!“
יֹֽאמְרוּ־נָ֭א יִרְאֵ֣י יְהוָ֑ה כִּ֖י לְעוֹלָ֣ם חַסְדּֽוֹ׃
BSB 118:4 Let those who fear the LORD say, “His loving devotion endures forever.”
5 Us der Not han i zum HERR grüeft; der HERR het mer Antwort ggä und mi i ds Wyte gstellt.
מִֽן־הַ֭מֵּצַ֥ר קָרָ֣אתִי יָּ֑הּ עָנָ֖נִי בַמֶּרְחָ֣ב יָֽהּ׃
BSB 118:5 In my distress I called to the LORD, and He answered and set me free.
בַמֶּרְחָב → i ds Wyte (set me free)
6 Der HERR isch für mi, i ha ke Angscht. Was cha mir e Mönsch scho mache?
יְהוָ֣ה לִ֭י לֹ֣א אִירָ֑א מַה־יַּעֲשֶׂ֖ה לִ֣י אָדָֽם׃
BSB 118:6 The LORD is on my side; I will not be afraid. What can man do to me?
7 Der HERR isch uf myr Syte als eine, wo mer hilft, und i cha uf die abe luege, wo mi hasse.
יְהוָ֣ה לִ֭י בְּעֹזְרָ֑י וַ֝אֲנִ֗י אֶרְאֶ֥ה בְשֹׂנְאָֽי׃
BSB 118:7 The LORD is on my side; He is my helper. Therefore I will look in triumph on those who hate me.
8 Es isch besser, Zueflucht z sueche bim HERR, als uf Mönsche z vertroue.
ט֗וֹב לַחֲס֥וֹת בַּיהוָ֑ה מִ֝בְּטֹ֗חַ בָּאָדָֽם׃
BSB 118:8 It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to trust in man.
9 Es isch besser, Zueflucht z sueche bim HERR, als uf Fürschte z vertroue.
ט֗וֹב לַחֲס֥וֹת בַּיהוָ֑ה מִ֝בְּטֹ֗חַ בִּנְדִיבִֽים׃
BSB 118:9 It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to trust in princes.
10 Alli Völker sy rings um mi gsi; im Name vom HERR ha n i se ja abgwehrt.
כָּל־גּוֹיִ֥ם סְבָב֑וּנִי בְּשֵׁ֥ם יְ֝הוָ֗ה כִּ֣י אֲמִילַֽם׃
BSB 118:10 All the nations surrounded me, but in the name of the LORD I cut them off.
אֲמִילַם → ha n i se abgwehrt (I cut them off)

11 Si sy rings um mi gsi, ja, rings um mi; im Name vom HERR ha n i se ja abgwehrt.
סַבּ֥וּנִי גַם־סְבָב֑וּנִי בְּשֵׁ֥ם יְ֝הוָ֗ה כִּ֣י אֲמִילַֽם׃
BSB 118:11 They surrounded me on every side, but in the name of the LORD I cut them off.
12 Si sy rings um mi gsi wi Imbi, si sy usgange wi nes Füür i de Dorne; im Name vom HERR ha n i se ja abgwehrt.
סַבּ֤וּנִי כִדְבוֹרִ֗ים דֹּ֭עֲכוּ כְּאֵ֣שׁ קוֹצִ֑ים בְּשֵׁ֥ם יְ֝הוָ֗ה כִּ֣י אֲמִילַֽם׃
BSB 118:12 They swarmed around me like bees, but they were extinguished like burning thorns; in the name of the LORD I cut them off.

13 Me het mi gstosse, dass i gheie söll, aber der HERR het mer ghulfe.
דַּחֹ֣ה דְחִיתַ֣נִי לִנְפֹּ֑ל וַ֖יהוָ֣ה עֲזָרָֽנִי׃
BSB 118:13 I was pushed so hard I was falling, but the LORD helped me.
14 My Chraft und my Lied isch der HERR, und er isch mir zur Rettig worde.
עָזִּ֣י וְזִמְרָ֣ת יָ֑הּ וַֽיְהִי־לִ֝֗י לִֽישׁוּעָֽה׃
BSB 118:14 The LORD is my strength and my song, and He has become my salvation.
15 Me ghört Fröid und Rettig i de Zält vo de Grächte: „D rächti Hand vom HERR tuet grossi Tate!
ק֤וֹל׀רִנָּ֬ה וִֽישׁוּעָ֗ה בְּאָהֳלֵ֥י צַדִּיקִ֑ים יְמִ֥ין יְ֝הוָה עֹ֣שָׂה חָֽיִל׃
BSB 118:15 Shouts of joy and salvation resound in the tents of the righteous: “The right hand of the LORD performs with valor!
עֹשָׂה חָיִל → tuet grossi Tate (performs valiantly)
16 D rächti Hand vom HERR isch höch, d rächti Hand vom HERR tuet grossi Tate!“
יְמִ֣ין יְ֭הוָה רוֹמֵמָ֑ה יְמִ֥ין יְ֝הוָה עֹ֣שָׂה חָֽיִל׃
BSB 118:16 The right hand of the LORD is exalted! The right hand of the LORD performs with valor!”
17 I stirbe nid, nei, i blybe läbig und verzelle d Wärch vom HERR.
לֹֽא אָמ֥וּת כִּי־אֶֽחְיֶ֑ה וַ֝אֲסַפֵּ֗ר מַֽעֲשֵׂ֥י יָֽהּ׃
BSB 118:17 I will not die, but I will live and proclaim what the LORD has done.
18 Der HERR het mi hert gstraft, aber em Tod het er mi nid usgliferet.
יַסֹּ֣ר יִסְּרַ֣נִּי יָּ֑הּ וְ֝לַמָּ֗וֶת לֹ֣א נְתָנָֽנִי׃
BSB 118:18 The LORD disciplined me severely, but He has not given me over to death.
19 Tüet mer d Tor vo der Grächtigkeit uuf! I wott dür se ynega und em HERR danke.
פִּתְחוּ־לִ֥י שַׁעֲרֵי־צֶ֑דֶק אָֽבֹא־בָ֝ם אוֹדֶ֥ה יָֽהּ׃
BSB 118:19 Open to me the gates of righteousness, that I may enter and give thanks to the LORD.
20 Das isch ds Tor vom HERR; d Grächte gö dür ihns yne.
זֶֽה־הַשַּׁ֥עַר לַיהוָ֑ה צַ֝דִּיקִ֗ים יָבֹ֥אוּ בֽוֹ׃
BSB 118:20 This is the gate of the LORD; the righteous shall enter through it.
21 I danke dir, denn du hesch mer Antwort ggä und bisch mir zur Rettig worde.
א֭וֹדְךָ כִּ֣י עֲנִיתָ֑נִי וַתְּהִי־לִ֝֗י לִֽישׁוּעָֽה׃
BSB 118:21 I will give You thanks, for You have answered me, and You have become my salvation.
22 Der Stei, wo d Boulüt verachtet hei, dä isch zum Eckstei worde.
אֶ֭בֶן מָאֲס֣וּ הַבּוֹנִ֑ים הָ֝יְתָ֗ה לְרֹ֣אשׁ פִּנָּֽה׃
BSB 118:22 The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone.
23 Vom HERR isch das cho; das isch es Wunder i üsnen Ouge.
מֵאֵ֣ת יְ֭הוָה הָ֣יְתָה זֹּ֑את הִ֖יא נִפְלָ֣את בְּעֵינֵֽינוּ׃
BSB 118:23 This is from the LORD, and it is marvelous in our eyes.
24 Das isch der Tag, won der HERR gmacht het; mir wei juble und üüs fröie a ihm!
זֶה־הַ֭יּוֹם עָשָׂ֣ה יְהוָ֑ה נָגִ֖ילָה וְנִשְׂמְחָ֣ה בֽוֹ׃
BSB 118:24 This is the day that the LORD has made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.
25 Ach, HERR, hilf doch! Ach, HERR, la's doch grate!
אָנָּ֣א יְ֭הוָה הוֹשִׁ֘יעָ֥ה נָּ֑א אָֽנָּ֥א יְ֝הוָ֗ה הַצְלִ֘יחָ֥ה נָּֽא׃
BSB 118:25 O LORD, save us, we pray. We beseech You, O LORD, cause us to prosper!
26 Globet syg dä, wo chunt im Name vom HERR! Mir sägne öich us em Huus vom HERR.
בָּר֣וּךְ הַ֭בָּא בְּשֵׁ֣ם יְהוָ֑ה בֵּ֝רַֽכְנוּכֶ֗ם מִבֵּ֥ית יְהוָֽה׃
BSB 118:26 Blessed is he who comes in the name of the LORD. From the house of the LORD we bless you.
27 Der HERR isch Gott, und er het üüs Liecht ggä. Bindet ds Opfer für ds Fescht mit Seili bis a d Hörner vom Altar!
אֵ֤ל׀יְהוָה֮ וַיָּ֪אֶר לָ֥נוּ אִסְרוּ־חַ֥ג בַּעֲבֹתִ֑ים עַד־קַ֝רְנ֗וֹת הַמִּזְבֵּֽחַ׃
BSB 118:27 The LORD is God; He has made His light to shine upon us. Bind the festal sacrifice with cords to the horns of the altar.
אִסְרוּ־חַג → Bindet ds Opfer für ds Fescht (Bind the festal sacrifice)
28 Du bisch my Gott, und i wott dir danke; my Gott, i wott di rüeme.
אֵלִ֣י אַתָּ֣ה וְאוֹדֶ֑ךָּ אֱ֝לֹהַ֗י אֲרוֹמְמֶֽךָּ׃
BSB 118:28 You are my God, and I will give You thanks. You are my God, and I will exalt You.
29 Danket em HERR, denn er isch guet, sy Tröiji blybt für geng!
הוֹד֣וּ לַיהוָ֣ה כִּי־ט֑וֹב כִּ֖י לְעוֹלָ֣ם חַסְדּֽוֹ׃
