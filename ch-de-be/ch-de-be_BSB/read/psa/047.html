BSB 47:2 How awesome is the LORD Most High, the great King over all the earth!

4 Er tuet Völker under üüs zwinge und Natione under üsi Füess.
יַדְבֵּ֣ר עַמִּ֣ים תַּחְתֵּ֑ינוּ וּ֝לְאֻמִּ֗ים תַּ֣חַת רַגְלֵֽינוּ׃
BSB 47:3 He subdues nations beneath us, and peoples under our feet.
5 Er het für üüs üses Erb usgläse, der Stolz vom Jakob, dä er lieb het. Sela
יִבְחַר־לָ֥נוּ אֶת־נַחֲלָתֵ֑נוּ אֶ֥ת גְּא֨וֹן יַעֲקֹ֖ב אֲשֶׁר־אָהֵ֣ב סֶֽלָה׃
BSB 47:4 He chooses our inheritance for us, the pride of Jacob, whom He loves. Selah
גְּאוֹן יַעֲקֹב → der Stolz vom Jakob (the pride of Jacob)
6 Gott isch ufegstige mit luter Juble, der HERR mit em Ton vo der Posuune.
עָלָ֣ה אֱ֭לֹהִים בִּתְרוּעָ֑ה יְ֝הֹוָ֗ה בְּק֣וֹל שׁוֹפָֽר׃
BSB 47:5 God has ascended amid shouts of joy, the LORD with the sound of the horn.

BSB 47:6 Sing praises to God, sing praises; sing praises to our King, sing praises!
8 Denn Gott isch der Chünig vo der ganze Ärde; singet ihm es Lied!
כִּ֤י מֶ֖לֶךְ כָּל־הָאָ֥רֶץ אֱלֹהִ֗ים זַמְּר֥וּ מַשְׂכִּֽיל׃
BSB 47:7 For God is King of all the earth; sing to Him a psalm of praise.

9 Gott isch Chünig über d Völker, Gott sitzt uf sym heilige Thron.
מָלַ֣ךְ אֱ֭לֹהִים עַל־גּוֹיִ֑ם אֱ֝לֹהִ֗ים יָשַׁ֤ב׀עַל־כִּסֵּ֬א קָדְשֽׁוֹ׃
BSB 47:8 God reigns over the nations; God is seated on His holy throne.
10 D Fürschte vo de Völker sy zämecho als Volk vom Gott vom Abraham; denn Gott ghöre d Schild vo der Ärde; er isch ganz höch obe.
נְדִ֘יבֵ֤י עַמִּ֨ים׀נֶאֱסָ֗פוּ עַם֮ אֱלֹהֵ֪י אַבְרָ֫הָ֥ם כִּ֣י לֵֽ֭אלֹהִים מָֽגִנֵּי־אֶ֗רֶץ מְאֹ֣ד נַעֲלָֽה׃
BSB 47:9 The nobles of the nations have assembled as the people of the God of Abraham; for the shields of the earth belong to God; He is highly exalted.
מָגִנֵּי־אֶרֶץ → d Schild vo der Ärde (the shields of the earth)
