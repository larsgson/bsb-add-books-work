1 Für e Chorleiter. Nach der Wys „Lilie vom Zügnis“. Vom Asaf. E Psalm.
לַמְנַצֵּ֥חַ אֶל־שֹׁשַׁנִּ֑ים עֵד֖וּת לְאָסָ֣ף מִזְמֽוֹר׃
2 Hirt vo Israel, los! Du, wo der Josef füersch wi ne Herde, du, wo über de Cherube uf em Thron sitzisch, zeig di im Glanz!
רֹ֘עֵ֤ה יִשְׂרָאֵ֨ל׀הַאֲזִ֗ינָה נֹהֵ֣ג כַּצֹּ֣אן יוֹסֵ֑ף יֹשֵׁ֖ב הַכְּרוּבִ֣ים הוֹפִֽיעָה׃
BSB 80:1 For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Lilies of the Covenant.” A Psalm of Asaph. Hear us, O Shepherd of Israel, who leads Joseph like a flock; You who sit enthroned between the cherubim, shine forth
יֹשֵׁב הַכְּרוּבִים → wo über de Cherube uf em Thron sitzisch (enthroned between the cherubim)
3 Vor Efraim und Benjamin und Manasse wecke dy Macht und chumm üüs z Hilf!
לִפְנֵ֤י אֶפְרַ֨יִם׀וּבִנְיָ֘מִ֤ן וּמְנַשֶּׁ֗ה עוֹרְרָ֥ה אֶת־גְּבֽוּרָתֶ֑ךָ וּלְכָ֖ה לִישֻׁעָ֣תָה לָּֽנוּ׃
BSB 80:2 before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh. Rally Your mighty power and come to save us.
4 Gott, bring is zrügg und lass dys Gsicht lüüchte, dass mer grettet wärde!
אֱלֹהִ֥ים הֲשִׁיבֵ֑נוּ וְהָאֵ֥ר פָּ֝נֶ֗יךָ וְנִוָּשֵֽׁעָה׃
BSB 80:3 Restore us, O God, and cause Your face to shine upon us, that we may be saved.
5 HERR, Gott Zebaoth, wi lang no bisch du toub trotz de Gebätt vo dym Volk?
יְהוָ֣ה אֱלֹהִ֣ים צְבָא֑וֹת עַד־מָתַ֥י עָ֝שַׁ֗נְתָּ בִּתְפִלַּ֥ת עַמֶּֽךָ׃
BSB 80:4 O LORD God of Hosts, how long will Your anger smolder against the prayers of Your people?
6 Du hesch ne Träne z ässe ggä wi Brot und ne Träne z trinke ggä, e ganzi Schüssle voll.
הֶ֭אֱכַלְתָּם לֶ֣חֶם דִּמְעָ֑ה וַ֝תַּשְׁקֵ֗מוֹ בִּדְמָע֥וֹת שָׁלִֽישׁ׃
BSB 80:5 You fed them with the bread of tears and made them drink the full measure of their tears.
שָׁלִישׁ → e ganzi Schüssle voll (in great measure)
Ein Hohlmass.
7 Du machsch üüs zum Grund für Stryt für üsi Nachbere, und üsi Finde spotte über üüs.
תְּשִׂימֵ֣נוּ מָ֭דוֹן לִשְׁכֵנֵ֑ינוּ וְ֝אֹיְבֵ֗ינוּ יִלְעֲגוּ־לָֽמוֹ׃
BSB 80:6 You make us contend with our neighbors; our enemies mock us.
מָדוֹן → zum Grund für Stryt (a source of contention)
8 Gott Zebaoth, bring is zrügg und lass dys Gsicht lüüchte, dass mer grettet wärde!
אֱלֹהִ֣ים צְבָא֣וֹת הֲשִׁיבֵ֑נוּ וְהָאֵ֥ר פָּ֝נֶ֗יךָ וְנִוָּשֵֽׁעָה׃
BSB 80:7 Restore us, O God of Hosts, and cause Your face to shine upon us, that we may be saved.
9 E Räbstock hesch du us Ägypte usegholt, du hesch Völker vertribe und ne aapflanzet.
גֶּ֭פֶן מִמִּצְרַ֣יִם תַּסִּ֑יעַ תְּגָרֵ֥שׁ גּ֝וֹיִ֗ם וַתִּטָּעֶֽהָ׃
BSB 80:8 You uprooted a vine from Egypt; You drove out the nations and transplanted it.
גֶּפֶן → E Räbstock (a vine)
10 Du hesch vor ihm Platz gmacht, und er het Wurzle gschlage und ds Land gfüllt.
פִּנִּ֥יתָ לְפָנֶ֑יהָ וַתַּשְׁרֵ֥שׁ שָׁ֝רָשֶׁ֗יהָ וַתְּמַלֵּא־אָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 80:9 You cleared the ground for it, and it took root and filled the land.
11 D Bärge sy mit sym Schatte zuedeckt worde, und mit syne Escht di gwaltige Zedere.
כָּסּ֣וּ הָרִ֣ים צִלָּ֑הּ וַ֝עֲנָפֶ֗יהָ אַֽרְזֵי־אֵֽל׃
BSB 80:10 The mountains were covered by its shade, and the mighty cedars with its branches.
12 Er het syni Escht bis a ds Meer usgstreckt und syni junge Schoss bis a Strom.
תְּשַׁלַּ֣ח קְצִירֶ֣הָ עַד־יָ֑ם וְאֶל־נָ֝הָ֗ר יֽוֹנְקוֹתֶֽיהָ׃
BSB 80:11 It sent out its branches to the Sea, and its shoots toward the River.
13 Warum hesch du syni Muure abgrisse, so dass alli, wo verbychunt, drab nimmt?
לָ֭מָּה פָּרַ֣צְתָּ גְדֵרֶ֑יהָ וְ֝אָר֗וּהָ כָּל־עֹ֥בְרֵי דָֽרֶךְ׃
BSB 80:12 Why have You broken down its walls, so that all who pass by pick its fruit?
14 D wildi Sou us em Wald frisst ne ab, und ds Tier vom Fäld frisst ne ab.
יְכַרְסְמֶ֣נָּֽה חֲזִ֣יר מִיָּ֑עַרוְזִ֖יז שָׂדַ֣י יִרְעֶֽנָּה׃
BSB 80:13 The boar from the forest ravages it, and the creatures of the field feed upon it.
חֲזִיר מִיָּעַר → D wildi Sou us em Wald (The boar from the forest)
15 Gott Zebaoth, chehr doch um! Lueg vom Himel abe und lueg und nimm di a däm Räbstock aa,
אֱלֹהִ֣ים צְבָאוֹת֮ שֽׁ֫וּב־נָ֥א הַבֵּ֣ט מִשָּׁמַ֣יִם וּרְאֵ֑ה וּ֝פְקֹ֗ד גֶּ֣פֶן זֹֽאת׃
BSB 80:14 Return, O God of Hosts, we pray! Look down from heaven and see! Attend to this vine—
16 a der Pflanz, wo dy rächti Hand aapflanzet het, und a däm Sohn, wo du für di starch gmacht hesch!
וְ֭כַנָּה אֲשֶׁר־נָטְעָ֣ה יְמִינֶ֑ךָ וְעַל־בֵּ֝֗ן אִמַּ֥צְתָּה לָּֽךְ׃
BSB 80:15 the root Your right hand has planted, the son You have raised up for Yourself.
וְכַנָּה → a der Pflanz (the root)
17 Er isch mit Füür verbrönnt, abghoue; vor em Schimpfe vo dym Gsicht gö si zgrund.
שְׂרֻפָ֣ה בָאֵ֣שׁ כְּסוּחָ֑ה מִגַּעֲרַ֖ת פָּנֶ֣יךָ יֹאבֵֽדוּ׃
BSB 80:16 Your vine has been cut down and burned; they perish at the rebuke of Your countenance.
18 Dy Hand söll uf em Ma vo dyr rächte Hand sy, uf em Mönschesuhn, wo du für di starch gmacht hesch!
תְּֽהִי־יָ֭דְךָ עַל־אִ֣ישׁ יְמִינֶ֑ךָ עַל־בֶּן־אָ֝דָ֗ם אִמַּ֥צְתָּ לָּֽךְ׃
BSB 80:17 Let Your hand be upon the man at Your right hand, on the son of man You have raised up for Yourself.
בֶּן־אָדָם → Mönschesuhn (son of man)
19 De wei mer nid vo dir abfalle. Mach is läbig, de wei mer dy Name aarüefe!
וְלֹא־נָס֥וֹג מִמֶּ֑ךָּ תְּ֝חַיֵּ֗נוּ וּבְשִׁמְךָ֥ נִקְרָֽא׃
BSB 80:18 Then we will not turn away from You; revive us, and we will call on Your name.
20 HERR, Gott Zebaoth, bring is zrügg, lass dys Gsicht lüüchte, dass mer grettet wärde!
יְה֘וָ֤ה אֱלֹהִ֣ים צְבָא֣וֹת הֲשִׁיבֵ֑נוּ הָאֵ֥ר פָּ֝נֶ֗יךָ וְנִוָּשֵֽׁעָה׃
BSB 80:19 Restore us, O LORD God of Hosts; cause Your face to shine upon us, that we may be saved.
