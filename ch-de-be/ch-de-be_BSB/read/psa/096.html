1 Singet em HERR es nöis Lied, singet em HERR, alli Länder!
שִׁ֣ירוּ לַ֭יהוָה שִׁ֣יר חָדָ֑שׁ שִׁ֥ירוּ לַ֝יהוָ֗ה כָּל־הָאָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 96:1 Sing to the LORD a new song; sing to the LORD, all the earth.
כָּל־הָאָרֶץ → alli Länder (all the earth)
Anrede an die Länder/Bewohner.
2 Singet em HERR, lobet sy Name! Verchündet vo Tag zu Tag sy Rettig!
שִׁ֣ירוּ לַ֭יהוָה בָּרֲכ֣וּ שְׁמ֑וֹ בַּשְּׂר֥וּ מִיּֽוֹם־לְ֝י֗וֹם יְשׁוּעָתֽוֹ׃
BSB 96:2 Sing to the LORD, bless His name; proclaim His salvation day after day.
3 Brichtet under de Völker vo syr Herrlechkeit, under allne Natione vo syne Wunder!
סַפְּר֣וּ בַגּוֹיִ֣ם כְּבוֹד֑וֹ בְּכָל־הָֽ֝עַמִּ֗ים נִפְלְאוֹתָֽיו׃
BSB 96:3 Declare His glory among the nations, His wonders among all peoples.
4 Denn der HERR isch gross und sehr z lobe, me muess ne förchte meh als alli Götter.
כִּ֥י גָ֘ד֤וֹל יְהוָ֣ה וּמְהֻלָּ֣ל מְאֹ֑ד נוֹרָ֥א ה֝֗וּא עַל־כָּל־אֱלֹהִֽים׃
BSB 96:4 For great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised; He is to be feared above all gods.
5 Denn alli Götter vo de Völker sy Götze, aber der HERR het der Himel gmacht.
כִּ֤י׀כָּל־אֱלֹהֵ֣י הָעַמִּ֣ים אֱלִילִ֑ים וַֽ֝יהוָ֗ה שָׁמַ֥יִם עָשָֽׂה׃
BSB 96:5 For all the gods of the nations are idols, but it is the LORD who made the heavens.
6 Pracht und Majestät sy vor ihm, Chraft und Schöni i sym Heiligtum.
הוֹד־וְהָדָ֥ר לְפָנָ֑יו עֹ֥ז וְ֝תִפְאֶ֗רֶת בְּמִקְדָּשֽׁוֹ׃
BSB 96:6 Splendor and majesty are before Him; strength and beauty fill His sanctuary.
7 Gät em HERR, dihr Familie vo de Völker, gät em HERR Herrlechkeit und Macht!
הָב֣וּ לַ֭יהוָה מִשְׁפְּח֣וֹת עַמִּ֑ים הָב֥וּ לַ֝יהוָ֗ה כָּב֥וֹד וָעֹֽז׃
BSB 96:7 Ascribe to the LORD, O families of the nations, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength.
8 Gät em HERR d Ehr, wo sym Name ghört, bringet es Opfer und chömet i sy Vorhof!
הָב֣וּ לַ֭יהוָה כְּב֣וֹד שְׁמ֑וֹ שְׂאֽוּ־מִ֝נְחָ֗ה וּבֹ֥אוּ לְחַצְרוֹתָֽיו׃
BSB 96:8 Ascribe to the LORD the glory due His name; bring an offering and enter His courts.
חַצְרוֹתָיו → i sy Vorhof (into His courts)
Singular wie Ps 84; 92 (Korpusform).
9 Bättet der HERR aa i der heilige Pracht! Zitteret vor ihm, alli Länder!
הִשְׁתַּחֲו֣וּ לַ֭יהוָה בְּהַדְרַת־קֹ֑דֶשׁ חִ֥ילוּ מִ֝פָּנָ֗יו כָּל־הָאָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 96:9 Worship the LORD in the splendor of His holiness; tremble before Him, all the earth.
10 Säget under de Völker: „Der HERR isch Chünig!“ Ja, d Wält steit fescht, si chunt nid is Wanke. Er richtet d Völker grächt.
אִמְר֤וּ בַגּוֹיִ֨ם׀יְה֘וָ֤ה מָלָ֗ךְ אַף־תִּכּ֣וֹן תֵּ֭בֵל בַּל־תִּמּ֑וֹט יָדִ֥ין עַ֝מִּ֗ים בְּמֵישָׁרִֽים׃
BSB 96:10 Declare among the nations: “The LORD reigns!” The world is firmly established; it cannot be moved; He will judge the peoples with equity.
11 Der Himel söll sech fröie, und d Ärde söll juble! Ds Meer söll ruusche und alls, wo drinn isch!
יִשְׂמְח֣וּ הַ֭שָּׁמַיִם וְתָגֵ֣ל הָאָ֑רֶץ יִֽרְעַ֥ם הַ֝יָּ֗ם וּמְלֹאֽוֹ׃
BSB 96:11 Let the heavens be glad and the earth rejoice; let the sea resound, and all that fills it.
12 Ds Fäld söll sech fröie und alls, wo druffe isch! De jublet alli Böim vom Wald
יַעֲלֹ֣ז שָׂ֭דַי וְכָל־אֲשֶׁר־בּ֑וֹ אָ֥ז יְ֝רַנְּנ֗וּ כָּל־עֲצֵי־יָֽעַר׃
BSB 96:12 Let the fields exult, and all that is in them. Then all the trees of the forest will sing for joy
13 vor em HERR; denn er chunt, ja, er chunt, für d Ärde z richte. Er richtet d Wält mit Grächtigkeit und d Völker mit syr Zueverlässigkeit.
לִפְנֵ֤י יְהוָ֨ה׀כִּ֬י בָ֗א כִּ֥י בָא֮ לִשְׁפֹּ֪ט הָ֫אָ֥רֶץ יִשְׁפֹּֽט־תֵּבֵ֥ל בְּצֶ֑דֶק וְ֝עַמִּ֗ים בֶּאֱמוּנָתֽוֹ׃
BSB 96:13 before the LORD, for He is coming— He is coming to judge the earth. He will judge the world in righteousness and the peoples in His faithfulness.
בֶּאֱמוּנָתוֹ → mit syr Zueverlässigkeit (in His faithfulness)
