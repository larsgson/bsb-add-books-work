1 Halleluja! Glücklech isch der Ma, wo Ehrfurcht vor em HERR het, wo a syne Gebot grossi Fröid het!
הַ֥לְלוּ יָ֨הּ׀אַשְׁרֵי־אִ֭ישׁ יָרֵ֣א אֶת־יְהוָ֑ה בְּ֝מִצְוֺתָ֗יו חָפֵ֥ץ מְאֹֽד׃
BSB 112:1 Hallelujah! Blessed is the man who fears the LORD, who greatly delights in His commandments.
2 Syni Nachkomme wärde mächtig sy im Land; d Generation vo de Ufrichtige wird gsägnet.
גִּבּ֣וֹר בָּ֭אָרֶץ יִהְיֶ֣ה זַרְע֑וֹ דּ֭וֹר יְשָׁרִ֣ים יְבֹרָֽךְ׃
BSB 112:2 His descendants will be mighty in the land; the generation of the upright will be blessed.
3 Vermöge und Rychtum sy i sym Huus, und sy Grächtigkeit blybt für geng.
הוֹן־וָעֹ֥שֶׁר בְּבֵית֑וֹ וְ֝צִדְקָת֗וֹ עֹמֶ֥דֶת לָעַֽד׃
BSB 112:3 Wealth and riches are in his house, and his righteousness endures forever.
4 Im Fyschtere geit für di Ufrichtige es Liecht uuf, gnädig und barmhärzig und grächt.
זָ֘רַ֤ח בַּחֹ֣שֶׁךְ א֭וֹרלַיְשָׁרִ֑ים חַנּ֖וּן וְרַח֣וּם וְצַדִּֽיק׃
BSB 112:4 Light dawns in the darkness for the upright — for the gracious, compassionate, and righteous.
זָרַח … אוֹר → geit … es Liecht uuf (Light dawns)
5 Guet geit's em Ma, wo gnädig isch und anderne tuet lehne, wo syni Sache mit Rächt füert.
טֽוֹב־אִ֭ישׁ חוֹנֵ֣ן וּמַלְוֶ֑ה יְכַלְכֵּ֖ל דְּבָרָ֣יו בְּמִשְׁפָּֽט׃
BSB 112:5 It is well with the man who is generous and lends freely, whose affairs are guided by justice.
חוֹנֵן וּמַלְוֶה → gnädig isch und anderne tuet lehne (is generous and lends freely)
6 Denn er chunt nie is Wanke; a e Grächte dänkt me für geng.
כִּֽי־לְעוֹלָ֥ם לֹא־יִמּ֑וֹט לְזֵ֥כֶר ע֝וֹלָ֗ם יִהְיֶ֥ה צַדִּֽיק׃
BSB 112:6 Surely he will never be shaken; the righteous man will be remembered forever.
7 Vor eme schlächte Bricht het er nid Angscht; sys Härz isch fescht, es vertrout uf e HERR.
מִשְּׁמוּעָ֣ה רָ֭עָה לֹ֣א יִירָ֑א נָכ֥וֹן לִ֝בּ֗וֹ בָּטֻ֥חַ בַּיהוָֽה׃
BSB 112:7 He does not fear bad news; his heart is steadfast, trusting in the LORD.
מִשְּׁמוּעָה רָעָה → Vor eme schlächte Bricht (of bad news)
8 Sys Härz isch fescht, er het nid Angscht, bis er uf syni Finde abe luegt.
סָמ֣וּךְ לִ֭בּוֹ לֹ֣א יִירָ֑א עַ֖ד אֲשֶׁר־יִרְאֶ֣ה בְצָרָֽיו׃
BSB 112:8 His heart is assured; he does not fear, until he looks in triumph on his foes.
9 Er het usteilt, er het den Arme ggä. Sy Grächtigkeit blybt für geng, syni Hörner wärde höch mit Ehre.
פִּזַּ֤ר׀נָ֘תַ֤ן לָאֶבְיוֹנִ֗ים צִ֭דְקָתוֹ עֹמֶ֣דֶת לָעַ֑ד קַ֝רְנ֗וֹ תָּר֥וּם בְּכָבֽוֹד׃
BSB 112:9 He has scattered abroad his gifts to the poor; his righteousness endures forever; his horn will be lifted high in honor.
קַרְנוֹ → syni Hörner (his horn)
Wie Ps 75; 89; 92.
10 Der Gottlos gseht's und ergeret sech; er tuet mit de Zähn schnadle und vergeit. Was d Gottlose wei, geit zgrund.
רָ֘שָׁ֤ע יִרְאֶ֨ה׀וְכָעָ֗ס שִׁנָּ֣יו יַחֲרֹ֣ק וְנָמָ֑ס תַּאֲוַ֖ת רְשָׁעִ֣ים תֹּאבֵֽד׃
BSB 112:10 The wicked man will see and be grieved; he will gnash his teeth and waste away; the desires of the wicked will perish.
