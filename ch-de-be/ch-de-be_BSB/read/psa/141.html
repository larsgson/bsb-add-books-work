Bhüet my Muul

1 E Psalm vom David.

//1 HERR, //2 i rüefe zu dir; //2 chumm gschwind zu mir! //2 Los uf my Stimm, //2 wen i zu dir rüefe! //1 2 My Gebät söll vor dir gälte wi Weihrouch, //2 wen myni Händ usgstreckt sy, wi ds Opfer am Aabe. //1 3 Stell, //2 HERR, //2 e Wach vor mys Muul, //2 bhüet d Tür vo myne Lippe! //1 4 La mys Härz nid nach böse Sache verlange, //2 dass i nid mit de Lüt, wo Unrächt tüe, //2 gottlosi Tate tüe; //2 vo irne guete Sache wott i nid ässe! //1 5 Wen mi e Grächte schlat, //2 isch's Tröiji; //2 wen er mi straft, //2 isch's Öl für e Chopf; //2 my Chopf söll sech nid dergäge wehre. //2 Denn geng no bätte n i //2 gäge iri böse Tate. //1 6 Wen iri Richter a de Syte vom Felse abe gstosse wärde, //2 de ghöre si myni Wort, //2 dass si fründlech sy. //1 7 Wi wen öpper d Ärde i Stück rysst, //2 so sy üsi Chnoche verströit bi dem Ygang vom Toterych. //1 8 Aber uf di, //2 HERR, my Herr, //2 luege myni Ouge; //2 bi dir suechen i Zueflucht; //2 la mi nid alleini! //1 9 Bhüet mi vor em Lätsch, //2 wo si mer gleit hei, //2 und vor de Falle vo dene, wo Unrächt tüe! //1 10 D Gottlose sölle i iri eigete Netz gheie, //2 alli zäme, //2 und i sälber //2 chumen dervo!

Hebräisch: Westminster Leningrad Codex (gemeinfrei), über MACULA Hebrew (CC BY 4.0, Clear Bible); Griechisch: Berean Greek Bible; Englisch: Berean Standard Bible (beide gemeinfrei). Die Kommentare sind die Notizen des Übersetzungsagenten.
1 E Psalm vom David. HERR, i rüefe zu dir; chumm gschwind zu mir! Los uf my Stimm, wen i zu dir rüefe!
מִזְמ֗וֹר לְדָ֫וִ֥דיְהוָ֣ה קְ֭רָאתִיךָ ח֣וּשָׁה לִּ֑י הַאֲזִ֥ינָה ק֝וֹלִ֗י בְּקָרְאִי־לָֽךְ׃
BSB 141:1 A Psalm of David. I call upon You, O LORD; come quickly to me. Hear my voice when I call to You.
2 My Gebät söll vor dir gälte wi Weihrouch, wen myni Händ usgstreckt sy, wi ds Opfer am Aabe.
תִּכּ֤וֹן תְּפִלָּתִ֣י קְטֹ֣רֶת לְפָנֶ֑יךָ מַֽשְׂאַ֥ת כַּ֝פַּ֗י מִנְחַת־עָֽרֶב׃
BSB 141:2 May my prayer be set before You like incense; my uplifted hands, like the evening offering.
מַשְׂאַת כַּפַּי → wen myni Händ usgstreckt sy (the lifting up of my hands)
מִנְחַת־עָרֶב → ds Opfer am Aabe (the evening offering)
3 Stell, HERR, e Wach vor mys Muul, bhüet d Tür vo myne Lippe!
שִׁיתָ֣ה יְ֭הוָה שָׁמְרָ֣ה לְפִ֑י נִ֝צְּרָ֗ה עַל־דַּ֥ל שְׂפָתָֽי׃
BSB 141:3 Set a guard, O LORD, over my mouth; keep watch at the door of my lips.
4 La mys Härz nid nach böse Sache verlange, dass i nid mit de Lüt, wo Unrächt tüe, gottlosi Tate tüe; vo irne guete Sache wott i nid ässe!
אַל־תַּט־לִבִּ֨י לְדָבָ֪ר׀רָ֡ע לְהִתְע֘וֹלֵ֤ל עֲלִל֨וֹת׀בְּרֶ֗שַׁע אֶת־אִישִׁ֥ים פֹּֽעֲלֵי־אָ֑וֶן וּבַל־אֶ֝לְחַ֗ם בְּמַנְעַמֵּיהֶֽם׃
BSB 141:4 Do not let my heart be drawn to any evil thing or take part in works of wickedness with men who do iniquity; let me not feast on their delicacies.
בְּמַנְעַמֵּיהֶם → vo irne guete Sache (of their delicacies)
5 Wen mi e Grächte schlat, isch's Tröiji; wen er mi straft, isch's Öl für e Chopf; my Chopf söll sech nid dergäge wehre. Denn geng no bätte n i gäge iri böse Tate.
יֶֽהֶלְמֵֽנִי־צַדִּ֨יק׀חֶ֡סֶד וְֽיוֹכִיחֵ֗נִי שֶׁ֣מֶן רֹ֭אשׁ אַל־יָנִ֣י רֹאשִׁ֑י כִּי־ע֥וֹד וּ֝תְפִלָּתִ֗י בְּרָעוֹתֵיהֶֽם׃
BSB 141:5 Let the righteous man strike me; let his rebuke be an act of loving devotion. It is oil for my head; let me not refuse it. For my prayer is ever against the deeds of the wicked.
אַל־יָנִי רֹאשִׁי → my Chopf söll sech nid dergäge wehre (let me not refuse it)
6 Wen iri Richter a de Syte vom Felse abe gstosse wärde, de ghöre si myni Wort, dass si fründlech sy.
נִשְׁמְט֣וּ בִֽידֵי־סֶ֭לַע שֹׁפְטֵיהֶ֑ם וְשָׁמְע֥וּ אֲ֝מָרַ֗י כִּ֣י נָעֵֽמוּ׃
BSB 141:6 When their rulers are thrown down from the cliffs, the people will listen to my words, for they are pleasant.
7 Wi wen öpper d Ärde i Stück rysst, so sy üsi Chnoche verströit bi dem Ygang vom Toterych.
כְּמ֤וֹ פֹלֵ֣חַ וּבֹקֵ֣עַ בָּאָ֑רֶץ נִפְזְר֥וּ עֲ֝צָמֵ֗ינוּ לְפִ֣י שְׁאֽוֹל׃
BSB 141:7 As when one plows and breaks up the soil, so our bones have been scattered at the mouth of Sheol.
כְּמוֹ פֹלֵחַ וּבֹקֵעַ → Wi wen öpper d Ärde i Stück rysst (as when one plows and breaks up)
8 Aber uf di, HERR, my Herr, luege myni Ouge; bi dir suechen i Zueflucht; la mi nid alleini!
כִּ֤י אֵלֶ֨יךָ׀יְהֹוִ֣ה אֲדֹנָ֣י עֵינָ֑י בְּכָ֥ה חָ֝סִ֗יתִי אַל־תְּעַ֥ר נַפְשִֽׁי׃
BSB 141:8 But my eyes are fixed on You, O GOD the Lord. In You I seek refuge; do not leave my soul defenseless.
אַל־תְּעַר נַפְשִׁי → la mi nid alleini (do not leave my soul defenseless)
9 Bhüet mi vor em Lätsch, wo si mer gleit hei, und vor de Falle vo dene, wo Unrächt tüe!
שָׁמְרֵ֗נִי מִ֣ידֵי פַ֭ח יָ֣קְשׁוּ לִ֑י וּ֝מֹקְשׁ֗וֹת פֹּ֣עֲלֵי אָֽוֶן׃
BSB 141:9 Keep me from the snares they have laid for me, and from the lures of evildoers.
10 D Gottlose sölle i iri eigete Netz gheie, alli zäme, und i sälber chumen dervo!
יִפְּל֣וּ בְמַכְמֹרָ֣יו רְשָׁעִ֑ים יַ֥חַד אָ֝נֹכִ֗י עַֽד־אֶעֱבֽוֹר׃
BSB 141:10 Let the wicked fall into their own nets, while I pass by in safety.
