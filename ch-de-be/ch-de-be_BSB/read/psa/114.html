1 Wo Israel us Ägypte uszoge isch, ds Huus vo Jakob us eme Volk, wo e frömdi Sprach redt,
בְּצֵ֣את יִ֭שְׂרָאֵל מִמִּצְרָ֑יִם בֵּ֥ית יַ֝עֲקֹ֗ב מֵעַ֥ם לֹעֵֽז׃
BSB 114:1 When Israel departed from Egypt, the house of Jacob from a people of foreign tongue,
מֵעַם לֹעֵז → us eme Volk, wo e frömdi Sprach redt (from a people of foreign tongue)
2 isch Juda sys Heiligtum worde, Israel sys Chünigrych.
הָיְתָ֣ה יְהוּדָ֣ה לְקָדְשׁ֑וֹ יִ֝שְׂרָאֵ֗ל מַמְשְׁלוֹתָֽיו׃
BSB 114:2 Judah became God’s sanctuary, Israel His dominion.
3 Ds Meer het's gseh und isch gflüchtet, der Jordan isch zrügg gloffe.
הַיָּ֣ם רָ֭אָה וַיָּנֹ֑ס הַ֝יַּרְדֵּ֗ן יִסֹּ֥ב לְאָחֽוֹר׃
BSB 114:3 The sea observed and fled; the Jordan turned back;
4 D Bärge sy gumpet wi Schafböck, d Hoger wi jungi Lämmli.
הֶֽ֭הָרִים רָקְד֣וּ כְאֵילִ֑ים גְּ֝בָע֗וֹת כִּבְנֵי־צֹֽאן׃
BSB 114:4 the mountains skipped like rams, the hills like lambs.
גְּבָעוֹת → d Hoger (the hills)
5 Was isch los mit dir, Meer, dass du gflüchtet bisch? Du Jordan, dass du zrügg gloffe bisch?
מַה־לְּךָ֣ הַ֭יָּם כִּ֣י תָנ֑וּס הַ֝יַּרְדֵּ֗ן תִּסֹּ֥ב לְאָחֽוֹר׃
BSB 114:5 Why was it, O sea, that you fled, O Jordan, that you turned back,
6 Dihr Bärge, dass dihr gumpet heit wi Schafböck, dihr Hoger, wi jungi Lämmli?
הֶֽ֭הָרִים תִּרְקְד֣וּ כְאֵילִ֑ים גְּ֝בָע֗וֹת כִּבְנֵי־צֹֽאן׃
BSB 114:6 O mountains, that you skipped like rams, O hills, like lambs?
7 Zittere vor em Herr, du Ärde, vor em Gott vo Jakob,
מִלִּפְנֵ֣י אָ֭דוֹן ח֣וּלִי אָ֑רֶץ מִ֝לִּפְנֵ֗י אֱל֣וֹהַּ יַעֲקֹֽב׃
BSB 114:7 Tremble, O earth, at the presence of the Lord, at the presence of the God of Jacob,
8 wo der Felse in e See vo Wasser verwandlet het und der hert Stei i ne Quelle vo Wasser!
הַהֹפְכִ֣י הַצּ֣וּר אֲגַם־מָ֑יִם חַ֝לָּמִ֗ישׁ לְמַעְיְנוֹ־מָֽיִם׃
BSB 114:8 who turned the rock into a pool, the flint into a fountain of water!
חַלָּמִישׁ → der hert Stei (the flint)
