1 Es Wallfahrtslied.

//1 Vil hei si mi plaget sit i jung bi – //2 Israel söll's doch säge –, //1 2 vil hei si mi plaget sit i jung bi, //2 aber si hei mi nid möge. //1 3 Uf mym Rügge hei si der Acher umgchehrt, //2 si hei iri Furche lang zoge. //1 4 Der HERR isch grächt; //2 er het d Seili vo de Gottlose verhoue. //1 5 Si sölle z Schande wärde //2 und zrügg müesse, //2 alli, wo Zion hasse! //1 6 Si sölle wärde wi ds Gras uf em Dach, //2 wo verdoret, //2 bevor es rächt gwachse isch; //1 7 dä, wo ärnet, cha syni Hand nid dermit fülle, //2 und dä, wo Garbe bindet, nid sy Arm. //1 8 Und die, wo verby chöme, säge nid: //2 „Der Säge vom HERR syg über öich! //2 Mir sägne öich im Name vom HERR!“

BSB 129:1 A song of ascents. Many a time they have persecuted me from my youth— let Israel now declare—

2 vil hei si mi plaget sit i jung bi, aber si hei mi nid möge.
רַ֭בַּת צְרָר֣וּנִי מִנְּעוּרָ֑י גַּ֝ם לֹא־יָ֥כְלוּ לִֽי׃
BSB 129:2 many a time they have persecuted me from my youth, but they have not prevailed against me.
לֹא־יָכְלוּ לִי → hei mi nid möge (have not prevailed against me)
3 Uf mym Rügge hei si der Acher umgchehrt, si hei iri Furche lang zoge.
עַל־גַּ֭בִּי חָרְשׁ֣וּ חֹרְשִׁ֑ים הֶ֝אֱרִ֗יכוּ לְמַעֲנִיתָֽם׃
BSB 129:3 The plowmen plowed over my back; they made their furrows long.

יְהוָ֥ה צַדִּ֑יק קִ֝צֵּ֗ץ עֲב֣וֹת רְשָׁעִֽים׃
BSB 129:4 The LORD is righteous; He has cut me from the cords of the wicked.
5 Si sölle z Schande wärde und zrügg müesse, alli, wo Zion hasse!
יֵ֭בֹשׁוּ וְיִסֹּ֣גוּ אָח֑וֹר כֹּ֝֗ל שֹׂנְאֵ֥י צִיּֽוֹן׃
BSB 129:5 May all who hate Zion be turned back in shame.
6 Si sölle wärde wi ds Gras uf em Dach, wo verdoret, bevor es rächt gwachse isch;
יִ֭הְיוּ כַּחֲצִ֣יר גַּגּ֑וֹת שֶׁקַּדְמַ֖ת שָׁלַ֣ף יָבֵֽשׁ׃
BSB 129:6 May they be like grass on the rooftops, which withers before it can grow,
שֶׁקַּדְמַת שָׁלַף → bevor es rächt gwachse isch (before it can grow)
Deutung unsicher (»ausreissen« / »aufschiessen«).
7 dä, wo ärnet, cha syni Hand nid dermit fülle, und dä, wo Garbe bindet, nid sy Arm.
שֶׁלֹּ֤א מִלֵּ֖א כַפּ֥וֹ קוֹצֵ֗ר וְחִצְנ֥וֹ מְעַמֵּֽר׃
BSB 129:7 unable to fill the hands of the reaper, or the arms of the binder of sheaves.
8 Und die, wo verby chöme, säge nid: „Der Säge vom HERR syg über öich! Mir sägne öich im Name vom HERR!“
וְלֹ֤א אָֽמְר֨וּ׀הָעֹבְרִ֗ים בִּרְכַּֽת־יְהוָ֥ה אֲלֵיכֶ֑ם בֵּרַ֥כְנוּ אֶ֝תְכֶ֗ם בְּשֵׁ֣ם יְהוָֽה׃
BSB 129:8 May none who pass by say to them, “The blessing of the LORD be on you; we bless you in the name of the LORD.”
