1 Für e Chorleiter. Vo de Söhn vom Korach. E Psalm.
לַמְנַצֵּ֬חַ׀לִבְנֵי־קֹ֬רַח מִזְמֽוֹר׃
2 Du bisch dym Land gnädig gsi, HERR; du hesch em Jakob ds Glück zrügg ggä.
רָצִ֣יתָ יְהוָ֣ה אַרְצֶ֑ךָ שַׁ֝֗בְתָּ שְׁבִ֣ית יַעֲקֹֽב׃
BSB 85:1 For the choirmaster. A Psalm of the sons of Korah. You showed favor to Your land, O LORD; You restored Jacob from captivity.
3 Du hesch d Schuld vo dym Volk vergä, du hesch all iri Sünde zuedeckt. Sela
נָ֭שָׂאתָ עֲוֺ֣ן עַמֶּ֑ךָ כִּסִּ֖יתָ כָל־חַטָּאתָ֣ם סֶֽלָה׃
BSB 85:2 You forgave the iniquity of Your people; You covered all their sin. Selah
נָשָׂאתָ עֲוֺן → d Schuld … vergä (You forgave the iniquity)
4 Du hesch dy ganzi Töubi zrüggzoge, du bisch vo dyr Wuet umgchehrt.
אָסַ֥פְתָּ כָל־עֶבְרָתֶ֑ךָ הֱ֝שִׁיב֗וֹתָ מֵחֲר֥וֹן אַפֶּֽךָ׃
BSB 85:3 You withheld all Your fury; You turned from Your burning anger.
5 Bring is zrügg, Gott vo üsere Rettig, und lass dy Töubi über üüs la sy!
שׁ֭וּבֵנוּ אֱלֹהֵ֣י יִשְׁעֵ֑נוּ וְהָפֵ֖ר כַּֽעַסְךָ֣ עִמָּֽנוּ׃
BSB 85:4 Restore us, O God of our salvation, and put away Your displeasure toward us.
6 Bisch du für geng toub über üüs? Lasch du dy Töubi vo Generation zu Generation duure?
הַלְעוֹלָ֥ם תֶּֽאֱנַף־בָּ֑נוּ תִּמְשֹׁ֥ךְ אַ֝פְּךָ֗ לְדֹ֣ר וָדֹֽר׃
BSB 85:5 Will You be angry with us forever? Will You draw out Your anger to all generations?
7 Wosch du üüs nid wider läbig mache, dass dys Volk sech a dir fröit?
הֲֽלֹא־אַ֭תָּה תָּשׁ֣וּב תְּחַיֵּ֑נוּ וְ֝עַמְּךָ֗ יִשְׂמְחוּ־בָֽךְ׃
BSB 85:6 Will You not revive us again, that Your people may rejoice in You?
8 Zeig üüs, HERR, dy Tröiji, und gib üüs dy Rettig!
הַרְאֵ֣נוּ יְהוָ֣ה חַסְדֶּ֑ךָ וְ֝יֶשְׁעֲךָ֗ תִּתֶּן־לָֽנוּ׃
BSB 85:7 Show us Your loving devotion, O LORD, and grant us Your salvation.
9 I wott lose, was Gott, der HERR, seit: Ja, er redt vo Fride zu sym Volk und zu syne Tröie, numen sölle si nid wider dumm wärde!
אֶשְׁמְעָ֗ה מַה־יְדַבֵּר֮ הָאֵ֪ל׀יְה֫וָ֥ה כִּ֤י׀יְדַבֵּ֬ר שָׁל֗וֹם אֶל־עַמּ֥וֹ וְאֶל־חֲסִידָ֑יו וְֽאַל־יָשׁ֥וּבוּ לְכִסְלָֽה׃
BSB 85:8 I will listen to what God the LORD will say; for He will surely speak peace to His people and His saints; He will not let them return to folly.
וְאַל־יָשׁוּבוּ לְכִסְלָה → numen sölle si nid wider dumm wärde (but let them not return to folly)
10 Ja, sy Rettig isch nach bi dene, wo ne förchte, dass Herrlechkeit i üsem Land wohnt.
אַ֤ךְ׀קָר֣וֹב לִירֵאָ֣יו יִשְׁע֑וֹ לִשְׁכֹּ֖ן כָּב֣וֹד בְּאַרְצֵֽנוּ׃
BSB 85:9 Surely His salvation is near to those who fear Him, that His glory may dwell in our land.
לִשְׁכֹּן כָּבוֹד → dass Herrlechkeit … wohnt (that His glory may dwell)
11 Tröiji und Wahrheit begägnet enand, Grächtigkeit und Fride gä sech es Müntschi.
חֶֽסֶד־וֶאֱמֶ֥ת נִפְגָּ֑שׁוּ צֶ֖דֶק וְשָׁל֣וֹם נָשָֽׁקוּ׃
BSB 85:10 Loving devotion and faithfulness have joined together; righteousness and peace have kissed.
12 Wahrheit wachst us der Ärde, und Grächtigkeit lueget vom Himel abe.
אֱ֭מֶת מֵאֶ֣רֶץ תִּצְמָ֑ח וְ֝צֶ֗דֶק מִשָּׁמַ֥יִם נִשְׁקָֽף׃
BSB 85:11 Faithfulness sprouts from the earth, and righteousness looks down from heaven.
13 Ja, der HERR git ds Guete, und üses Land git sy Ärn.
גַּם־יְ֭הוָה יִתֵּ֣ן הַטּ֑וֹב וְ֝אַרְצֵ֗נוּ תִּתֵּ֥ן יְבוּלָֽהּ׃
BSB 85:12 The LORD will indeed provide what is good, and our land will yield its increase.
14 Grächtigkeit geit vor ihm här und macht uf em Wäg Platz für syni Schritte.
צֶ֭דֶק לְפָנָ֣יו יְהַלֵּ֑ךְ וְיָשֵׂ֖ם לְדֶ֣רֶךְ פְּעָמָֽיו׃
BSB 85:13 Righteousness will go before Him to prepare the way for His steps.
וְיָשֵׂם לְדֶרֶךְ פְּעָמָיו → macht uf em Wäg Platz für syni Schritte (prepares the way for His steps)
