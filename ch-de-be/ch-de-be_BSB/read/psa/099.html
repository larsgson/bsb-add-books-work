Heilig isch der HERR, üse Gott

1 Der HERR isch Chünig! D Völker sölle zittere! Er sitzt uf em Thron über de Cherube, d Ärde söll wanke!
יְהוָ֣ה מָ֭לָךְ יִרְגְּז֣וּ עַמִּ֑ים יֹשֵׁ֥ב כְּ֝רוּבִ֗ים תָּנ֥וּט הָאָֽרֶץ׃
BSB 99:1 The LORD reigns; let the nations tremble! He is enthroned above the cherubim; let the earth quake!
2 Der HERR isch gross i Zion und höch isch är über alli Völker.
יְ֭הוָה בְּצִיּ֣וֹן גָּד֑וֹל וְרָ֥ם ה֝֗וּא עַל־כָּל־הָֽעַמִּֽים׃
BSB 99:2 Great is the LORD in Zion; He is exalted above all the peoples.
3 Si sölle dy Name lobe, dä isch gross und fürchterlech: Är isch heilig!
יוֹד֣וּ שִׁ֭מְךָ גָּד֥וֹל וְנוֹרָ֗א קָד֥וֹשׁ הֽוּא׃
BSB 99:3 Let them praise Your great and awesome name — He is holy!
4 Der mächtig Chünig het ds Rächt gärn. Du hesch fescht gmacht, was ufrichtig isch; Rächt und Grächtigkeit hesch du i Jakob gschaffe.
וְעֹ֥ז מֶלֶךְ֮ מִשְׁפָּ֪ט אָ֫הֵ֥ב אַ֭תָּה כּוֹנַ֣נְתָּ מֵישָׁרִ֑ים מִשְׁפָּ֥ט וּ֝צְדָקָ֗ה בְּיַעֲקֹ֤ב׀אַתָּ֬ה עָשִֽׂיתָ׃
BSB 99:4 The mighty King loves justice. You have established equity; You have exercised justice and righteousness in Jacob.
וְעֹז מֶלֶךְ → Der mächtig Chünig (The mighty King)
5 Rüemet der HERR, üse Gott, und bättet aa vor em Schämeli vo syne Füess! Är isch heilig!
רֽוֹמְמ֡וּ יְה֘וָ֤ה אֱלֹהֵ֗ינוּ וְֽ֭הִשְׁתַּחֲווּ לַהֲדֹ֥ם רַגְלָ֗יו קָד֥וֹשׁ הֽוּא׃
BSB 99:5 Exalt the LORD our God, and worship at His footstool; He is holy!
6 Der Mose und der Aaron under syne Prieschter und der Samuel under dene, wo sy Name aarüefe, die hei zum HERR grüeft, und är het ne Antwort gä.
מֹ֘שֶׁ֤ה וְאַהֲרֹ֨ן׀בְּֽכֹהֲנָ֗יו וּ֭שְׁמוּאֵל בְּקֹרְאֵ֣י שְׁמ֑וֹ קֹרִ֥אים אֶל־יְ֝הוָ֗ה וְה֣וּא יַעֲנֵֽם׃
BSB 99:6 Moses and Aaron were among His priests; Samuel was among those who called on His name. They called to the LORD and He answered.
7 I der Süüle us Wulche het er mit ne gredt; si hei syni Zügnis ghalte und d Ornig, won er ne ggä het.
בְּעַמּ֣וּד עָ֭נָן יְדַבֵּ֣ר אֲלֵיהֶ֑ם שָׁמְר֥וּ עֵ֝דֹתָ֗יו וְחֹ֣ק נָֽתַן־לָֽמוֹ׃
BSB 99:7 He spoke to them from the pillar of cloud; they kept His decrees and the statutes He gave them.
בְּעַמּוּד עָנָן → I der Süüle us Wulche (from the pillar of cloud)
8 HERR, üse Gott, du hesch ne Antwort ggä; du bisch ne e Gott gsi, wo vergit, aber o eine, wo straft für das, wo si Böses gmacht hei.
יְהוָ֣ה אֱלֹהֵינוּ֮ אַתָּ֪ה עֲנִ֫יתָ֥ם אֵ֣ל נֹ֭שֵׂא הָיִ֣יתָ לָהֶ֑ם וְ֝נֹקֵ֗ם עַל־עֲלִילוֹתָֽם׃
BSB 99:8 O LORD our God, You answered them. You were a forgiving God to them, yet an avenger of their misdeeds.
אֵל נֹשֵׂא → e Gott, wo vergit (a forgiving God)
וְנֹקֵם עַל־עֲלִילוֹתָם → aber o eine, wo straft für das, wo si Böses gmacht hei (yet an avenger of their misdeeds)
9 Rüemet der HERR, üse Gott, und bättet aa uf sym heilige Bärg! Denn der HERR, üse Gott, isch heilig!
רֽוֹמְמ֡וּ יְה֘וָ֤ה אֱלֹהֵ֗ינוּ וְ֭הִֽשְׁתַּחֲווּ לְהַ֣ר קָדְשׁ֑וֹ כִּֽי־קָ֝ד֗וֹשׁ יְהוָ֥ה אֱלֹהֵֽינוּ׃
BSB 99:9 Exalt the LORD our God and worship at His holy mountain, for the LORD our God is holy.
