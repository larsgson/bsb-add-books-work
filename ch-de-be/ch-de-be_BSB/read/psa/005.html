לַמְנַצֵּ֥חַ אֶֽל־הַנְּחִיל֗וֹת מִזְמ֥וֹר לְדָוִֽד׃

2 Los uf myni Wort, HERR, gib acht uf mys Süüfzge!
אֲמָרַ֖י הַאֲזִ֥ינָה׀יְהוָ֗ה בִּ֣ינָה הֲגִֽיגִי׃
BSB 5:1 For the choirmaster, to be accompanied by flutes. A Psalm of David. Give ear to my words, O LORD; consider my groaning.
הֲגִיגִי → mys Süüfzge (my groaning)
»süüfzge« 6×.
3 Los uf my Stimm, wen i um Hilf rüefe, my Chünig und my Gott, denn zu dir bätten i!
הַקְשִׁ֤יבָה׀לְק֬וֹל שַׁוְעִ֗י מַלְכִּ֥י וֵאלֹהָ֑י כִּֽי־אֵ֝לֶ֗יךָ אֶתְפַּלָּֽל׃
BSB 5:2 Attend to the sound of my cry, my King and my God, for to You I pray.
4 HERR, am Morge ghörsch du my Stimm, am Morge legen i dir alls vor und luege us.
יְֽהוָ֗ה בֹּ֭קֶרתִּשְׁמַ֣ע קוֹלִ֑י בֹּ֥קֶר אֶֽעֱרָךְ־לְ֝ךָ֗ וַאֲצַפֶּֽה׃
BSB 5:3 In the morning, O LORD, You hear my voice; at daybreak I lay my plea before You and wait in expectation.
אֶעֱרָךְ־לְךָ וַאֲצַפֶּה → legen i dir alls vor und luege us (I lay my plea before You and wait)
ʿārak ohne Objekt (Opfer oder Rede).
5 Denn du bisch ke Gott, wo a der Gottlosigkeit Fröid het, der Bös darf nid bi dir z Gascht sy.
כִּ֤י׀לֹ֤א אֵֽל־חָפֵ֘ץ רֶ֥שַׁע׀אָ֑תָּה לֹ֖א יְגֻרְךָ֣ רָֽע׃
BSB 5:4 For You are not a God who delights in wickedness; no evil can dwell with You.
לֹא יְגֻרְךָ רָע → der Bös darf nid bi dir z Gascht sy (no evil can dwell with You)
gûr »als Gast weilen«.
6 D Hoffärtige chöi nid vor dyne Ouge besta; du hassisch alli, wo Unrächt tüe.
לֹֽא־יִתְיַצְּב֣וּ הֽ֭וֹלְלִים לְנֶ֣גֶד עֵינֶ֑יךָ שָׂ֝נֵ֗אתָ כָּל־פֹּ֥עֲלֵי אָֽוֶן׃
BSB 5:5 The boastful cannot stand in Your presence; You hate all workers of iniquity.
פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן → alli, wo Unrächt tüe (workers of iniquity)
Fest (`formula:poale_aven`).
7 Du bringsch die um, wo Lugi rede; vor Mörder und hinderlischtige Lüt gruuset em HERR.
תְּאַבֵּד֮ דֹּבְרֵ֪י כָ֫זָ֥ב אִישׁ־דָּמִ֥ים וּמִרְמָ֗ה יְתָ֘עֵ֥ב׀יְהוָֽה׃
BSB 5:6 You destroy those who tell lies; the LORD abhors the man of bloodshed and deceit.
אִישׁ־דָּמִים וּמִרְמָה → Mörder und hinderlischtigi Lüt (the man of bloodshed and deceit)
8 Aber ig darf i dys Huus cho, wil dy Tröiji so gross isch; i bätte aa gäge dym heilige Tämpel i Ehrfurcht vor dir.
וַאֲנִ֗י בְּרֹ֣ב חַ֭סְדְּךָ אָב֣וֹא בֵיתֶ֑ךָ אֶשְׁתַּחֲוֶ֥ה אֶל־הֵֽיכַל־קָ֝דְשְׁךָ֗ בְּיִרְאָתֶֽךָ׃
BSB 5:7 But I will enter Your house by the abundance of Your loving devotion; in reverence I will bow down toward Your holy temple.
אֶשְׁתַּחֲוֶה → i bätte aa (I will bow down)
Fest (`term:H7812`).
9 HERR, füer mi i dyr Grächtigkeit wäge myne Finde, mach dy Wäg äbe vor mir!
יְהוָ֤ה׀נְחֵ֬נִי בְצִדְקָתֶ֗ךָ לְמַ֥עַן שׁוֹרְרָ֑י הַיְשַׁ֖ר לְפָנַ֣י דַּרְכֶּֽךָ׃
BSB 5:8 Lead me, O LORD, in Your righteousness because of my enemies; make straight Your way before me.
10 Denn i irem Muul isch nüüt, wo me sech druf cha verla; ires Innerschte isch Verdärbe. Iri Gurgle isch es offes Grab, mit irne Zunge rede si glatt.
כִּ֤י אֵ֪ין בְּפִ֡יהוּ נְכוֹנָה֮ קִרְבָּ֪ם הַ֫וּ֥וֹת קֶֽבֶר־פָּת֥וּחַ גְּרוֹנָ֑ם לְ֝שׁוֹנָ֗ם יַחֲלִֽיקוּן׃
BSB 5:9 For not a word they speak can be trusted; destruction lies within them. Their throats are open graves; their tongues practice deceit.
קֶבֶר־פָּתוּחַ גְּרוֹנָם → Iri Gurgle isch es offes Grab (Their throats are open graves)
Röm 3,13 »Gurgle«.
11 Lass se d Schuld trage, Gott! Si sölle über iri eigete Plän gheie! Wäg irne vile Verbräche jag se furt, denn si hei sech gäge di gstellt.
הַֽאֲשִׁימֵ֨ם׀אֱֽלֹהִ֗ים יִפְּלוּ֮ מִֽמֹּעֲצ֪וֹתֵ֫יהֶ֥ם בְּרֹ֣ב פִּ֭שְׁעֵיהֶם הַדִּיחֵ֑מוֹ כִּי־מָ֥רוּ בָֽךְ׃
BSB 5:10 Declare them guilty, O God; let them fall by their own devices. Drive them out for their many transgressions, for they have rebelled against You.
הַאֲשִׁימֵם → Lass se d Schuld trage (Declare them guilty)
12 Aber alli, wo bi dir Zueflucht sueche, sölle sech fröie, für geng sölle si juble. Breit dy Schutz über se uus, de fröie sech die über di, wo dy Name lieb hei.
וְיִשְׂמְח֨וּ כָל־ח֪וֹסֵי בָ֡ךְ לְעוֹלָ֣ם יְ֭רַנֵּנוּ וְתָסֵ֣ךְ עָלֵ֑ימוֹ וְֽיַעְלְצ֥וּ בְ֝ךָ֗ אֹהֲבֵ֥י שְׁמֶֽךָ׃
BSB 5:11 But let all who take refuge in You rejoice; let them ever shout for joy. May You shelter them, that those who love Your name may rejoice in You.
13 Denn du, HERR, sägnisch der Grächt, wi mit emene Schild umgisch du ne mit dyr Gnad.
כִּֽי־אַתָּה֮ תְּבָרֵ֪ךְ צַ֫דִּ֥יק יְהוָ֑ה כַּ֝צִּנָּ֗ה רָצ֥וֹן תַּעְטְרֶֽנּוּ׃
BSB 5:12 For surely You, O LORD, bless the righteous; You surround them with the shield of Your favor.
כַּצִּנָּה רָצוֹן → wi mit emene Schild … mit dyr Gnad (with the shield of Your favor)
ṣinnāh grosser Schild; rāṣôn »Gnad«.
