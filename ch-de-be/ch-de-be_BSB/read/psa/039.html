BSB 39:2 I was speechless and still; I remained silent, even from speaking good, and my sorrow was stirred.

BSB 39:3 My heart grew hot within me; as I mused, the fire burned. Then I spoke with my tongue:

BSB 39:4 “Show me, O LORD, my end and the measure of my days. Let me know how fleeting my life is.

BSB 39:5 You, indeed, have made my days as handbreadths, and my lifetime as nothing before You. Truly each man at his best exists as but a breath. Selah

BSB 39:7 And now, O Lord, for what do I wait? My hope is in You.
9 Rett mi us all myne Sünde, mach mi nid zum Gspött vo de Dumme!
מִכָּל־פְּשָׁעַ֥י הַצִּילֵ֑נִי חֶרְפַּ֥ת נָ֝בָ֗ל אַל־תְּשִׂימֵֽנִי׃
BSB 39:8 Deliver me from all my transgressions; do not make me the reproach of fools.
10 I bi still, i tue mys Muul nid uf, denn du hesch's gmacht.
נֶ֭אֱלַמְתִּי לֹ֣א אֶפְתַּח־פִּ֑י כִּ֖י אַתָּ֣ה עָשִֽׂיתָ׃
BSB 39:9 I have become mute; I do not open my mouth because of what You have done.
11 Nimm dy Plag vo mir wäg! Vom Schlag vo dyr Hand bin i am Änd.
הָסֵ֣ר מֵעָלַ֣י נִגְעֶ֑ךָ מִתִּגְרַ֥ת יָ֝דְךָ֗ אֲנִ֣י כָלִֽיתִי׃
BSB 39:10 Remove Your scourge from me; I am perishing by the force of Your hand.
