שׁ֤וּבִי שׁ֨וּבִי֙ הַשּׁ֣וּלַמִּ֔ית שׁ֥וּבִי שׁ֖וּבִי וְנֶחֱזֶה־בָּ֑ךְ מַֽה־תֶּחֱזוּ֙ בַּשּׁ֣וּלַמִּ֔ית כִּמְחֹלַ֖ת הַֽמַּחֲנָֽיִם׃
BSB 6:13 Come back, come back, O Shulammite! Come back, come back, that we may gaze upon you. Why do you look at the Shulammite, as on the dance of Mahanaim ?

מַה־יָּפ֧וּ פְעָמַ֛יִךְ בַּנְּעָלִ֖ים בַּת־נָדִ֑יב חַמּוּקֵ֣י יְרֵכַ֔יִךְ כְּמ֣וֹ חֲלָאִ֔ים מַעֲשֵׂ֖ה יְדֵ֥י אָמָּֽן׃
BSB 7:1 How beautiful are your sandaled feet, O daughter of the prince! The curves of your thighs are like jewels, the handiwork of a master.
→ du fyrstedatter (O daughter of the prince)
bat-nādîb.
3 Din navle er en rund skål, lad den aldrig mangle krydret vin! Dit liv er en hvededynge, omgærdet af liljer.
שָׁרְרֵךְ֙ אַגַּ֣ן הַסַּ֔הַר אַל־יֶחְסַ֖ר הַמָּ֑זֶג בִּטְנֵךְ֙ עֲרֵמַ֣ת חִטִּ֔ים סוּגָ֖ה בַּשּׁוֹשַׁנִּֽים׃
BSB 7:2 Your navel is a rounded goblet; it never lacks blended wine. Your waist is a mound of wheat encircled by the lilies.
→ Din navle … Dit liv er en hvededynge (Your navel … Your waist is a mound of wheat)
4 Dine to bryster er som to hjortekalve, tvillinger af en gazelle.
שְׁנֵ֥י שָׁדַ֛יִךְ כִּשְׁנֵ֥י עֳפָרִ֖ים תָּאֳמֵ֥י צְבִיָּֽה׃
BSB 7:3 Your breasts are like two fawns, twins of a gazelle.
→ to hjortekalve, tvillinger af en gazelle (two fawns, twins of a gazelle)
Som 4,5.

צַוָּארֵ֖ךְ כְּמִגְדַּ֣ל הַשֵּׁ֑ן עֵינַ֜יִךְ בְּרֵכ֣וֹת בְּחֶשְׁבּ֗וֹן עַל־שַׁ֨עַר֙ בַּת־רַבִּ֔ים אַפֵּךְ֙ כְּמִגְדַּ֣ל הַלְּבָנ֔וֹן צוֹפֶ֖ה פְּנֵ֥י דַמָּֽשֶׂק׃
BSB 7:4 Your neck is like a tower made of ivory; your eyes are like the pools of Heshbon by the gate of Bath-rabbim; your nose is like the tower of Lebanon, facing toward Damascus.
→ et tårn af elfenben … Heshbon … Bat-Rabbim … Libanontårnet … Damaskus
Heshbon, Damaskus som DFB.

BSB 7:5 Your head crowns you like Mount Carmel, the hair of your head like purple threads; the king is captured in your tresses.

7 Hvor smuk du er, og hvor yndig, du kærlighed, med dine fryder!
מַה־יָּפִית֙ וּמַה־נָּעַ֔מְתְּ אַהֲבָ֖ה בַּתַּֽעֲנוּגִֽים׃
BSB 7:6 How fair and pleasant you are, O love, with your delights!
→ du kærlighed, med dine fryder (O love, with your delights)

8 Din vækst ligner en palme, og dine bryster er som klaser.
זֹ֤את קֽוֹמָתֵךְ֙ דָּֽמְתָ֣ה לְתָמָ֔ר וְשָׁדַ֖יִךְ לְאַשְׁכֹּלֽוֹת׃
BSB 7:7 Your stature is like a palm tree; your breasts are clusters of fruit.

אָמַ֨רְתִּי֙ אֶעֱלֶ֣ה בְתָמָ֔ר אֹֽחֲזָ֖ה בְּסַנְסִנָּ֑יו וְיִֽהְיוּ־נָ֤א שָׁדַ֨יִךְ֙ כְּאֶשְׁכְּל֣וֹת הַגֶּ֔פֶן וְרֵ֥יחַ אַפֵּ֖ךְ כַּתַּפּוּחִֽים׃
BSB 7:8 I said, “I will climb the palm tree; I will take hold of its fruit.” May your breasts be like clusters of the vine, the fragrance of your breath like apples,

10 og din gane som den gode vin. Den glider lige ned til min elskede, den strømmer over de sovendes læber.
וְחִכֵּ֕ךְ כְּיֵ֥ין הַטּ֛וֹב הוֹלֵ֥ךְ לְדוֹדִ֖י לְמֵישָׁרִ֑ים דּוֹבֵ֖ב שִׂפְתֵ֥י יְשֵׁנִֽים׃
BSB 7:9 and your mouth like the finest wine. May it flow smoothly to my beloved, gliding gently over lips and teeth.
→ over de sovendes læber (over lips and teeth)
MT; {T:} BSB.
11 Jeg er min elskedes, og hans længsel er efter mig.
אֲנִ֣י לְדוֹדִ֔י וְעָלַ֖י תְּשׁוּקָתֽוֹ׃ס
BSB 7:10 I belong to my beloved, and his desire is for me.
→ Jeg er min elskedes, og hans længsel er efter mig (I belong to my beloved, and his desire is for me)
Jf. 2,16; 6,3. tᵊšûqāh som 1 Mos 3,16.
12 Kom, min elskede, lad os gå ud på marken, lad os overnatte blandt hennablomsterne.
לְכָ֤ה דוֹדִי֙ נֵצֵ֣א הַשָּׂדֶ֔ה נָלִ֖ינָה בַּכְּפָרִֽים׃
BSB 7:11 Come, my beloved, let us go to the countryside; let us spend the night among the wildflowers.
→ blandt hennablomsterne (among the wildflowers)
{T:} »i landsbyerne«.
13 Lad os tidligt gå til vingårdene, lad os se, om vinstokken har sat knopper, om blomsten er sprunget ud, om granatæbletræerne blomstrer; der vil jeg give dig min elskov.
נַשְׁכִּ֨ימָה֙ לַכְּרָמִ֔ים נִרְאֶ֞ה אִם פָּֽרְחָ֤ה הַגֶּ֨פֶן֙ פִּתַּ֣ח הַסְּמָדַ֔ר הֵנֵ֖צוּ הָרִמּוֹנִ֑ים שָׁ֛ם אֶתֵּ֥ן אֶת־דֹּדַ֖י לָֽךְ׃
BSB 7:12 Let us go early to the vineyards to see if the vine has budded, if the blossom has opened, if the pomegranates are in bloom — there I will give you my love.
→ har sat knopper … er sprunget ud … blomstrer (budded … opened … in bloom)
Som 6,11.
→ min elskov (my love)
dōdîm.
14 Alrunerne spreder duft, og over vores døre er alle slags kostelige frugter, både nye og gamle, som jeg har gemt til dig, min elskede.
הַֽדּוּדָאִ֣ים נָֽתְנוּ־רֵ֗יחַ וְעַל־פְּתָחֵ֨ינוּ֙ כָּל־מְגָדִ֔ים חֲדָשִׁ֖ים גַּם־יְשָׁנִ֑ים דּוֹדִ֖י צָפַ֥נְתִּי לָֽךְ׃
BSB 7:13 The mandrakes send forth a fragrance, and at our door is every delicacy, new as well as old, that I have treasured up for you, my beloved.
→ Alrunerne (The mandrakes)
Jf. 1 Mos 30,14.
