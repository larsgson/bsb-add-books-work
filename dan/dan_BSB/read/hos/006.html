6 For trofasthed ønsker jeg og ikke offer, og kundskab om Gud mere end brændofre.
כִּ֛י חֶ֥סֶד חָפַ֖צְתִּי וְלֹא־זָ֑בַח וְדַ֥עַת אֱלֹהִ֖ים מֵעֹלֽוֹת׃
BSB 6:6 For I desire mercy, not sacrifice, and the knowledge of God rather than burnt offerings.

BSB 6:9 Like raiders who lie in ambush, so does a band of priests; they murder on the way to Shechem; surely they have committed atrocities.
