1 Kvindernes visdom bygger sit hus, men dårskaben river det ned med sine egne hænder.
חַכְמ֣וֹת נָ֭שִׁים בָּנְתָ֣ה בֵיתָ֑הּ וְ֝אִוֶּ֗לֶת בְּיָדֶ֥יהָ תֶהֶרְסֶֽנּוּ׃
BSB 14:1 Every wise woman builds her house, but a foolish one tears it down with her own hands.

2 Den der vandrer i oprigtighed, frygter HERREN, men den der går krogveje, foragter ham.
הוֹלֵ֣ךְ בְּ֭יָשְׁרוֹ יְרֵ֣א יְהוָ֑ה וּנְל֖וֹז דְּרָכָ֣יו בּוֹזֵֽהוּ׃
BSB 14:2 He who walks in uprightness fears the LORD, but the one who is devious in his ways despises Him.
3 I dårens mund er et ris for hans hovmod, men de vises læber vogter dem.
בְּֽפִי־אֱ֭וִיל חֹ֣טֶר גַּאֲוָ֑ה וְשִׂפְתֵ֥י חֲ֝כָמִ֗ים תִּשְׁמוּרֵֽם׃
BSB 14:3 The proud speech of a fool brings a rod to his back, but the lips of the wise protect them.
→ et ris for hans hovmod (a rod to his back)
ḥōṭer gaʔăwāh.
4 Hvor der ingen okser er, er krybben tom, men megen afgrøde kommer ved oksens kraft.
בְּאֵ֣ין אֲ֭לָפִים אֵב֣וּס בָּ֑ר וְרָב־תְּ֝בוּא֗וֹת בְּכֹ֣חַ שֽׁוֹר׃
BSB 14:4 Where there are no oxen, the manger is empty, but an abundant harvest comes through the strength of the ox.
5 Et troværdigt vidne lyver ikke, men et falsk vidne farer med løgn.
עֵ֣ד אֱ֭מוּנִים לֹ֣א יְכַזֵּ֑ב וְיָפִ֥יחַ כְּ֝זָבִ֗ים עֵ֣ד שָֽׁקֶר׃
BSB 14:5 An honest witness does not deceive, but a dishonest witness pours forth lies.
6 Spotteren søger visdom, og der er ingen, men for den forstandige er kundskab let.
בִּקֶּשׁ־לֵ֣ץ חָכְמָ֣ה וָאָ֑יִן וְדַ֖עַת לְנָב֣וֹן נָקָֽל׃
BSB 14:6 A mocker seeks wisdom and finds none, but knowledge comes easily to the discerning.
7 Gå bort fra tåbens nærhed, for du har ikke mødt kundskabens læber dér.
לֵ֣ךְ מִ֭נֶּגֶד לְאִ֣ישׁ כְּסִ֑יל וּבַל־יָ֝דַ֗עְתָּ שִׂפְתֵי־דָֽעַת׃
BSB 14:7 Stay away from a foolish man; you will gain no knowledge from his speech.
8 Den kloges visdom er at forstå sin vej, men tåbernes dårskab er svig.
חָכְמַ֣ת עָ֭רוּם הָבִ֣ין דַּרְכּ֑וֹ וְאִוֶּ֖לֶת כְּסִילִ֣ים מִרְמָֽה׃
BSB 14:8 The wisdom of the prudent is to discern his way, but the folly of fools deceives them.
9 Dårer spotter skyldofferet, men blandt de oprigtige er der velbehag.
אֱ֭וִלִים יָלִ֣יץ אָשָׁ֑ם וּבֵ֖ין יְשָׁרִ֣ים רָצֽוֹן׃

BSB 14:10 The heart knows its own bitterness, and no stranger shares in its joy.

BSB 14:11 The house of the wicked will be destroyed, but the tent of the upright will flourish.

BSB 14:12 There is a way that seems right to a man, but its end is the way of death.

BSB 14:13 Even in laughter the heart may ache, and joy may end in sorrow.

BSB 14:15 The simple man believes every word, but the prudent man watches his steps.

BSB 14:20 The poor man is hated even by his neighbor, but many are those who love the rich.

BSB 14:23 There is profit in all labor, but mere talk leads only to poverty.

Den vise kvinde bygger sit hus
